## Summary
The speaker is discussing the concept of pre-pack insolvency and its relevance for MSMEs. Pre-pack insolvency is a hybrid solution that combines the flexibility of One Time Settlement (OTS) and the disciplined process of Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The speaker explains that the pre-pack insolvency process was introduced to provide relief to the MSME sector, especially for small companies with defaults less than one crore. The process is available for micro, small, and medium enterprises and the provisions related to this process are dealt with in Chapter 3A of the IBC. The speaker also highlights the key features of the pre-pack insolvency process, such as the role of insolvency professionals, the timeline for resolution, and the voting threshold for financial creditors.
