## Summary
The speaker, Pankaj Khetan, discussed several challenges faced in personal guarantor to corporate data cases under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). These include slow proceedings, valuation issues, difficulty in getting fees, and the need for a standardized fee scale. Suggestions were made for the IBB to formulate a scale of fees and for regulations to include templates for repayment plans and submission of audited financial statements by the bankrupt. Participants also discussed the need for clearer timelines and procedures for appointment of resolution professionals, and for explicit provisions in the code for handling rejected repayment plans.
