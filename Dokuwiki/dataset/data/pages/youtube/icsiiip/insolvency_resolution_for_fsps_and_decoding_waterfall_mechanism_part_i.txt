## Summary
The workshop is focused on understanding the need for a separate framework for financial service providers (FSPs) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The speaker explains that banks, as large financial utilities, cannot be handled through normal insolvency processes due to the large number of creditors and depositors involved. The impact of a banking institution's insolvency can have systemic effects, affecting trust in the system and potentially causing a contagion effect. The speaker then discusses the definition of a corporate person, which excludes FSPs, but notes that the government has the power to modify IBC procedures for FSPs through Section 227. The section was operationalized in May 2018, and the government has since rolled out rules for insolvency resolution and liquidation of FSPs, excluding banks. The speaker raises a question about the lack of regulations for insolvency of financial institutions in the IBC.
