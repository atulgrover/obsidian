## Summary
This webinar, held by ICSIIIT, discusses the role of the Limitation Act in the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) through the analysis of three Supreme Court cases. The first case, Punjab National Bank v. Vijay Sita Raman RAM and others, involves an application under Section 7 of the IBC by Punjab National Bank against M/s Sugar Products Limited. The discussion revolves around the right to claim and the dispute, and how the Limitation Act plays a role in the admission of the application. The second case, Aluminium Industries Private Limited v. Clean Steel Corporation, deals with the question of limitation and whether the application under Section 7, filed in 2019, is beyond three years from the date of default (NPA) in 2013. The Supreme Court clarified that the Limitation Act applies to the proceedings under IBC, and the maintainability of the application under Section 7 is affirmed. The definitions of the word 'default' and the provision of Section 238 of the IBC are discussed in detail.
