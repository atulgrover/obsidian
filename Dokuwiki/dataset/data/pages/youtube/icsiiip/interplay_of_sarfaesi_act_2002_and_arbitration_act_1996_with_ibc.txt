## Summary
This workshop covers the SARFAESI Act of 2002 and its interplay with the IBC. The speaker explains the need for SARFAESI and IBC, stating that SARFAESI was introduced to enable banks to enforce their security interest without court intervention for faster debt recovery. However, when industrial factors affect a borrower's repayment capacity, resolution of the company may be required, leading to the introduction of the IBC. The speaker then discusses the scheme of SARFAESI, highlighting 13 provisions and defining key terms such as 'borrower,' 'secured creditor,' and 'security interest.' They also mention recent judgments and proceedings related to SARFAESI and IBC.
