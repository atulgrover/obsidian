## Summary
The webinar focuses on the Supreme Court judgment in the case of Anujan Jain Protect vs. Axis Bank Limited and GP Infraprojects Limited. The main issues involve the treatment of a creation of charge in favor of lenders as a preferential transaction and the recognition of lenders as financial creditors. The case concerns JP Infra Limited (JIL), a subsidiary of JP Associates Limited (JAL), which raised loans from various banks secured by assets owned by JAL. When JIL was admitted to insolvency proceedings, the lenders filed claims as secured financial creditors, which were dismissed by the IRP. The IRP also deemed the mortgage a preferential transaction, which was upheld by the NCLT and later by the Supreme Court. The Supreme Court interpreted Section 5(8) of the IBC to determine who should be treated as a financial creditor. The lenders of JIL approached the Supreme Court to challenge their non-recognition as financial creditors of JIL, which was also upheld by the court.
