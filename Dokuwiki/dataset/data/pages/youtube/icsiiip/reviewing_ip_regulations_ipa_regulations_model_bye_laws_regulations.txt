## Summary
Welcome to the fifth webinar in the series reviewing regulations. Today's topic is IPA regulations, IP regulations, and model bylaws. The speaker, Pankaj Khetan, begins by discussing the IPA regulations and invites comments on any amendments needed. The regulations related to short title, commencement, definition, and model bylaws do not seem to require any changes. The eligibility for registration, insolvency professional agency bylaws, and grant of certificate of registration also do not require any changes. The discussion then moves on to the need for a separate ombudsman and the possibility of simplifying the registration and disclosure process. The participants also suggest the need for uniformity in compliance and reporting practices across IP professionals. The issue of empanelment every six months is raised, and it is suggested that the impairment list should be automated and based on the expiry of the AFA. The participants also suggest that the ibbi should create a list of IPS based on the nclt and allow automatic assignment of cases to IPS from the ibbi website. The maximum number of assignments for an IPS is discussed, and it is suggested that there should be a ceiling on the total number of IPS assignments, including CIRP and liquidation cases. The participants also suggest that the ibbi should ensure transparency in the hiring of IPS by banks and allow IPS to take help from other IPEs if needed. The issue of infrastructure and experience of IPS is also discussed.
