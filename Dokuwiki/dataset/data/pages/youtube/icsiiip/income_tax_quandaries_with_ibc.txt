## Summary
The webinar discusses various quandaries related to income tax in the context of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The first part focuses on the nature of income tax liabilities and the anomaly arising from the Rainbow Papers ruling. The second part covers tax implications of non-resolution, taxation of remission of liability, and tax holidays. The third part discusses tax implications on liquidation and tax certification on voluntary liquidation. The fourth part covers procedural aspects and practical issues faced by Insolvency Professionals (IPS). The discussion is divided into four scenarios: tax liabilities prior to the initiation of insolvency, during the insolvency period, post-approval of resolution plan, and in case of no successful resolution leading to liquidation. The webinar highlights the Supreme Court rulings in Monitor's Path and Rainbow Papers, which have created confusion regarding the treatment of tax dues as secured or unsecured. The speakers, Vinod Kothari and Shikha Bansal, invite participants to share their experiences and views on these issues.
