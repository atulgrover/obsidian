Deepika V Prasad, a qualified cost and management accountant and insolvency professional, discusses the waterfall mechanism under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016. The session also covers the insolvency resolution process costs, liquidation costs, workman's dues, debts to secured and unsecured creditors, financial debts, and the role of the Liquidator.
