## Summary
Welcome to the workshop on insights of the power and manufacturing sectors under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The session is led by Mr. Navneet Kumar Gupta, a chartered accountant and insolvency professional with over 20 years of experience, and Mr. Anish Nanavaty, a power sector expert with over 28 years of experience. The workshop covers the economic status, major issues, and statistics of the sectors, their relation to IBC, and the current status of major industries and case laws. Mr. Gupta shares his experience in resolving a power company's issues, the challenges faced, and the successful handover to Tata Steel. Mr. Nanavaty discusses the technical aspects of the power plant, the environmental issues, and the need for continuous operation and safety. He emphasizes the responsibility of the Insolvency Resolution Professional (IRP) as the de facto owner of the factory and the importance of managing the power plant safely and efficiently.
