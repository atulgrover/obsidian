## Summary
In this webinar on reviewing pre-packed insolvency resolution regulations, speaker Pankaj Khetan discusses the purpose and success rate of pre-pack insolvency. The government introduced this for MSMEs as a fast track resolution process, but the success rate has not been high. One reason for its failure is that financial creditors must be on board before starting the exercise, which has not been achieved. Banks are hesitant to adopt pre-pack insolvency, possibly due to lethargy or lack of proactiveness. The panelists suggest that financial creditors need to be professionally equipped to handle the industry and that the ecosystem should be considered before implementing any law. The quality of information exchange with bankers and the commercial wisdom of the COC are also important factors. The webinar also covers suggestions for improvements in the regulations, including the definition clause, essential supplies, and security services.
