## Summary
The discussion, led by Ravi Prakash Ganti, focuses on the recent discussion paper circulated by IBBI on the subject of voluntary liquidation process regulations 2017. The paper proposes certain amendments concerning timelines and compliance certificate. The main objective of these amendments is to improve the ease of exit and fast track the voluntary liquidation process. The first proposal is to reduce the time allowed for preparation of the list of stakeholders in a no creditor situation from 45 days to 15 days. The participants discuss the definition of stakeholders and the practical challenges that liquidators might face, especially in cases involving foreign claimants. The second proposal is to reduce the distribution period from 6 months to 30 days. The participants discuss the starting point of the 30-day period and the challenges in opening a liquidation account. They also discuss the possibility of distributing from the existing account if the liquidation account is not opened due to banking difficulties.
