## Summary
The speaker, Madhusudan Sharma, discussed the challenges faced by Insolvency Professionals (IPs) in terms of regulatory compliances. The ecosystem in which IPs operate is characterized by poor governance and a premium for non-compliance, leading to increased non-compliance. The speaker mentioned that there are too many compliances required by different agencies, such as NCLT, IBBI, and IPA, which can be difficult to manage simultaneously. The speaker also mentioned that the remuneration for IPs should be sufficient and transparent to ensure the sustainability of the profession. The speaker then discussed the obligations cast on IPs by various regulations, such as grievance complaint IP inspection investigation IPA and model of the governing board. The speaker mentioned that the compliances have become more complex over time, and IPs need to be like a Lotus, always floating on the mud and not getting influenced by it. The speaker also discussed the Bankruptcy Law Reform Committee report, which mentioned the complexity of laws and regulations that can give rise to temptations for professionals to deviate from the path of right conduct. The speaker mentioned that the role of IPs is vital to the efficient operation of the insolvency and bankruptcy resolution process. The speaker also shared examples of misconduct by IPs that have led to stricter regulations and monitoring by the regulators and adjudicating authorities.
