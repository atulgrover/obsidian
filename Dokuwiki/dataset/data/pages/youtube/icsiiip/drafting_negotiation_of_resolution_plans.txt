## Summary
Welcome to the webinar on drafting and negotiation of resolution plans. The speaker, Ashish Makhija, is a leading legal practitioner in insolvency and corporate law. He emphasizes the importance of resolution plans in the insolvency process and the high responsibility of the resolution professional in examining these plans. The webinar will discuss pertinent questions such as the withdrawal and modification of plans, as well as the role of the RP in drafting and negotiation. The structure of the IBC and relevant judicial precedents will also be covered. One notable trend is the late entry of promoters in the consideration of plans, which will be discussed along with other issues.
