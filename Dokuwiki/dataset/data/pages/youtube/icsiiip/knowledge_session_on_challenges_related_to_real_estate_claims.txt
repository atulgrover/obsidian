## Summary
In this webinar session, Shalini Shrivastav and Abhirup Ganguli discussed the challenges and real-life scenarios related to claims in the real estate sector. They emphasized the importance of contracts, purchase orders, and work orders in determining the conditions of transactions. Invoices, material receipt notes, and service completion certificates are crucial in validating claims from operational creditors. Home buyers who form part of financial creditors are eligible for interest on payments given to the builder, as per the Builder-buyer agreement or IBBI regulation. Arbitration awards to home buyers can be financial or other creditor claims, depending on the nature of the claim. Cancelled allotments in real estate sector can be contested by home buyers, and the refund of money depends on the terms and conditions of the Builder-buyer agreement. The speakers also highlighted the importance of assessing the nature of the claim for decision-making and COC constitution.
