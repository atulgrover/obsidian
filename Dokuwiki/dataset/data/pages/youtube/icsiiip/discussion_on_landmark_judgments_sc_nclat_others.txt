## Summary
Anjali Sharma, a commercial and corporate lawyer, and an insolvency professional, shares her experiences and key judgments related to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). She discusses the Supreme Court judgment Sunil Kumar Jain vs. Sundaresh Bhak on the inclusion of employee wages and salaries in CIRP costs, and the NCLAT judgment on fees and expenses payable to resolution professionals. Anjali Sharma emphasizes the need for full focus and application in both insolvency professional practice and legal practice, and looks forward to the NCLT becoming more vibrant with increased membership.
