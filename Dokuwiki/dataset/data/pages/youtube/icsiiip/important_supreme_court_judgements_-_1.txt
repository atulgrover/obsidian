## Summary
Welcome to the webinar on important Supreme Court judgments, specifically related to real estate and home buyers' problems. The speaker, SIVA RAMA PRASAD PUVVALA, a seasoned professional in finance and banking, discusses two significant cases that impact the real estate sector. The first case, Chitra Sharma vs Union of India, focuses on the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code and the rights of home buyers as financial creditors. The second case, Pioneer Urban Land and Infrastructure Ltd vs Union of India and Others, delves into the special purpose vehicle, JIL, which was financed by a consortium of banks, including IDBA Bank. The speaker highlights the challenges faced by home buyers, the role of the Supreme Court in protecting their interests, and the importance of understanding the complexities of real estate projects and subsidiary companies.
