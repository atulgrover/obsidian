## Summary
The webinar focuses on the turnaround of a power company, specifically a thermal power company with a 600 megawatt capacity. The company had been non-operational for 68 months due to a lack of Power Purchase Agreements (PPA), Fuel Supply Agreements (FSA), and financial stress caused by cost overruns. The company's size is 2500 crores and only phase one of the two-phase construction has been completed. The common facilities for both phases have been constructed. The presentation covers a case study of the company's turnaround using a framework by Slater and Barlo, which includes crisis stabilization, new leadership, stakeholder management, strategic focus, critical process improvement, organizational change, and financial restructuring. The presentation also discusses the power sector in India, its growth, and the role of thermal power in the country's electricity generation. The power sector contributes 25% to global greenhouse gas emissions, and India has committed to being net zero by 2070. The presentation also covers common terminologies used in the thermal power sector, such as kilowatt hour, power purchase agreement, plant load factor, fuel supply agreement, pit head power plants, capacity, and ultra-mega power plants.
