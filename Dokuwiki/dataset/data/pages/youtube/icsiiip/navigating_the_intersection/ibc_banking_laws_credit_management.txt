## Summary
Good morning to all participants. Today's workshop, led by Rajendran S and Sukumaran S, is on navigating the intersection of IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code) banking laws, and credit management. The first session will be taken by Rajendran Sir on the topic 'Interplay of IBC with banking laws'. He is a member of ICS, ICSI, AI, CA, and IP, with experience in Canara Bank, PSUs, private sector enterprises, and practicing company secretary since 2014. He has handled several IBC matters and is the founder of S Sasan and Company LLP, PCS and founder of Create and Grow Research Foundation. The session aims to share understanding and experiences on the interplay between IBC and banking laws, with a focus on Indian insolvency act, US, and UK provisions. The team includes Nageswaran, an IP and ex-banker, and insolvency practitioner in the US, pursuing UK insolvency recognition, Chared, an accountant, and Nisha and Snea, advocates. The session will be interactive, discussing case laws and experiences, with a convenience break halfway through.
