## Summary
Shri Gopal Krishna Agrawal, Independent Director of ICSI IIP, discussed the achievements and challenges of the IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code) in a video. Over 5,258 cases have been processed under IBC, with creditors receiving over 2.25 trillion rupees. However, there are issues such as pre-packs for MSME, prioritizing creditors, and the role of the NCLT. The ibbi is monitoring and improving the process, and important issues include maintaining a timeline, realizing assets, balancing stakeholder rights, and grievance redressal. IBBI awareness campaigns and icsi iip training programs are highlighted. Ips (Insolvency Professionals) should maintain a timeline, disclose relationships, avoidable transactions, and handle resolution plans. Important court cases include Cape Pharmaceutical vs Karoo Grass Bank, Sale Tax Officer vs Rainbow Paper Limited, and Sudarshan Liquidator of ABG Shipyard vs Central Bank of India.
