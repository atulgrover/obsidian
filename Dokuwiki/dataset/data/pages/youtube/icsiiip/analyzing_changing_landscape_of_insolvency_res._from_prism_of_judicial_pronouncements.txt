## Summary
The speaker, Sumant Batra, an independent insolvency practitioner and a member of the executive committee at Insol India, discusses the restructuring of financial service providers (FSPs) in India. He introduces his partners who have been involved in the firm's handling of three FSP resolutions: IFIN, DHFL, and Reliance Capital. Batra provides background on the Indian Financial Code and the Financial Resolution and Deposit Insurance (FDRI) Bill, which aimed to establish a resolution corporation for financial firms. However, as the IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code) excluded financial service firms, the government had to use the Companies Act to take over IFIN, which was partially successful. The FSP rules were then introduced to allow regulators to take financial institutions into IBC, with key differences from non-financial firm insolvency, such as the moratorium kicking off at filing, only regulators can pull it into IBC, any resolution plan needs regulator approval, the registration with the relevant regulator continues, and third-party assets are excluded from liquidation.
