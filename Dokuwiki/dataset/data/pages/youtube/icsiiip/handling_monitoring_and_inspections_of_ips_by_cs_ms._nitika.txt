## Summary
Welcome to session two in the series of sessions on perspectives of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Today, we have Mayank Mehta, who will speak on the topic of monitoring and inspections. Mayank has been working with IBPI for four years, handling inspections and complaints, and prior to that, he worked with EXIM Bank for a decade, managing corporate credit and monitoring department. In this session, Mayank will discuss the factors that should be taken care of while handling any assignment to ensure proper justifications during monitoring and inspections. The main objective of the IBC is to maximize the value of stakeholders, including financial creditors, employees, and small contractors. Insolvency professionals must comply with all laws and regulations and maintain unbiased decisions with high ethical standards. If any stakeholder gets dissatisfied, they can approach the respective IPA or IBBI for redressal. IBBI monitors the conduct of professionals on a regular basis through regulations and provisions in the code. In this session, Mayank and Nitika will discuss the monitoring policy of insolvency professionals at IPA level, including compliances needed to be done on a regular basis, and the procedure for inspections and document submission to the regulator. A live case study will also be discussed to learn from past mistakes.
