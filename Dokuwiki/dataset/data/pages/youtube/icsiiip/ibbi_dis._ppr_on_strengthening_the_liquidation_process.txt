## Summary
The discussion is about the proposed amendments to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) regarding the liquidation process. The discussion is led by a moderator named Dhanapal and will cover the provisions related to liquidation, with a focus on the changes proposed by IBBI. The participants are professionals who will provide their inputs and feedback on the proposed amendments. The discussion will cover the eligibility criteria for bidders, the powers and duties of the liquidator, the preparation of primary and progress reports, and the dissolution process. The proposed amendments aim to strengthen the liquidation process and improve the efficiency of the IBC.
