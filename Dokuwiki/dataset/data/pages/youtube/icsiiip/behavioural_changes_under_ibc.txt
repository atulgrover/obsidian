## Summary
The speaker discussed the evolution of behavioral changes under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and its impact on the debtor-creditor relationship. The IBC, a creditor-in-control regime, has brought about transformations in corporate finance, with a shift from debt to equity. The law has been a powerful tool for creditors, enabling them to take control of the company's assets and drive a resolution process. The speaker also touched upon the various recovery mechanisms that existed prior to IBC, including schemes under the Companies Act, RBI guidelines, and the Recovery of Debts Due to Banks and Financial Institutions Act. The IBC journey has seen a dynamic shift in the creditor's control, with data coming in and settling the creditors, and the Committee of Creditors taking control over the assets. The speaker emphasized the need for all stakeholders to take time-bound actions to facilitate resolution under IBC, as the law alone cannot solve the problems of the corporate debtor.
