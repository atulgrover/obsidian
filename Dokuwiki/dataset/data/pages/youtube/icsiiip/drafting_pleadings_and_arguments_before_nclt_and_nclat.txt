Prachi Wazalwar, an advocate, spoke on women's participation in the legal community and the importance of drafting pleadings and arguments in the context of the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) and the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT). She provided examples of cases that went up to the Supreme Court due to preliminary issues and maintainability applications. Women's participation in the workforce and legal community has grown, and the government and community are doing their best to protect them. The session focused on the main topic of drafting pleadings and arguments, with a glance at the specifics of laws and procedures.
