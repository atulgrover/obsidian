## Summary
The webinar is a final discussion on IBC by ICSIIID, with speakers Badri Narayanan and Shweta Bharti. The session covers three Supreme Court cases, with Shweta Bharti discussing the first case, Kotak Mahindra Bank vs Key Precision Parts. The case involves the application of the Limitation Act in IBC cases, specifically in relation to the acknowledgment of debt and the payable debt. The discussion includes various Supreme Court judgments, such as Nilkant Township, BK Educational Services, Gaurav Hargobind, and Babulal Gujar, which have shaped the understanding of the Limitation Act in IBC cases. The speakers also discuss the impact of Section 238A of the IBC and Section 18 and 25 of the Limitation Act and Contract Act. The session aims to clarify the confusion around the applicability of the Limitation Act in IBC cases.
