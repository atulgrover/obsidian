## Summary
In this webinar, Apoorv Sarvaria, an advocate and partner at VGC Law Firm, discusses eight recent cases that have shaped the jurisdiction of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. The first case, Vishal Chelani and Others, was pronounced by the Supreme Court of India on 6th October 2023. The case involved home buyers who had opted for allotment in a real estate project of CD Bull and Bul Tech Private Limited. These home buyers had approached the Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA) and secured an order for a refund of their amounts. However, when IBC proceedings were initiated, a distinction was made in the resolution plan between home buyers who had opted for other remedies and those who had not. The Supreme Court held that this distinction was highly inequitable and that all home buyers should be treated equally in the resolution plan. The court also noted that IBC has an overriding effect over RERA and that any discrimination between financial creditors would fall foul of the non-discrimination provision in the Indian Constitution.
