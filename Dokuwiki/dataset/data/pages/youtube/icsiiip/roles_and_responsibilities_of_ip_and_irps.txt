## Summary
Good morning to the workshop on roles and responsibilities of Insolvency Professionals (IPS) and Insolvency Resolution Professionals (IRPs). The speaker, Madhusudan Sharma, is a seasoned advocate, mediator, and insolvency professional with 40 years of diverse experience. He highlights the challenges and responsibilities of IPS in the current ecosystem, including the changing regulations, increased reporting and compliances, and the need for a multidisciplinary approach. The IPS role is unique compared to other professions, requiring higher compliances, working like an entrepreneur, and thinking from various perspectives such as NCLT, IBBI, and other legal professionals. The corporate debtor is least cooperative, and the Committee of Creditors (COC), though given more power, has lesser teeth to nail down issues. The IPS is bound by many parameters and authorities, and employees, promoters, statutory agencies, and even the new management may pressure them. The lack of governance and misconceptions about COC governance further complicate the environment. The IPS should prepare themselves for the ground realities and equip themselves to work as per the law. The two regulators for IPS are IPA and IBBI.
