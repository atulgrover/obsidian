## Summary
The procedure for handling a complaint received by IBBI involves the appointment of an investigating authority, issuance of a notice to the insolvency professional for written submissions, preparation of an investigation report, and submission to IBBI. If the report finds contravention, IBBI may issue a show cause notice to the professional. A complaint can be filed by any stakeholder with IBBI within 45 days of the cause of action, along with a fee of 2500 rupees. The case of Miss Maya Gupta, an interim resolution professional, is discussed, where she was found to have contravened regulation 183 of the CP regulations by not conducting a COC meeting at the request of a financial creditor holding 59.1% of voting rights. However, a lenient view was taken by the DC due to subsequent events.
