## Summary
The speaker, Rajendran, moderated a virtual discussion on the topic of voluntary liquidation as per the IBBI regulations. He explained that the process was introduced in 2017 as an easier route for companies to exit from the business for good reasons, and is covered under section 59 of the IBC. He compared the process with strike off under Section 248 of the Companies Act, and discussed the critical issues in proper closure and exit from business. He highlighted the time-bound nature of the voluntary liquidation process, and the delays encountered in completing this process due to regulatory compliances, realization of assets, and disputes amongst members and stakeholders. He also discussed the flexibility given to the corporate entity in the matter of valuation and selling the assets, and the distribution of proceeds from realization. He mentioned the provisions of section 596 of IBC that make the provisions of chapter 3 and 7 applicable to voluntary liquidation proceedings, and the proposed amendments to the regulations. He then opened the floor for comments, suggestions, and ideas from the participants.
