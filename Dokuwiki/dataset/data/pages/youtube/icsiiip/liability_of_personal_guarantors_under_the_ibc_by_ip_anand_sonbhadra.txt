## Summary
The speaker, Mr. Anand, is discussing the liability of personal guarantors under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. He highlights the relevance of the topic in the current scenario and the importance of insolvency professionals in the IBC. He explains the concept of personal guarantors and the circumstances under which their guarantees can be invoked. He also mentions the legal challenges and controversies surrounding the resolution process for personal guarantors, including the role of resolution professionals and the violation of natural justice. The speaker provides data on the number of cases filed and admitted under the IBC and encourages insolvency professionals to take their responsibilities seriously. He concludes by recognizing the contribution of the IBC to the recovery of non-performing assets (NPAs) and the role of insolvency professionals in its success.
