## Summary
In the first session of the workshop, Harmeet Kaur, an insolvency practitioner with 19 years of experience, discussed the intricacies related to personal guarantees. She is a designated partner in TRIP R Insolvency Service Experts LLP and has handled voluntary liquidation independently. She has experience in legal drafting, appearances, insolvency practice, business strategy, financial and corporate restructuring, and more. The concept of personal guarantee was discussed, which is an individual who provides a guarantee to secure the obligations of a borrower towards a lender. The definition of a guarantor is given under the Indian Contracts Act, and the guarantor's liability arises as soon as the principal debtor fails to fulfill its obligations. The present scenario is that banks still insist on personal guarantees even if sufficient security is provided by the corporate debtor. The notification dated 15th November 2019 empowers NCLT to act as a resolution authority for insolvency proceedings against personal guarantors. The Supreme Court, while exercising its powers under Articles 139a and 32, decided that the sanction of a resolution plan does not per se discharge the guarantor's liability.
