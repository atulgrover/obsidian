## Summary
The topic of discussion is mediation, its recognition as a profession in India, and its role in dispute resolution. The speaker, a mediator and advisor, explains that mediation is a process where parties in a dispute resolve their conflict with the help of a neutral third party. It is a non-adversarial, flexible, and confidential process that prioritizes party autonomy. Mediation can be used before or during legal proceedings and can help preserve relationships. The speaker believes that mediation has a role throughout the insolvency resolution process, not just at the beginning. They also mention that the Indian Parliament is currently debating the Mediation Bill. The benefits of mediation include saving time, money, and relationships, and it can be used in various contexts such as family, employment, community, and commercial disputes. The speaker emphasizes that mediation is an art and a way of life, and it should be the first step in resolving a dispute. The objective of promoting mediation in the insolvency resolution process is to focus on maximizing the value of assets for stakeholders within a fixed timeline.
