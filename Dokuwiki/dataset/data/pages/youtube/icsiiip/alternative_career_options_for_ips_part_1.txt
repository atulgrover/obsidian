## Summary
Rocky Ravinder Gupta, an insolvency professional and restructuring lawyer, led a workshop on turnaround and business rescue as alternative career options for IPs. Gupta emphasized the importance of recognizing financial distress early and applying appropriate strategies, such as turnaround or business rescue, depending on the stage. A successful business rescue requires the employment of a business rescue practitioner and involves financial and legal expertise. Gupta shared his experience in restructuring, insolvency, and distressed debt transactions across various industries. He also discussed the challenges faced by IPS, financial creditors, and the judicial infrastructure in India, and suggested the need for a parallel approach through restructuring and out-of-court settlements within the IBC. Gupta concluded by highlighting the opportunity for IPS in turning around the vast number of MSMEs in India.
