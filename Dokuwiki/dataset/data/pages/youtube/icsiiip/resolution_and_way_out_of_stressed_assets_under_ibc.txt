## Summary
In this workshop, the speakers discussed the classification of Non-Performing Assets (NPA) and the process of upgrading them to standard assets. It was highlighted that if the repayment is made, the NPA status is removed. However, if the asset is considered uncollectable and of little value, it should be accounted as a loss. The discussion also covered the case of overdraft (OD) products, where the principal and interest need to be paid for the account to come out of NPA status. The bank gives opportunities to make the account good before classifying it as NPA, and the company can question the bank's actions if they are not following RBI guidelines. The IBC law has come into force due to the inaction of banks in recovery mechanisms. The concept of due and default was also discussed, where the bank can declare the whole amount as default even if the due amount is not paid. The IBC provides a way out if the overall due amount is repaid before submitting a resolution plan.
