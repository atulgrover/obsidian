## Summary
The second meeting of the Committee of Creditors (COC) for ABC Hotels Private Limited is being held to discuss the corporate insolvency resolution process (CIRP) of the company. The company is in the hotel and lodging business and has been defaulting on payments to operational creditors, leading to the initiation of CIRP. The Interim Resolution Professional (IRP) was appointed on January 16, 2023, and the moratorium period has been in effect since then. The COC was finalized on March 6, 2023, and the first meeting was held on March 13, 2023. The meeting is being conducted by the RP, who introduces themselves and the financial and operational creditors present. The RP requests members to maintain confidentiality and submit a confidentiality undertaking. The RP reminds the promoters to cooperate with them and provide the required information for a smooth CIRP. The RP also informs the members about the appointment of registered valuers for determining the fair value and liquidation value of the company's assets. The RP has invited operational creditors to attend the meeting, but they have no voting rights. The meeting discusses the minutes of the first COC meeting held on March 13, 2023, and the appointment of the IRP as RP. The RP informs the members about the change in the voting share of financial creditors after the order dated March 21, 2023. The RP also provides details of the financial and operational creditors and their voting share. The RP requests suggestions from the members regarding the appointment of registered valuers. The members discuss the cost implications involved in the evaluation and request the RP to bring out the value of the company appropriately. The RP assures the members that the valuation will be done as per the IBBI requirements and approved valuers only. The RP also informs the members that a gist of the valuation report will be given to the COC members who are going to vote on the resolution plan. The meeting discusses the statutory limitations for not sharing the evaluation report and the need to consider the request for valuers who speak the same language as the company's location.
