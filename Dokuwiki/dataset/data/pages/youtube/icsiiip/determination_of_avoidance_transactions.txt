## Summary
The webinar discusses determination and avoidance in transactions, specifically in the context of IBC and 2.8 lakh crore road dance and fue application. The presenter highlights the challenges faced by RPs and lenders in classifying transactions due to lack of evidence or substantial documentation, as well as the limitations of the adjudicating authority's bandwidth. The importance of identifying willful defaulters and understanding the definition of willful default, including fraudulent transactions, is emphasized. Diversion of funds and siphoning are discussed as important elements to understand in transaction audits. The presenter also covers the key challenges faced in transaction audits, such as time consumption, expensive affairs, availability of documents, and prioritization. Mitigating measures are suggested, such as prioritizing forensic audits, securing evidence and documents, and having a strong legal process in place. Red flags to look out for in transaction audits include complex webs of entities, poor ERP systems, inflation of revenue and assets, undisclosed bank accounts, and adjustment entries, among others.
