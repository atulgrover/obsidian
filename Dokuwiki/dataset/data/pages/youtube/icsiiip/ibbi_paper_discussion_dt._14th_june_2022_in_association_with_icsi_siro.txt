## Summary
The roundtable discussion, organized by the Southern India Regional Council of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI), is being held online and is being moderated by Rajendran. The discussion revolves around two discussion papers presented by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) in June. The first paper focuses on streamlining the liquidation process, and the second one enables entities to become insolvency professionals. Nathan Rinser, an expert in the field, will share the rationale behind the proposals and enlighten the participants on different aspects of the law. Participants are encouraged to provide their views and inputs on the topic, which will be compiled and submitted to IBBI for further action. The discussion also includes a presentation on the two topics, highlighting the important proposals and aspects of each.
