## Summary
The speaker, the Chief Operating Officer of ICSI Institute of Insolvency Professionals, discussed the role of Insolvency Professional Agencies (IPAs) in the insolvency regime. IPAs serve as frontline regulators with executive, regulatory, and quasi-judicial functions. They enroll, monitor, inspect, and investigate IPs, draft standards and codes of conduct, and address stakeholder grievances. The speaker highlighted the importance of the code of conduct, best practices, and timely compliance. IPAs have seen issues such as delay in disclosure submission, non-compliance with timelines, unauthorized assignments, and lack of transparency. The monitoring and inspection by IPAs aim to protect IPs from future penalties and improve the profession's performance. The speaker also mentioned various orders by adjudicating authorities supporting and appreciating the efforts of IPs. IPAs are open to suggestions and are working on resolving issues, including the fee structure for IPs. The speaker concluded by requesting IPs to send their suggestions to IPAs and participate in roundtable discussions on discussion papers.
