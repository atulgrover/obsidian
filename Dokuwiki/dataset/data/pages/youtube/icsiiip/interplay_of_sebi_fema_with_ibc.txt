## Summary
The workshop discusses the roles and responsibilities of the Board of Directors in a Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), SEBI, FEMA, and IBC regulations. The speaker emphasizes that the IP should place all important decisions before the Committee of Creditors (COC) for consideration and transparency. The duties of the Board remain even after the rights are suspended, and the IRP or RP exercises the powers of the Board. The speaker also discusses the importance of compliance with all laws applicable to the CD and maintaining records of all communications and efforts made to ensure compliance. The LODR Amendment, as per SEBI regulations, states that provisions relating to the formation of the Board and Committees are not applicable during the insolvency resolution period, but the roles and responsibilities of the Board and Committee shall be fulfilled by the IRP or RP. The speaker suggests utilizing the existing facilities and resources of the CD and involving the team with confidence to ensure compliance during the CIRP process.
