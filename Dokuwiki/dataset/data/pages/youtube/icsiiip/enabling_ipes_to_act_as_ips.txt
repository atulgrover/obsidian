## Summary
In a webinar, Speaker Ravi Prakash Ganti discussed the amendments to the fee structures for IPEs and IPs. The registration fee for IPS has increased from 10,000 to 20,000 rupees, and for IPEs, it has increased to 2 lakhs. A new regulatory fee of 1% is introduced for services rendered as an IP. These fees are to be paid from the resolution amount or the revenues of the company undergoing CRP and are not to be deducted from the professionals' fees. An IPE recognized by the board and enrolled as a member of an IPA can now act as an IP. The registration fee for an IPE to register as an IP is 2 lakhs. The nominated IP is responsible for carrying out the entire process and cannot be changed during the process. There is no restriction on individual IPS to take support from IPEs for their assignments. The liability of any other partner or director of the IP would depend on the facts and circumstances of the case.
