## Summary
In this webinar series, the speaker Barsha Dixit is reviewing the Liquidation Regulations, specifically focusing on regulation 32 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. Regulation 32 provides for the sale of assets and the modes in which a liquidator can realize the assets during liquidation. The speaker suggests a minor clarification regarding the sale of assets subject to security interest. The discussion then moves to the difficulties faced by liquidators when the asset cannot be detached from other assets and the need for clarification in the definition of liquidation estate. The speaker also suggests a provision for withdrawing a petition under liquidation similar to section 12 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. The next regulation discussed is 32A, which provides for the sale of the corporate debtor as a going concern. A clarification is required regarding the documentation for the sale of buildings when the land is leasehold property.
