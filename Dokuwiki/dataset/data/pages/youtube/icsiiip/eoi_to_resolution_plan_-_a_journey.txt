## Summary
Satwinder Singh, Managing Partner of Aekom Legal, is discussing the journey from EOI (Expression of Interest) to a Resolution Plan under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in a three-hour webinar hosted by the Institute of Insolvency Professionals. Singh's expertise includes corporate and commercial laws, with a focus on inbound and outbound investments, joint ventures, and private equity transactions. He has previously worked as a partner at Welsh Associates and has authored a book on the Companies Act 2013. In this session, Singh aims to create an interactive and engaging learning experience by discussing the provisions of the law, relevant case laws, and practical experiences from the resolution applicant side or while working on resolution plans and liquidation processes. He is joined by Kalkit Khandelwal, a partner at Aekom Legal, and other team members who will help address questions and discuss issues during the course of the presentation.
