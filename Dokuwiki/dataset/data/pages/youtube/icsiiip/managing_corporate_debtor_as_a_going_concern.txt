## Summary
The workshop discussed the topic of managing a corporate debtor going concern during liquidation. Speakers Anil Kohli and Bhrugesh Amin explained that beneficial liquidation aims to maximize the value of the corporate debtor's business as a going concern, rather than selling individual assets. The decision to explore a sale of the corporate debtor as a going concern is mandatorily kept under SE regulation 39C during the approval of the plan or for a decision to liquidate the corporate debtor. The Committee of Creditors (COC) shall identify the group of assets and liabilities of the corporate debtor which can be sold as a going concern for commercial consideration. Some assets may be grouped as standalone assets and sold individually during the liquidation process. The resolution professional shall submit the recommendation of the COC to the adjudicating authority while submitting the application of the approval of resolution plan or application for liquidation of the corporate debtor. Regulation 32A provides that the corporate debtor may be sold as a going concern exclusively in the first auction, and the Liquidator cannot sell the assets unless security interest has been relinquished even during the sale as a going concern. The Liquidator shall identify and group the assets and liabilities to be sold as a going concern in consultation with the SEC within the first seven days of liquidation. The valuation of the assets and businesses shall be conducted by two registered valuers appointed by the Liquidator within the first seven days of liquidation. The average of the two estimates received shall be taken as the value. The reserve price shall be the value of the assets arrived at in accordance with regulation 35, and it should be taken up into consideration that only the assets included for the sale as a going concern should be used in the business valuation. The Liquidator should guide the creditors for filling up their forms for claims submission of claims that which assets they can relinquish and which they cannot. The employees workman should not include their terminal benefits like gratuity and PF while submitting their claims on the liquidation commencement date.
