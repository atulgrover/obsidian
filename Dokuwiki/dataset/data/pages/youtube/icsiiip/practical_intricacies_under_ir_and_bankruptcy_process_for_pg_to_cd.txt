## Summary
Speaker Vinit Nagar discussed the intricacies of personal guarantees in the context of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. He summarized the history of personal guarantees, starting from their applicability under the Presidency Towns Insolvency Act and the Provincial Insolvency Act of 1920, to their current status under the IBC. He highlighted the landmark case of Lit Kumar Jen vs Union of India, which upheld the Constitutional validity of the circular of 15th November 2000 and clarified that the liability of a personal guarantor is not discharged merely on the discharge of the liability of the principal debtor. He also discussed the jurisdictional issues related to Section 60 of the IBC and the jurisdiction of the DRT or NCLT in matters pertaining to personal guarantees. He further delved into the practical difficulties in inviting claims or constituting a Committee of Creditors (COC) in cases where the number of creditors is numerous, but the value of their claims is small. He concluded by stating that resolution plans are being prepared in a conscious manner, keeping in mind the Clauses pertaining to the invocation of personal guarantees after the approval of the plan.
