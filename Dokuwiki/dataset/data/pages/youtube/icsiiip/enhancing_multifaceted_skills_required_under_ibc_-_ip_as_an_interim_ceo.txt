## Summary
In this workshop, Ashish Rathi, an interim CEO, discusses enhancing multifaceted skills for IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code) interim roles. He emphasizes the need for an interim CEO as per the Companies Act, and shares insights from a McKinsey study on the eight lessons an interim CEO should consider. These lessons include understanding that you're never fully ready, developing insights from diverse sources, readying a value creation thesis, acting as a CHRO, focusing on what only you can do, moving from a private to a public persona, managing personal energy, and committing to preparation. Rathi also shares key considerations for an interim CEO, such as recognizing the scope of responsibilities, fostering teamwork, picking battles, and prioritizing tasks. He outlines challenges in the CRP period, documenting key decisions, and managing a company without relevant domain expertise.
