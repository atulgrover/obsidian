## Summary
Rajendran and Anagha will discuss case laws on related party transactions. The methodology is to discuss the definition of related party and then the facts of the case. The definition of related party in IBC Section 5 Subsection 24 includes a director or partner of the corporate debtor or a relative of a director or partner, a KMP or a relative of the KMP, a partnership firm in which a director partner or manager of the corporate debtor is a partner, a private company in which a director partner or manager is a director and holds more than 2% of the share capital, a public company in which a director partner or manager holds more than 2% of the paid-up share capital, a body corporate whose board of directors acts on the advice, directions or instructions of the director partner or manager, a partnership firm whose partners or employees act on the advice, directions or instructions of the director partner or manager, a person on whose advice, directions or instructions a director partner or manager is accustomed to act, a body corporate which is a holding, subsidiary or associate company of the corporate debtor, a person who controls more than 20% of the voting rights in the corporate debtor, a person in whom the corporate debtor holds more than 20% of the voting rights, a person who can control the composition of the board of directors, and a person who is associated with the corporate debtor on account of participation in policy making processes. The definition of related party in relation to an individual includes a person who is a relative of the individual or the relative of the spouse of the individual, a partner of a LLP or a partnership firm in which the individual is a partner, a private company in which the individual is a director and holds more than 2% of the share capital, a public company in which the individual holds more than 2% of the share capital, a body corporate whose board of directors acts on the advice, instructions or directions of the individual, a LLP or a partnership firm whose partners or employees act on the advice, directions or instructions of the individual, a person on whose advice, directions or instructions the individual is accustomed to act, a company where the individual or the individual along with its related party own more than 50% of the share capital of the company or control the appointment of the board, and a person who is a trustee of a trust in which the beneficiary of the trust includes the individual or the terms of the trust confers the power on the trustee which may be exercised for the benefit of the individual.
