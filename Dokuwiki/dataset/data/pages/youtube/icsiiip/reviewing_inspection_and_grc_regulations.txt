## Summary
In this webinar of the series, Pankaj Khetan is reviewing the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of Inspection and Investigation regulations. The discussion revolves around the Disciplinary Committee, its members, and the need for third-party involvement. Suggestions include having experts from the IPA, legal community, and IP community as members of the Disciplinary Committee. Additionally, there should be a representation from the IP community and legal community in the investigating and inspecting authority. The timeline for completion of inspection should not exceed 30 days, and there should be a time frame for sending the interim and final inspection report. The investigation report should be shared with the service provider. There should be a common email ID for inspection-related communication and a mechanism for the investigating authority to wave the notice period in certain circumstances.
