## Summary
Rohit Sehgal, a fellow founding partner at Crew Pro Insolvency Services and an accomplished IP, is leading a workshop on the topics of compliances under CIRP and management of a CD as a going concern. The first part of the workshop, presented by Praveen Bansal, covers the legal provisions and timelines for CRP compliance, including the filing of forms CRP 1 to 8 and the compliance certificate form H. The second part of the workshop, presented by Rohit Sehgal, will discuss the management of a CD as a going concern, including regulatory fees, disclosures, and reporting requirements.
