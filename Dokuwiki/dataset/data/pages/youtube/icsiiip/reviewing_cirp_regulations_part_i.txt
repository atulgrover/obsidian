## Summary
In this webinar, part of a series reviewing regulations under the IBC, the topic of discussion is the CRP regulations. The speakers are reviewing the regulations in two parts, with today's session covering regulations 1-25. One of the key points of discussion is the definition of 'class of creditors' in regulation 2, with suggestions for clarification on the inclusion of employees and associations of employees. The speakers also discuss the challenges faced by professionals in accessing books and records of companies undergoing liquidation, and the need for a standard format for providing records. The issue of MCA enforcement and the role of auditors in maintaining books and records is also discussed. The speakers suggest that ibbi clarify the timeline for the 180-day period for places of business and the date from which it should be considered to have started.
