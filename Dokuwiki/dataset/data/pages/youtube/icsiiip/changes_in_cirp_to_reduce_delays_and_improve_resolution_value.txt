## Summary
The discussion revolves around the IBBI discussion paper proposing changes in the CIRP to reduce delays and improve resolution value. The paper contains 14 proposals, with the first three focused on changes in timelines. The first proposal suggests eliminating the IM at the EOI stage and expanding the time for RFRP preparation. The second proposal aims to reduce the timeline for avoidance transactions, while the third proposes providing a copy of the avoidance transaction application to the resolution applicant. The floor is open for discussion on these proposals, with a focus on the mandatory consideration of avoidance transactions in the resolution plan.
