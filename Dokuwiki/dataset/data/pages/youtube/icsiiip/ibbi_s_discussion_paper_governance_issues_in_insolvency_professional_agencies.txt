## Summary
The discussion is about the governance of Insolvency Professional Agencies (IPAs) in India, with a focus on the institutional framework and the role of IPAs as regulated self-regulatory bodies. The presentation covers the background of the discussion paper, the institutional setting of the insolvency profession, and the proposed amendments to the regulations governing the appointment of directors of IPAs. The proposed amendments include the requirement for both shareholder and independent directors to satisfy the condition of being a fit and proper person and having prescribed qualifications. The discussion also touches upon the international context of the functioning of IPAs and the objectives of the 2015 amendment in the UK. The participants discuss the need for a nomination committee and a well-governed perspective in the appointment of shareholder directors and the importance of having insolvency professionals on the board of IPAs.
