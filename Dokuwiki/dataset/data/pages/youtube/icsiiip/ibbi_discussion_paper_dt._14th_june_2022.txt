## Summary
The discussion, held in a virtual roundtable in July, focuses on two discussion papers dated June 14, 2022. The first paper discusses enabling IPEs (Insolvency Professional Entities) to become IEPs (Insolvency Execution Professionals), while the second paper proposes streamlining the liquidation process. The moderator, Ravi Prakash Ganti, introduces the topic and discusses the proposed regulations. The first regulation discussed involves civil recognition of IPEs, making them eligible for registration as IEPs if they meet certain qualifications and experience criteria. The second regulation discussed pertains to the liability of IPEs when an IP (Insolvency Professional) is designated as the signing partner for an assignment. The liability is joint and several, but does not extend to other directors or partners who are not part of the assignment. The discussion also covers concerns about the potential gaming of the system and the rationale behind allowing IPEs to become IEPs.
