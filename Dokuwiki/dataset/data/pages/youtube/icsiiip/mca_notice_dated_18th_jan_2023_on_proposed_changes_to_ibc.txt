## Summary
The discussion, led by moderators Vinod Kothari and Sikha Bansal, focuses on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) circular from January 2023, which proposes over 30 changes to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The participants discuss the impact of these suggestions, the cultural change brought by the IBC, and the need for timely completion of processes. The proposed amendments include the use of technology for automation, mandatory admission of applications if default evidence is available, decriminalization of certain provisions, and changes to the waterfall mechanism. The roundtable aims to gather feedback from professionals on these amendments.
