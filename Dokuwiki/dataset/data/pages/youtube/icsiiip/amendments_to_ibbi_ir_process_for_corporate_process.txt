## Summary
The discussion is about the process of appointing valuers for determining the fair value and liquidation value of a corporate entity's assets. Currently, two registered valuers are appointed, but IBBI proposes to amend CRP regulations to allow the RP to assign the valuation of CD as a whole to a single registered valuer. This valuer may then appoint other valuers for specific assets if required. The aim is to streamline the process and avoid multiplicity of valuation reports. Participants discuss the implications of this proposal, including the role of the RP, COC, and the responsibility of valuers.
