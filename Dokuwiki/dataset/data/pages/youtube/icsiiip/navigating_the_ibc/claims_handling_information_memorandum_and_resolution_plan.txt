## Summary
The speaker, Nilesh Sharma, discussed the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and its definition of insolvency based on default compared to the previous definition based on net worth. He explained the purpose of the insolvency resolution process, which is to prevent insolvent entities from committing fraud and to explore the possibility of business revival. He also discussed the importance of claim filing under IBC as a method to register pre-insolvency dues and the process of claim filing. The IBC is an all-encompassing statutory regime that accommodates challenges pertaining to insolvency and bankruptcy of corporations, partnership firms, and individuals. The definition of claim, the process of claim filing, and the importance of claim filing in the IBC were emphasized.
