## Summary
The speaker, Vinit Nagar, discussed the implications of the Supreme Court's judgment on personal guarantors in the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). He highlighted that personal guarantors will not be discharged from their liability even after the approval of the resolution plan. This judgment has given higher leverage to creditors to recover outstanding amounts. The personal guarantor's liability will continue, and they will have limited scope of protection under the court. The judgment has also clarified the role of the Resolution Professional (RP) and the opportunity given to the personal guarantor. The discussion also touched upon the impact of personal guarantees in relation to a corporate entity undergoing insolvency proceedings. The recent amendment in 2018 has made personal guarantors directly subject to insolvency proceedings without the requirement of the creditor primarily initiating the insolvency proceedings against the corporate debtor. The Supreme Court has upheld the constitutional validity of the code and dismissed all the R petitions. The judgment has granted higher bargaining power to the creditors, leaving the personal guarantor with limited scope of protection under the court.
