## Summary
Nilesh Sharma, an advocate, gave a workshop on the topic of due diligence before finalizing a resolution as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), Series VII. He began by explaining the objective of the code, which is to consolidate and amend the laws relating to reorganization and insolvency resolution of corporate persons, partnership firms, and individuals in a time-bound manner. He discussed the importance of a time-bound insolvency resolution process, which denies creditors the right to make recovery during this period, and the consequences of insolvency, which used to be winding up or liquidation but under this law, insolvency resolution is given importance before winding up or liquidation. He then defined resolution plan as per section 526 of IBC, which is a plan proposed by the resolution applicant for the insolvency resolution of corporate debtor as a going concern. He clarified that a resolution plan may include provisions for the restructuring of the CD, including by way of merger, amalgamation, and demerger. He defined a resolution applicant as a person who submits a resolution plan to the resolution professional. He further explained that a resolution plan is a rehabilitation plan for a corporate debtor that is going into insolvency, and under a resolution plan, all past liabilities will be dealt with as per the resolution plan. He emphasized that the most important thing in a resolution plan is that all the past liabilities get waved off, and all litigation gets waved if a liability which is crystallized is waved. He also mentioned that in a resolution plan, all creditors in a particular class have to be dealt with in a similar manner.
