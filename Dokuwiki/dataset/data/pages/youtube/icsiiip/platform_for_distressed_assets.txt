## Summary
IBBI and NESL have launched a platform for distressed assets (PDA) that offers an end-to-end solution for insolvency case management. The platform provides four vital services: report generation, alert scheduling, task management, and document storage cost reduction. The Insolvency Case Management System (ICMS) from NESL is a unified software workflow that streamlines the resolution process with macro and micro-level reports for various stakeholders. The PDA platform ensures transparency, increases efficiency, and is cost-effective, providing insolvency professionals with the tools they need for successful resolution.
