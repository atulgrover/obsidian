## Summary
The webinar discusses two Supreme Court cases: State Tax Officer vs Rainbow Papers Limited and Wider vs Excess Bank Limited. Ashish Makhija, a faculty member at IBC, takes the first session on the Rainbow Papers case. He is a chartered accountant, cost accountant, and an advocate with 33 years of experience in commercial, corporate, and allied laws. The case involves the Sales Tax Department, which attached properties of the Corporate Debtor after the CIRP started. The Sales Tax Department filed a claim of 47.36 crores, which was rejected by the RP for being filed beyond the time limits. The Sales Tax Department challenged the resolution plan, but the NCLAT dismissed the appeal. The Supreme Court concluded that the timelines in IBC are directory, causing confusion as people may take advantage of it. The court also concluded that the Sales Tax Department was not required to file a claim, which is not correct as it was looking at the unamended regulation 12. The RP is required to consider the claim as per the books of account of the Corporate Debtor, and the information memorandum must contain every information under the sun about the company.
