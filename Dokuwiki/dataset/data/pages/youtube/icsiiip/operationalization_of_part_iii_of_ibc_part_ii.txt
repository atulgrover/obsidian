## Summary
The speaker, Vinit Nagar, discussed the operationalization of Part III of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) related to the insolvency resolution of personal guarantors of corporate debtors. He summarized the Genesis of the IRP insolvency resolution process and the amendment made on 15th November 2019 that made the provisions of the IBC applicable to personal guarantors. The speaker mentioned that the validity of the notification was challenged in the LIT Kumar Jen versus Union of India case, but the Supreme Court upheld its Constitutional validity. The speaker also discussed the impact of the amendment on Section 60 of the IBC, which deals with the jurisdiction of the adjudicating authority. He further mentioned that the Supreme Court has upheld the Constitutional validity of Section 94 to Section 100 of the IBC in the recent judgment of Dilip B Jivka versus Union of India. The speaker also discussed a recent NCL judgment related to the UAS Energy matter, which was upheld by the Supreme Court. The speaker will take all three judgments into consideration before dealing with Part III of the IBC, specifically Sections 94 to 118/119.
