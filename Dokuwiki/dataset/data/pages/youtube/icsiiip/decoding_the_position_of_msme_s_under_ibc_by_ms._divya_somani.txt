## Summary
The workshop is on Decoding Position of MSME and IBC. The speaker, Divya Somani, asks participants to share what they have understood so far about MSME. She informs that section 43B of the Income Tax Act has been introduced, which supplements the MSME Act by making it mandatory to clear MSME dues within 45 days. She also mentions that two participants are practicing as Insolvency Professionals. Jna ma'am, who cleared PPIR in 2017, is handling an MSME case as a Resolution Professional. She explains that the threshold limit for PPIR is 10 lakhs, and it can be filed under section 10 of IBC. She also mentions that the workflow, forms, and time limit for PPIR will be discussed in detail. The session then moves on to understanding the definition and classification criteria of MSME.
