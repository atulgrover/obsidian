## Summary
In this workshop on enhancing multifaceted skills for IBC financial experts, the speaker Avil Menezes discusses the importance of verification of claims, which is one of the most crucial aspects for any insolvency professional. He explains that once an insolvency petition is admitted, it becomes a representative suit for the benefit of all the creditors, and hence, verification of claims, classification of claims, and appropriate due diligence are essential. He emphasizes that as an insolvency professional, it is the duty to verify all the claims received and classify them correctly. He also discusses the verification of operational creditor claims, especially in infrastructure and contracting companies, where the insolvency professional needs to apply their mind to determine if it is a valid claim or a breach of contractual obligations. He clarifies that the admission of claims is critical to safeguard the interest of all the stakeholders in the process, and if done correctly, it can resolve many issues and avoid needless litigation.
