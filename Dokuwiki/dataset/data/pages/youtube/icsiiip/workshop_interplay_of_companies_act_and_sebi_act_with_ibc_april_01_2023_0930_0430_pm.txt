## Summary
The workshop on Interplay between Companies Act and IBC was conducted by Dhanapal, a law graduate and founder of SDhanpal Associates. He has over 15 years of experience in corporate secretarial and legal matters. The session began with Dhanapal asking participants to share their views on the required changes that should be given effect in the Companies Act to ensure smooth implementation with IBC. Participants shared their difficulties and suggestions, including the need for clarity in regulations, exemptions for certain classes of companies, and ease of operations during the CRP process. Dhanapal agreed on the need for action to relieve IPs and discussed his prepared PPTs focusing on the connections and areas where the two acts are not gelling with each other.
