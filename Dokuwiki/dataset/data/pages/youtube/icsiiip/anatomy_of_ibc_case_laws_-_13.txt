## Summary
This transcript is a webinar presented by Apoorv Sarvaria, an advocate, on the anatomy of IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code) case laws. The webinar covers recent judgments given by the Supreme Court and other forums. The first case discussed is Vishal Chelani and Others, where the Supreme Court observed that there is no distinction made between different classes of financial creditors in drawing a resolution plan under IBC. The second case is Totm P Salal vs State Bank of India, where the Supreme Court upheld the limitation principle but allowed the application to be maintained for recovery certificates issued in 2017. The third case is Manava Trade Link Private Limited vs Mangaran Wicom Private Limited, where the NCLT Kolkata allowed an application filed under Section 7 of IBC by an assignee of debt, despite the assignment agreement being unregistered.
