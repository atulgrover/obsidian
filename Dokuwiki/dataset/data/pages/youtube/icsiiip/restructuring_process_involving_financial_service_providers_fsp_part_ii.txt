## Summary
The speaker, Nilang Desai, discussed the differences between the insolvency of financial institutions and other types of companies, focusing on the peculiar issues that arise under the bankruptcy code when it applies to financial service sector companies. He also talked about the insolvency of ILFS and DHFL, which were resolved under Section 241 242 of the company's act, and the government's realization that such an ad hoc mechanism cannot be the blueprint for dealing with systemically important financial institutions. The IBC was then extended to operate for other financial institutions through Section 227. The speaker then introduced two cases that will be covered, SFL and SCFL, both of which are facing financial distress with a combined debt of close to 33,000 crores. The speaker highlighted the complex financial structuring of the SFL group, which housed under the same group of companies is the infrastructure business, equipment financing business, insurance business, and the asset management business. The speaker then discussed the business transfer agreement (BTA) between SFL and SCFL, which was executed in August 2019, but most of the lenders did not approve of this transaction. The speaker also discussed the confusion around how to resolve this dispute and how the administrator gave liberty to all lenders of SFL and SCFL to file their proof of claim in both companies. The speaker then discussed the scheme of arrangement proposed by SFL, which was to ratify the business transfer, but the majority of the creditors voted down the scheme. The speaker highlighted that the NT exceeded the statutory powers that it has to facilitate the scheme of arrangement. The speaker then discussed the challenges faced by the resolution professional when the company finally went into insolvency, including the difficulty in allocating the assets and the inability to alienate any assets of the company under the IBC.
