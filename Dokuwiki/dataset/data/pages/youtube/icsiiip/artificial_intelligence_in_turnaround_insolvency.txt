## Summary
Rocky, a lawyer and insolvency professional, discussed the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in the field of turnaround and insolvency. He used the example of 1500 drones being managed by AI to create images in the sky, showcasing how AI can perform tasks that cannot be done or synchronized by many people. He then introduced ChatGPT, a tool that collates information from various websites and presents it in a short amount of time. Rocky mentioned the limitation of ChatGPT, which is that it dwells upon information till September 2021, and introduced ChatGPT 4, a subscription-based model that provides more information. He also discussed the use of AI in creating documents, understanding laws, and fraud detection in financial institutions. Rocky emphasized that AI is not a primary source of information and that users should collaborate the information taken from AI with primary sources of evidence. He also mentioned the growing reliance on technology and AI in the profession of insolvency and turnaround, and how it can help insolvency professionals in their work.
