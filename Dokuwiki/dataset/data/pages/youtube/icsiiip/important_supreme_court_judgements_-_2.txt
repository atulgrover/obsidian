## Summary
The speaker, SIVA RAMA PRASAD PUVVALA, CMA, discussed the importance of maintaining a company as a going concern, especially during insolvency and bankruptcy proceedings. He emphasized the need for recovery of jobs and protection of employees in resolution plans, and the role of insolvency professionals in turning around unviable enterprises to make them viable. The speaker also highlighted the amendment to regulation 32 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016, which ensures that even in liquidation, the company should be tried as a going concern. He warned against selling company assets piece by piece, as it can lead to a decrease in the overall value of the assets and have negative effects on the economy, employment, and GDP growth. The speaker also recognized the importance of the views of the Committee of Creditors and the Adjudicating Authority in making decisions. He concluded by stating that the primary responsibility of a liquidator is to try to keep the company as a going concern, and that this can be achieved through compromise or arrangement with creditors, or by selling the business as a going concern along with employees and workers.
