## Summary
The presentation discusses the problem statement of the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) where equity shareholders' capital is reduced to zero value, leading to delisting of the company. The speaker, Vinod Kothari, proposes a solution that SEBI should mandate the new acquirer to provide at least 25% of the equity capital to non-public shareholders (excluding promoters) in the new corporate entity. This would allow minority shareholders to participate in the acquisition process, addressing concerns of section 29A. The proposal is applicable only to listed companies undergoing CIRP, and the minimum level of acceptance is set at 5%. The existing promoters will become public shareholders, and the offer of 25% equity to public shareholders will be made on a proportional basis. The shares will be offered at the same price as offered to investors in the new corporate entity.
