## Summary
Vinod Kothari and Barsha Dikshit led a webinar on reviewing liquidation regulations. They discussed the dynamic nature of regulations in the profession and the several amendments made over time in both the code and the regulations. The main objective of liquidation is to maximize value, and it should be efficient and result in value realization. Complicated issues continue to arise, such as the treatment of tax dues and workman's dues. The targeted timeline for completing liquidation is ambitious, with more than 55% of proceedings pending for over two years. The discussion identified potential areas for reform, including the collation and verification of claims, the constitution and role of the stakeholder consultation committee, the treatment of workman dues and employee benefit funds, the priorities of secured creditors, and the conditions of sale. Participants were encouraged to share their experiences and views on the working of the stakeholder consultation committee and other flagged issues.
