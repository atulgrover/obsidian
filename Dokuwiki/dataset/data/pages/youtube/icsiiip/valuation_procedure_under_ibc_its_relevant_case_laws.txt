## Summary
The workshop, organized as part of Women's Week, features Anantha Lakshmi, a Chartered Accountant and Registered Valuer, who shares her experiences as a woman in the corporate world. The session focuses on the importance of storytelling in valuation, IBC regulations on valuation, accurate valuation, and the valuation process by RB under liquidation. The speakers discuss the appointment of registered valuers, the role of the IP, and the significant difference in the valuation of an asset class. They also cover the details required for a registered valuer, particularly for land and building, and the importance of documentation.
