## Summary
The speaker, Preeti Garg, is discussing the interplay of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) with other allied laws. She mentions that as an Interim Resolution Professional (IRP) or Resolution Professional (RP), it is crucial to understand the interplay between IBC and other important enactments. Section 238 of the IBC gives it an overriding effect over other laws, but this does not mean that it overrides every law; it only prevails when there is a conflict. The speaker highlights the repeal of the Presidency Town Insolvency Act and the Provincial Insolvency Acts, which dealt with the insolvency of individuals, and the consolidation of around 11 laws, including the Companies Act, Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy Act, and SARFAESI Act 2002. She also discusses the amendments made in other existing acts due to the enactment of IBC, such as the Indian Partnership Act, Central Excise Act, Income Tax Act, Customs Act, RDBFI Act, and SICA. The speaker emphasizes the change in the stand of the Supreme Court with respect to crown debts and the responsibility of the IRP or RP to comply with the laws applicable to the corporate debtor (CD). She also mentions the circular brought on November 15, 2021, which says that the process of applying and obtaining a no-objection certificate (NOC) from the IT department for voluntary liquidation is no longer required. The speaker concludes by discussing the overriding clause of section 238 of the IBC and its conflict with various other statutes that already have overriding clauses.
