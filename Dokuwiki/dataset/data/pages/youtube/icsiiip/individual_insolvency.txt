## Summary
The speaker, Amit Gupta, explains the meaning and process of bankruptcy as defined in the IB Code. Bankruptcy is a legal proceeding where a bankruptcy order is made against an individual or firm, and their property vests in the bankruptcy trustee. The bankruptcy process cannot commence unless the insolvency process has been explored. The consequences of bankruptcy include disqualification from being a trustee, representative of a trust, public servant, or elected to public office, among others. The bankruptcy trustee has the function to investigate, realize, and distribute the state, and is obligated to give information of their affairs. The first meeting of creditors is held within 21 days to constitute the committee of creditors, who have the power to approve certain actions of the trustee. The valuation of assets is not mandatory, but can be done if necessary, and assets will ordinarily be sold by auction.
