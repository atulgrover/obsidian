## Summary
The working group discussed the need for a framework for group insolvency under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) to provide certainty and reduce asymmetry of information between stakeholders. They noted the Videoon case, where multiple companies were put through insolvency resolution process and the Supreme Court ordered that all matters regarding insolvency resolution process be dealt with by the same bench to avoid conflicting orders. The working group also discussed the Lavasa case, where NCLT consolidated lavasa group insolvencies to avoid potential losses caused by fractured insolvencies. They highlighted the advantages of group insolvency such as promotion of information symmetry, reduction in cost of insolvency proceedings, maximization of value, reduction in cost of capital, and increase in certainty for stakeholders. However, they also mentioned potential disadvantages such as subsidiary companies not wanting to consolidate, potential for unfair capture of value by some stakeholders, and the potential for high expenses for the framework.
