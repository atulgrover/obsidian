## Summary
In this refresher workshop on IBC, Advocate Apoorv Sarvaria, an insolvency professional and a qualified independent director, discusses the interplay of IBC with other laws, focusing on the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952. He explains the intent behind the establishment of EPFO, its priority over other debts, and the impact of IBC on EPFO. He highlights the inclusion of 'all sums due to any workman or employee from the Provident fund pension fund and gratuity fund' in section 364A of IBC, which was not present in the initial drafts. He also discusses the priority of payment of contributions over other debts as per section 11 of the EPF Act and the exclusion of Provident fund and pension fund from the clutches of the liquidation process. He emphasizes that EPF dues have the first charge over the assets of the establishment and that they are held by a company as a trust for the benefit of the employees. He also mentions the recent judgment in Jet Aircraft Maintenance Engineers vs. Ashish Chia, which holds that a successful resolution applicant should provide for the payment of the Provident fund and gratuity dues of the employees in full, even if the resolution plan had provided only a percentage of these dues.
