## Summary
The workshop discussion is centered around the moratorium feature of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The moratorium is intended to provide a quiet period for effective resolution of corporate debt, but its interpretation has varied in different judicial pronouncements. The speaker, Madhusudan Sharma, CMA, discusses various aspects of moratorium, including its application to the assets of promoters, assessments by statutory authorities during the moratorium period, and the definition of essential goods and services. The discussion also covers the challenges faced by insolvency professionals in preserving the value of assets and managing the operations of the corporate debtor as a going concern during the insolvency resolution process.
