## Summary
Pooja Berryman leads a panel discussion on four important case laws related to liquidation. The first case law discussed is regarding the matter of capital gain tax where there was a lack of clarity regarding payment of capital gain taxes, especially with reference to the sale of secured assets by a liquidator. The second case law discussed is about the completion of liquidation and disillusion, where investigations can bar dissolution of a corporate debtor. The third case law discussed is about the Mohun James matter, where the NCLT dismissed the application for closure of liquidation, stating that sale as a going concern is not envisaged under IBC. The fourth case law discussed is the latest judgment in the matter of Navjeet Singh, regarding the liquidation process.
