## Summary
The workshop discussed the UN Central Legislative Guide on insolvency law for micro and small enterprises (MSMEs) released in 2021, which recommends a simplified insolvency regime, comprehensive treatment of all debts, and a competent authority for MSME insolvency. The subcommittee's report provided the basis for the pre-packaged insolvency resolution process (PPIRP) ordinance, which includes procedures for pre-pack and other resolution options. The report highlights the benefits of pre-pack, such as speed, cost-efficiency, and minimal business disruption, while also acknowledging concerns like lack of transparency and potential misuse. The report suggests careful design and implementation of pre-pack based on learning from other jurisdictions, starting with a variant that requires the least preparation and can be implemented with the existing ecosystem.
