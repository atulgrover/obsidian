## Summary
Welcome to the workshop on Learner Session of Moratorium under IBC. Anaga Ma'am, partner of Company LLP Pune, will be discussing the topic. The moratorium under IBC prohibits the institution of suits or continuation of pending suits or proceedings against the corporate debtor, transferring, encumbering, alienating or disposing of any of its assets or legal rights, any action to foreclose, recover or enforce any security interest, and recovery of any property by an owner or lessor. The moratorium runs across the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) period. The power grid corporation vs JY structures case has interpreted the term 'proceedings' to mean debt recovery actions against the assets of the corporate debtor. Existing licenses, permits, quotas, concessions, or registrations given by any government or local authorities cannot be suspended or terminated on the grounds of insolvency, subject to the condition that there is no default in payment of current dues arising for the use or continuation of such licenses, permits, quotas, concessions, or registrations during the moratorium period.
