## Summary
The workshop is about related party transactions under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Related parties are defined under the IBC and the Companies Act, and include directors, partners, key managerial personnel, and relatives of these individuals. Regulating related party transactions is important to prevent diversion of funds and assets, preferential transactions, undervalued transactions, and fraudulent trading that may harm non-related stakeholders. The IBC, MCA, NCLT, NCLAT, and ITAT regulate related party transactions. The presentation will cover related party definitions under the IBC, liquidation regulations, and pre-pack regulations. Determining related parties can be challenging, especially in the absence of information from the corporate entity or the existence of oral arrangements. The concept of control, whether positive or negative, is also important in determining related parties.
