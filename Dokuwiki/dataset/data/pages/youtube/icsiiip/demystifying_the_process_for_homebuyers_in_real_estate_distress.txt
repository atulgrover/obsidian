## Summary
In this webinar, speaker Ashish Singh discusses the process of homebuyers in real estate distress under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). He explains how the code was amended in 2018 to recognize homebuyers as financial creditors, giving them the power to initiate the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) and be a part of the Committee of Creditors (CoC). Singh also discusses the 2020 amendment that requires at least 100 homebuyers or 10% of total homebuyers, whichever is lower, from the same real estate project to support the initiation of CIRP against a developer. He emphasizes the importance of homebuyers filing their claims within 14 days of the publication of Form A and submitting relevant supporting documents to have a say in the appointment of the RP and replace them if necessary. Singh also explains the process of claim verification by the RP, the timelines for submission of proof of claims, and the reconstitution of the CoC. He encourages homebuyers to address their concerns directly with the RP in case of any discrepancies with their submitted claims.
