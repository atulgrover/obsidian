## Summary
The webinar, presented by Harmeet Kaur, focuses on the code of conduct for insolvency professionals. The speaker emphasizes the importance of understanding and adhering to this code, which is unwritten but regulated by various authorities. The code includes five major commandments: integrity, objectivity, independence and impartiality, professional competence and behavior, and confidentiality. Key aspects of these commandments include avoiding bias, maintaining records, disclosing conflicts of interest, and ensuring arm's length relationships. Participants are encouraged to interrupt and share their opinions for a more interactive session. The presentation covers various regulations, guidelines, and circulars that impact the role and responsibilities of insolvency professionals, with a focus on regulation 7, subregulation 2, and the eligibility criteria for registration.
