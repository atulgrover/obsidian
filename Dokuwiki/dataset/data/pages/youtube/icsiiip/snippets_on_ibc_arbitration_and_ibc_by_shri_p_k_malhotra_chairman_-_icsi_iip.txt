## Summary
The enforceability of arbitral awards in insolvency proceedings has been a contentious issue in India since the passing of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in 2016. The IBC was introduced to regulate insolvency resolution and prioritize the interests of all stakeholders in a time-bound manner. However, the interaction of arbitration and insolvency has often been at loggerheads, particularly when the corporate data is subject to the insolvency resolution process. The Supreme Court has stated that insolvency disputes are not arbitrable, but a recent decision by the NCLT in the case of Kotak India Venture vs Indus Biotech Private Limited has referred parties to arbitration during insolvency proceedings, relying on the principle of special law prevailing over general law. The arbitrability of insolvency petitions remains a gray area, and it is recommended that the different rulings be examined carefully by insolvency resolution professionals and await a final decision from the Supreme Court or clarification from the parliament.
