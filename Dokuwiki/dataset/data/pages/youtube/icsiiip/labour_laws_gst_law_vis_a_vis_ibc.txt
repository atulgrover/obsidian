## Summary
Umesh Sahu, a practicing Advocate and Insolvency Professional, discussed labor laws in the context of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). He emphasized the importance of rescuing employees and maintaining the industry's economic growth. When he took an assignment for a company with 700 employees, he found it challenging to control and convince the employees who were unfamiliar with IBC. His first strategy was to speak with some employees and make a meeting to understand their concerns. He discussed the objective of being a mediator and resolution professional, and assured them that they would handle the matter according to the law. He also mentioned the importance of collecting and verifying claims during the custody and control phase. Umesh explained that the labor contractor part was one case, while the other part was the employees on the payroll. He emphasized the significance of the master roll and other documents like PF system hard documents, Provident Fund, ESI, gratuity, and statutory obligations. He also discussed the importance of addressing pending litigations and how he handled the case with 156 employees on the muster roll.
