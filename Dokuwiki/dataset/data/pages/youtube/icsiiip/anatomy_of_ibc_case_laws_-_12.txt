## Summary
In this webinar, Shiva Ram Prasad, a former State Bank of India executive and insolvency professional, discusses the anatomy of IBC case laws, focusing on four judicial pronouncements up to January 2024. The first case involves Aqua City home buyers and MAA Pradesh rera against ATI group and Fed DB Corporation. The case highlights the challenges faced by home loan buyers due to malpractices in the real estate sector, particularly during the pandemic. Prasad emphasizes the importance of thoroughly verifying claims, reading applications, and staying updated with the latest regulations in IBBI. He also discusses the role of the IP or resolution professional in following the code of conduct and ensuring justice for all parties involved.
