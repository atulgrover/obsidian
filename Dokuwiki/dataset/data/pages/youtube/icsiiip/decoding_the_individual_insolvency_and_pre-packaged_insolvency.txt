## Summary
The video discusses the Pre-Packaged Insolvency Resolution Process (PPIRP) under Chapter VII of the IBC code, which came into force on December 1, 2019, three years after the IBC code was enacted. The PPIRP provides a new venue for bankers and guarantors, with a default threshold of 1,000 rupees, unlike the 1 lakh rupees required for CP or CRP. The PPIRP is applicable to personal guarantors, partnership firms, and immediate family members, among others. The adjudicating authority for PPIRP cases is currently the NCLT, not the DRT. The definitions of key terms such as 'adjudicating authority,' 'associate of a debtor,' 'bankrupt,' and 'insolvent' are discussed. The PPIRP process involves the submission of a repayment plan by the debtor in consultation with the RP. The PPIRP provides a fresh start process for individuals with no money left, but it is not yet notified. The video also discusses the challenges in the PPIRP process and the differences between PPIRP and CP.
