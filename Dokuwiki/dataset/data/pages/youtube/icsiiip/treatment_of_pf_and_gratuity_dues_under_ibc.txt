## Summary
In this webinar, Speaker Partha and Kamal Sen discuss the treatment of Provident Fund (PF) and gratuity news under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Partha explains that the IBC is an important topic with many case laws yet to be settled. He discusses the objectives of the IBC, the definition of employees and workmen, and the relevant provisions and treatment of dues under the IBC. He also talks about some legal pronouncements and critical points related to employees and workmen, specifically in relation to gratuity. Partha emphasizes that the IBC is a consolidated law that operates in a time-bound manner for the maximization of asset value and to balance the interests of all stakeholders, including government dues. He mentions that employees and workmen are operational creditors under the IBC and have the same rights as individual creditors. The discussion also covers the definition of operational debt and the waterfall mechanism for the distribution of assets in liquidation, which includes the definition of workman dues.
