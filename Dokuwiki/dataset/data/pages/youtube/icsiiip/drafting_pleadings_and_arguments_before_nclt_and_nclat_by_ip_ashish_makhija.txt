## Summary
Good morning everyone, welcome to the workshop on drafting pleadings and arguments before NCLT and NCLAT. The speakers for the event are Rajesh Kumar Gupta, Chief General Manager at IBBI, and Ashish Makhija, Managing Attorney at AMC Longperm. Kumar Gupta, with his 28 years of experience in the banking and financial service industries, speaks about the achievements of the IBC and the challenges it currently faces, including reduced and delayed recoveries.
