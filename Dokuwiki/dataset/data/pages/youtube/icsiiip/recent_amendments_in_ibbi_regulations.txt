## Summary
The webinar, led by Shri Dilip Arjun Khandale, Deputy General Manager of IBBI, discusses recent amendments to seven regulations related to the IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code) ecosystem. The amendments focus on improving the efficiency and effectiveness of insolvency professionals and the processes they handle, including corporate insolvency resolution, liquidation, and voluntary liquidation processes. Specific amendments mentioned include changes to the CRP (Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process) regulations, which now provide a minimum fixed fee for insolvency professionals based on the quantum of claims and introduce a performance-linked fee for timely completion and value maximization. The IP regulations have also been amended to prevent IPs from sharing fees with other professionals and to ensure the process remains efficient and effective. Vinod Kothari, a fellow member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India and Associate Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, and Shikha Bansal, an associate member of the ICSI with expertise in insolvency law, security interest, corporate laws, and financial regulations, provide further insights on the amendments.
