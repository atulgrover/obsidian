## Summary
Vinod Kothari, the speaker in a recent webinar on IBC regulations, discussed the challenges faced by Insolvency Professionals (IPs) and the need to engage with them to strengthen the insolvency resolution process. He also suggested that in voluntary liquidation, a provision should be made for resignation of the Liquidator, with concurrence from a committee of stakeholders. Kothari also talked about the changes in liquidation process regulations, including mandatory consultations with the Stakeholders Consultation Committee (SCC), and the need for the SCC to assist the Liquidator in making fast decisions and reducing litigation.
