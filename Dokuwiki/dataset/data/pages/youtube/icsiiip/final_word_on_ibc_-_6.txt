## Summary
Good afternoon everyone, welcome to the final webinar of ICSR IIP on IVC. Today we have Mr. Devil B. and Mr. Abhishek Anand. Mr. Devil B. will be discussing the Shiva Industries Judgment by the Honorable Supreme Court. The judgment is based on the commercial wisdom of the COC, and Mr. Devil B. will discuss the circumstances and facts under which the COC can exercise this commercial wisdom. The case involved a CIRP order, and subsequent to that, the RP filed an application for liquidation. After the completion of the CIRP process, the suspended promoter filed a settlement proposal, which was directed to be considered by the Adjudicating Authority. The proposal did not achieve the 90 threshold limit and was rejected, but was later approved after one of the financial creditors gave its consent. The resolution professional then filed an application for rebooting the process. The Adjudicating Authority held that section 12 is for withdrawal and not for settlement, and passed an order for liquidation. The Supreme Court upheld the commercial wisdom of the COC and stated that the adjudicating authority should not interfere unless it is arbitrary, malafide or capricious. The judgment allows suspended promoters to submit a settlement proposal at any stage of the CIRP process, which may support the cause of CIRP. The Supreme Court also stated that the rights of the promoter should not be defeated by subsequent orders of liquidation.
