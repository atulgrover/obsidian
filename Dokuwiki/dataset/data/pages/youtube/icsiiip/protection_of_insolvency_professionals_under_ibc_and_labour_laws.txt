## Summary
The speaker discussed the role and functions of Insolvency Professionals (IPs) in handling cases under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) of India. They emphasized the importance of staying updated with the latest regulations and products released by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India. Challenges faced by IPs include initial expenditure, lack of support and cooperation from officials and employees, difficulty in obtaining information on bank accounts and assets, convincing utility service providers, and communication halts. The absence of key managerial persons in a company is a significant challenge as it makes it difficult to obtain valuable information about the company. Verification of claims and the formation of a committee of creditors (CC) also present challenges due to the non-submission of claims and the unavailability of secretarial services. The speaker also mentioned the conflict raised by suspended directors, operational creditors, suppliers of raw materials, unions, and workers. They highlighted the need for adequate infrastructure and protection for IPs from the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India and the Insolvency Professional Agencies.
