## Summary
Welcome to the round table discussion on the IBBI discussion paper dated 7th June 2023, focusing on measures to increase the possibility of resolution value and enable timely resolution. The session is being moderated by IP Sanjeev Ahuja. The discussion will cover three main aspects: increasing the frequency of resolution plans, maximizing the value of plans, and enabling timely resolution. The IBBI paper addresses the criticism of fewer companies being resolved, lesser value realization, and longer resolution time than prescribed. The discussion will also cover the role of the resolution applicant, the time value of money, and the importance of certainty for resolution applicants.
