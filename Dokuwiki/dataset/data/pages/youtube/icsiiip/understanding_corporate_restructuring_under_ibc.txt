## Summary
The workshop, held in February and onwards, discusses corporate restructuring under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) with a focus on Section 230 of the Companies Act 2013. The speaker, Anagha Anasingaraju, discusses the interplay between IBC and Section 230, primarily dealing with mergers and amalgamations. She refers to relevant portions of the CRP regulations and liquidation regulations, and presents a compilation of 40 case laws related to the topic. Participants are encouraged to share their experiences and contribute to the discussion. The presentation covers the process of submitting a scheme to the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), the role of the NCLT in sanctioning the scheme, and the time limits involved. The use of Section 230 in resolution plans and liquidation is also discussed, along with the relevant legal provisions in the IBC, CRP regulations, and liquidation regulations.
