## Summary
In this workshop, Barsha Dixit and Amit Gupta will discuss voluntary liquidation, its types, and the process as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. The session will be divided into three parts: prerequisites, procedural aspects, and practical experiences. The speakers will also cover the concept of Declaration of solvency, how to check a company's solvency, and the role of the Liquidator. The session will include case studies and discussions on challenges faced during the voluntary liquidation process, including tax implications and dealing with pending assessments.
