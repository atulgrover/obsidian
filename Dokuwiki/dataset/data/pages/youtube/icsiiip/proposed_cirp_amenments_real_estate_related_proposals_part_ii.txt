## Summary
Welcome to the Round Table discussion on proposed amendments in CP regulations and real estate related proposals. The discussion is centered around the IBBI discussion paper on real estate insolvency and its technicalities. Ashish Makhija, an advocate, will moderate the discussion. Five major issues have been identified by IBBI, including mandatory registration and extension of projects under RERA, opening a separate bank account for each real estate project, extension of registration or sale deeds with the approval of the COC during CIRP, COC to examine and invite separate plans for each project, and exclusion of the property in possession of the home buyers from the liquidation estate. The first issue, mandatory registration and extension of projects under RERA, is discussed in detail, focusing on the responsibility of the IRP or RP to register and extend the registration of real estate projects under RERA, associated charges and documentation, and the financial implications for the RP.
