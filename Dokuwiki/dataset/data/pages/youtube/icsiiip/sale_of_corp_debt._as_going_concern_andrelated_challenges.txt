## Summary
The speaker discusses the concept of a company in liquidation being sold as a going concern, emphasizing that it is beneficial for the buyer and need not require the company to be operational. The speaker references the Insolvency Law Committee Report and notes that there is no clear regulation on how to conduct a sale of a going concern in liquidation. The speaker shares their experience handling cases and notes the lack of specific provisions in the IBC code or the company law, which leads to practical difficulties and inconsistent orders by the NCLT. The speaker references an order from the Mumbai Bench of the NCLT in the matter of Lakshmi Paper Limited, which provides instructions to the liquidator to explore a scheme and consider selling the business as a going concern before selling the assets. The speaker questions whether section 230 is still applicable and whether this process is mandatory in liquidation.
