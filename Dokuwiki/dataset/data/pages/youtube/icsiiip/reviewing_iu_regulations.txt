## Summary
This is the eighth webinar in a series reviewing regulations by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India. Today's topic is the IBBI Information Utilities Regulations 2017, presented by Mr. Shri Debajyoti Ray Chaudhuri, MD and CEO of NESL. The regulations, considered the fourth pillar of the IBC, provide for conditions for registration as an information utility, governance, technical standards, core services, duties of information utilities, and services to insolvency professionals. The regulations require a minimum net worth of 50 crores, fit and proper persons as promoters, directors, and KMPs, and electronic repository systems with technical competence, financial capacity, and privacy and confidentiality measures. The regulations also provide for high corporate governance standards, risk management, and quality standards. The IU is obligated to provide core services such as creating and storing financial information, authenticating and verifying financial information, and providing access to information. The regulations also allow for incidental services such as case management solutions. The IU shall comply with technical and detecting standards while providing services and verify the identity of persons submitting or accessing information. The regulations provide for the authentication and verification of information of default, issuance of a record of default, and the process for the same. The record of default is issued to the registered users who are creators of the debtor and parties and sureties if any to the debt in respect to which information of default has been received.
