## Summary
Welcome to the webinar on the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP), specifically designed for bankers. The webinar is conducted by Persistence Insolvency Professional Private Limited, which provides services in IBC and non-IBC streams such as restructuring, one-time settlements, financing for one-time settlements, resolution funding, and more. The speaker for this session is Suresh Kumar, a chartered accountant with two decades of experience and extensive knowledge in the IBC domain. The webinar covers the background of IBC, its evolution, and the initiation of CIRP. Historically, India had a very poor rate of recoveries in terms of lending, and it took a long time to get it. The IBC code was enacted to reduce the 4.3 years timeline to less than a year and improve the recovery rate of 26% to 75% within one year's time. The government's intention is to facilitate failing enterprises to exit and create employment opportunities. The IBC code is the overall broader view of the government to run the entire commerce and financial stream of it. The code will subsume other laws such as the SARFAESI Act and the RDDBFI Act over a period of five to ten years.
