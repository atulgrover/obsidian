## Summary
Online mediation, also referred to as enhanced or superior mediation, is becoming increasingly popular and has the capacity to bring out the full potential of mediation techniques. To be a successful online mediator, one must first be a good mediator and have strong mediation skills, knowledge, and experience. It is important to educate parties and lawyers about the online mediation process and address any concerns they may have. Confidentiality is a key aspect that needs to be taken care of on a legal angle. Preparation and technology are crucial for effective online mediation. Using a wide screen to zone in on parties and adjusting the camera to meet the eye of the person speaking are practical tips for mastering virtual mediation. Additionally, articulation and communication may differ in a virtual setting, so it is important to invest time and resources in getting feedback and adjusting accordingly.
