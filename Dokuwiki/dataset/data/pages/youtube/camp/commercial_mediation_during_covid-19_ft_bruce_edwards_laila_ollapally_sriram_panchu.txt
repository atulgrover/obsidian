## Summary
Tara Olapally, co-founder of CAMP Arbitration and Mediation Practice, introduces the Mediation Webinar Series hosted by CAMP and IDEX Legal. The series will discuss topics related to mediation and collaborative dispute resolution every two weeks, starting with a discussion on virtual mediation for complex commercial disputes. Bruce Edwards, a veteran commercial mediator, moderates the discussion with panelists Sriram Panchu, known as the godfather of mediation in India, and Laila Ollapally, founder of CAMP. They discuss the potential of online dispute resolution (ODR) for various types of disputes, the shift of traditional mediators to virtual mediators due to the COVID-19 pandemic, and the importance of mastering mediation skills before moving into an online environment. Bruce emphasizes the value of mediation skills in an online setting, while Sriram highlights the superiority of online mediation as a method tailor-made for delivering mediation services. The panelists share their thoughts on the history of ODR and its evolution in the United States and India.
