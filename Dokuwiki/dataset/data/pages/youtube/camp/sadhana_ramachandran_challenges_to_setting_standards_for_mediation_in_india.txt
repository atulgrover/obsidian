## Summary
Sadhana Ramachandran discusses the challenges and importance of setting standards in the diverse Indian context. She highlights India's rich heritage of mediation, which dates back to 1500 BCE, and emphasizes the concepts of reason, wisdom, prudence, justice, equity, and good conscience that have been passed down through generations. Ramachandran also mentions the integrative problem-solving approach introduced during the Mughal era and how mediation was the preferred method of resolving disputes in India before the British introduced an adversarial system. She believes that mediation is still a relevant and living heritage in India, and setting standards for it should take into account the country's diversity and rich history.
