## Summary
TiE Bangalore and CAMP have launched a mediation desk to help entrepreneurs and startups resolve disputes. The panel discussion includes experts in mediation, arbitration, and investment. Mediation is a party-centric, confidential, and voluntary process where the parties decide the outcome, and it is less time-consuming and costly than litigation or arbitration. Arbitration is a process where a third party decides the dispute based on evidence presented, and it is enforceable like a court judgment. Both mediation and arbitration have their benefits and are not mutually exclusive. They can work together to provide a more comprehensive dispute resolution mechanism. The initiative by TiE Bangalore and CAMP aims to make it easier for entrepreneurs to resolve disputes and focus on their journeys.
