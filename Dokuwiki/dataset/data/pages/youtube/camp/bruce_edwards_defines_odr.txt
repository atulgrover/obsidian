## Online dispute resolution - Adr plus technology
0:10
Odr really simply means alternative dispute resolution plus technology in some form. So it's a brave new world that we are all entering. Online dispute resolution encompasses a whole range of various technologies, from the early days of people just using keystrokes and a narrative back and forth without any visual engagement, and that's online dispute resolution. 

0:36
The virtual world, as I refer to it, or the enhanced world, as charm has called it, has really involved platforms more like zoom, where you have the opportunity to visually interact with people on the platform, and yet they're all fall under the heading of online dispute resolution, which, I remind you, I define most broadly as adr plus some form of technology you.
