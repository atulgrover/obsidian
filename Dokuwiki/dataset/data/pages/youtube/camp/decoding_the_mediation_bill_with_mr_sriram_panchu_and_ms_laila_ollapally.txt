## Summary
Welcome and introduction to the discussion on the Mediation Bill 2023, led by Sanjeev Ahuja, founder of Missing Bridge and CAMP. The panelists include Sriram Panchu, a senior advocate and renowned mediator, Laila Ollapally, a senior advocate and founder of CAMP, and Jawad, an advocate and senior mediator. Sriram Panchu rates the mediation bill a 5 out of 10, citing the potential for professionalization of mediation, the inclusion of institutions, and the provision for a decree-like enforceable settlement. However, he criticizes the composition of the Mediation Council of India, the exclusion of government disputes, and the loose language regarding pre-litigation meetings. Laila Ollapally agrees with Sriram Panchu but gives the bill a 6 out of 10, emphasizing the importance of the judges and the mediation community embracing mediation and delivering high-quality services. She also stresses the need for lawyers to see themselves as problem solvers rather than inflamers. The panelists discuss the balance between mandatory and voluntary mediation, with Laila Ollapally expressing support for mandatory mediation if done well, and the importance of the quality of the mediator in testing the willingness of the parties.
