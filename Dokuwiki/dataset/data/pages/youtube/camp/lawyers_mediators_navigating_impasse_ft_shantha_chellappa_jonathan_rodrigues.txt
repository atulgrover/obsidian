## Summary
In this recording of the Q&A section of the Win This Together webinar series, mediators Jonathan Rodrigues and Shantha Chalapa discuss how mediators can help parties explore options for resolution during an impasse, when they cannot give suggestions or opinions. Shantha explains that mediators can revisit the underlying needs and interests of the parties, encourage them to acknowledge the interests of the other party, help them identify common interests, and ask open-ended questions to clarify targets and explore options. Mediators can also suggest hypothetical scenarios, spotlight progress, enlist the help of influential parties, and brainstorm options. Jonathan adds that the mediator's toolkit can be used to break an impasse, and it is the role of the mediation advocate to tap into this toolkit and use the different tools and techniques to resolve the dispute. Shantha suggests that if both parties do not agree to take a break or park a certain issue, the mediator can explore the underlying needs and interests of both parties, help them find common interests, and revisit the agenda to define the issues to be resolved from most important to least important. The mediator can also help the parties understand the value in resolving other issues to narrow down the gap and look at the issue they are particular about in a different perspective. Jonathan emphasizes the importance of the mediator and the advocate being on the same page to work wonders.
