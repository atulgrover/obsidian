## Summary
The webinar, organized by Jeepa Professional Association, focuses on the discussion and protection of Insolvency Professionals (IPs) against disciplinary proceedings by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI). The speakers emphasize the need for awareness and facilitation services for business professionals and students. They discuss the importance of understanding laws and regulations, monitoring, and accountability for all stakeholders involved in the insolvency process. The speakers highlight the need for transparency in the selection of IPs and the importance of ethical working. They also discuss the challenges faced by IPs, including the issuance of show-cause notices and hefty penalties. The webinar includes insights from a renowned IP, Mr. Sunil Kumar Agarwal, who shares his experience of facing disciplinary proceedings and the need for consistency in orders.
