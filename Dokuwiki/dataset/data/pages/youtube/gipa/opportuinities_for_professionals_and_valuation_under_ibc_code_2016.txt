## Summary
Welcome to the event on Opportunities, Professionals, and Valuation under IBC 2016. The chairman, Praveen Genzer, introduces the speakers and the topic. Ankit Golgi, a practicing chartered accountant and registered valuer, speaks about the opportunities for professionals in IBC 2016. He explains the objectives of IBC, including improving the ease of doing business, maximization of the value of assets, and differentiation between insolvency, bankruptcy, and liquidation. The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) is the regulator for IBC and it regulates solvency professional agencies, insolvency professionals, and information utilities. The number of IBC cases has been increasing, with most cases going for liquidation and some for approval plan resolution. Real estate, renting, and business activities are the major part of companies that come into insolvency. Insolvency professionals are required to be honest, straightforward, and maintain complete independence. They can be interim resolution professionals, resolution professionals, liquidators, representatives of creditors and committees of creditors, and bankruptcy trustees. A member of the committee of creditors can appoint an insolvency professional as a representative for attending meetings.
