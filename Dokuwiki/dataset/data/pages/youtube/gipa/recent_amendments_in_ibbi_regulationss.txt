## Summary
The webinar, organized by Jeepa Professional Association, focuses on the recent amendments in IBBI regulations. The amendments include changes in IBBI IP regulations, CIP regulations, and new circulars. The speakers discuss the impact of these amendments on the fee structure, timelines, and filing requirements. They also discuss the need for more power to be given to IBBI to avoid the constitutionality of any regulation becoming a subject matter of dispute. The speakers highlight the importance of institutional IPs and the need for insolvency professionals to adapt to the changing market dynamics. They also discuss the need for an analysis of these amendments to determine the future of individual IPs and whether they should practice on their own or be part of an IP firm.
