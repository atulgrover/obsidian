## Summary
The webinar, organized by Jeepa Organization, discusses the Insolvency Resolution Process for Personal Guarantors. The session is introduced by a representative of Jeepa Organization, followed by a brief introduction of the speakers and the chief guest, Honorable Sri Virendra Gupta, a former technical member of NCLP and legal professional with rich judicial experience. Mr. Gupta shares his views on the controversies and aspects that need to be considered in personal guarantee cases, including the recent Supreme Court ruling that allows proceedings against personal guarantors before or after initiating proceedings against the principal borrower. He emphasizes the need for suitable changes in norms from both statutory and RBI sides to meet the objectives of IBC. The session continues with other professionals sharing their insights and experiences on personal guarantor matters.
