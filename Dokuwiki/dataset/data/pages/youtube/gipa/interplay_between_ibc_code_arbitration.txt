## Summary
The webinar, led by PR, discusses the interplay between the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996. The discussion focuses on what happens when an arbitration proceeding is pending and the interplay between IBC and arbitration during various phases of insolvency proceedings, from the pre-admission stage to the approval of a plan or liquidation. Key case laws and the influence of the UNCITRAL legislative guide on the laws of insolvency are discussed. The speaker highlights the importance of understanding the differences between arbitration and IBC for stakeholders such as corporate debtors, directors, and creditors.
