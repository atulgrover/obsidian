## Summary
The webinar, hosted by JEA in association with the National Company Law Bar Association, discusses the challenges faced by resolution professionals in managing a business facing financial distress and bankruptcy. The speakers emphasize the importance of identifying the root cause of the distress, developing a realistic turnaround strategy, and staying focused on the goal. They also highlight the challenges they face in their roles as interim resolution professionals, resolution professionals, and liquidators, including compliance with laws and regulations, taking over the position of the assets of the corporate debtor, and obtaining interim finance. The speakers suggest that the IBC regulator should consider providing financing to such companies and share their experiences in handling various types of cases across different industries. They encourage learning from each other's experiences, Google, and industry groups to develop strategies for managing insolvency and bankruptcy cases.
