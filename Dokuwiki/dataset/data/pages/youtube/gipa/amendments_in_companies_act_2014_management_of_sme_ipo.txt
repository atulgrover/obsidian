## Summary
The speaker discussed recent amendments in the Companies Act, 2013, highlighting the proactive steps taken by the Government of India to relax norms and rules during the pandemic. The amendments include relaxing penalties, extending time limits, allowing virtual meetings, and treating certain COVID-19 expenses as CSR expenses. The speaker also talked about the SME IPO segment, emphasizing the government's efforts to promote and encourage SMEs to enter the formal sector. Two stock exchanges, BSE and NSE, have introduced special platforms for SMEs to raise funds through IPOs. The speaker mentioned the benefits and challenges of SME IPOs and the reasons why not many companies have opted for this option, including lack of awareness and unattractive financials. The speaker is an expert in corporate law with eight years of experience and has co-authored a company law premier book. They have also contributed to the issuance of guidance notes by the Institute of Company Secretaries of India.
