## Summary
This webinar is a seminar on bank audit and IBC, presented by Vivek, a chartered accountant with expertise in bank audits and insolvency. The seminar covers the latest amendments to advances, NPA, and IRAC norms, as well as the importance of audit planning. The speaker discusses the three parts of a bank branch audit: pre-commencement, commencement, and post-commencement. The main focus is on the audit planning, which includes understanding the banking industry, government policies, RBI circulars, accounting standards, and software used by public sector banks. The speaker also discusses the importance of advances, classification of assets, and the treatment of NPA and SMA accounts. The webinar also covers the clarifications issued by RBI on due dates, repayment dates, and the calculation of NPA. The speaker has over 35 years of experience in bank audits and insolvency.
