## Summary
Welcome to the virtual round table organized by the Association of Insolvency Professionals on the role of mediation in the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. The panelists for the session are Sanjeev Ahuja, Virendra Ganda, Rajendra Ganatra, Sanjay Gupta, and Gautam Singhal. Sanjeev Ahuja, a chartered accountant, insolvency professional, and mediator, talks about the importance of mediation as a way of life and a thought process. He emphasizes the need to understand the other party's perspective and find a way to give and take. Virendra Ganda, a senior advocate, talks about his experience in corporate litigation, insolvency, and bankruptcy. Rajendra Ganatra, a qualified engineer and chartered associate of Indian Institute of Bankers, talks about his experience in recovery and restructuring project finance, asset reconstruction, and corporate insolvency. Sanjay Gupta, a fellow member of The Institute of cost accountants of India, talks about his experience in serving on many coveted positions including the president of the cost accountants of India. Gautam Singhal, an advocate and IP, talks about his experience in dealing with financial matters and qualified insolvency programs. The panelists discuss the importance of mediation in insolvency and bankruptcy and how it can be used to find amicable solutions within the four corners of law.
