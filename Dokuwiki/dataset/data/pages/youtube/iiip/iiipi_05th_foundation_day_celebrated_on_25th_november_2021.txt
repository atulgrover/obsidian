## Summary
The fifth foundation day celebration of the Indian Institute of Insolvency Professionals of ICAI (IIIPI) is being held, with Dr. Ashok Halvia, Chairman of IIIPI, welcoming the guests. The institute has over 60% of the Insolvency Professionals (IPs) in the country. IIIPI has three roles: professional development, regulation of the profession, and disciplinary proceedings. The institute has been striving for excellence and working hard to meet the challenges of the evolving Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The Chairman of IBBI, Dr. Navrang Saini, is the guest of honor. The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) is the founder and patron of IIIPI. The IBC has matured enough to deliver its objective, and new concepts like group insolvency and cross-border insolvency are being considered. IIIPI is working on capacity building of members, and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code Committee at ICAI supports IIIPI in this regard. The IBBI, as the apex regulator under IBC, has been a torchbearer of the insolvency regime in India.
