## Summary
Navrang Saini, speaking at WTM and IBBI, discussed the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016 and its impact on the Indian ecosystem. He highlighted the history and objectives of the IBC, and its role in promoting entrepreneurship, availability of credit, and balance the interests of all stakeholders. Saini also discussed the behavioral change among data creators and buyers, and the significant recoveries made outside the code. He emphasized the importance of freedom at three stages of a firm's life - entry, competition, and exit. The IBC provides a market mechanism for rescuing or liquidating a firm, and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) regulates the processes and professionals involved. The IBBI has four pillars - the adjudicating authority, information utility, insolvency professionals, and the IBBI itself. Saini also provided statistics on the number of cases admitted, under appeal, withdrawn, resolved, and under liquidation since the implementation of the IBC.
