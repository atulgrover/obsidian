## Summary
Welcome address given at the International Research Conference on Insolvency and Bankruptcy organized by IBBI and IIM Ahmedabad. The speaker discussed the importance of the conference in addressing issues and making recommendations for the improvement of the insolvency and bankruptcy system in India. Key points include the need for an efficient process, fair distribution of value, appropriate decision rights assignment, and the impact on credit flow and project financing. The speaker also highlighted the need for evidence-based research to understand the successes and failures of the current system and to guide future policy decisions.
