## Summary
The second day of the international research conference on insolvency and bankruptcy begins with a discussion on reforms in the insolvency regime. The panel, chaired by Dr. Deepti Mukesh and moderated by Sumant Batra, reflects on past reforms and discusses future ideas for making the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) more efficient and enforceable. Dr. Mukesh highlights the significance of the IBC in saving lives and livelihoods during the pandemic. She mentions that the IBC has become an upcoming branch of practice for lawyers, chartered accountants, and company secretaries. The panel discusses the need for a shift in focus from recovery to operational and management restructuring for the long-term turnaround of enterprises. The panelists emphasize the importance of resolution as the primary objective of the IBC, while being mindful of recovery. They also discuss the need for adequate attention to other dimensions of resolution plans, such as the expertise and projections of potential resolution applicants.
