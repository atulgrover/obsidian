## Summary
The lecture covers the topic of sale as a going concern in the liquidation process under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. The speaker emphasizes on the importance of keeping a company as a going concern to maximize the value of assets, save employment, reduce the need for additional finance, and maintain the brand value. The speaker also discusses the benefits of sale as a going concern during the liquidation process and the regulations that provide for it. The speaker highlights that the term 'going concern' is not defined in the code or regulations, but it implies that the company would continue functioning as before, with all its assets and resources intact. The speaker also mentions that there have been 13 going concern sales under the liquidation process as of March 31, 2022, with a realized value of 448 crore rupees, which is a 20% improvement over the liquidation value. The speaker concludes by saying that it is important to save the company during the liquidation process if possible, as it benefits all stakeholders, including employees, vendors, and customers.
