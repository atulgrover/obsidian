## Summary
This session at the international conference discusses the impact of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) on credit channels for firms in the Indian manufacturing sector. The research, presented by Dr. Raj Deep Singh, Mr. Kanvajit Singh, and Mr. Yadwin Blessing, focuses on examining the causal impact of IBC on demand and access to credit by distressed and non-distressed firms in the Indian manufacturing sector. The study uses a panel data set of manufacturing firms in India from 2011 to 2021, covering both listed and unlisted firms. The findings suggest that IBC has improved credit channels for distressed firms in the Indian manufacturing sector, leading to increased access to finance and a decrease in the cost of debt. However, the study also notes that there is a need to improve the recovery rate and streamline the admission process under IBC. The researchers suggest the introduction of a remote litigation structure, central depository of information, and mediation and conciliation mechanisms under the code to expedite the process of constructive destruction and develop the corporate debt market.
