## Summary
Manish Chaudhari, CGM of IBBI, discusses the service provider organizations under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016. He explains the two-tier regulatory system involving the Independent Regulator (IBBI) and front-line regulators, known as service provider organizations. These organizations include Insolvency Professional Agencies (IPAs), Information Utilities (IUs), and Registered Valuer Organizations. The IPAs are responsible for regulating insolvency professionals, granting membership, setting conduct standards, and monitoring members. IUs ascertain the occurrence of default and disseminate financial information to stakeholders. Registered Valuer Organizations ensure the education and certification of registered valuers and monitor their conduct. Manish also explains the eligibility criteria for becoming a service provider organization.
