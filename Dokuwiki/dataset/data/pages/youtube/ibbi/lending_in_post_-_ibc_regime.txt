## Summary
Speaker Sanjeev Pandey, DGM, discussed changes in lending practices in the post-IBC regime. IBC introduced a paradigm shift in Indian corporate lending, changing the behavior of borrowers and requiring adjustments from lenders. The trigger for recovery action has changed from NPA to default, and financial or operational creditors can now initiate insolvency proceedings. Lenders can take control of a corporate debtor's assets once a case is admitted in NCLT. Credit analysts should assess a firm's current value, creditworthiness, growth potential, and insolvency risk while considering the firm's business strategy, industry position, and ability to innovate. The Information Utility, created under IBC, provides comprehensive information about a company's history of defaults. Analysts should look for earning and cash flow manipulations, potential preferential transactions, and evaluate the quality of collateral. Additionally, analysts should consider intangible assets like brand equity and patents while evaluating a proposal.
