## Summary
Manoj Kulshrestha, a professional, discussed the insolvency resolution process cost under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. He explained that the cost is incurred during the process and includes fees of the resolution professional, interim finance, and other expenses necessary to run the company as a going concern. The cost is initially borne by the committee of creditors and then by the corporate debtor. He also mentioned that the insolvency professional must maintain written records of the proceedings, charge fees in a transparent manner, and disclose all fees charged. Any other expenses or fees not directly related to the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) should not be included in the cost.
