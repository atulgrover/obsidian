## Summary
Abhishek, Manager from Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI), and Sushanta Kumar Das, DGM IBBI, present on the Individual Insolvency Resolution Process and Bankruptcy Process for Personal Guarantors to Corporate Debtors. A Personal Guarantor is defined as an individual who is a surety in a contract of guarantee to the corporate debtor or an individual whose guarantee has been invoked by the creditor and remains unpaid. The Adjudicating Authority for personal guarantors is the Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT) or the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), depending on the circumstances. The Insolvency Resolution Process for personal guarantees can be initiated by both the creditor and the debtor. Once the application is filed, an Interim Moratorium commences, providing the personal guarantor with time to consider repayment options. The Resolution Professional (RP) is appointed within 14 days and submits a report recommending approval or rejection of the application. If approved, the personal guarantor prepares a repayment plan for the creditors' review and approval. If at least 75% of the creditors approve the repayment plan, it is considered approved. If rejected, the creditors or debtors can initiate the bankruptcy process. The Bankruptcy Trustee is appointed to deal with the estate of the bankrupt, and an application for bankruptcy can be made by the debtor or the creditor if the application is rejected, the repayment plan is rejected, or the repayment plan is not implemented properly.
