## Summary
In the concluding session of this international research conference on insolvency and bankruptcy, Mr. Sudhakar Shukla, a whole-time member of IBBI, thanked everyone for their participation and feedback. He mentioned that there are still gaps that need to be addressed and encouraged attendees to share their thoughts on what they liked and what could be improved for the next time. He emphasized the importance of a feedback loop and the need for validation of research questions. Mr. Shukla discussed various topics that were raised during the conference, including haircuts, delays, and the impact of section 29A and 32A. He also talked about the need for early warning systems, peer review, and third-party evaluation. Mr. Shukla encouraged researchers to engage with IBBI and take advantage of their policy to provide data and resources for credible research. He thanked IMA and the officials from IBBI for their contributions in making the conference a success and expressed his hope for continued collaboration and support going forward.
