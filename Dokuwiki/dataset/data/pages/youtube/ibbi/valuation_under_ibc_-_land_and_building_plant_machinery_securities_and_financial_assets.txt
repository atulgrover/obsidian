## Summary
In this transcript, Vinay Goel, CEO of IOVRVF, discusses the evolution of valuation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. Prior to IBC, recovery methods were fragmented and recovery-focused, but IBC introduced a shift towards resolution and ease of exit. IBC aims for time-bound resolution and liquidation, maximization of asset value, promotion of entrepreneurship, availability of credit, and balance of interest for all stakeholders. Valuation plays a crucial role in achieving these objectives. Registered valuers must conduct valuations for IBC proceedings, and their conclusions must be robust and able to withstand legal scrutiny. Valuation has undergone significant transformation from mere market value estimation to a critical role in regulatory spheres. Registered valuers must comply with various regulations, guidelines, and international valuation standards. Three categories of asset classes exist for valuers: land and building, plant and machinery, and securities. Registered valuer organizations regulate and ensure ethical standards, providing education and monitoring registered valuers.
