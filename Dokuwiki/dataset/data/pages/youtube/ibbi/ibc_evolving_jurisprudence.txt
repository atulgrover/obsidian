## Summary
This episode of IBC Evolving Jurisprudence discusses the terms insolvency and bankruptcy, and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016. Insolvency is defined as a state where a company's income is less than its expenditure, and it is unable to service its debts. Bankruptcy, on the other hand, is the legal procedure to save insolvency through a formal mechanism driven by a judicial or quasi-judicial body. The IBC 2016 is a self-contained, exhaustive, and exclusive law that deals with insolvency and bankruptcy and was enacted under entry nine of the list three of the seventh schedule of the Indian Constitution. The code has codified many laws, including the pre-independence legislation of 1909 and 1920, and has made amendments to various existing laws. The code has evolved through judicial decisions, and the Supreme Court's decision in Innovative Industries Limited vs ICICI Bank and Another marked a paradigm shift in the law. The court emphasized the need for proper understanding and interpretation of the code's provisions to ensure that the insolvency resolution process is carried out correctly.
