## Summary
Welcome to the panel discussion on insolvency and bankruptcy resolutions, chaired by Dr. T.K. Viswanathan and moderated by Sri Bhiram Bakreel. The discussion covers the history and creation of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India, its implementation, and the role of various stakeholders including the government, RBI, NCLT, and CLB. The panelists discuss the importance of time in the resolution process, the need for simplicity in procedures, and the potential of IBC to create a healthy credit market and restructuring hub in India. They also highlight the challenges faced in the implementation of IBC, such as the excessive financial dependence on financial institutions, the need for a bond market to flourish, and the legacy issues that need to be addressed. The panelists emphasize the importance of empirical research, data-driven decision making, and the need for rehabilitation in addition to recovery. They also discuss the potential of IBC to set trends for new forms of relief and the need for continued research and amendments to the code.
