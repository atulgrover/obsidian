## Summary
The video discusses the Committee of Creditors (CoC) in the context of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The CoC comprises financial creditors who control the resolution process through the Resolution Professional (RP). The CoC's role includes finalizing eligibility criteria for resolution, deciding on the feasibility and viability of resolution plans, negotiating terms for maximum value, and approving various decisions during the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP). The CoC is a process custodian, fiduciary for stakeholders, and a guardian of public interest. The video also explains the voting powers and meeting conduct of the CoC. The CoC meetings are conducted as per IBBI CIRP regulations, and only financial creditors have voting powers. The quorum for the meeting is 33% of the voting rights, and the notice period is five days, which can be reduced to 24 hours. Voting can be physical or electronic, and an agenda is approved only if members with the requisite voting share vote in favor. Decisions on resolution plans, RP appointment/replacement, and certain other matters require 66% voting, while other operational issues require a simple majority of 51%.
