## Summary
Dr. SK Gupta discussed the overview and importance of valuation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in a volatile, uncertain, complex, and ambiguous business world. IBC was passed in 2016 to rescue viable but failing companies and promote entrepreneurship, credit, and balanced stakeholder interests. Valuation is key in IBC for identifying a resolution applicant and estimating the potential worth of a distressed company. Valuation is a process of determining the economic worth of an asset or liability, based on future benefits, assumptions, constraints, and data availability. In IBC, registered valuers determine the fair value and liquidation value of assets under three different classes. Valuation reports are comprehensive and structured, containing mandatory contents and guidelines provided by IBC.
