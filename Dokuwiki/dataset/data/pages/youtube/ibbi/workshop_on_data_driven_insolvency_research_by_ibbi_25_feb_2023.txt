## Summary
The second day of the conference on Data Driven Insolvency Research, organized by IBBI and MCA, featured panel discussions on insolvency resolutions for SMEs and next generation reforms in insolvency. The day concluded with a workshop on data driven insolvency research, led by Jayanti Prasad, whole time member of IBBI, and Anita Shah Akella, Joint Secretary of MCA. Prasad discussed the importance of data in the insolvency ecosystem, the role of IBBI in regulating insolvency professionals and processes, and the ongoing efforts to create an integrated database for insolvency data. He also mentioned the various forms required to be filled during the ERP and the role of NESL in gathering and maintaining data. IBBI offers a lot of data on its website, with CRP data being the largest chunk of information. The research initiative guidelines launched by IBBI in 2019 aim to promote legal, economic, and interdisciplinary research in areas relevant to the insolvency and bankruptcy regime. The workshop aimed to encourage evidence-based research and timely regulatory interventions in the insolvency space.
