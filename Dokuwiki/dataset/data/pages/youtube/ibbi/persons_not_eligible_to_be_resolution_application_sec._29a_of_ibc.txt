## Summary
The video covers Section 29A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016, which lists persons not eligible to be resolution applicants. The provision aims to protect creditors of the company by preventing unscrupulous persons from gaining control of the company. The speaker discusses the evaluation of Section 29A, its applicability during insolvency stages and liquidation, and its role in determining the person to whom the company's assets may be sold after liquidation. The speaker also discusses the role of the resolution professional and committee of creditors in ensuring compliance with Section 29A. The constitutional validity of Section 29A is also discussed, with reference to the Swiss Ribbons Private Limited case. The speaker concludes by explaining the types of defaults and clauses that attract disqualification under Section 29A.
