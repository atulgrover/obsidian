## Summary
Mayank Mehta and Medha Shekar, associates at Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI), discuss the grievance redressal mechanisms. The IBBI is responsible for monitoring the performance of Insolvency Professionals (IPs) and addressing grievances filed against them. Complaints can be filed through the IBBI's website, by post, or hand-delivered, with a request fee of INR 2,500, which is refundable if the complaint is found to be non-frivolous. Complaints must be filed within 45 days of the cause of action, with a possibility of a 30-day extension. The IBBI assigns a unique registration number to each complaint and communicates it to the complainant within a week. The IBBI examines grievances and complaints, seeking responses from the IPs within seven days. If required, the IBBI may seek additional information from the complainant. After examining the comments, the IBBI comes to a conclusion within 30 days, potentially issuing a show-cause notice or ordering a detailed investigation. The IBBI has received 5,940 complaints and grievances since its inception and has disposed of 5,751 of them.
