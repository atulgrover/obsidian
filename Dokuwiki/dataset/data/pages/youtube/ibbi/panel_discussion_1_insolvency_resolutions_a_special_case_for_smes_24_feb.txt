## Summary
The panel discussion focuses on insolvency resolutions for SMEs. Sriram discusses the current state of SMEs in India, mentioning the identification of about 500 blocks this year and the Finance Minister's announcement of taking them up for inclusive growth. He also discusses the issues of timely payments by large private firms and the creation of a credit guarantee trust fund in 2020 to provide collateral-free automatic loans to SMEs. Antonia Menezes, a senior financial sector specialist at the World Bank, talks about the importance of developing specific insolvency regimes for micro and small enterprises (MSEs) and discusses the challenges they face in accessing finance and the insolvency process. She mentions the need for a tailored insolvency regime for MSEs and discusses the principles for effective insolvency and creditor data regimes. Hiroshi Kasuya, a partner at Baker McKenzie Japan, discusses the background of SMEs in Japan, the overview of restructuring and insolvency laws, and out-of-court workouts for SMEs. He also presents case studies and touches upon the new measures introduced by the Japanese government for SME revitalization.
