## Summary
The International Research Conference on Insolvency and Bankruptcy is being organized jointly by I am Bangalore and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI). The conference is supported by various organizations and aims to bring together researchers, scholars, professionals, and policymakers to discuss pivotal and emerging areas in insolvency and bankruptcy. The IBC has had an eventful journey since its inception in 2016, and the IBBI strives to be a knowledge organization by engaging with researchers, academia, and practitioners. The conference will include the presentation of research papers and discussions on various topics, including the use of artificial intelligence in insolvency, real estate insolvency, and the treatment of crown debts under the IBC. The IBBI values the research ideas and recommendations presented at the conference to aid in policy formulation.
