## Summary
The webinar discussed the importance of timely economic reforms in improving the Indian economy, with a focus on insolvency resolution and liquidation. The speakers highlighted the need for collaboration between industry and international partners to drive through reforms, and the role of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in resolving insolvency of corporate persons in a time-bound manner. They also discussed the various options available for reviving a company before resorting to liquidation, and the importance of learning from other jurisdictions like the UK. The speakers emphasized the need for a professional and market-determined approach to liquidation, and the importance of maximizing the assets of the corporate entity. They also discussed the challenges encountered in the initial days of implementing the IBC, and the measures taken to address them. The speakers highlighted the need for further cooperation with the UK government in establishing and developing the insolvency ecosystem in India.
