## Summary
Speaker Vikram Bajaj discusses Section 32A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in the context of liability and professional responsibilities. Section 32A was introduced to address concerns of resolution applicants regarding ongoing prosecution proceedings and attachment of assets of the corporate debtor. It provides immunity to bona fide resolution applicants from prosecution and attachment proceedings, subject to certain conditions. The first condition is that there should be a change in management through a resolution plan. The second condition is that the investigating authorities must not have reason to believe that certain persons are responsible for the offenses. The immunity applies to assets taken over under a resolution plan and assets sold by a liquidator as part of the liquidation process. However, it does not apply if the investigating authorities have specific reasons to believe that a person claiming immunity has perpetrated or collaborated in the offense. The section was challenged in the Supreme Court, but its constitutional validity was upheld.
