## Summary
The lecture covers two topics: 1) Analysis of provisions of the Limitation Act with respect to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016, and 2) Analysis of withdrawal of Corporate Insolvency Resolution Proceedings pursuant to settlement under Section 12A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016. The speaker, Mr. Ramachandra Rao, a General Manager with Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India, discusses the basics of insolvency in the IBC, its objectives, broad features, and the role of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India. The Limitation Act, 1963, and its applicability to the IBC are analyzed, with reference to the Insolvency Law Committee Report of March 2018 and various court judgments. The session also discusses the introduction of Section 12A by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (Second Amendment) Act 2018, its provisions, and the judgments interpreting the same. The speaker emphasizes the importance of acknowledgement of debt under Section 18 of the Limitation Act and its impact on the limitation period.
