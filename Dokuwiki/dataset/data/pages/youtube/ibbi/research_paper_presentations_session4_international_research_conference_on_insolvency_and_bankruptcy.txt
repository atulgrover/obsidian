## Summary
Welcome to the final session of the international conference on insolvency and bankruptcy. The session features paper presentations on the causal factors of corporate bankruptcy in India. The first paper, presented by Abhishek Haldar, Pratik Nahar, and Shashank Prakash, uses a recursive PLs-SEM approach to determine the causal factors. The study is motivated by the increase in bad loans and the implementation of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. The research objectives are formed based on literature classifying bankruptcy regimes and studies on creditor rights. The sample size is 5044 firms over the period of 2017-2021. The study finds that the bankruptcy trend is significantly caused by three factors: credit to deposit ratio, external debt, and GST collection. The implications of the study include insights for regulations and reducing congestion in bankruptcy courts. The limitations of the study include data unavailability and the need for a more granular data analysis. The study is unique in establishing a unidirectional causal relationship between bankruptcy trend and the three variables, and it could help improve regulatory reforms in bankruptcy regimes in India.
