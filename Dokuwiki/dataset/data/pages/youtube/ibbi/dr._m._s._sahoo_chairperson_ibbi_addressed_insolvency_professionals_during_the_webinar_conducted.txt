## Summary
Sahoo, Chairperson of IBBI, discussed the interesting half-day program 'Pimpal Stand-in' which had a panel discussion on the topic of 'Interesting Research Wanted to Make Full: Specific Offices for Fast Trials in Danger Trials for Humanitarian Countries and their Countries'. He emphasized the importance of artificial intelligence, unarmed immunity, and non-violent defense for world peace, and mentioned that this time at the G20 summit, the Indian Prime Minister was leading the IS leaders along with the strongest army from the UK. He also talked about the importance of mental health and providing care for difficult districts, and the need for health care workers to increase their responsibility towards health care. He mentioned the importance of businesses providing protection and action against tech companies and not fighting over net neutrality. He urged people to subscribe and subscribe to the channel and page.
