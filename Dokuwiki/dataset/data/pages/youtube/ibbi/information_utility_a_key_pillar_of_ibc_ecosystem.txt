## Summary
The Information Utility (IU) is a unique Indian innovation and a pillar of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). It is a legal credit repository that removes information asymmetry between debtors and creditors. The IU, National e-Governance Services Limited (NeSL), provides digital services such as accepting electronic submission of financial information, authenticating and verifying the information, and providing access to stored information. The IU helps insolvency professionals in the corporate insolvency resolution process by providing access to financial and operational credit information, dispute resolution, and report generation. The IU also provides a default alert broadcast service, claim verification, and data storage services. NeSL's Digital Document Execution (DDE) service ensures optimization of financial and human resources by demonetizing document execution and customer acquisition transactions, online stamp duty payment, and digital execution of documents. DDE has facilitated over 5.5 lakh transactions and 2.92 lakh crores in loan sanctions. DDE can be used across all customer segments and loan amounts, and it can be done anytime, anywhere, with sanction disbursement happening seamlessly through a mobile app.
