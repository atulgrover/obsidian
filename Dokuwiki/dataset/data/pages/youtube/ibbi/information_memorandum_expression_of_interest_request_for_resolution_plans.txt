## Summary
The video discusses the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and its focus on resolution over liquidation. The speaker explains the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP), which includes the application, admission, appointment of an Interim Resolution Professional (IRP), formation of a Committee of Creditors (CoC), and the preparation of an Information Memorandum (IM) by the Resolution Professional (RP). The IM provides information on the company's assets, liabilities, financial statements, creditors, guarantees, litigations, and other relevant details. It is submitted electronically to the CoC within two weeks of the RP's appointment and is confidential. The RP also provides the IM to all resolution applicants. The video then discusses the invitation for Expression of Interest (EoI) or Form G, which is published by the RP in English and the regional language of the corporate debtor's location. It includes the criteria for prospective resolution applicants, which is decided by the CoC with the RP's approval. The RP verifies the eligibility and other details of the EoI and releases a provisional and final list of eligible resolution applicants.
