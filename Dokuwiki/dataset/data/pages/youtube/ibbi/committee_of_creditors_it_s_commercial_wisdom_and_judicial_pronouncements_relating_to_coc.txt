## Summary
Sunil Mehta, Chief Executive of Indian Banks' Association, discusses the role and responsibilities of the Committee of Creditors (CoC) in the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016. The CoC, which is a statutory body and custodian of public trust, is responsible for pursuing resolution, evaluating the best resolution plan, and preventing liquidation. The CoC acts in the best interest of all stakeholders and discharges its duties with care, fairness, and transparency. The commercial wisdom of the CoC is supreme in deciding the best resolution plan, as established by various judicial pronouncements. The IBBI, in consultation with banks, has created a self-imposed Code of Conduct for the CoC to ensure fairness, balance the interests of all stakeholders, and ensure timely completion of the resolution process. Members of the CoC must maintain integrity, disclose conflicts of interest, cooperate with the insolvency professional, and respect the moratorium and confidentiality of information.
