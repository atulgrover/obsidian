## Summary
Aurelio Gurrea Martinez was invited to deliver a lecture on insolvency law, an honor he attributes to his admiration for India's recent reforms and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India's work. He shares insights from his research on insolvency systems in 80 jurisdictions, highlighting commonalities and differences. Martinez introduces his upcoming book, which challenges the notion of copying and pasting best practices from other jurisdictions. He emphasizes the importance of insolvency law in emerging economies, which represent 85% of the world's population and 60% of its GDP. He discusses the impact of insolvency law on solvent and insolvent companies, and the role it plays in efficiently reallocating assets. Martinez points out common features of emerging economies, such as a huge potential for growth, a high percentage of micro and small enterprises, and weak institutions. He highlights the importance of understanding these characteristics when designing insolvency frameworks and laws.
