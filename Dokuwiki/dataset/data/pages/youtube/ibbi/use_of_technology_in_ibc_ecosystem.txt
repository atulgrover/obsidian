## Summary
Welcome to the discussion on the use of technology in the IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code) ecosystem. The IBC has four pillars: Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India, adjudicating authorities, information utility, and insolvency professionals. Technology plays a crucial role in each of these pillars. The Information Utility (IU) is a technologically-driven institution where complete information flows automatically from Financial Creditors to the IU and is disseminated to different stakeholders. Adjudicating authorities have developed a technology-driven platform for online documentation and case proceedings. The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) uses technology for online filings and information sharing with Insolvency Professionals (IPs). IPs are provided with a digital platform to manage multiple processes and regulatory filings. IBBI has also implemented PDAs (Platform for Distress Assets) for distress asset management, including auction platforms for efficient price discovery. The IBC has transformed the life cycle of a company, incorporating technology for data capture and management at different stages.
