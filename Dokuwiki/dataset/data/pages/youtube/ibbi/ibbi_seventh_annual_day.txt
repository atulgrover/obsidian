## Summary
Welcome address on the 7th annual day of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) by the Chairperson, Dr. M. Goyal. He highlighted the achievements of IBC in the last 7 years, including the direct recovery of over 3 lakh crores for creditors, resolution of accounts worth 2 lakh crores increased to 6 lakh crores post-resolution, and a decrease in bank NPAs. He acknowledged the delays in IBC but compared it favorably with previous resolution mechanisms, stating that the number of resolutions has increased by 80% in the last year. He requested resolution professionals to increase their speed to match the increased speed of the IBBI. He also mentioned that IBBI has issued discussion papers to reduce delays and improve working.
