## Summary
The panel discussion focuses on the banking and industry perspective on the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. The chairperson, a member of the National Company Law Tribunal, highlights the welfare nature of the IBC and its role in making corporate entities stand on their feet. The discussion points include the impact of IBC on banking and finance business, the time-bound resolution process, the role of adjudicating authorities, and the inclusion of non-banking financial institutions in the IBC. The panel also discusses the need for checks and balances in the commercial wisdom of the Committee of Creditors and the impact of IBC on the recovery rates and stock prices of banks. The discussion also covers the need for a wider market of stress assets, operational efficiency, and timely appointments for effective implementation of IBC.
