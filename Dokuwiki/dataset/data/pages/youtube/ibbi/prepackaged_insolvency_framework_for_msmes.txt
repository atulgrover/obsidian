## Summary
Rajesh Kumar Gupta, Chief General Manager at IBBI, discussed the new prepackaged insolvency resolution process (PPIRP) for MSMEs under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. The PPIRP aims to provide a simplified, time-efficient, and cost-efficient hybrid process for MSMEs, ensuring business continuity and preventing loss of employment. The PPIRP originated from the Insolvency Law Committee's subcommittee report in October 2020 and was implemented through the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code Amendment Ordinance 2021. The PPIRP is applicable to MSMEs with a minimum default of INR 10 lakh. It combines formal and informal resolution processes, allowing stakeholders to discuss various options before moving to a formal process. The resolution professional's role includes ensuring transparency, fairness, and compliance with the law. The committee of creditors guides the process, approving transactions and managing the company during the process. Public announcements, claim collation, and information memorandums are part of the process. Valuation of assets, registration plans, and a 120-day timeline for finalizing the resolution plan are also included. Checks and balances, such as limited access to updated audited financial statements and determining transactions, prevent abuse of the process.
