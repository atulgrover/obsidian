## Summary
Welcome to the last session of the day, which features a presentation on Vikalpa, the journal for decision makers, and a research initiative by the IBBI on insolvency and bankruptcy. The speaker, Prashant Das, discusses the use of Google Trends data as a legitimate proxy for macroeconomic trends and its potential in predicting insolvencies and bankruptcies. He shares his experience of using Google Trends data in his research and highlights the importance of data quality for research. Das then introduces the topic of his talk, which is the use of data in insolvency and bankruptcy research, particularly in the context of the Indian economy. He emphasizes the need for good quality data and the potential of the IBBI's research initiative in providing such data. The next speaker, Rebecca Perry, discusses the importance of digital economies and the challenges they pose in the event of an insolvency. She highlights the need for a sector-specific approach to insolvencies and the potential impact of digital economies on insolvency law in India and other countries.
