## Summary
The video covers a session on group insolvency as part of a lecture series by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India in partnership with Indira Gandhi National Open University. Rajesh Tiwari, General Manager, discusses the definition, need, issues, and challenges of group insolvency. A group is defined as entities that directly or indirectly exercise 66% voting rights or appoint more than 50% members of the board. Group insolvency is when the insolvency of one company impacts other group companies, and it is considered beneficial to resolve all group companies together for value maximization and optimization. Challenges include different stakeholders, security interests, and coordination between foreign and internal representatives. In India, group insolvency is dealt with under sections 62 and 63 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, and the education authorities and Supreme Court have the power to coordinate proceedings. Aniket Sharma then discusses international experiences, practices, and case studies on group insolvency.
