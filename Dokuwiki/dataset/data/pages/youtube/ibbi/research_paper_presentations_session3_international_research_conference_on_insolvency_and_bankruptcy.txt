## Summary
The afternoon session of paper presentations for the international research conference on insolvency and bankruptcy focuses on the IBC and balancing fairness and equity. The first presentation by Pooja Singla and Vinay Pandey discusses the importance of claims in the resolution process, suggesting that the Resolution Professional (RP) should be empowered to make quasi-judicial determination of claims. They also propose an appropriate code of conduct for Committee of Creditors (COC) members and the need for an independent site over the entire resolution process. The second presentation by Vinay Pandey discusses the fee of RP versus fee of liquidator, suggesting a success fee for the RP, and the extent of procedure fairness and judiciary involved in the IBC.
