## Summary
In this episode of the talk show, the speaker discusses their experience with a corporate hospital that was going through insolvency. The hospital had both regular employees and consultants. The consultants, who were also practicing elsewhere, claimed to be employees of the hospital, receiving a salary and having an appointment letter that designated them as senior consultants. However, they were unable to provide proof that their income from the hospital was taken as salary in their income tax return, as required by sections 192 or 194J. The issue was resolved in the next hearing.
