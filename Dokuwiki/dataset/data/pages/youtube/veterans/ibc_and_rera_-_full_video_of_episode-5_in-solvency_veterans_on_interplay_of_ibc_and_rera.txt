## Summary
In this episode of Insolvency Veteran Talk Show, hosts Rajesh Sharma and Shri Santosh Shukla, Executive Director of IBBI, welcome two distinguished guests, Shri Kamal Kant Jain, a chartered accountant and former member of Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Appellate Tribunal, and Shri Santosh Shukla. The topic of discussion is the interplay between IBC and RERA. The guests share their perspectives on how the two laws can coexist and complement each other, focusing on different areas of resolution and regulation. They also discuss the confusion among stakeholders regarding where to seek relief and the need for amendments in RERA, particularly in the section where it interacts with IBC. The guests highlight the importance of the real estate sector in the IBC ecosystem and the need for a clear understanding of moratorium and its implications on both corporate debtors and real estate projects.
