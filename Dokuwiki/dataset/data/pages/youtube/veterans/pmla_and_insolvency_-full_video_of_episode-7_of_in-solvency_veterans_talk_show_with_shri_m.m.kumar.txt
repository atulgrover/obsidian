## Summary
Rajesh Sharma, a former member of the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), hosts a talk show called 'Insolvency Veterans' and welcomes Shri Kumar, former Chief Justice and founder president of NCLT. Kumar shares his experience of working with the NCLT and the importance of discussions for those connected with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). He recounts an experience of a member seeking his views on a case and how he guided the member to decide independently. Kumar emphasizes the importance of judicial independence and ethics in the functioning of NCLT. He also shares his experience of dealing with a natural calamity during his tenure as Chief Justice of Jammu and Kashmir High Court and how he managed to keep the courts functioning. Kumar discusses his experience as chairperson of the Company Law Board and the differences between the pre-IBC and post-IBC regimes. He shares his views on the recent Delhi High Court judgment regarding the power of the Enforcement Directorate (ED) to attach properties under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) during liquidation proceedings under the IBC.
