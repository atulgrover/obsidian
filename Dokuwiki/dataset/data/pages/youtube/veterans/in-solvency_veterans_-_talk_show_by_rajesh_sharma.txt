## Summary
In this weekly talk show, Solvency Veterans, hosted by Rajesh Sharma, former member judge of the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), eminent guests who are sitting or former judges of NCLT or NCLAT, or have been instrumental in implementing the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016, share their thoughts on landmark judgments and the current situation and course corrections required for the smooth implementation of the code. The show is intended for NCLT and NCLAT judges, members, practitioners, advocates, chartered accountants, company secretaries, management accountants, insolvency resolution professionals, and students and teachers of law colleges and postgraduate insolvency programs.
