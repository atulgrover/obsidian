## Summary
The speaker discusses the relationship between the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act (RERA). According to the speaker, both acts came around the same time with a gap of 4-5 months and are meant to complement each other, not conflict. The IBC has the main focus of insolvency resolution of a distressed situation, and in case of any conflict, it would take precedence over RERA.
