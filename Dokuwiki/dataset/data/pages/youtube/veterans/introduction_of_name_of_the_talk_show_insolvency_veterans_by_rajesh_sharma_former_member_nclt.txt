## Summary
The name of this talk show, 'Insolvency Veterans,' is a play on words that represents the purpose of the IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code). The term 'insolvency' is written in red, symbolizing financial distress, while 'solvency' is written in black, indicating the goal of IBC to restore the financial health and viability of companies facing insolvency through a time-bound and transparent resolution process.
