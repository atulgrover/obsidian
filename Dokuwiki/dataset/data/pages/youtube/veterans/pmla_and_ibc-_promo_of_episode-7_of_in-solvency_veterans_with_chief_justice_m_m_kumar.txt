## Summary
The speaker, who has practical experience with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), explains that once a resolution plan is approved by the resolution applicant, they cannot back out. IBC is a business law that prioritizes the continuance of the business over other considerations. The speaker questions who would tell someone to continue incurring losses in business, emphasizing that IBC is primarily concerned with the business aspect of a resolution plan.
