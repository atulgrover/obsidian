## Summary
Before the IBC (Indian Bankruptcy Code), the concept of insolvency was not well-known among lawyers, with insolvency matters typically handled by company judges in high courts. The importance given to insolvency matters was not as significant as it is under the code. Notable guests on the show include Deepti, a former member of NCLT (National Company Law Tribunal), and Mukesh, a veteran in the field of solvency.
