## Summary
Welcome to the inaugural episode of Insolvency Veterans, a talk show hosted by Rajesh Sharma, former member of NCLT. The guest for this episode is Dr. Vineet Kothari, former Acting Chief Justice of Gujarat High Court and a senior advocate in Supreme Court of India. Dr. Kothari begins by expressing his gratitude for being invited as the inaugural guest and speaks highly of Rajesh Sharma's efforts in organizing such an educative program. He then shares his thoughts on how the IBC code of laws aims to restore the financial health of companies in a time-bound and transparent manner, and how the experts and practitioners involved in its implementation can be considered 'veterans' in the field. Dr. Kothari goes on to discuss the benefits of the IBC for NCLT and NCLAT judges, advocates, insolvency professionals, and other stakeholders, as well as the role his CA qualification has played in his judicial career. He then shares his thoughts on the pre-IBC era and the post-IBC scenario, highlighting the shift in focus from debtors to creditors. Dr. Kothari also speaks about the need for expeditious disposal of cases in NCLT and NCLEX, and the importance of amendments in the IBC law to keep up with the needs of the business. He commends the proactive nature of the Supreme Court in delivering judgments in IBC cases and interpreting the law for the benefit of all stakeholders.
