## Summary
Welcome to the inaugural episode of Insolvency Veterans, a talk show focused on the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code and its impact on the Indian economy and society. The host, Rajesh Sharma, a former member of the National Company Law Tribunal, will discuss current issues and challenges with experts and practitioners in the field. The special guest for this episode is Vineet Kothari, former Acting Chief Justice of the Gujarat High Court.
