## Summary
The speaker, an insolvency professional, discusses the resolution plan in the context of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). They highlight the importance of creating a favorable environment for resolution applicants and the superiority of IBC over previous regulations. The speaker also discusses the recent modification in the definition of resolution plans, which now includes reasons for restructuring such as merger, amalgamation, and demerger. The speaker emphasizes that the eligibility of a resolution plan applicant should be checked at the time of submission of the resolution plan, not the expression of interest. They also mention that there is no limit on the number of resolution plans or modifications that can be submitted. The speaker highlights the importance of the information memorandum provided by the RP to the PRs, stating that any incorrect information could lead to litigation. They also mention the negotiation between the PRs and the COC regarding the eligibility criteria and the responsibility of the RP for representations and warranties.
