## Summary
This webinar, part 2 of a series, discusses how the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) can help home buyers. The speakers include CA Anand Soon Bhadra, a senior partner at Triple A Insolvency, and Umesh Goel, a banker. They discuss the recent Supreme Court judgment in the Manish Kumar vs Union of India case, which allows 10 or 100 home buyers to move an application for the insolvency of a real estate company. Triple A Insolvency Professionals LLP has launched a platform to facilitate home buyers in registering their stress and collating 100 or 10% of home buyers of one project to file an application for insolvency. The IBC process can help home buyers by taking control of the company's assets, protecting them from misuse by promoters, and verifying claims by allottees. The new structure introduced by an ordinance on 4th April and regulations on 9th April allows for a pre-packaged insolvency resolution process, which can help find a resolution based on an agreement between home buyers and the builder. Home buyers are considered financial creditors, and the valuation of a real estate company's assets is crucial in finding a resolution. The highest and best use of the property is considered in valuation, and the value is realized by completing the project and handing it over to the creditors.
