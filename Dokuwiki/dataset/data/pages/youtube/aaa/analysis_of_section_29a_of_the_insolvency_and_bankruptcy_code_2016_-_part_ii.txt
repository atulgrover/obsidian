## Summary
The speaker is discussing the importance of Section 29A of the IBC and its practical aspects in the 50th webinar of their series. They highlight the difficulty of verifying eligibility under Section 29A, which requires collecting data from various public portals and software. The process involves verifying the resolution applicant or proposal, corporate data, and related parties. The speaker emphasizes the need for an affidavit from the prospective bidders declaring their eligibility and the consequences of providing incorrect information. The verification process at Triple A includes using different kinds of software and information available on financial credit information bureaus, RBI lists of willful defaulters, CBSC lists of debarred parties, and other litigation hotels. The speaker also mentions the use of simple Google searches and software that can find related parties based on common email IDs, domain names, common employees, and even past employees and directors. They stress that the ultimate responsibility of verification lies with the RP or the liquidator, and disclaimers should be included in the reports given to the COC.
