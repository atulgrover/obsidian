## Summary
The webinar discussed recent judgments from NCLAT, including SMS Foundation and Investment LP vs Harsha Engineering Private Limited and Mr Rathore vs Mrs Gopal Sami Ganesh Babu in the case of Milan Textile Enterprises Private Limited. The first judgment involves a claim rejection by the RP due to related party transactions and the claimant's real intention not being to hold shares. The NCLT Chennai found that the applicant did not duly transfer shares and is not a shareholder. The second judgment discusses the delay in filing a claim and incorrect Form B submission. The NCLT dismissed the application, stating that condonation of delay cannot be granted as a matter of course. The webinar also discussed the importance of claim verification, the RP's duty to collect and collate claims, and the need to record reasons for claim rejection.
