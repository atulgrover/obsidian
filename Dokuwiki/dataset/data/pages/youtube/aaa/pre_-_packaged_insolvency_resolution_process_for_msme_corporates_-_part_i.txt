## Summary
The webinar is about the new Pre-Packaged Insolvency Resolution Process (PIRP) for MSME corporates in India, which is a quicker and cost-effective alternative to the existing CIRP. The PIRP is primarily for MSMEs and is supposed to be less litigative due to its consensual nature. The financial creditors need to provide consent, and the promoters are not under fear as they provide detailed information beforehand. The regulations were released on 9th April 2021, and the default amount is 10 lakhs of rupees for MSMEs. The PIRP period starts from the application's admittance by the adjudicating authority and ends with the approval of the resolution plan or termination of the PIRP. The promoters need to be cautious while engaging in PIRP, as no PIRP can be initiated within three years of undergoing one. The priority of applications is analyzed, and it is concluded that if any PIRP application is pending, no CIRP application can be accepted. If a CIRP application is pending, a PIRP application can be filed within 14 days and will get preference. If a CIRP application is pending, and the PIRP application is filed after 14 days, the CIRP applications will be attended first. In case the PIRP resolution plan is contravened, the company can go into liquidation, and the resolution professional will be appointed as a liquidator.
