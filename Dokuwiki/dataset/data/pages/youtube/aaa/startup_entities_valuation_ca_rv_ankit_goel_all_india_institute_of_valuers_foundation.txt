## Summary
Welcome to the second day of the Valuation CP program. Today's topic is the valuation of startup entities. The speaker, Ankit Goel, starts the discussion by asking what the participants understand by a startup and why someone finds value in these companies. A participant, Supreme, asks how a startup with just an idea, like an electric bike, can be valued and receive funding. Ankit Goel mentions that they will discuss various methods for valuing startups, including those with no historical data or performance. He also mentions that they will discuss the regulatory requirements for valuation of startups like FEMA and Companies Act. Another participant, Narendra, asks how to differentiate between stable and highly risky startups and value them accordingly. Ankit Goel plans to cover the stages of a startup's life and how the risk perception changes based on each stage. He also mentions that a startup is primarily a business where the business idea is fueled by capital sourced from investors. He compares this with the traditional brick and mortar model where the business is fueled by the promoter's capital and collateral. He takes the example of Google and how it started and created a niche in the market by providing a search engine service and later monetizing it through ads. He also mentions that WhatsApp, which had no revenue for a long time, is now monetizing its data and providing commercial services to businesses.
