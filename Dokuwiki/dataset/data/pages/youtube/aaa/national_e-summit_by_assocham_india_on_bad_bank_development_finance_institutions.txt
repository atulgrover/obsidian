## Summary
Anil Goel, Founder and Chairman of ASSOCHAM, discussed the pre-packed insolvency resolution process (PIRP) under the IBC as a consensual and less expensive process for financial restructuring, with a timeline of about 240 days. He mentioned the benefits of PIRP over CIRP, including the continued possession of assets by the corporate debtor, the responsibility of business continuity by the board of directors, and the lack of coercion. Goel also highlighted the need for a guideline by the RBI for banks to accept employees by way of assignment of debt to ARCs in cases of insolvency prepaid insolvency or compromise. He also mentioned the additional advantage of no provisioning requirement for banks for PIRP, making it a preferred option over CIRP. Goel concluded by stating that both PIRP and NARC are moving towards consensual resolution rather than coercive actions.
