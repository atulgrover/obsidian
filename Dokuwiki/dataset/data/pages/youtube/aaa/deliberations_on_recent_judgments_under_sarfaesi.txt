## Summary
The webinar focuses on the Surfacey law and its applicability in comparison to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The Surfacey law is applicable to all kinds of security interests, including home loans and loan against property, while IBC is applicable only to limited companies and limited liability partnerships. The Surfacey law is used to enforce security interests, while IBC is used to resolve business issues. The webinar discusses various Supreme Court and High Court judgments on the Surfacey law, including the classification of agriculture land, powers of district magistrate, CMM or DM, rights of lessee, and the right of redemption of a borrower. The right of redemption has been changed in 2016, and now the borrower has to tender the total amount payable to the lender before the asset is sold. The webinar also discusses the impact of the amended Surfacey law on the borrower's right of redemption in an auction conducted under the Surfacey law and the effect of the amendment to Surfacey law read with Section 60 of the Transfer of Property Act.
