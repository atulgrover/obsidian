## Summary
The report discussed in this webinar focuses on the analysis and deliberations of a committee examining issues related to legacy styled real estate projects, headed by Amitabh Khan. The committee was set up to find ways to complete stalled projects, particularly in Noida and Greater Noida. The report highlights that financial viability, internal rate of return, and construction cost overruns are primary reasons for stalled projects. The committee recommends state-announced real estate packages, RERA taking over projects, and IBC as the last resort for project resolution. The report is divided into seven parts, including mandatory registration of projects with RERA, execution and registration of sale deeds, occupancy and possession of substantially completed projects, a state government rehabilitation package, a framework for RERA administrator-led revival of projects, financing of stalled projects, and use of IBC for resolving projects as a measure of last resort.
