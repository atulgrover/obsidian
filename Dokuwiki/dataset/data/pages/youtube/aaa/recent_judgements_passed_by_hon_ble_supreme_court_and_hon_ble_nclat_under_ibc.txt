## Summary
The webinar discusses recent Supreme Court and NCLAT judgments related to IBC. The first case is regarding a Gujarat Value Added Tax law where the Supreme Court held that the state tax officer cannot become a security creditor, and the attachment of assets during CIRP does not imply an automatic first charge on the property. The second case, K Per Masivam vs. Maharaja Theme Park and Resorts, involves a corporate guarantor and the Supreme Court's decision to allow IBC proceedings against the guarantor. The third case, Access Bank Limited vs. Power Limited, is a review petition regarding the Supreme Court's judgment that it is not mandatory for the adjudicating authority to admit all applications filed under Section 7 of the IBC.
