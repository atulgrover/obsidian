## Summary
The webinar, organized by the Federation of Rajasthan Trade and Industry, discusses the Pre-Packaged Insolvency Resolution Process (Pirp) for MSME enterprises. The speaker, Anil Goel, Founder and Chairman of Triple A Insolvency Professional LLP, highlights the rationale behind introducing Pirp for MSMEs, which is to ensure quicker, cost-effective, and value-maximizing outcomes for all stakeholders while preserving jobs in a least disruptive manner. Pirp is applicable to micro, small, and medium enterprises as per the MSME Act 2006, having invested in plant and machinery up to 1 crore, 10 crores, and 50 crores, and turnover not exceeding 5 crores, 25 crores, and 100 crores, respectively. Goel explains the differences between Pirp and the existing Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP), where the control and custody of the business and assets remain with the board of directors or promoters, and the responsibility of keeping the company as a going concern lies with them. In contrast, in CIRP, the control and custody of the business and assets are taken over by the RP, and the responsibility of keeping the company as a going concern lies with them. Goel also discusses the other options available for MSMEs, such as the RBI framework and the Informal Restructuring OTS, which are popular presently in banks. The webinar aims to provide insights into the new Pre-Packaged Insolvency Resolution Process for MSME enterprises and its benefits over the existing structures.
