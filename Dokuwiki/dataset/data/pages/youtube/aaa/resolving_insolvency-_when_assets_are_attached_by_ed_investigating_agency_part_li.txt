## Summary
Today's webinar, part two of the series, discusses various judgments where the assets of companies undergoing insolvency resolution are attached by enforcement agencies under PMLA or other laws. The judgments can be categorized into three parts: before the commencement of insolvency, during the CP or liquidation process, and after the resolution plan has been approved or assets have been sold. The moratorium under section 14 of IBC and its applicability to cases where assets are attached by enforcement agencies is also discussed. A detailed analysis is provided of the Bombay High Court judgment in the case of Shiv Chan and Others v. Adjudicating Authority Under PMLA, which clarified the adjudicating authority's jurisdiction to direct the enforcement directorate to release attached assets and the applicability of section 32A of the IBC to protect the corporate debtor's assets. Other judgments, such as the Versana ISAT case, the Romac Group of Companies case, and the Sterling SCI Infrastructure Limited case, are also discussed.
