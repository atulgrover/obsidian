## Summary
The webinar covers two parts: the first part focuses on the RBI guidelines for fraud detection and willful defaults, and the second part focuses on transactional audits under IBC. The RBI guidelines, applicable to all commercial banks except RRBs, aim to prevent and detect fraud, examine staff accountability, and manage fraud risks. The guidelines classify frauds based on the Indian Penal Code and cover misappropriation, criminal breach of trust, manipulation of books of account, and unauthorized credit facilities. The IBC defines fraud as any act with the objective of defrauding creditors. The RBI guidelines have a new framework for accounts with a balance of INR 60 crores and above and expose of INR 50 crores and above, with early warning signals, red-flagging, and reporting to the fraud monitoring group, SCBF, central repository of information, and central fraud monitoring cell. The IBC requires the RP to report frauds to the NCLT adjudicating authority, which passes an order, and IDBI takes criminal action if necessary.
