## Summary
The speaker is discussing the recent amendments to the CIRP and Liquidation Regulations, 2021, notified on 30th September by the IBBI. The amendments include limitations on the modifications to the Invitation for Expression of Interest (EoI), Request for Resolution Plan (RFRP), and Bid Valuation Metrics. The speaker highlights the rationale behind these amendments, which is to streamline the process, reduce delays, and ensure that the resolution applicants are well-prepared. The speaker also discusses the implications of these amendments, including the restriction of multiple expressions of interest, the requirement for the RP to carefully consider the clauses of the RFRP and evaluation matrix, and the limitation on the number of modifications allowed in the resolution plan. The speaker believes that these amendments will bring clarity, reduce uncertainties, and promote the submission of the best possible offers from the resolution applicants.
