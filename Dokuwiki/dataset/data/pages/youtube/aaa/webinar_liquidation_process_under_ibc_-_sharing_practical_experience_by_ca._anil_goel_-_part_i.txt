## Summary
This is a webinar on the liquidation process, led by Anil Goel, a chartered accountant and senior partner at Triple A's for Busy Professionals LLP. He has experience with 23-24 liquidation cases and will share his practical experience on the subject. The webinar will also cover various judgments and hurdles faced during the liquidation process. The liquidation process is important, especially during economic downturns, as it allows companies to be taken over and revived. The webinar will also discuss the roles of different teams within Triple A's, including the liquidation team and the asset sale team. The presentation will cover the different scenarios under which a company can be liquidated, including the rejection of a resolution plan or the lack of a resolution plan within the specified time period. The liquidation process has immediate effects, such as the restarting of legal proceedings and the appointment of a liquidator who is in charge of the company and its assets. The liquidator is typically the resolution professional who handled the CIRP, but this may not always be the case.
