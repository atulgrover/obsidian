## Summary
This webinar discusses SEBI compliances for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The conversation revolves around various circulars and amendments to SEBI LODR regulations, focusing on disclosure requirements for Interim Resolution Professionals (IRPs) or Resolution Professionals (RPs) when a company enters Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP). The 16 disclosure requirements include admission of CIRP applications, public announcements, list of creditors, appointment or replacement of RPs, meetings of the committee of creditors, and details of the resolution plan, among others. The speakers emphasize the importance of understanding these requirements as a listed company's trading does not get suspended merely due to the commencement of CIRP; it is suspended because of non-compliance with regulations. The speakers also discuss the recent SEBI consultation paper for the protection of public and small shareholders.
