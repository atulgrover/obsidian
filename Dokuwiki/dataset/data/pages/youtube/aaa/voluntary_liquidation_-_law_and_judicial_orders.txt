## Summary
Today's webinar focuses on voluntary liquidation as introduced by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. This is the main mode of setting a company and involves the company self-imposing its own liquidation and dissolution after approval of the shareholders. The process starts with a declaration from the majority of directors, followed by a special resolution passed by the company in a shareholders' meeting where the Liquidator is appointed. The Liquidator then files a preliminary report, and a public announcement is made for creditors to file their claims. The Liquidator verifies the claims, prepares a list of stakeholders, and distributes the assets to creditors and shareholders. The company ceases to carry its business, but the powers of the board of directors and key managerial personnel continue, and the Liquidator has significant powers to protect the interests of shareholders and creditors. The Liquidator is responsible for meeting all compliances, paying taxes, and keeping books of account. The Liquidator can engage professionals with the consent of the stakeholders and board of directors.
