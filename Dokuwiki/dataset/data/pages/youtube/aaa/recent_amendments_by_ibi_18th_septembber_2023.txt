## Summary
The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) has introduced updates to the regulations, primarily focused on addressing the challenges faced by insolvency professionals and reducing the workload of adjudicating authorities. The updates include the insertion of Regulation 2D, which requires financial and operational creditors to submit a chronology of debt and default, including the date of debt becoming due, part payments, acknowledgment of debt, and the applicable limitation period, along with the application. Additionally, Regulation 3, inserted on 18th September 2023, empowers the IRP or RP to take custody and control of corporate data from key managerial personnel, promoters, and any other person associated with the management of the corporate debtor. The information required includes records of assets, finances, and operations of the corporate debtor for the previous two years, financial and operational payments, a list of assets and liabilities as on the initiation date, and other specified matters. The updates aim to simplify the application process, reduce the time taken by the NCLT, and define a process for seeking information, control, and custody from corporate data.
