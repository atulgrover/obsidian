## Summary
Today's discussion is about dissolving a company or removing the company name from the register of companies maintained by the ROC under the Companies Act. The options include liquidation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016, voluntary liquidation under the IBC, removal of name of the company from the register of companies, winding up by the tribunal, and a summary procedure for winding up of the companies. The advantages, disadvantages, and preconditions of each method are discussed. The impact of income tax, pending litigations, and regulatory compliances are also considered. The speakers share their personal experiences and answer questions from the audience.
