## Summary
This is the 74th episode of a webinar series discussing waivers, concessions, and relaxations under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The host, Anil Goel, is conducting the webinar from a golf club in Pune. In this part two, the discussion revolves around regulation 37 of the IBC, which outlines measures that can be taken up and approved by the committee of creditors for insolvency resolution and maximization of asset value. Various scenarios are discussed, such as the transfer or sale of assets, restructuring of the company, mergers or demergers, and cancellation or delisting of shares. Compliance requirements, non-compliances, and modifications of debt terms are also addressed, emphasizing that these matters can be resolved through a resolution plan without requiring separate approvals from shareholders, creditors, or debenture holders.
