## Summary
Today's webinar discusses the concept of project-wise insolvency in real estate companies under IBC, focusing on resolving projects rather than entire companies. The concept was first introduced by Justice SJ Mukad of NCLAT and has been upheld by the Supreme Court. It allows for the exclusion of one project from insolvency while another project is resolved through reverse insolvency. Project-wise insolvency benefits promoters by giving them control over other projects, relief from RERA and other litigations, and the moratorium on the project. Challenges include tracing assets between projects, lack of transparency in accounting, and common liabilities extending the moratorium to other parts of the company. The webinar also covers various judicial pronouncements and practical difficulties faced in project-wise insolvency.
