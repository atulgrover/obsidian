## Summary
The video discusses the role and utility of Information Utilities (IUs) in the Indian insolvency process, as outlined in the proposed amendments to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The speaker, Deba Jyoti Ray Chaudhary, Managing Director of National E-Governance Services Limited (NESL), the only company currently handling IU in India, explains the unique features of IUs, including the option for data providers to accept, dispute, or ignore submitted information, and the use of push notifications at various life cycle events in the insolvency resolution process. The presentation also covers the benefits of IUs for financial creditors, operational creditors, and professionals such as resolution professionals and liquidators. The proposed amendments include making it mandatory for both financial and operational creditors to submit information of debt and default on the IU. The speaker notes that there is currently no penalty for financial creditors who do not comply with this section of the IBC, but suggests that the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) could potentially take action against financial institutions such as banks, NBFCs, and ARCs for non-compliance.
