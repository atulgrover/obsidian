## Summary
The webinar discusses the issue of remuneration of Insolvency Professionals (IPs) and the obligations of creditors to contribute to the Insolvency Resolution Process Cost (IRP Cost). The speakers analyze the relevant provisions of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), regulations, and circulars related to the definition and payment of IRP Cost. They highlight that the IRP Cost includes any expenditure incurred for running the business of the corporate debtor as a going concern, fees payable to the IP, and other costs directly related to the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP). The speakers also discuss various landmark judgments and pronouncements that impact the remuneration of IPs and the jurisdiction of the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) in adjudicating the quantum of fees. They emphasize the need for proper scrutiny and examination of the quantum of fees by the NCLT and the Adjudicating Authority, without passing ad-hoc orders. The speakers encourage participants to ask questions and clarify their doubts regarding the remuneration of IPs and the IRP Cost.
