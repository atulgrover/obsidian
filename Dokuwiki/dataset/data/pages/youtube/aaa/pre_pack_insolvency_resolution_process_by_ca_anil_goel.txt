## Summary
The video discusses the Pre-Packaged Insolvency Process, a new resolution process introduced for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in India. The intention behind this introduction is to provide an efficient alternative for corporate persons like MSMEs in terms of resolution process, which should not disturb their current ongoing business or should not lead to loss of employment. The speaker, Anil Goel, is a central council member and an insolvency professional with over 30 years of experience in company law, corporate structuring, management advisory, audit, and assurance services. He was the chairman of the Western Regional Council in the year 2013-14 and has conducted more than 150 seminars on various forums and conferences on company law, LLP, and IBC. The Pre-Packaged Insolvency Process aims to mitigate the distress caused by the COVID-19 pandemic, which has impacted the business operations of MSMEs and exposed many of them to financial distress. The new scheme will ensure a quicker, cost-effective, and value-maximizing resolution plan for all stakeholders, while preserving jobs and boosting the economy. The introduction of this Pre-Packaged Insolvency Process will open up new opportunities for professionals, including chartered accountants, to act as claim consultants, resolution plan preparers, valuers, transaction auditors, forensic auditors, and more.
