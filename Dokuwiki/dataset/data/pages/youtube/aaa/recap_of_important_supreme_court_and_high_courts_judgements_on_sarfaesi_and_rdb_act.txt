## Summary
In this webinar, the speaker discusses various judgments delivered by the Indian Supreme Court in 2022-23 related to SARFAESI, RDB Act, and insolvency laws. The judgments discussed include those related to agricultural lands, tenancy, and the appointment of advocates. The speaker highlights how the Supreme Court has made the SARFAESI law more effective and speedier than other recovery modes for secured creditors. The speaker also discusses two specific judgments: one related to the burden of proof on the borrower to prove that the property is an agricultural land, and the other related to the calculation of the pre-deposit amount required before an appeal is taken up by the Appellate Tribunal.
