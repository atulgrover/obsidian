## Summary
The webinar discusses the importance of Information Utility (IU) as one of the four pillars of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. The IU aims to address information asymmetry between debtors and creditors by authenticating and broadcasting information on defaults. This helps creditors take risk mitigation measures and facilitates smooth admission processes. The IU also provides financial information about parties, which is crucial for insolvency resolution, loan disbursal, and regular business transactions. The original concept of IU was to cover all transactions, but currently, it focuses on the resolution process, providing an undisputed set of facts for efficient decision-making. The discussion paper emphasizes the verification process for financial information submitted to the IU.
