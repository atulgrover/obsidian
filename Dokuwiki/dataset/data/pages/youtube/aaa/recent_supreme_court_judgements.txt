## Summary
In a recent webinar, the speaker discussed two recent Supreme Court judgments related to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The first judgment, MK Raja gopalan vs Palani, involved Appu Hotels and dealt with the distribution of proceeds in a resolution plan. The court held that there is no provision in the IBC that requires mandatory payment to related party creditors and that the distribution of proceeds is the domain of the resolution applicant. The second judgment, Vistra ITCL vs Dilker Venkatasubramanian, involved M Tech auto and dealt with the discrimination between claims of related and non-related parties. The court observed that the Phoenix Arc judgment specifically treats related parties as a separate class and cannot be discriminated against in the distribution of proceeds. The speaker also discussed the implications of these judgments for related parties and the importance of ensuring that the insolvency process benefits all creditors equally.

## Summary
In a recent webinar, the speaker discussed two recent Supreme Court judgments related to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The first judgment, MK Raja gopalan vs Palani, involved Appu Hotels and dealt with the distribution of proceeds in a resolution plan. The court held that there is no provision in the IBC that requires mandatory payment to related party creditors and that the distribution of proceeds is the domain of the resolution applicant. The second judgment, Vistra ITCL vs Dilker Venkatasubramanian, involved M Tech auto and dealt with the discrimination between claims of related and non-related parties. The court observed that the Phoenix Arc judgment specifically treats related parties as a separate class and cannot be discriminated against in the distribution of proceeds. The speaker also discussed the implications of these judgments for related parties and the importance of ensuring that the insolvency process benefits all creditors equally.
