## Summary
The vcm, organized by the Agra branch of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), features a panel of esteemed professionals discussing opportunities in valuation and capital markets. The speaker, Ankit Goel, is a registered valuer, founder partner of a well-known valuation firm, and a regular speaker on insolvency, valuation, and forensic audit subjects. The event aims to enlighten members on the importance of valuation in capital markets and the numerous ways to determine the value of securities. The ICAI has recently launched certificate courses on derivatives, treasury management, and forex and currency management, specifically designed for chartered accountants in the Gulf countries. The organization believes that chartered accountants can play a significant role in capital markets and contribute to the country's economic growth.
