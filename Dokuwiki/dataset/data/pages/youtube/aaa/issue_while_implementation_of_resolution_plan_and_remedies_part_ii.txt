## Summary
Today's webinar is a continuation of the discussion on issues during the implementation of a resolution plan and remedies. The topics covered include the appointment of directors as per the resolution plan, allotment of shares, and transfer of physical assets. The importance of immediate management of the company by the successful resolution applicant (SRA) through the appointment of directors is emphasized. The issues related to ROC formalities, such as filing Inc 28, dir 12, and dir 2, are discussed. Different types of resolution plans and their implications on share capital are explained. The availability of audited balance sheets and timely completion of compliances are highlighted as critical points for a smooth handover to the SRA.
