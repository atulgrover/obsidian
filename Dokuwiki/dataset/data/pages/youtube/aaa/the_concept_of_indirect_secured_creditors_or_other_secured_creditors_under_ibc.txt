## Summary
Today's webinar discusses the filing of claims by creditors when a corporate debtor has pledged or mortgaged an asset to a claimant for a loan given to a third party. The definitions of 'creditor' and 'secured creditor' are explained, with a focus on the priority of distribution of assets in liquidation and resolution processes. The distinction between financial and operational creditors is discussed, along with the treatment of secured and unsecured creditors. Judicial pronouncements from the NCLAT and Supreme Court are cited to support the discussion. The Pioneer Urban judgment is highlighted for its emphasis on the time value of money and disbursement for a financial creditor.
