## Summary
In this episode, the speaker discusses the recent amendments made by the IBBI (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India) through the release of five regulations and two circulars on 31st January and 1st February 2024. The amendments are important for all professionals and stakeholders involved in the insolvency process. The speaker focuses on the amendments that impact Insolvency Professional Entities (IPEs) and Insolvency Professionals (IPs). The first amendment is in Clause 22 of the code of conduct for insolvency professionals, which restricts the number of assignments an IP can have as an RP (Resolution Professional) in the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) of a company. However, the limitation does not apply to an IP acting as an IP. The second amendment clarifies that show cause notices under the IBBI Inspection and Investigation Regulations will be issued to the partner or director of the IP who is responsible for handling a case, rather than the entire IP. The third amendment clarifies that the minimum fee of a RP, as specified in regulation 34B of the CIRP regulations, does not apply to an IP. The speaker also discusses other amendments related to IPs and IPEs, such as the engagement of partners or directors for work related to assignments, and the appointment of non-partner professionals in an IP.
