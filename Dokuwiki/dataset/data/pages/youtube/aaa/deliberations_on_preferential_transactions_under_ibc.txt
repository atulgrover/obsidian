## Summary
Today's webinar, led by Anil Goel and Ankit, focuses on the deliberations of preferential transactions under IBC, specifically under Section 43 and 44. They discuss the responsibilities of the Resolution Professional (RP) and Liquidator in filing applications for avoidance of transactions, and the lookback period for related and unrelated parties. The kind of preferential transactions include transfer of property or interest of the corporate debtor to any third party. The four thresholds to consider are: 1) transfer of property or interest, 2) transfer to a creditor, surety, or guarantor, 3) transfer against a liability, and 4) the person who receives the property is benefited as compared to their standing under Section 53. Common preferential transactions include payment to unsecured financial loans during the lookback period, payment of old operational creditors, payment to workmen and employees, and adjustment of receivables with payables. The webinar also touches upon the issue of delayed admission of cases by the NCLT.
