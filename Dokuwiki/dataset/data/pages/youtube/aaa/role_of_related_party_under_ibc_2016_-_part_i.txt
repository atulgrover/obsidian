## Summary
In today's webinar, we will discuss the role of related parties under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. The webinar will cover related party provisions under IBC, related party and Committee of Creditors (CoC), the concept of related parties, and relevant judicial pronouncements. A related party, as defined in Section 5(24) of IBC, includes directors, partners, or key managerial personnel of the corporate debtor or their relatives, holding companies, associate companies, subsidiary companies, and more. The definition of a relative is wide and includes siblings, spouse, and even siblings' children. Related party Financial creditors are disqualified from being represented, participating, or voting in the CoC to prevent them from controlling the CoC for their unfair benefit. The webinar will be divided into two parts, with part one focusing on legal provisions and part two on case studies and examples.
