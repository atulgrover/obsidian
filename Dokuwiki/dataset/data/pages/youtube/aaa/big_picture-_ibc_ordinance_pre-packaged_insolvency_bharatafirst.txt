## Summary
The Indian government has introduced a pre-packaged insolvency resolution process (PIRP) for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) through an ordinance to provide a faster and less costly resolution than the existing Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP). The PIRP allows promoters to continue running the company while a resolution professional monitors the operations. The financial creditors will review the resolution plan, ensuring no loss to operational creditors and a matching loss to promoters if there is any loss to financial creditors. The total time for resolution is 120 days, with a resolution plan to be approved by the Committee of Creditors within 90 days. The new process aims to help thousands of MSMEs struggling due to the COVID-19 pandemic and reduce litigation often triggered by defaulting promoters. The panelists, including Anil Goel, Ajay Shankar, and Charan Singh, discuss the benefits and potential challenges of the new PIRP, highlighting the need for vigilance to prevent misuse and ensure a smooth economic recovery.
