## Summary
In this episode of the AAA weekly webinar series, Anil Goel discusses the latest judgments from the Indian Supreme Court, National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT), and High Courts related to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Seven Supreme Court judgments are discussed, including the consolidation of CIRP processes between holding and subsidiary companies, the eligibility of successful resolution applicants to receive electricity connections while electricity dues are pending, the strengthening of limitation law, Section 18 of the Limitation Act, the power of NCLAT to recall its own judgments, and the authority of liquidators to cancel auctions. Three NCLAT judgments and three High Court judgments are also analyzed, focusing on topics such as the admission of claims by resolution professionals, the interdependence of businesses, and the role of the RP in filing appeals.
