## Summary
This webinar discusses the topic of willful defaulters, triggered by a recent judgment from the Bombay High Court. The speakers analyze the RBI's Master Circular on willful defaults, which aims to disseminate credit information about such defaulters and penalize them. The circular defines willful defaulters as those who default in meeting payment obligations while having the capacity to honor them, divert funds for purposes other than those for which they were borrowed, or siphon off funds so that they are not utilized for the business for which they were borrowed. Penalties for being declared a willful defaulter include being prohibited from obtaining loans or institutional finance, affecting the person's social status and business credibility, and being barred from participating in resolution processes or becoming an insolvency professional. The RBI advises banks to implement measures to identify willful defaulters quickly and transparently, and to exercise discretion minimally when declaring someone a willful defaulter.
