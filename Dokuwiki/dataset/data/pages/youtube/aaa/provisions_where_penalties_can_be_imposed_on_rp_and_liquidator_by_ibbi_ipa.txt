## Summary
This episode discusses the provisions and penalties that can be imposed by Insolvency Professional Agencies (IPAs) on insolvency professionals for non-compliance. The powers and responsibilities of insolvency professionals, such as operating bank accounts and selling assets of distressed companies, come with the potential for penalties if regulations are not followed. The IBBI and IPAs can investigate and inspect the affairs of insolvency professionals, and issue show-cause notices leading to disciplinary proceedings. The penalties can range from suspension or cancellation of registration, to admonition or imposition of monitoring penalties. The specific non-compliances and corresponding penalties are outlined, including failure to submit disclosures, accept assignments with conflicts of interest, maintain records, exercise due diligence in claim verification, comply with directions from adjudicating authorities, outsource duties, appoint registered valuers, supply information, make public announcements, provide notice regarding meetings of creditors, reject resolution plans from ineligible applicants, take action in respect of avoidance transactions, enter into contracts or agreements with professionals in an incomplete or improper manner, and contravene provisions of bylaws or regulations. The penalty amounts range from a minimum of 50,000 to a maximum of 2 lakhs, or 25% of the fee earned from the case, whichever is higher.
