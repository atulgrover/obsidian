## Summary
The video discusses the changes in the fee structure for Insolvency Resolution Professionals (IRPs) and Registered Valuers in the fourth amendment of the IBBI regulations. The minimum fee for IRPs has been reduced to INR 150,000 per month for smaller cases and INR 500,000 per month for larger cases. The fee is not intended to cover the expenses of the IRP or RP, and all expenditures must be approved by the Committee of Creditors (COC). In case there is no money available with the company, the COC will contribute for the fee and expenditure. The performance-linked incentive for IRPs has been changed from the difference between the realizable value and fair value to the difference between the realizable value and liquidation value, with a small acceleration added. The realizable value is defined as the amount payable to creditors in the resolution plan approved under section 31. The IRP is required to create a process email account for correspondence with stakeholders and hand over the credentials to the new IRP or liquidator in case of replacement. The IRP shall send a communication along with the public announcement made under section 6 to all the creditors as per the last available books of account of the CDs. The email credentials of the CRP email should be handed over to the new RP, and the minimum fee would be changed if the claim increases after the first COC meeting.
