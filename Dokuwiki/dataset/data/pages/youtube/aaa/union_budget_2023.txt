## Summary
In this week's webinar, Ankit and Mohit Gupta, a chartered accountant and expert in transfer pricing and international taxation, discuss the impact of the Union budget on the insolvency profession and other features of the budget. They cover two provisions related to restructuring, including Section 170A, which outlines the process for a business organization or the effect of an order of tribunal to be placed or accessed by the successor. They also discuss the decriminalization of Section 276A, which removes the provision of prosecution with rigorous imprisonment of two years for a liquidator who fails to give notice or set aside the amount as required. Other topics covered include the insertion of the joint commissioner appeal as an office to ease the burden of CIT appeals, and the introduction of a new system within Section 155 read with 151 4 for rectification and claiming TDS in the year of assessment. The speakers also discuss changes to the tax slabs and surcharges for individuals.
