## Summary
The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) released a discussion paper on proposed amendments to the corporate insolvency resolution process (CIRP) on November 1, 2023. The discussion paper aims to safeguard the interests of creditors, streamline the CIRP process, improve monitoring of the committee of creditors (CoC), and promote a time-efficient mechanism. Major amendments include seeking approval from the CoC for operational costs of keeping a company as a going concern, monthly CoC meetings, valuation methodology and report disclosure, and clarity in the process. The first amendment discussed is the approval of the CoC for insolvency resolution process costs, specifically the cost of keeping the company as a going concern. The proposal is for the insolvency professional to seek approval from the CoC for all expenses, including those incurred in running the business as a going concern. The second amendment is about monthly CoC meetings, with a maximum of 30 days between meetings. The third amendment focuses on the discussion of the valuation methodology and report with the CoC, requiring valuers to explain the valuation methodology to the CoC before computation of estimates. The proposal also includes the disclosure of fair value in the information memorandum.
