## Summary
This week's webinar focuses on recent judgments from the Supreme Court of India and high courts related to SARFAESI, a law used for enforcement of security by banks and financial institutions. The discussion covers the priority of statutory authorities' dues, such as taxes, over secured creditors' dues, and the impact of the Rainbow Papers judgment on the distribution pattern in resolution plans and liquidation. The webinar also highlights the importance of registering security interests with the Central Registry of Mortgages under SARFAESI for priority over other debts, revenues, taxes, and cesses payable to central, state, or local governments. The speakers emphasize the need to use the Ramanath case judgment to defend against Rainbow Paper and similar cases, and the importance of clarity in Provident Fund-related dues. The webinar also touches upon the priority of Workman dues under IBC and SARFAESI.
