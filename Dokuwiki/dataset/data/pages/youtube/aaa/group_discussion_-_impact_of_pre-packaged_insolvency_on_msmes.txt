## Summary
The regional director of Asacham welcomes attendees to a webinar on recent decisions related to the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016 in India, with a focus on Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs). The webinar features distinguished guests, including the Chief Justice, the Executive Director of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India, and a renowned resolution professional. The discussion covers the impact of IBC on MSMEs, the introduction of the Pre-Packaged Insolvency Resolution Process (PIRP), and the benefits it brings to MSMEs, including continuity of business, consensual decision-making, and reduced stigma. The speakers also address the question of why PIRP was initially introduced for MSMEs only and express the need for a wider presence of benches and more members in the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) and the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) to ensure efficiency and timely resolution of cases.
