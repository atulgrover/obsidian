## Summary
The webinar discusses recent case laws and judgments on avoidance transactions under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The focus is on sections 43, 45, 49, 50, and 66 of the IBC, which cover preferential transactions, undervalued transactions, extortionate credit transactions, and fraudulent transactions. Seven judgments from the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) and five judgments from the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) are discussed. The issues deliberated include the initiation of proceedings for fraudulent transactions in the absence of a transaction audit, the lookback period for fraudulent transactions, the scope for resolution professionals to take action for avoidance transactions, the timeline for filing an application for avoidance transactions, and the competency of the NCLT to pass orders during the moratorium under section 14 of the IBC. The webinar also discusses whether a dissenting director can file an application under Section 66 of the IBC, the preview period for preferential and undervalued transactions, and whether bad commercial decisions can be considered fraudulent transactions under the provisions of section 66.
