## Summary
In this webinar, the speaker discusses the final part of the recap on landmark judgments of the honorable Supreme Court in the year 2022. The first judgment discussed is Bank of Baroda vs MBL Infrastructure Limited, dated 18th of January 2022, which deals with the applicability of Section 29A on the promoters of a company. The second judgment discussed is India Resurgence Arc Private Limited vs Amit Metallics Limited, regarding the feasibility and viability of a resolution plan and the distribution proposed with reference to the order of priority amongst creditors. The third judgment discussed is K Parma Shivam vs Bank Limited, dated 6th of September 2022, which deals with the liability of a corporate guarantor when the main borrowers are not being proceeded against. The speaker also mentions the proposed amendments in the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and the webinar next week on Saturday at 11 am to deliberate on the comments or modifications that can be submitted to MCA.
