## Summary
The webinar discusses recent judgments related to the NCLAT and IBC, specifically focusing on the treatment of Provident Fund dues as secured debt. The first case discussed is Assam Tea Employees Provident Fund Organization vs. RP of Haileybury Limited, where the Provident Fund dues were claimed as a first charge on the assets of the company. The NCLAT, however, directed the total amount of 2 crores to be paid to the Provident Fund, stating that the dues are not assets of the corporate debtor. The second case discussed is Anjani Technoplast, where the NCLAT examined whether a decree comes under the purview of Section 5(8) of the Code, which defines financial debt. The NCLAT held that the decree does not become a financial asset, and the appellant, being a lender, did not become a financial creditor.
