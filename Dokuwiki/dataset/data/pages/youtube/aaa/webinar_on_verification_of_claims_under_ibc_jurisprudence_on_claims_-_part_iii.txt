## Summary
Today's webinar, part three of the claim verification process under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, focuses on claim filing and verification in the liquidation process. Regulation 12 of the liquidation process regulations deals with the verification of claims, and regulation 31 requires the liquidator to prepare a list of creditors category-wise based on proofs and claims submitted and accepted. The liquidator must provide specific information in the list of creditors, including the amount of claim admitted, the extent to which the debt or dues are secured or unsecured, and the details of the stakeholders. Section 138 of the IBC discusses the consolidation of claims, and section 38 states that the liquidator must collect claims within 30 days from the day of commencement of the liquidation process. The liquidator can also collect claims based on books of accounts and statutory liabilities like workman and home buyers' claims. The financial creditor may submit a claim to the liquidator by providing a record of such claim with an information utility. The creditor may withdraw or vary his claim under section 38 within 14 days of its submission. Section 39 talks about verification of claims, and the liquidator shall verify the claim as per the board's direction. The liquidator may require the creditor to file certain more documents and evidences. Section 40 deals with the admission or rejection of the claim, and the liquidator must reject the claim in writing with reasons for such rejection. Section 41 deals with the determination of the valuation of the claim, and the liquidator shall determine the value of such claims admitted under section 40. The claimant shall submit its claim or update its claim submitted during the corporate insolvency resolution process. The liquidator shall constitute a consultation committee within 60 days of the complete list of creditors' submission. The liquidator may admit the claims of a workman or an employee based on the books of account of the corporate debtor if such workman or employee has not made a claim. The cost incurred by the liquidator for the verification of determination of claims shall form part of the liquidation cost.
