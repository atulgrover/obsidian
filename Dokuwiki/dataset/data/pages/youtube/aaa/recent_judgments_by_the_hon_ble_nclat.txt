## Summary
Today's webinar discussed recent NCLAT judgments, including Haryana State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation Ltd vs RR Technoplast Pvt Ltd and Alok Bhushan, Chairman vs Chima Spintex. The first case involved a dispute over land ownership and operational debts in a liquidation case, where the NCLAT ruled that a successful bidder in an auction is not liable to pay period dues attached to the property when the company is in liquidation. The second case concerned the conduct of an Interim Resolution Professional (IRP) who incurred expenses during the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) before the formation of the Committee of Creditors (CoC). The NCLAT held that the IRP should have taken clear directions from the adjudicating authority instead of pushing forward the process and increasing expenses.
