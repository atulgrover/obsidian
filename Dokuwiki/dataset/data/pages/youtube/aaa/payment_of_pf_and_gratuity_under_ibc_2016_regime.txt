## Summary
Today's webinar focuses on the concept of Provident Fund (PF) and Gratuity, relevant sections, and judicial pronouncements. PF is a structural liability governed by the EPF employees Provident funds and miscellaneous Provisions act, with the employer's contribution calculated as a percentage of basic wages. Gratuity is a statutory liability payable to an employee on termination of employment after five years of continuous service. The webinar discusses categorizations such as workmen and employees contribution towards PF, dues in respect of employers contribution for PF, and pension and gratuity funds at the time of commencement of liquidation. The obligations on the resolution plan and under liquidation are explained, along with the interpretation of IBC section 53 and 36, which state that PF, pension, and gratuity funds are not part of the liquidation estate. The Precision Fasteners limited versus epfo September 2018 nclt Mumbai judgment is also discussed, which highlights the interwoven nature of workmen's PF dues with the right to life.
