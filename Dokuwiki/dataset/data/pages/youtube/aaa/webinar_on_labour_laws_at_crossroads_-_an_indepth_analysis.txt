## Summary
This webinar, led by Anil Goel with Ankit as the moderator, delves into the complexities of claims from workmen and employees under insolvency and liquidation, focusing on the Indian Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The speakers discuss the definitions of 'employee' and 'workman' as per the IBC and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and highlight the differences between them. They emphasize the importance of correctly identifying workmen and employees, as it impacts the priority and admittance of their claims. The discussion covers various kinds of dues, such as wages, salaries, compensation, retrenchment compensation, workman compensation, accrued holidays, and gratuity, among others. The webinar also touches upon the Waterfall Mechanism for the distribution of assets and the treatment of workmen's dues in relation to secured creditors. Anil Goel and Ankit address various judgments, litigations, and controversies surrounding labor laws, provident fund, gratuity fund, and pension fund. They encourage viewers to ask questions to ensure active listening and interest in the topic.
