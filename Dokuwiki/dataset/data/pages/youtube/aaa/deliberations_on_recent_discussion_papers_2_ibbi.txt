## Summary
In this webinar, the speakers discuss the discussion papers released by IBBI regarding real estate proposals in the CIRP and liquidation regulations. The primary objectives of these proposals are to ensure a transparent, accountable, and efficient process for real estate companies, expedite the resolution process, and avoid unnecessary delays. The discussion paper was released on 6th November, and the last date for giving comments is approaching. The webinar covers the amendments proposed in two separate regulations, focusing on project-wise CIRP, registration of projects under RERA, transfer of ownership and possession to allottees, and operating a separate bank account for each real estate project. The speakers also discuss the fees for RERA registration, conflicting judgments, and handing over the ownership of occupied units.
