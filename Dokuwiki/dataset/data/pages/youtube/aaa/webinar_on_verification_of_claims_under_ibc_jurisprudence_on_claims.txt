## Summary
Today's webinar discusses the verification of claims under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), focusing on the CIRP process and liquidation process. The webinar covers the importance of various judicial pronouncements, disciplinary orders from IBBI, and proposed changes in the claim verification process. The speakers are lawyers from the legal team, including Charlie Agarwal and Amar Vivek. The flow of the webinar includes definitions, sections, and regulations, claim forms, claim verification, section 53, challenges, and judicial pronouncements categorized by the stage of the CIRP. The speakers highlight that there is no straight line in claim verification, as blatant claims may be accepted by the adjudicating authority, and various factors impact the resolution plan, process, home buyers, and workers. The speakers emphasize the importance of the right to payment and the right to remedy for breach of contract. The webinar also discusses the submission of claims by financial creditors in electronic form and the verification of claims from the information utility registered with IBBI.
