## Summary
Today's discussion focuses on recent judgments and IBBI orders related to valuation issues under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The first judgment is from NCLT Kolkata, in the case of EMC Limited, where the court decided that no provision in the IBC allows for revaluation of assets during a resolution process if a plan has already been approved. The second judgment is from NCLAT, in the case of GB Global Limited, where the court held that the decision of the Committee of Creditors (CoC) to obtain a more recent and realistic valuation report is not contrary to the provisions of the IBC or its regulations. The third judgment is from Justice Ashok Bhushan, in the case of Amit Hero vs Naga Annamalai Singer, where the court observed that the Liquidator must abide by the valuation determined during the CIRP process. The fourth judgment is from NCLAT, in the case of Kotak Mahindra Bank vs Universal Buildwell Pvt. Ltd., where the court held that the liquidation value arrived by the valuer cannot be changed by the CoC without any valid reason. The discussion also includes IBBI orders related to valuation matters, which are to be presented by Ankit.
