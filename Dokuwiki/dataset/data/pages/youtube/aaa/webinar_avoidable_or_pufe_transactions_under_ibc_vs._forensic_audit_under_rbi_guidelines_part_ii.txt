## Summary
Today's webinar, led by the founder of Triple A Valuation Professionals LLP, focuses on transactional audits under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. The speaker discusses the practical difficulties and case laws related to avoidable transactions, specifically preferential transactions (Section 43), undervalued transactions (Section 45), extortionate transactions (Section 66), and fraudulent transactions (Section 49 and 50). The responsibilities of resolution professionals and liquidators in identifying and reporting these transactions are highlighted. The webinar also covers the process of appointing transactional auditors and the importance of their role in avoiding blame for any undetected transactions. The speaker emphasizes the need to investigate potential asset diversion or misappropriation even when there is no apparent recovery possibility.
