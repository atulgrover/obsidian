## Summary
The webinar discusses various SARFAESI judgments and their implications on charge recognition and insolvency. One important judgment is the Bal Krishna Rama Tarle vs Phoenix ARC Private Limited case delivered by the Supreme Court on 26th September 2022. The case involves a property that was mortgaged and then assigned to ARC, which became the symbolic owner. The DM declined to provide physical possession due to an existing tenancy. The Supreme Court ruled that the DM has no authority to adjudicate tenancy rights and should pass an order for taking possession and handing it over to the secured creditor. The DM's role is only to assist the secured creditor in obtaining the projection and document related to the secured assets. The tenant's legal rights can still be protected through other means, such as going to DRT for eviction applications. The DM should only check the documents and provide support services to the lender to take over the possession.
