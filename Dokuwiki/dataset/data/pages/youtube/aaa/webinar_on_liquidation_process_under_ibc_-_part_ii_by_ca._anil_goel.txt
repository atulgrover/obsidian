## Summary
The webinar discusses the implementation of the liquidation process as per the date of 25th March 2020, and the clause that no application can be filed. The process of admitting applications and verifying the date of default is explained. The threshold limit for the application depends on judicial pronouncements. The section 66 application is only possible if the CIRP is initiated. The individual bankruptcy law is useful for personal guarantors, and the business in the market can be handled by invoking personal bankruptcy. The panelists, CA Anil Agarwal and Keith Boyle, will share their experiences in evaluation and liquidation cases. The recap of part 1 of the liquidation process includes the passing of liquidation orders, recent amendments, and the appointment of liquidators. The COC has the power to appoint a liquidator and negotiate fees. The stakeholders consultation committee is gradually becoming powerful. The RPs must be prepared for the appointment of a liquidator during the liquidation process.
