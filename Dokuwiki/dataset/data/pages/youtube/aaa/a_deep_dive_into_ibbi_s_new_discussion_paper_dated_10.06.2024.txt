## Summary
In this discussion paper released by IBBI, they propose to reduce the compliance burden on Insolvency Professionals (IPs) by merging multiple forms into one and requiring them to be submitted on a single date, the 10th of every month. This change aims to streamline the process and make it more efficient for IPs, who have been facing pressure due to the large number of forms and varying timelines required by regulation 40 of the CIRP regulations. The existing legal framework includes forms like CP1, CP2, CP3, CP4, CP5, CP6, and CP7, but under the proposed changes, these would be merged into six forms: CP1, CP2, CP3A, CP3B, CP4, and CP5. The most significant change is the shift from date-specific timelines to a monthly compliance framework, with all information due on the 10th of every month. There is no proposal to change the penalty regulations for not filing forms by the prescribed date.
