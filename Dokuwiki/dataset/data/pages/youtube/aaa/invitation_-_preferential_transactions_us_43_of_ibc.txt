## Summary
Today's webinar discussed section 43 of the IBC, which deals with preferential transactions. The session clarified the conditions for invoking section 43, including the formation of an opinion by the Liquidator or Resolution Professional and transactions taking place within the relevant time. The webinar also covered the positive and negative tests for preferential transactions, such as transfer of property or interest, for the benefit of a creditor, and in the ordinary course of business or financial affairs. The discussion was based on the Judgment of Anuj Jain in the case of JP Infra Tech Limited and the recent NCLAT judgment dated 24th April 2023. The webinar emphasized that only the Liquidator or Resolution Professional can file an application under section 43, and the undervalued transactions can also be reported to NCLT by a creditor.
