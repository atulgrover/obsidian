Today's webinar is a special 100th episode, where the hosts reflect on their journey and thank their audience for their support. They have compiled a booklet of the 100 webinars, which will be released tomorrow and will be sent to anyone who requests it. The webinar focuses on questions and case studies from the audience. One question is about engaging a new auditor for a voluntary liquidation assignment, and the hosts clarify that there is no requirement for the existing auditor to resign. Another question is about the RP's responsibility to give notice of COC to related parties, and the hosts explain that only members of the committee of creditors and suspended management are required to receive notices. The hosts also discuss the process of valuation in situations where control and custody of the assets cannot be taken by the RP.
