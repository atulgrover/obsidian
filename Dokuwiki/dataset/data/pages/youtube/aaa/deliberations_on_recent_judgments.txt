## Summary
In a recent Supreme Court judgment, it has been held that MSME entities have the right to participate in the resolution process and submit a resolution plan, regardless of whether the MSME registration was obtained earlier or not. The MSME registration can be done by the Resolution Professional (RP) or the promoters on the RP's advice. The Supreme Court clarified that the intention of the law was to give an opportunity to MSMEs, and name excuses should not be used to keep them away from the resolution process. The judgment overturns the NCLAT judgment that held that the promoters could not present a plan if the MSME status was obtained after the commencement of the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP). The Supreme Court stated that the crucial date is the submission of the resolution plan, and if the company is an MSME at the time of submission, it complies with Section 29a. The promoters are exempted from Clause C and Clause H of Section 29a. The judgment allows suspended management to apply for MSME registration, and the RP can direct the suspended management to make the application if there are technical difficulties. The judgment is in line with the intent of the IBC to maximize the value of assets and avoid liquidation.
