## Summary
Ankit Goyal, an insolvency professional, and Nitin Janji, also an insolvency professional, discuss the linkages between the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and the Goods and Services Tax (GST) regime. They highlight the compliances required by insolvency professionals with respect to Central/State Government dues, GST returns, and the impact of moratorium on GST demands and related compliances. They also mention the Sales Tax Officer vs Rainbow Papers Ltd judgment and its implications on first charge and the status of government authorities as operational creditors.
