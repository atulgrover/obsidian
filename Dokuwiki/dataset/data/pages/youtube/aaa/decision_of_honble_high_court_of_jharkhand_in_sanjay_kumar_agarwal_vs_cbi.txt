## Summary
Today's webinar discusses the decision of the Honorable High Court of Jharkhand in the case of Sanjay Kumar Agarwal vs CBI, which held that an insolvency professional, appointed by the Committee of Creditors and confirmed by the NCLT, is a public servant under the Prevention of Corruption Act. The webinar explores the implications of this decision, including the benefits and obligations of being a public servant, and the protection provided to insolvency professionals under the IBC and the Prevention of Corruption Act. The discussion also covers the definition of 'good faith' and 'due care and attention' in this context.
