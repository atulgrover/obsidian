## Summary
The case involves an appeal by 272 employees and workmen of ABG Shipyard against the order of the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) and the Appellate Tribunal, which denied their claim for salary during the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) and prior periods. The employees argue that they are entitled to wages and salaries during the CIRP period as well as Provident fund, gratuity, and pension dues. The Liquidator opposes the appeal, stating that only the wages and salaries of employees who have assisted the Resolution Professional during the CIRP period should be considered as CIRP costs and approved by the Committee of Creditors (COC). The Supreme Court will determine whether the workmen's dues fall under section 53 1A or 53 1B and C of the IB Code and if the Provident fund, gratuity, and pension amounts are to be paid in priority over other disbursements.
