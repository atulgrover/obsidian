## Summary
In this episode, the speakers discuss the recent amendments to the Liquidation Process Regulations and the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process Regulations by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI). The first focus of the amendment is on the compromise or arrangement process, which should be finished within 90 days from the date of order of the liquidation. The amendment also clarifies that only the Liquidator can file a proposal for compromise or arrangement in cases where such a recommendation has been made by the committee under regulation 39 VA of CP regulation, and not after the expiry of 30 days from the liquidation commencement date. The amendment also includes provisions for early dissolution of the company, stakeholders consultation committee, and the sale of assets as a going concern. The speakers also discuss the detailed analysis of the Greater NOIDA Industrial Development Authority versus Prabhjit Singh Sony Judgment of the honorable Supreme Court given on 12th February 2024.
