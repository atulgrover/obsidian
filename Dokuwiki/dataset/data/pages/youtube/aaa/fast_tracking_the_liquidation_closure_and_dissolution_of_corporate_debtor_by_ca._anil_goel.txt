## Summary
Anil Goel, founder and chairman of Insolvency Professionals LLP, is a well-known insolvency professional who has been active since the inception of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. He has been on the advisory committee of ICAI and co-chairman of the National Council for Insolvency and Bankruptcy. Goel has also been a member of the working group established by the IBBI for the implementation of individual bankruptcy in India and a member of the SEBI group for seeking concessions for companies under the IBC. He is currently acting as a liquidator in 10 companies, including REI Agro and Rotomac Group of Companies, and has a team of 32 designated partners and 12 associate partners across the country. Goel is known for his humility and willingness to help other professionals with queries related to insolvency. In this transcript, Goel discusses the process of fast-tracking the liquidation process, focusing on the dissolution of corporate data. He explains the provisions in the IBC and regulations regarding the dissolution of corporate data, including the requirement to maintain certain reports and registers for eight years after the dissolution. Goel also discusses the possibility of early dissolution and the distribution of unsold assets to stakeholders. He emphasizes the importance of appropriate valuation of assets and having a specialized asset sale team for faster closure of liquidation processes.
