## Summary
The virtual interactive session, organized by ASSOCHAM, discusses the one-time restructuring of financial assets stressed due to the COVID-19 crisis. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) allowed a loan moratorium for three months, extended to August 2020, and recently provided banks with a resolution plan window for restructuring loans while maintaining them as standard assets. The expert committee, chaired by Sri K.V. Kamath, submitted recommendations on financial parameters and sector-specific benchmarks for the resolution framework. The eligibility for restructuring is limited to standard borrowers with arrears, and the framework must be invoked by December 31, 2020, with implementation within 180 days. Provisions for restructured loans depend on whether the lender has signed the Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA). The monitoring period focuses on milestones and recovery of 10% of the debt. The provisioning norms for corporates and MSMEs are reasonable at 10% and 5%, respectively. The session covers the impact on various sectors, the disclosures required by banks, and potential areas for improvement.
