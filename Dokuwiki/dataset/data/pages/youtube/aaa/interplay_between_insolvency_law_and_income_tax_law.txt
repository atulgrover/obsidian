## Summary
This webinar discusses the interplay between insolvency and bankruptcy law, specifically focusing on the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and income tax laws. The speakers highlight the impact of the Rainbow Paper Private Limited judgment from the Honorable Supreme Court in June 2022 and the subsequent orders passed by the NCLT. They emphasize the importance of understanding possible tax implications for resolution applicants and the need to avoid conflicts between IBC and income tax laws. The speakers also mention the recent amendments in budgets since 2018 that facilitate the implementation of insolvency law and address potential clashes with income tax laws. They touch upon the GST law and its impact on company defaults. The speakers then discuss the role of income tax authorities in initiating CIRP to recover their dues, the impact of the moratorium on income tax proceedings, and the responsibilities of the Resolution Professional (RP) in cooperating with income tax departments during assessments and liquidation processes.
