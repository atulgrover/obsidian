## Summary
In a recent judgment, the Supreme Court of India ruled on the rights of landowners in real estate matters, specifically in the case of Greater NOIDA Industrial Development Authority vs Prabhjit Singh Soni. The court analyzed the powers of the adjudicating authority under section 60 subsection 5 of the IBC code and decided that they have the power to recall an order of approval passed under section 31 subsection 1. The court also ruled that an application for recall is not barred by time, based on the facts of the case. The judgment has significant implications for the real estate industry and other industries as well, particularly in regards to the recall of orders and the recognition of landowners as secured creditors.
