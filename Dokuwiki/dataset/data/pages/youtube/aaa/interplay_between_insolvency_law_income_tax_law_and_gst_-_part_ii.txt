This webinar episode focuses on the responsibilities and obligations of resolution professionals or liquidators regarding income tax and GST dues prior to CIRP. The primary focus is on various judgments and judicial pronouncements related to income tax and GST dues as operational debts. The discussion includes the treatment of income tax and GST dues, the moratorium under Section 14 of the IBC, and the obligations regarding tax compliance during CIRP. The webinar also clarifies that the Rainbow Paper judgment is applicable only to income tax and state tax, not to Central Sales Tax, GST, or Customs during CIRP.
