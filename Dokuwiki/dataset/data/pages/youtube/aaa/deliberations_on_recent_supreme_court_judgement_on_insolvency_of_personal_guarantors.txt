## Summary
In a recent judgment, the Supreme Court upheld the constitutional validity of sections 95 to 100 of the IBC, which are specifically applicable to personal insolvency. The judgment opened up opportunities for insolvency professionals and creditors to recover debts from personal guarantors. The court also discussed the importance of section 94, which deals with the application by a creditor for insolvency resolution process, and the interim moratorium granted during this process. The appointment of a resolution professional without verifying the debt, default, and unpaid status was challenged, and the court discussed the role and duties of the resolution professional in providing a report under section 99 of the IBC. The court addressed the petitioners' contentions regarding jurisdiction, the opportunity to be heard, and the adjudicating authority's satisfaction of the creditor-debtor relationship before appointing a resolution professional. The automatic appointment of a resolution professional and the inquiries made by the professional were also discussed.
