## Summary
The founder of AAA, a professional organization in India, discusses the process of conducting CIRP and liquidation proceedings during the lockdown period. They explain how they have been able to continue these proceedings by dividing teams based on capability and working from home. The founder highlights the advantages of working from home such as flexibility, increased family time, and creating a personalized working environment. However, they also acknowledge the challenges such as the lack of a bidder in the market, motivation, and social interaction. The founder provides guidelines for working from home including strong passwords, secure networks, and updated operating systems. They also discuss the various teams involved in the CIRP and liquidation process, such as the law graduates team, claim verification team, front-end team, resolution plant facilitation team, legal team, liquidation team, asset sale team, compliance team, marketing team, and security and watch and ward team. The founder notes that physical movement, possession, and inspection are not possible during the lockdown, but email opening data has increased.
