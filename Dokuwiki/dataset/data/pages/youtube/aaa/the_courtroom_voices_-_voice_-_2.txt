## Summary
In a judgment delivered on April 26, 2024, the Delhi High Court ruled in favor of international aircraft leasing companies in the insolvency process of Go Airlines. The case involved the deregistration of aircraft leased to Go Airlines, and the court held that the moratorium under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) does not extend to the deregistration of aircraft. The judgment emphasized the supremacy of international treaties, such as the Cape Town Convention, and specific provisions of aviation law over general insolvency procedures. This ruling reinforces the rights of aircraft lessors during insolvency proceedings and ensures that international norms and agreements will be upheld, providing security and clarity for international financers. The Indian aviation industry is expected to benefit from this judgment, as it will likely result in more aircraft leases at comfortable pricing. The judgment also highlights that international treaties and sectoral specific laws are supreme and cannot be overridden by the IBC.
