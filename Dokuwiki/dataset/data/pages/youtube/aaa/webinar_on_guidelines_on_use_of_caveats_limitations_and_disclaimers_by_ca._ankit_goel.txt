## Summary
The webinar, led by Ankit Goel and Rakesh Mehta, discusses the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) guidelines on the use of caveats, limitations, and disclaimers in valuation reports. The guidelines come into effect from October 1, 2020, and are applicable to registered valuers registered under the Companies Act. The guidelines aim to provide guidance on the use of caveats, limitations, and disclaimers, which should not be used to limit a valuer's responsibility or make the valuation unsuitable for its intended purpose. The webinar covers the differences between caveats, limitations, and disclaimers, general requirements for a valuation report, commonly allowed and not permitted CLDs for various asset classes, and the role of resolution professionals. The speakers emphasize the importance of collecting information, using alternative information, and making estimates when necessary, rather than relying solely on the guidelines. They also discuss the impact of CLDs on the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) and bankers' reports. The guidelines aim to ensure that valuation reports are based on up-to-date laws and rules, including Rule 8 of the Companies Registered Valuers and Valuation Rules, 2017, and follow international valuation standards.
