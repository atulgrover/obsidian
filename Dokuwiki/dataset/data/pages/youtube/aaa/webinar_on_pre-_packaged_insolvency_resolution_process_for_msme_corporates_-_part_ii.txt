## Summary
The webinar discussed the pre-packaged insolvency resolution process (Pirp) and its benefits over other restructuring processes like one-time settlement and rehabilitation. Pirp will replace these processes as it provides a more structured and judicial approach for resolving financial stress for MSMEs and corporates. The first Pirp case was discussed, highlighting its features and benefits for entrepreneurs and bankers. The process involves the appointment of a resolution professional (RP) and the preparation of a base resolution plan, which can be improved based on feedback from financial creditors. The Pirp process is cost-effective, with most of the contribution coming from the corporate debtor. The webinar also discussed the fee structure for resolution professionals and the regulations for public announcements and list of claims. Pirp is considered a better option than CIRP and OTS as it benefits both corporate debtors and financial creditors. The webinar focused on the procedure, process, regulations, and rules of Pirp and encouraged questions related to specific scenarios and illustrations.
