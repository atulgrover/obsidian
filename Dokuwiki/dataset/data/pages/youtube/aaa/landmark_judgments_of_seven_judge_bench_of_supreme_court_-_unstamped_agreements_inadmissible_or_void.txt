## Summary
This summary is based on the 90th episode of Triaa Weekly Webinar Series discussing the seven-judge bench judgment of the Indian Supreme Court in the case of Basar Raju and Brothers vs Dharat NRA Ray and others. The judgment is significant in understanding the importance of a document's stamp duty in various legal proceedings, including insolvency and arbitration cases. The webinar also discusses the concept of separability of underlying commercial contracts and arbitration agreements, and the impact of under-stamping or non-stamping on the validity and enforceability of such agreements. The Supreme Court has held that an arbitration agreement in an unstamped or inadequately stamped contract would not be non-existent, unenforceable, or invalid if the underlying contract was not stamped. The court has also clarified that the non-payment of stamp duty is a curable defect and does not invalidate the underlying contract. The court has further held that any objections in relation to the stamping of the agreement fall within the jurisdiction of the arbitral tribunal. The webinar also features discussions with two advocates, Amar V and Loesh Malik, who have experience in arbitration cases and insolvency laws.
