## Summary
This webinar discusses the Forensic Accounting and Investigation Standards released by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, which are applicable to transactional audits under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. The standards, drafted by the Digital Accounting and Assurance Board, provide a minimum standard for undertaking forensic accounting and investigation assignments. The framework governing these standards consists of basic principles, key concepts, standards, and guidance. The 20 standards are classified into series based on their focus areas, such as engagement management, executing assignments, specialized areas, standard of reporting, and quality control. The speaker clarifies various definitions and concepts, including forensic accounting, investigation, litigation support, and fraud detection. The speaker emphasizes the importance of these standards as the country considers different kinds of evidence in various legal proceedings.
