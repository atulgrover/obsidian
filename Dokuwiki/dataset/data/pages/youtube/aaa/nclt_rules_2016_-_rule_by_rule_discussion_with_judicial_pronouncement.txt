## Summary
Today's discussion is on NCLT Rules 2016, which were formulated by the central government when cases from the Company Law Board and High Courts were transferred to NCLT. These rules are applicable to all proceedings in NCLT, including insolvency applications under IBC Sections 7, 9, 10, liquidation rules, and PPIRP Rules. The webinar will cover important definitions, the constitution of benches, powers of bench members, and the process of service of notices. The NCLT Rules 2016 are applicable to both the tribunal under the Companies Act and the adjudicating authority under IBC. The session will be divided into three parts and will provide insights into the rules, processes, and powers of NCLT members.
