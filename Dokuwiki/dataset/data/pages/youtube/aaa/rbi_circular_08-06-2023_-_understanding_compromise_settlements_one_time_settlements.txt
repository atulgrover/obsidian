## Summary
The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) issued a framework on June 8, 2023, for compromise settlements or one-time settlements (OTS) and technical write-offs. The framework applies to all regulated entities, including public sector banks, commercial banks, small finance banks, local area banks, regional rural banks, primary urban cooperative banks, state cooperative banks, central cooperative banks, all India financial institutions, non-banking financial companies, and housing finance companies. The framework aims to rationalize existing regulatory guidelines, consolidate various instructions, tighten related provisions, and ensure greater transparency. It also provides a clear definition of technical write-off and clarifies the regulatory framework for compromise settlements. The RBI guidelines enable banks to structure their OTS subject to certain checks and balances, and provide clarity on handling fraudulent cases and cases with bigger problems. They also emphasize the importance of staff accountability and the need to define the process for compromise settlements and technical write-offs. The guidelines recommend setting a reasonable threshold for eligible cases and defining the timeline for processing applications. They also provide guidelines for determining the market value or realizable value of the property and the methodology for arriving at a reasonable value. The objective of the guidelines is to maximize recovery for the banks, and they do not include any social objective or support for entrepreneurs. The guidelines also clarify the treatment of contingent assets and contractual provisions between lenders and borrowers.
