## Summary
The video is a webinar presented by Rushabh Shah, Partner at Mehta Insol LLP, and Jasveen Bindra, Branch Head at Aurangabad Insolvency Professional Agency. The webinar focuses on the recent amendments to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016. The panelists include Hemendra Paliwal, Nipun Singhvi (Advocate and Certified Forensic Auditor), and other professionals from the field of insolvency, restructuring, and finance. The amendments discussed include the requirement for suspended board of directors to provide information in a timely manner, the introduction of prescribed fees and performance-based incentives for Insolvency Professionals (IPs), and the responsibility of IPs to write letters to all creditors and obtain relevant documents. The webinar also covers the changes in the resolution plan, form G, and the timelines in the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP). The panelists express their views on the amendments and discuss the impact on IPs, creditors, and the insolvency process.
