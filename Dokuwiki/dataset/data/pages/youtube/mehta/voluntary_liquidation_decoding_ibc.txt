## Summary
Umesh Gudelli, presenter of the bi-monthly insolvency webinar, introduces the topic of voluntary liquidation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). He mentions that the company secretary and chartered accountant have experience with voluntary liquidation under the Companies Act and now under the IBC. The panelists for this session are Dipti Mehta, Designated Partner, and Vaibhavkumar Rai, Director, from Bobby Parikh Associates. Vaibhav will share his experience with the big group having presence in different countries and going through closure liquidation. Umesh will present a slide deck on decoding voluntary liquidation under the IBC, comparing it with the Companies Act, discussing who can initiate the process, who can be the liquidator, and the procedure of voluntary liquidation. The declaration of solvency, affidavit by majority directors, audited financial statements, records of business operations, and report by the registered valuers are required for the process. The company has to convey a board meeting to discuss and approve resolutions, and a special resolution has to be passed by the members within four weeks of the declaration of solvency.
