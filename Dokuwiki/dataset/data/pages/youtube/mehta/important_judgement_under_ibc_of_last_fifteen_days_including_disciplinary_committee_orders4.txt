## Summary
This transcript discusses recent important judgments under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016. The first case, SVA Family Welfare Trust and Others vs Energy Limited and Others, discusses the extinguishing of personal guarantees given to financial creditors in a resolution plan. The Appellate Tribunal ruled that a resolution plan can contain a clause for the extinguishment of personal guarantees if accepted by the financial creditors. The second case, Forever Limited vs Axis Bank Limited, discusses the eligibility of a corporate debtor for the benefit of Section 10A of the IBC when the date of default is prior to 25th March 2020 and an OTS proposal was withdrawn during the CIRP suspension period. The tribunal ruled that the corporate debtor was not entitled to the benefit of Section 10A. The third case, Peter Beck and Partners vs Sharon Biomedical Limited and Others, discusses the payment to ascending and descending financial creditors as proposed in the resolution plan. The tribunal ruled that ascending financial creditors are entitled to payment as proposed in the plan, and descending financial creditors are entitled to the minimum amount prescribed in Section 32B of the IBC. This judgment clarifies the provisions for financial creditors and their rights.
