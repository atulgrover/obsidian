## Summary
The webinar discussed important judgments and disciplinary orders under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The first case, an order passed by the Kolkata bench of the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), clarified that there is no need for specific directions from the adjudicating authority for aspects covered in the code. The second case, an NCLAT order, discussed whether the approval of a resolution plan extinguishes the entire debt of a corporate data and if a personal guarantor or third party can have no further recourse against the corporate data. The third case, an NCLAT order, reinforced the rights of financial creditors in settlement agreements and highlighted the importance of properly drafted and worded agreements. The fourth case, an NCLT order, discussed the jurisdiction of the NCLT in dealing with disputes arising solely from the insolvency of a corporate data.
