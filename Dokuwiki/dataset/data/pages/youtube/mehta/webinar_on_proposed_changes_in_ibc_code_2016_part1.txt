## Summary
The webinar, led by Jasveen Bindra and Amit Gupta, discusses the proposed changes in the IBC code. Amit Gupta, a fellow member of ICS Institute, IP, and a corporate law expert, highlights the key areas of amendments including the use of technology, admission of CIRP applications, streamlining the insolvency resolution process, recasting the liquidation process, and the role of service providers. Gupta shares his views on the proposed amendments and expresses his concerns about the increasing number of changes, which may affect the stability and basic essence of resolution. The discussion also covers the mandatory use of information utilities by financial and operational creditors during the admission process, the role of NCLT, and the impact of these changes on the insolvency ecosystem.
