## Summary
The webinar, presented by Jasveen Bindra and Ravindra Beleyurs, focuses on managing a corporate debtor as a going concern during the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) period. The speakers discuss the importance of keeping the company as a going concern to preserve the value of its assets and the statutory provisions under IBC that support this. They highlight the challenges of taking control and custody of the company, including freezing bank accounts and taking physical custody of assets and records. The speakers also discuss the importance of managing human resources and ensuring the continuity of operations. They share their experiences in handling going concern cases, including the challenges of raising interim finance and dealing with litigation from promoters. The speakers emphasize the importance of freezing debit transactions immediately in a going concern to prevent cash losses.
