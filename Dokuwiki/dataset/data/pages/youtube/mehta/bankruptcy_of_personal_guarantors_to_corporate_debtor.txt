## Summary
The webinar, presented by Jasveen Bindra and Sanjeev Pandey, discusses the bankruptcy process for personal guarantors to corporate debtors. The legal framework includes the MCA notification, IBC sections 70-78, bankruptcy rules and regulations, and the IBBI bankruptcy process for personal guarantors. A bankrupt is defined as a personal guarantor or individual adjudicated as bankrupt, and bankruptcy debt includes any debt owed by the person on the bankruptcy commencement date. The application process for bankruptcy involves creditors, personal guarantors, and the record of the insolvency resolution process. The effect of the bankruptcy order includes the appointment of a bankruptcy trustee, a moratorium on legal actions against the property of the personal guarantor, and disqualifications for the bankrupt. The bankrupt cannot travel overseas without the adjudicating Authority's permission. Upon passing of the bankruptcy order, the bankrupt must file a statement of financial position within seven days, and the bankruptcy trustee shall serve a personal notice to every creditor. The resolution plan in a CIRP, if it relieves the personal guarantor of liability, prevents descending creditors from proceeding against the guarantor under Section 95.
