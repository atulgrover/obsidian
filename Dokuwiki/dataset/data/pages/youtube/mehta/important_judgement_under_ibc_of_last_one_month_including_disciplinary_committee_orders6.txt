## Summary
The webinar, led by Mr. GP Madan, discusses recent judgments under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016. The first case, Vijaykumar v. Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, held that the performance guarantee, called under the transmission agreement, does not fall under the moratorium as defined in section 14 of the IBC. The second case, Doin Cables Private Limited v. Mr. Prain Bansal RP of Aun Infotech Private Limited, determined that the adjudicating authority had the authority to direct the resolution professional to file documents in a sealed envelope without electronic filing. The third case, B Techch Limited v. AA IT Service Private Limited, clarified that section 10A of the IBC prevents the initiation of the CP proceeding for the default during its period, but does not apply if the default started before the commencement of section 10A. The fourth case, Aggarwal Polyfab Private Limited v. KK Agro Foods and Storage Limited, concluded that it is not necessary for a financial creditor to have a written financial contract to prove a financial debt.
