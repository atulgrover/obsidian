## Summary
The webinar, organized by Hyderabad Insolvency Professional Association, discusses the recent amendments to the Liquidation regulations. The panelists include Sanjeev Pandey, a retired DGM of SBI; Himanshu Kapadia, a company secretary and partner in M&M; Nikhil Singer, an advocate and forensic auditor; and Nipun Singhvi, the founder of SKYZ Chartered Accountants. The amendments discussed include changes to compromise and arrangement, Liquidator's fees, filing of claims by stakeholders, and progress reports. The amendments aim to provide more clarity and transparency in the liquidation process, and the panelists discuss the potential impact of these changes on insolvency professionals and stakeholders.
