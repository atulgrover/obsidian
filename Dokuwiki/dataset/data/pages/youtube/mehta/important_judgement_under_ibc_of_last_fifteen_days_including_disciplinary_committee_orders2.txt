## Summary
The webinar, organized by MehtaInsolvencyandRestructuringLLP, discusses important judgments and orders related to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The first judgment discussed is between Sahib Singh versus Apex Council of Delhi and District Association, where the High Court of Delhi granted liberty to the petitioner to approach the NCLT for redressal of grievances. The second judgment is between Asset Reconstruction Company India Limited versus Universal Textiles Limited, where the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) held that the adjudicating authority ignored certain facts and remanded the case back to decide on merits. The third judgment is between Mrs. Tina Saraswik Pandey versus Regional Provident Fund Commissioner, where the Mumbai NCLT bench directed the removal of lien and release of amount held in custody during CIRP. The fourth judgment is between Jenny Gauche versus Niraj Agarwal, RP of Castle Extrusion and Private Limited, where the NCLT, New Delhi, upheld the acceptance of a resolution plan that entails restructuring. The fifth judgment is between Casey and Kumar versus Mr. Raghu Babu Gunther, RP of Spec Systems Limited, where the nclt Hyderabad held that the RP should not reject a director's request to submit a resolution plan if he is eligible. The sixth judgment is by GP Captain Atul Jain retired versus Tripadi Hospital Private Limited, where the NCLAT, New Delhi, held that the applicant cannot be considered as a financial creditor as he failed to produce any document to show that he has given a loan to the corporate debtor. The webinar also discusses orders passed by the Disciplinary Committee, including contraventions such as charging a fee for verification of claims, delay in filing report, delay in conducting the first COC meeting, non-compliance of the adjudicating authority's order, and inclusion of related parties of the corporate debtor into COC.
