    <h3>Summary</h3><p><i>The IBBI has proposed two discussion papers for stakeholder views, one on strengthening the regulatory framework of liquidation processes and the other on three specific issues in the corporate insolvency process. The webinar is focused on discussing these papers and collating views to send to the IBBI. The first discussion paper covers three main topics: code of conduct for Committee of Creditors (CoC), restrictions on requesting resolution plans and use of Swiss Challenge method, and treatment of live bank guarantees and line of credit in the CIRP. The need for these regulations arose from various cases where the CoC's discretion was misused, leading to unethical practices. The IBBI has asked for public comments on whether a code of conduct should be specified by the board and has provided a list of suggested guidelines for the CoC's functioning. The speakers discuss the need for enforcement mechanisms and the powers of the IBBI in regulating the CoC.</i></p>
