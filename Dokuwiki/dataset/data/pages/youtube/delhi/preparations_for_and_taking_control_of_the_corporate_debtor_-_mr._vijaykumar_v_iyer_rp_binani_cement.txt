## Summary
Welcome and introduction to the Delhi Study Circle of Insolvency Professionals' workshop on 'Preparations for and Taking Control of the Corporate Debtors under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016'. The session is being led by Vijaykumar Vijay, a merger and amalgamation specialist with over 20 years of experience. The discussion will focus on the preparations required before starting the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) and the various elements that a Resolution Professional must focus on. These include claim verification, corporate data compliance, regulatory compliances, finance and accounts, and managing various stakeholders. The study circle has been active since 2017 and has been regularly organizing workshops, seminars, and discussions. The session is being recorded and will be uploaded on the study circle's YouTube channel.
