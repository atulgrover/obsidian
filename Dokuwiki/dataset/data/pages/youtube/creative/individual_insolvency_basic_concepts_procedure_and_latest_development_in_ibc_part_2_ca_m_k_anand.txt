## Summary
In this session, Mr. Anand, a senior advocate and former acting chief justice of Gujarat, discusses the basic concepts, procedures, and latest developments in the Individual Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016. He talks about the importance of understanding the different sections of the IBC and how it can be used to manage personal liabilities. He also discusses the concept of a discharge order, which is crucial in the bankruptcy process. The session covers the difficulties faced while filing an application and the importance of public notices. Mr. Anand also explains the different types of moratorium and their implications. He emphasizes the need for planning and understanding the procedures involved in the bankruptcy process.
