## Summary
The video discusses recent developments and amendments related to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. The key points include the submission of a report by an expert committee on voluntary mediation, which recommends the establishment of a specialized National Company Tribunal mediation cell. The presentation also covers recent judgments, such as the Greater Noida Industrial Development Authority vs. P.S. Singh Sony and others, which treats the authority as a secured creditor, and the Income Tax Department vs. Kolkata Bench, which upholds that income tax dues during the liquidation period cannot be adjusted. The presentation also highlights the need for a code of conduct for the Committee of Creditors and the importance of Section 43 Sub B Subsection H of the Income Tax Act for payments to micro and small enterprises during the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP). The speakers also discuss the amendments to the CIRP and liquidation regulations, including the introduction of a separate bank account for the Resolution Professional (RP) or the Interim Resolution Professional (IRP) in regulation 4D.
