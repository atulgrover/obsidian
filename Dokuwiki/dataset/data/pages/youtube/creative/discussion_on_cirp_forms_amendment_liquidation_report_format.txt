## Summary
The discussion revolves around the insolvency and bankruptcy code (IBC) and its regulations. The speakers are discussing the possible amendments in the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) regulations and liquidation process report formats. They highlight the need for simplification of forms and processes, and the need for infrastructure development at NCLT to reduce delays in approvals and admissions. The Ministry is organizing a meeting with IBC stakeholders to discuss delay haircuts and improvements in the IBC. The speakers also discuss the need for a single form for submitting information, which can automatically open and autopopulate relevant fields. They emphasize the importance of active participation and collaboration from insolvency professionals for the success of the profession. The discussion also includes the pros and cons of the proposed amendments related to valuers, voting rights of authorized representatives, and release of guarantees. The speakers express their gratitude to the Institute of Cost Accountants of India for associating with the webinar. They also request IBBI to consider a concessional subscription for members of the Insolvency Professional Agency.
