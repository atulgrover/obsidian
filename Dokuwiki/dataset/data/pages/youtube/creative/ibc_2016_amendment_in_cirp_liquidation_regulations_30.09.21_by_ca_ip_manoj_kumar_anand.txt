## Summary
The session, organized by the Continuing Professional Education Committee of ICAI, focuses on two emerging areas of professional interest: the amendment to CIRP and liquidation regulations as of 30th September 2021 and search and seizure under the Income Tax Act and retraction of statement. The speakers are Manoj Kumar Anand, a Chartered Accountant, Insolvency Professional, and Associate Director, and Kumar. The session covers changes in CIRP and liquidation regulations, including the introduction of pre-pack insolvency and an electronic platform for hosting liquidation actions. The amendments to the regulations aim to provide guidance for the Committee of Creditors, restrict modifications to registration plans, and clarify the contents and timing of notices. The session also discusses the rationale behind these changes and their expected impact on the effectiveness of the IBC.
