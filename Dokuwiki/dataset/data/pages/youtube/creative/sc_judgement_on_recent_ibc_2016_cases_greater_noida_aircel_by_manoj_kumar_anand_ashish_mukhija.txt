## Summary
The video features a workshop on understanding the nuances of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) in India. The workshop is organized by various prestigious organizations and the chief guest is MM Kumar, a retired Justice who has been instrumental in the IBC. The speakers include Ashish Makija, Manoj Kumar Anand, Anil Goyel, and N Sharma. The workshop will delve into important Supreme Court judgments on IBC, including the Greater NOIDA and BTI Air Tale cases. MM Kumar shares his experiences as the first president of the NCLT and the challenges faced in operationalizing it. He also discusses the delegation of power in the IBC and its impact on the MSME sector. The video also highlights a recent judgment by Justice Jant Verma on the conflict between PMLA and IBC, where the IBC was upheld.
