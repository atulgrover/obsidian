## Summary
Manoj Kumar Anand, a professional chartered accountant and insolvency expert, discussed the topic of voluntary liquidation of corporates and the unhidden treasure it can reveal. He emphasized the importance of understanding the procedure and the benefits it can bring, such as the ability to withdraw capital with minimum cost and within a timeline of 90 days. Kumar also highlighted the advantages of webinars for both speakers and audience, as it allows for continuous learning and professional growth. He mentioned the existence of hidden treasures in every corporate, particularly in big groups, which can be extracted through voluntary liquidation. Kumar also explained the changes in the winding-up process brought by the IBC and the benefits of voluntary liquidation over the old regime, including the shorter timeline of 90 days and the ease of transferring property to shareholders without paying taxes. He concluded by mentioning the importance of the right to exit in a vibrant and tech-savvy economy like India.
