## Summary
The Kerala Insolvency Professionals Forum (KIPF), an association of insolvency professionals, held a webinar with speakers including the chairman of the IBC committee of ICAI, the managing director of IPA, the president of AIIPA, and the chairman of the IBC committee of ICAI. The speakers discussed the new discussion paper released by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) on reducing compliance for insolvency professionals. The speakers discussed the background of the KIPF, the need for reducing compliance, and the impact of the new discussion paper. They also discussed the importance of collaboration between insolvency professionals and the need for more research on the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The webinar was led by Pooja Bahry, a gold medalist in Commerce and a trailblazer among insolvency professionals.
