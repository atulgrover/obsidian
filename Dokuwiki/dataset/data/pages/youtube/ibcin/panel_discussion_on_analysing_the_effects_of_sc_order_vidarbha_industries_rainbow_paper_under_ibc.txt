## Summary
The panel discussion, titled 'Analysing the Judgment of Honorable Supreme Court and Rainbow Paper by Industries', features three panelists: a former Deputy Commissioner of Commercial Tax, a consultant at DSK Legal Law Firm, and a corporate tax professional with expertise in corporate, civil, criminal, property, and commercial laws. The discussion revolves around two recent judgments, one of which is the Rainbow Paper case, in which the Supreme Court made important observations regarding the parity of tax authorities and secured financial creditors under section 53 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The court held that tax dues should be treated at par with secured financial creditors in a resolution plan, and any unfair treatment could lead to the rejection of the plan. The panelists share their perspectives on the implications of these judgments, focusing on the priority of tax collection, the definition of secured creditors, and the responsibilities of resolution professionals.
