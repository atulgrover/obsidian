## Summary
The webinar, organized by the Association of Corporate Advisors and Executives, the Center for Financial and Regulatory Governance Studies of the West Bengal National University of Juridical Sciences, IBC Laws, the NCLT Kolkata Bar Association, and the International Women's Insolvency and Restructuring Confederation India Network, discussed India gearing up for cross-border insolvency. The speakers included Sumant Batra, Vaneeta Patnaik, and Mamta Binani. The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016 has been a landmark law in India, and the country has been learning and adapting to it. The insolvency law has been particularly relevant in dealing with real money and economic power. The case of Jet Airways opened up challenges in cross-border insolvencies, leading to the government of India forming a committee to find suggestions and feedback on the matter. The draft rules and regulations for cross-border insolvency have been released for public consultation. The speakers discussed the importance of cross-border insolvency and the impact it will have on the Indian economy.
