## Summary
This webinar introduces the insolvency regime and the role of an Insolvency Professional (IP). It covers eligibility requirements, the limited insolvency exam, the IP program, and career opportunities in this field. The speaker emphasizes the intellectual and rewarding nature of this profession, which requires deep understanding of legal, financial, and business issues, strategic thinking, and problem-solving techniques. The speaker also discusses the entry requirements, which include a 10-year experience for chartered accountants, company secretaries, cost accountants, or advocates, and a 15-year experience for other graduates. For young professionals under 28 years of age, there is a Graduate Insolvency Program (GIP) available. The steps to enter the insolvency field include passing the limited insolvency exam, enrolling with an Insolvency Professional Agency (IPA), completing a 50-hour pre-registration education course, and registering with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI). The speaker encourages those interested to reach out to IBBI for any doubts regarding eligibility.
