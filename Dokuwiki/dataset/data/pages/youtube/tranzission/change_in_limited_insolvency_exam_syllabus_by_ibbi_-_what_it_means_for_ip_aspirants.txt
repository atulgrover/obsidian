## Summary
IBBI has announced changes to the Limited Insolvency Examination syllabus, effective from 1st January 2021. The current syllabus will be applicable for exams taken up to 30th December 2020. The new syllabus focuses more on conceptual and analytical understanding, reducing the theoretical portion's weightage from 40% to 25%. Case studies' weightage increases from 16 to 40 marks, with four types of case studies. The examination under the new syllabus starts from 1st January 2021. Aspirants are encouraged to pass the exam before 31st December 2020, as the new syllabus includes additional acts and regulations, and the focus shifts towards case analysis. To pass the exam, aspirants should register with IBVI, prepare with at least 100 hours of study, and consider transition's super combo plan for comprehensive learning and practice exams.
