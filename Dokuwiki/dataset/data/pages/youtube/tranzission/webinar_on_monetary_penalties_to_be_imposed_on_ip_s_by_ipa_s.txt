## Summary
This webinar, hosted by Transition, discusses the imposition of penalties by IPAs on IPs. The speakers are Ashish Makija, Gopal Krishna Raju, and Snehal Kamdar. The webinar covers the impact of a recent IBBI circular on the IP profession and the quantum of penalties. The speakers will also discuss the legal tenability of the circular. Participants are encouraged to mute themselves and use the chat box for questions. The flow of the presentation will be: an introduction to the circular by Ashish Makija, reactions on the impact on the IP profession by Gopal Krishna Raju, a perspective on the quantum of penalties by Snehal Kamdar, and concluding remarks on the legal tenability of the circular by Ashish Makija.
