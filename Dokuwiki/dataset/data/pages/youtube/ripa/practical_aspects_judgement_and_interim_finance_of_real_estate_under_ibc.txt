## Summary
This webinar, organized by the RIPA, features three speakers who are experienced in real estate insolvency cases. Devendra Umrao, a practicing advocate and partner at Mehta Insolvency and Restructuring LLP, Jayesh Sanghrajka, co-founder of Incop Advisory Services Private Limited, and Himanshu Meta, past president of RIPA and founder member, discuss the challenges and key statistics of the real estate sector in insolvency cases. They highlight the unique aspects of real estate cases, such as the involvement of home buyers, landlords, and government authorities, and the need to comply with their regulations. The speakers also discuss the legal challenges, such as section 14 violations and encroachment by local leaders, and the importance of successful resolution plans. The webinar aims to provide guidance and share knowledge and experience with existing and non-member IPs.
