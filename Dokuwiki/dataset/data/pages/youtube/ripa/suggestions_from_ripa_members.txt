## Summary
The webinar was organized by RIPA to gather suggestions from members to present before IBBI. The discussion revolved around regulatory interventions required for taking control and custody of corporate data, and the underutilized Section 70 of the IBC. The speakers suggested that IPs should be educated about directly approaching IBBI under Section 70, and IBBI could provide clarification on the process and required documents. The need for tribunals to have the power to implement orders and impose penalties that impact non-cooperating parties was also highlighted. The speakers agreed that the issue of non-cooperation and procedural impediments is prevalent across regions, and suggested that IBBI provide guidance on the process after Section 19 for better clarity and awareness.
