<ul>
<li>384 tagged petitions were clubbed in this case whereby constitutional validity of sections 95 to 100 of the Code pertaining to inpidual insolvency were challenged: The SC decided the following issues:
<ul>
<li>Interim moratorium under section 96 is in respect of a debt. It restrains the initiation or the continuation of legal action or proceedings against the debt: Its purpose is protective.</li>
<li>RP does not possess an adjudicatory function in terms of section 99. He is a facilitator who gathers relevant information on the application submitted by debtor or creditor and to submit a report recommending the acceptance or rejection of the application.</li>
<li>The RP may require the debtor to prove whether the debt remains unpaid or has been paid. Thus, process before RP is not an ex-parte process. He is empowered to seek further information or explanation only in connection with the application from the debtor or the creditor which means he cannot conduct a roving inquiry.</li>
<li>The adjudicatory function of the AA commences after the submission of recommendatory report by the RP. Section 100 does not explicitly mention hearing for a debtor; the requirement of hearing has to be read in section 100.</li>
</ul>
