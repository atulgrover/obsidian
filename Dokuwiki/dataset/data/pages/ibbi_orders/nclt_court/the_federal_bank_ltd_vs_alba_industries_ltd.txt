		<h1>
			Even though Rule 127 of NCLT Rules 2016 states that every affidavit should be sworn or affirmed by a Notary or an Affidavit, non-&shy;compliance of the same would not entail dismissal of an Application filed under Section 7 of IBC, 2016 &ndash; The Federal Bank Limited Vs. Alba Industries Limited &ndash; NCLT Chennai Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			April 27, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority observed that the grounds as raised by the Applicant in the present IAs do not hold much, since the affidavit not having been notarized is only a procedural irregularity and it is a curable defect. This irregularity can be rectified by the Applicant at any point of time. It is also pertinent to note here that the proviso to Section 7(5) of IBC, 2016 states that this Adjudicating Authority before rejecting the application under sub - clause (b) of sub-p (5) of Section 7 of IBC, 2016 shall give a notice to the Applicant to rectify the defect in the application. Thus, even though Rule 127 of NCLT Rules 2016 states that every affidavit should be sworn or affirmed by a Notary or an Affidavit, non-&shy;compliance of the same would not entail dismissal of an Application filed under Section 7 of IBC, 2016. Since the Rules enumerated under NCLT Rules, 2016 cannot override the provisions of IBC, 2016. Taking into consideration above facts, the Financial Creditor has proved existence of 'debt' and 'default', with respect to outstanding due against the Corporate Debtor. Therefore, this Insolvency and Bankruptcy Application IBA/43/2020 stands admitted.
		</p>
