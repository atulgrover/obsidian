		<h1>
			NCLT rejects the application u/s 7 of the IBC in the absence of Financial Contract as defined in clause (d) of Rule 1 of AAA Rules, 2016 &ndash; Karmal Garment Exports Vs. M/s. Jai India Weaving Mills Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Chennai Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			September 12, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority referring to the clause (d) of Rule 1 of AAA Rules, 2016, held that while filing this petition to place on record before this Authority, the 'Financial Contract' and demonstrate without any ambiguity from the financial contract, the amount disbursed as per the loan/debt, the tenure of the loan/debt, the interest payable and the conditions of repayment. In the present case, it is evident that the Financial Creditor has not placed on record any Financial Contract or any Financial Agreement, in pursuance of which the loan was disbursed to the Corporate Debtor. In the present case, there is no financial contract which is seen on record in order to establish that the relationship between the Financial Creditor and the Corporate Debtor herein in order for the Applicant to qualify as a 'Financial Creditor' and in the absence of the same, the default as alleged by the Financial Creditor cannot be determined. Thus, in the absence of any 'Financial Contract' between the parties, we are constrained to dismiss this petition, however without any costs.(p7-10)
		</p>

							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The instant petition has been filed under Section 7 of the IBC, 2016 r/w Rule 4 of the AAA Rules, 2016.The term&nbsp;
							<em>
								&lsquo;Financial contract&rsquo;&nbsp;
							</em>
							is defined in clause (d) sub&shy;p 1 of Insolvency and Bankruptcy (Application to Adjudicating Authority) Rules, 2016;
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								&ldquo;(d)&nbsp;
								<strong>
									&ldquo;financial contract&rdquo;&nbsp;
								</strong>
								means a contract between a corporate debtor and a financial creditor&nbsp;
								<u>
									setting out the terms of the financial debt. including the tenure of the debt, interest payable and date of repayment:&rdquo;
								</u>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority referring to the aforementioned Rule, held that it becomes clear that it is incumbent upon the Financial Creditor while filing this petition to place on record before this Authority, the&nbsp;
							<em>
								&lsquo;Financial Contract&rsquo;&nbsp;
							</em>
							and demonstrate without any ambiguity from the financial contract, the amount disbursed as per the loan/debt, the tenure of the loan/debt, the interest payable and the conditions of repayment. In the present case, it is evident that the Financial Creditor has not placed on record any&nbsp;
							<em>
								Financial Contract&nbsp;
							</em>
							or any&nbsp;
							<em>
								Financial Agreement,&nbsp;
							</em>
							in pursuance of which the loan was disbursed to the Corporate Debtor. The Part V of the Petition also discloses the fact that the Financial Creditor has only attached the copy of the ledger of the Financial Creditor maintained in the books of accounts of the Corporate Debtor.&nbsp;Even the Financial Creditor has not placed on record any Promissory Note, in the present case, which can be construed as a &lsquo;Financial Contract&rsquo; in order to prove the debt qualifies to be a &lsquo;financial debt&rsquo;. In the present case, there is no financial contract which is seen on record in order to establish that the relationship between the Financial Creditor and the Corporate Debtor herein in order for the Applicant to qualify as a &lsquo;Financial Creditor&rsquo; and in the absence of the same, the default as alleged by the Financial Creditor cannot be determined.&nbsp;Thus, in view of the facts mentioned supra, and in the absence of any &lsquo;Financial Contract&rsquo; between the parties, we are constrained to&nbsp;
							<strong>
								dismiss&nbsp;
							</strong>
							this petition, however without any costs.
							<strong>
								(p7-10)
							</strong>
						</p>
