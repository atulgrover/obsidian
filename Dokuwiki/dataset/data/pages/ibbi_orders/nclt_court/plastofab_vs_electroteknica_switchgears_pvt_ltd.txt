		<h1>
			In case of Operational Debts, the interest component cannot be clubbed with the Principal Debt to arrive at the minimum pecuniary threshold of Rs. 1 Crore &ndash; M/s Plastofab Vs. Electroteknica Switchgears Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			May 26, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority referring to the Jumbo Paper Products vs. Hansraj Agrofresh Pvt. Ltd. (2021)  497 NCLAT, held that in case of Operational Debts, the interest component cannot be clubbed with the Principal Debt to arrive at the minimum pecuniary threshold of Rs. 1 Crore. Further, that the threshold limit of Rs. 1 Crore will be applicable for applications filed u/s 7 or 9 on or after 24.3.3020 even if the date of default precedes 24.3.2020.
		</p>
	</div>

							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Facts of the case
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							This is a Company Petition filed under p 9 of the IBC by Operational Creditor seeking to initiate CIRP against Electroteknica Switchgears Private Limited (Corporate Debtor).&nbsp;The instant petition has been filed after 24.03.2020 and in the instant petition, the principal amount in default is Rs. 45,33,363/-. According to the Corporate Debtor, the Operational Creditor has used the interest component being Rs. 68,23,688/- to make the total debt due cross the threshold of Rs. 1 Crore. Hence the instant petition is not maintainable. The Corporate Debtor has relied on the decision taken by NCLT, New Delhi in the matter of&nbsp;
							<em>
								CBRE South Asia Pvt. Ltd. Vs. M/s. United Concepts and Solutions Pvt. Ltd.&nbsp;(2022)  02 NCLT
							</em>
							&nbsp;to establish its case.&nbsp;
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that&nbsp;it is clear that the pecuniary threshold of Rs. 1 Crore has been achieved by the Operational Creditor by clubbing together the principal sum of Rs. 45,33,363/- and the interest of Rs. 68,23,688/-. Further, the Ld. Counsel for the Operational Creditor has taken the plea that since the date of default precedes 24.03.2020, the limit of Rs. 1 Crore shall not be applicable to the instant petition.&nbsp;
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority referring to the&nbsp;
							<em>
								Jumbo Paper Products vs. Hansraj Agrofresh Pvt. Ltd.&nbsp;(2021)  497 NCLAT
							</em>
							, held that&nbsp;
							<strong>
								in case of Operational Debts, the interest component cannot be clubbed with the Principal Debt to arrive at the minimum pecuniary threshold of Rs. 1 Crore
							</strong>
							. Further, that&nbsp;
							<strong>
								the threshold limit of Rs. 1 Crore will be applicable for applications filed u/s 7 or 9 on or after 24.3.3020 even if the date of default precedes 24.3.2020
							</strong>
							. Therefore, without going into further merits of the instant petition, since in the instant petition, the principal amount of debt is lesser than Rs. 1 Crore, the same is not maintainable under p 4 of the Code and is accordingly dismissed.&nbsp;
							<strong>
								(p10-13)
							</strong>
						</p>
