	<h1>
		Whether invoice/Bills raised in the name of Corporate Debtor to be changed in the name successful Resolution Applicant? &ndash; Bijoy Prabhakaran Pulipra, Monitoring Agent, PVS Memorial Hospital Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Special Officer (Revenue), Kerala State Electricity Board Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Kochi Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		December 19, 2022
	</div>
	<p>
		Even after the approval of Resolution plan the KSEB used to raise invoices/Bills in the name of corporate debtor and prayed to give direction to the 1st respondent/KSEB to raise the Bills in the name of successful resolution applicant M/s Lissie Medical Institutions. The applicant or the Successful Resolution applicant has not filed name transfer application to the KSEB authorities to effect name transfer as provided in the Kerala State Electricity Supply Code 2014. The successful resolution applicant has to submit application for name transfer in a prescribed form with the requisite fee as set out in Kerala State Electricity Supply Code 2014. The corporate debtor ceased to exist as legal entity after the approval resolution plan, so the existing entity SRA shall enter into a fresh service contract with the service provider. In the situation the prayer for direction to the respondent to effect name transfer in the name of SRA instantly is unsustainable. Thus, this point is answered.
	</p>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						On the applicant side stated that even after the approval of Resolution plan the KSEB used to raise invoices/Bills in the name of corporate debtor and prayed to give direction to the 1st respondent/KSEB to raise the Bills in the name of successful resolution applicant M/s Lissie Medical Institutions. The applicant or the Successful Resolution applicant has not filed name transfer application to the KSEB authorities to effect name transfer as provided in the Kerala State Electricity Supply Code 2014. As submitted on the 1st respondent side the bills will be raised only in the name of consumer as stands in the registers of KSEB. The successful Resolution applicant is not a party to this application. The successful resolution applicant has to submit application for name transfer in a prescribed form with the requisite fee as set out in Kerala State Electricity Supply Code 2014. The corporate debtor ceased to exist as legal entity after the approval resolution plan, so the existing entity SRA shall enter into a fresh service contract with the service provider. In the situation the prayer for direction to the respondent to effect name transfer in the name of SRA instantly is unsustainable. Thus, this point is answered.
					</p>
					<p>
						As per the Kerala State Electricity Supply Code 2014, whenever the ownership of premises is transferred, the transferee has to apply to Board to effect name transfer, name transfer is not automatic. The Successful Resolution applicant is directed to submit a name transfer application to the KSEB. The KSEB/1st respondent, shall consider any of such name transfer application is submitted by the successful resolution applicant in accordance with law without insisting to make any payment/arrears in whatever nature payable by the corporate debtor prior to 23.07.2021.
