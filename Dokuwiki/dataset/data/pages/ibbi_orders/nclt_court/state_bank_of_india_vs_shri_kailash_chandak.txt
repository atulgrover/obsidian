	<h1>
		When no cooperation by Personal Guarantor, there is no requirement to summon the meeting of Creditors and Creditors shall be entitled to file an application for Bankruptcy under Chapter IV of the IBC &ndash; State Bank of India Vs. Shri Kailash Chandak &ndash; NCLT Jaipur Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		August 14, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		The Adjudicating Authority held that in the present case, no repayment plan has been filed as the Personal Guarantor/ Debtor did not cooperate with the RP even he did not respond to the calls made by the RP. In its report u/s 106, RP has submitted that due to non-cooperation of the Personal Guarantor/ Debtor. No repayment plan could be filed. Hence meeting of the creditors also could not be conducted. Since in this matter Personal Guarantor/ Debtor has not come forward to co-operate with the RP, hence, the only option left for the RP is to file an application for bankruptcy under Chapter IV.&nbsp;This application is allowed and Creditor shall be entitled to file an application under Chapter IV of the Code.
	</p>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 III. Full text of the judgment
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						1. This is the report under Section 106 of the Code pursuant to the order dated 31.05.2022 passed under p 100 of the Code by this Adjudicating Authority in the matter of Kailash Chandak.
					</p>
					<p>
						2. Ms. Reshma Mittal has been appointed as Resolution Professional in the matter of insolvency resolution process of Sh. Kailash Chandak (hereinafter referred to as Personal Guarantor/ Debtor) a Personal Guarantor for debt owed by Maximum Synthetics Private Limited (Corporate Debtor) to State Bank of India (Creditor) vide Order dated 23.12.2021 in CP No. (IB)- 70/95/JPR/2021 passed by this Adjudicating Authority.
					</p>
					<p>
						3. RP was directed by this Adjudicating Authority vide above mentioned order dated 23.12.2021 to exercise all the powers as enumerated under Section 99 of the IBC read with Rules made thereunder. Applicant was further directed to make the recommendations with reasons in writing for acceptance or rejection of the application under the provisions of Section 99 of the IBC, 2016.
					</p>
					<p>
						4. Thereafter RP had filed a report under Section 99 of the IBC on 24.02.2022 vide application no. IA (IBC) No.106/JPR/2022 duly complying with relevant ps of the IBC.
					</p>
					<p>
						5. IA (IBC) No.106/JPR/2022 was admitted by this Adjudicating Authority vide order dated 31.05.2022 under Section 100 of the IBC and Insolvency Resolution Process was initiated against the Personal Guarantor/debtor and moratorium was declared.
					</p>
					<p>
						6. RP was directed as per above mentioned Order to cause a public notice to be published on behalf of the Adjudicating Authority within 7 days of uploading of this order on the website of NCLT, inviting claims from all Creditors within 21 days of such issuance. The RP made a public announcement on 04.06.2022 in following newspapers inviting claims from all the creditors: a) Indian Express Jaipur-English Edition) dated 05.06.2022. b) Virat Vaibhav (Jaipur-Hindi Edition) dated 05.06.2022 and copy of the same has been annexed as Annexure- 3.
					</p>
					<p>
						7. In terms of Section 102 (1) of IBC, the last date of the submission of proof of claims by the creditors was 25.06.2022. The Resolution Professional received claim from one Financial Creditor only i.e., State Bank of India. Thereafter, in terms of Regulation 7(5) of Insolvency &amp; Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution&rsquo; Process for Personal Guarantors to Corporate Debtors) Regulations, 2019. The Resolution Professional had verified the claim and thereupon prepared a list of creditors in the prescribed manner considering all the claims received by her up to 25.06.2022.
					</p>
					<p>
						8. List of creditors drawn on 04.07.2022 considering all claims received up to 25.06.2022 as prescribed in terms of 104 of the IBC read with Regulation 9(1) of PG Regulations is submitted by the Resolution Professional to NCLT on 10.09.2022.
					</p>
					<p>
						9. Reference is made to the Order and provisions of p 106(1) of the Code, wherein the Resolution Professional is required to submit the repayment plan under p 105 along with her report on such plan to NCLT within a period of twenty-one days from the last date of submission of claims under p 102.
					</p>
					<p>
						10. It is stated in the application that before coming to the stage of Section 106, the RP has duly complied Section 102, 103 &amp; 104 of the IBC, 2016. RP has made several communications with the Personal Guarantor/ Debtor who did not respond. RP noticed that Personal Guarantor/ Debtor was appearing online repeatedly. Therefore, RP requested him to respond. But RP did not receive any reply from the Personal Guarantor/ Debtor. Hence, in view of the above it appears that the Personal Guarantor/ Debtor is not willing to submit any repayment plan. Since the Rp has not received any repayment plan from the Personal Guarantor/ Debtor, there was no requirement to summon the meeting of creditors.
					</p>
					<p>
						11. RP has prayed that this Adjudicating Authority may kindly pass appropriate order under Section 114 of the Code that the Creditors shall be entitled to file an application for bankruptcy under Chapter IV of the Code.
					</p>
					<p>
						12. Due to non-co-operation of Personal Guarantor/ Debtor repayment plan could not be submitted before the Adjudicating Authority. Hence meeting of the Creditors could not be conducted.
					</p>
					<p>
						13. The Adjudicating Authority shall pass an order approving or rejecting the repayment plan on the basis of the report of the meeting of the creditors submitted by the Resolution Professional under Section 112.
					</p>
					<p>
						14. Where a meeting of creditor is not summoned, the Adjudicating Authority shall pass an appropriate order on the basis of the record submitted by the Resolution Professional under Section 106.
					</p>
					<p>
						15. In the present case, no repayment plan has been filed as the Personal Guarantor/ Debtor did not cooperate with the RP even he did not respond to the calls made by the RP. In its report u/s 106, RP has submitted that due to non-cooperation of the Personal Guarantor/ Debtor. No repayment plan could be filed. Hence meeting of the creditors also could not be conducted. Since in this matter Personal Guarantor/ Debtor has not come forward to co-operate with the RP, hence, the only option left for the RP is to file an application for bankruptcy under Chapter IV.
					</p>
					<p>
						16. This application is allowed and Creditor shall be entitled to file an application under Chapter IV of the Code.
					</p>
