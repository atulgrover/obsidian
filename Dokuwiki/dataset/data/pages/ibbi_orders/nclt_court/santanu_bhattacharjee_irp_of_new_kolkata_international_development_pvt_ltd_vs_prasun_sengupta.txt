		<h1>
			Adjudicating Authority cannot direct the parties either to execute the agreement of settlement or to deposit the consideration as per the settlement agreement which has not been produced before us &ndash; Santanu Bhattacharjee (IRP of New Kolkata International Development Pvt. Ltd.) Vs. Prasun Sengupta &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			July 25, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The Applicant&rsquo;s case is that a settlement has been arrived between the Operational Creditor and the Corporate Debtor on 24.03.2020. However, more than a year has passed since the Corporate Debtor was admitted into the CIRP, there is neither a settlement agreement nor an application under p 12A for withdrawal of the underlying Company Petition before this Adjudicating Authority. The Adjudicating Authority observed that this appears to be a classic case of wastage of time. The lockdown had been lifted long before the application under consideration had been filed. If there had been a settlement at all, an application under p 12A should have been filed. Unless an application for withdrawal were filed, it was IRP&rsquo;s duty to conduct the CIRP in the time bound manner. However, nothing seems to have moved in the present case expect for publication or Form A after the admission of the Corporate Debtor into CIRP. All the parties involved have taken the CIRP proceedings for a ride. Even if a settlement has been arrived at, the prayers made by the Applicant herein do not fall within the ambit of powers of this Adjudicating Authority. It is our understanding that under p 12A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, this Adjudicating Authority may allow an application for withdrawal of the underlying Company Petition, however, the Adjudicating Authority cannot direct the parties either to execute the agreement of settlement or to deposit the consideration as per the settlement agreement which has not been produced before us.
		</p>
	</div>
</p>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Applicant&rsquo;s case is that a settlement has been arrived between the&nbsp;Operational Creditor and the Corporate Debtor on 24.03.2020 whereby an&nbsp;amount of ₹4,10,00,000/- has been paid&nbsp;against the total amount of debt being ₹4,04,23,264/-.&nbsp;However, it appears that it has&nbsp;only been agreed between the parties that the Corporate Debtor would pay&nbsp;an amount of Rs.4,10,00,000/- as full and&nbsp;final settlement and the same had not been paid at least till the said date.&nbsp;Due to the lockdown imposed, they could not&nbsp;execute a formal settlement agreement and the Operational Creditor could&nbsp;not file the application for withdrawal of the underlying Company Petition.&nbsp;They would execute the MoU and the Operational Creditor would&nbsp;withdraw the underlying Company Petition soon. Therefore, the IRP&nbsp;should not proceed with the CIRP.&nbsp;As per Regulation 30A of the CIRP&nbsp;Regulations, 2016, an application for withdrawal may be made before&nbsp;constitution of CoC under p 12A of the&nbsp;Code through the IRP to the Adjudicating Authority.&nbsp;However, more than a year has passed since the Corporate Debtor was&nbsp;admitted into the CIRP. Although, it is the contention of the Applicant that&nbsp;a settlement has been arrived at, there is neither a settlement agreement nor&nbsp;an application under p 12A for withdrawal of the underlying&nbsp;Company Petition before this Adjudicating Authority.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that this appears to be a classic case of wastage of time. The lockdown had been&nbsp;lifted long before the application under consideration had been filed. If there&nbsp;had been a settlement at all, an application under p 12A should have&nbsp;been filed.&nbsp;Unless an application for withdrawal were filed, it was IRP&rsquo;s duty to&nbsp;conduct the CIRP in the time bound manner. However, nothing seems to&nbsp;have moved in the present case expect for publication or Form A after the&nbsp;admission of the Corporate Debtor into CIRP. All the parties involved have&nbsp;taken the CIRP proceedings for a ride.&nbsp;Even if a settlement has been arrived at, the prayers made by the Applicant&nbsp;herein do not fall within the ambit of powers of this Adjudicating Authority.
						</p>
						<p>
							Further, it is held that It is our understanding that under p 12A of the Insolvency and&nbsp;Bankruptcy Code, 2016, this Adjudicating Authority may allow an&nbsp;application for withdrawal of the underlying Company Petition, however,&nbsp;the Adjudicating Authority cannot direct the parties either to execute the&nbsp;agreement of settlement or to deposit the consideration as per the settlement&nbsp;agreement which has not been produced before us.&nbsp;In the light of aforesaid discussions the present application is misconceived&nbsp;and is an abuse of process. Resultantly, IA (IB) No.1462/KB/2020 shall&nbsp;stand dismissed as being devoid of merit.
							<strong>
								(p11-18)
							</strong>
