						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							It is found that on 03.02.2020 notice was issued to the Respondent/Corporate Debtor by the registry showing the date of hearing as 26.02.2020, which was delivered on 10.02.2020 as per the delivery report of the postal department. However, non appeared from the side of the respondent on the date of hearing i.e. 26.02.2020. Consequent upon which the instant IA was heard in the absence of the respondent.
						</p>
						<p>
							It is further submitted by the Ld. Lawyer that there is no willful and intentional latches on the part of the conducting Advocate and if the same is not restored the petitioner will have to suffer for the fault of the Advocate. It is further submitted that that the delay in filing the application is caused due to oversight and it was not the intentional. Hence, it may be restored by condoning the delay in the interest of justice.
						</p>
						<p>
							NCLT held that it is found that there was no fault of the petitioner, rather it was dismissed due to oversight of the Advocate. Under such situation and also as observed by the Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court in number of cases that the party should not suffer due to fault of the Advocate. Thus, for the Interest of justice the CP(IB) 297 of 2018 is restored to file and the petitioner is directed to issue fresh notice to the respondent.&nbsp;Accordingly, the instant application is allowed and disposed off.
						</p>
