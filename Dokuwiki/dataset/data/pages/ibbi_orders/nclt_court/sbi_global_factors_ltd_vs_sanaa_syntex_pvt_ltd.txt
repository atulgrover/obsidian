		<h1>
			Whether the secured creditor exercising his right under p 52(1)(b) of the Code has to make payment of workmen&rsquo;s dues out of the amount realised from the sale of such secured assets as the EPF/workmen&rsquo;s dues, which do not form part of the liquidation estate? &ndash; SBI Global Factors Ltd Vs. Sanaa Syntex Private Limited &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			April 8, 2019
		</div>
		<p>
			Following questions are considered in this case: (1) Whether a secured Financial Creditor is legally entitled to stay out of liquidation? Adjudicating Authority held that it is an undisputed assertion that the secured creditor's rights have to be protected and respected. They must have the choice of taking their collateral and selling it on their own. Hence, the first question with respect to the secured creditor opting out of the liquidation estate, stands answered in affirmative. (2) Whether p 29A is applicable to liquidation proceedings in a situation when the secured creditor realises the security interest on its own? Adjudicating Authority held that the defaulters disqualified get any benefit under this code. This is a clear message conveyed through Section 29A. A defaulter must not be benefitted by entering into those very assets through side doors, otherwise not permitted to enter from the front doors, for e.g. by submission of resolution plan. Therefore, it is logical as well as legally justifiable to extend the scope of Section 29A while dealing with the liquidation of the assets a debtor company. This verdict of the Hon'ble Supreme Court has strictly dealt with the defaulters and therefore held that the provisions of Section 29A continues to apply not merely to Resolution Applicants but to Liquidation also. Section 52, therefore, is not out of Chapter III i.e. Liquidation Process, but within this chapter, hence ought to apply to the secured Financial Creditors if they exercise their option to liquidate an asset independently. (3) Whether the secured creditor exercising his right under p 52(1)(b) of the Code has to make payment of workmen's dues out of the amount realised from the sale of such secured assets as the EPF/workmen's dues, which do not form part of the liquidation estate? Adjudicating Authority held that the EPF dues are not being treated as the assets to be covered in the liquidation estate, however, the same are the liability of the Corporate Debtor which has to be paid by the liquidator as per Section 53 of the Code, and not by the secured creditor out of the proceeds from the sale of secured assets if exercised their option under Section 52(1)(b) of the Code. Hence, this prayer of the applicant is rejected on above findings. Question (iii) is answered in negative.
		</p>
