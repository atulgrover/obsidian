	<h1>
		A creditor enjoys the protection of a security interest he cannot be treated any higher than the other creditors &ndash; ICICI Bank Ltd. Vs. Mr. PratimBayal, The RP (for BKM Industries Ltd.) &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		March 17, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		The Adjudicating Authority held that&nbsp;just because of the fact that a creditor enjoys the protection of a security interest he cannot be treated any higher than the other creditors, who may have financed the Corporate Debtor while not enjoying any kind of protection in the shape of a security interest and thus such creditors have consistently run the risk of not getting paid their dues in the shape of realisation from the security interest or otherwise for a considerably longer period while the secured creditor was very happilystaying put with the protection of a security interest for if it so happens, then all the secured creditors would like to give dissenting view in the CoC, which will not lead to the maximization of the value of the Corporate Debtor and thus defeating the very purpose of resolution envisaged in the Code.
	</p>
</div>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							This is an application by ICICI Bank seeking directions on the Resolution Professional to take into account the priority of distribution of the plan realizations taking into account the priority assigned to the dissenting financial creditors who are also secured creditors.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Applicant i.e. ICICI Bank is a secured Financial Creditor of BKM Industries Limited. Pursuant to the admission of the Corporate Debtor into Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process, the Applicant submitted its claim for an amount of Rs.15.52Crore with the Interim Resolution Professional on 14 January 2021. The claim was admitted.
						</li>
						<li>
							It is further submitted that the Applicant is the sole term lender having first pari passu charge over the movable and immovable properties of the Corporate Debtor situated at Medak (Andhra Pradesh) and Silvassa (Dadra and Nagar Haveli) as security for its outstanding dues vis-a-vis the Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							It has been contended that while determining the calculation methodology of the creditor&rsquo;s proportional share the interest of the dissenting Creditor having a security interest have not been taken into consideration and they have been treated at par with the other creditors who are eligible to get the realizations from the plan in terms of Section 53 of the Code.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<p>
						The matter essentially relates to the prayer of ICICI bank to treat them at par with the assenting financial creditors as they are also in the catergory of Secured financial creditor.
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority held that&nbsp;just because of the fact that a creditor enjoys the protection of a security interest he cannot be treated any higher than the other creditors, who may have financed the Corporate Debtor while not enjoying any kind of protection in the shape of a security interest and thus such creditors have consistently run the risk of not getting paid their dues in the shape of realisation from the security interest or otherwise for a considerably longer period while the secured creditor was very happilystaying put with the protection of a security interest for if it so happens, then all the secured creditors would like to give dissenting view in the CoC, which will not lead to the maximization of the value of the Corporate Debtor and thus defeating the very purpose of resolution envisaged in the Code.
						<strong>
							 (p23)
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						In view of the above, we do not find any reason to interfere in the commercial wisdom of the CoC and accordingly reject the prayer of the applicant. Accordingly, IA 471/KB/2022 shall stand rejected.
						<strong>
							 (p24-25)
						</strong>
					</p>
