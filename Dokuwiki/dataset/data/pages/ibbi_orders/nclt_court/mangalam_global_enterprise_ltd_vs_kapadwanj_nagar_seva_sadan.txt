	<h1>
		Local Authority has no right to raise a claim against Successful Resolution Applicant (SRA) for the dues pertaining to the period prior to the approval of the Resolution Plan &ndash; Mangalam Global Enterprise Ltd. Vs. Kapadwanj Nagar Seva Sadan &ndash; NCLT Ahmedabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		October 30, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		In this case, the Successful Resolution Applicant approached Local Authority established under Gujarat Municipalities Act, requesting for change of name in the bills issued by it from the erstwhile Corporate Debtor. Local Authority responded that all past and pending were payable. Dues are in nature of Tax which is to be collected as per provisions of Gujarat Municipalities Act. NCLT Ahmedabad Bench held that: (i) It is well settled law that CIRP is a claim driven time bound process. (ii) The clean slate principle would stand negated if the successful resolution applicant is asked to pay the arrears payable by the corporate debtor. (iii) Local Authority has no right at this belated stage to raise a claim against the Corporate Debtor/SRA for the dues pertaining to the period prior to the approval of the resolution plan w.r.t. the Corporate Debtor.
	</p>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 I. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							Applicant, Mangalam Global Enterprises Ltd. is the Successful Resolution Applicant (SRA) of Corporate Debtor &ndash; H. M. Industrial Pvt. Ltd.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Resolution Plan of the SRA was approved&nbsp;vide order in IA 616 of 2022 dated 20.09.2022.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Resolution Plan has been fully implemented and the applicant has taken complete charge of the Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Successful Resolution Applicant approached the Respondent No. 1, Local Authority established under Gujarat Municipalities Act(Local Authority), requesting for change of name in the bills issued by it from the erstwhile Corporate Debtor to that of the applicant.
						</li>
						<li>
							Local Authority responded vide letter dated 31.1.2023 stating that all past and pending were payable since there was no specific observation with regards Local Authority in the order dated 20.9.2022. Dues are in nature of Tax which is to be collected as per provisions of Gujarat Municipalities Act.&nbsp;
						</li>
						<li>
							The Local Authority has not filed the claim before the RP during the CIRP of the Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							It is well settled law that CIRP is a claim driven time bound process and that through catena of judgment the above position has been upheld by Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court of India and recently again in the matter of
							<em>
								 Tata Power Western Odisha Distribution Ltd. (TPWODL) &amp; Anr. Vs. Jagannath Sponge Pvt. Ltd.&nbsp;(2023) 104 SC
							</em>
							 reiterated that the clean slate principle would stand negated if the successful resolution applicant is asked to pay the arrears payable by the corporate debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							Relying on the judgment of Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court of India in the&nbsp;matter of
							<em>
								 Ghanashyam Mishra and Sons Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Edelweiss Asset Reconstruction Company Ltd. &amp; Ors.&nbsp;(2021) 54 SC
							</em>
							 as well as
							<em>
								 Tata Power Western Odisha Distribution Ltd. (TPWODL) &amp; Anr. Vs. Jagannath Sponge Pvt. Ltd. (supra)
							</em>
							 we hold that Local Authority has no right at this belated stage to raise a claim against the Corporate Debtor/SRA for the dues pertaining to the period prior to the approval of the resolution plan i.e. 20.09.2022 w.r.t. the Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							Accordingly, we allow prayer (a) in the application declaring that all claims of the Respondent No.1 before 20.09.2022 stand extinguished pursuant to the approval of Resolution Plan by this Hon&rsquo;ble Tribunal.
						</li>
						<li>
							With the above directions this application is allowed and is disposed off. No order to costs.
							<strong>
								 (p12-13)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
