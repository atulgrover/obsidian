	<h1>
		Whether the Monitoring Committee of a Corporate Debtor through its Chairman has the authority under IBC to file CIRP application against other Corporate Debtor &ndash; M/s. Educomp Infrastructure &amp; School Management Ltd. Vs. M/s. Millenium Education Foundation &ndash; NCLT New Delhi Bench Court-V
	</h1>
	<div>
		June 9, 2022
	</div>
	<p>
		The Adjudicating Authority observed that the Applicant, in the present Application, being the Monitoring Professional for M/ s. Educomp Infrastructure &amp; School Management Limited, is covered under the provisions of the Code, by virtue of Section 2(d) of the code which read as; "such other body incorporated under any law for the time being in force, as the Central Government may, by notification, specify in this behalf;". Here, it can be observed that the Monitoring Committee is formed under the provisions of the Code and the Chairman therein, is also appointed in accordance with the provisions of the Code. Hence, he has proper authority to proceed to take any steps as necessary to protect the interest of the Applicant.
	</p>

							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						The present application is filed under Section 9 of IBC, 2016 by M/ s. Educomp Infrastructure &amp; School Management Ltd. (Applicant&rsquo;), through the chairman of Monitoring Committee by virtue of admission Order dated 25.04.2018, by the Adjudicating Authority, Chandigarh Bench, with a prayer to initiate the CIRP, against M/ s. Millenium Education Foundation (&lsquo;Respondent). The order in the matter was reserved in the pre-admission stage, for issuance of Notice on the Application under Section 9 of the Code. The question of law to be considered by the bench; &ldquo;
						<strong>
							 Whether the Application being filed through Monitoring Professional be considered proper in respect of Code?
						</strong>
						 &ldquo;.
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority observed that the Applicant, in the present Application, being the Monitoring Professional for M/ s. Educomp Infrastructure &amp; School Management Limited, is covered under the provisions of the Code, by virtue of Section 2(d) of the code which read as; &ldquo;
						<em>
							 such other body incorporated under any law for the time being in force, as the Central Government may, by notification, specify in this behalf;
						</em>
						 &ldquo;. Here, it can be observed that the Monitoring Committee is formed under the provisions of the Code and the Chairman therein, is also appointed in accordance with the provisions of the Code. Hence, he has proper authority to proceed to take any steps as necessary to protect the interest of the Applicant.
