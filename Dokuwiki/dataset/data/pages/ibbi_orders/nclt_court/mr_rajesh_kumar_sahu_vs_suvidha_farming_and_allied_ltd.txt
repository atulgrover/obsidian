		<h1>
			Jointly filed application by the number of depositors is maintainable and the amount invested by them in the scheme floated by the Corporate Debtor as the financial debt within the meaning of Section 5(8) of the Code &ndash; Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sahu Vs. M/s. Suvidha Farming and Allied Limited &ndash; NCLT Ahmedabad Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			September 21, 2020
		</div>
		<p>
			NCLT held that the applicants herein are the Financial Creditors within the meaning of Section 5(7) of the Code. The amount invested by them in the scheme floated by the Corporate Debtor as the financial debt within the meaning of Section 5(8) (a) of the Code. NCLT admitted the application and held that this application is jointly filed by the number of depositors and it is maintainable. The Corporate Debtor did not contested the claim of the Financial Creditors that is has committed default in paying the Financial Debt of Rs.90,44,010/-. The application is defect free and hence, it is directed the Corporate Debtor to be admitted in CIRP.
		</p>

							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Applicant, Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sahu, and others have&nbsp;invested various amount of money in the scheme floated by the Corporate Debtor under various plans which include recurring deposits, fixed deposits, monthly income and bonds plans etc.&nbsp;The Corporate Debtor had issued receipts/ certificates of&nbsp;their investments. The investments were to mature for the payment on the dates mentioned in the deposits receipts on certificates.
						</p>
						<p>
							Each of the applicants have invested amount on different&nbsp;dates. As far as the applicant no.1, one Mr. Rajesh Kumar&nbsp;Sahu, he had invested a sum of Rs.60,000/- (Rupees Sixty Thousand) and he was to get his amount back with the interest on or before 28.02.2017.In this application, some of the applicants have got amount&nbsp;due for more than rupees one lakh. Whereas, some of the applicants may have dues of less than rupees one lakh. Considering the provisions of explanation of Section 7 of the I.B. Code, 2016, we are of the view that this application is maintainable, as there exist certain applicants /financial creditors, in whose case, the default is of more than rupees one lakh.&nbsp;It is seen from the record that, since, some of the&nbsp;depositors did not get amount back, had lodged the complaint before the Security and Exchange Board of India (&ldquo;SEBI&rdquo;).
						</p>
						<p>
							The SEBI issued notice to the Corporate Debtor. The&nbsp;hearing was held on 22.12.2017. The SEBI directed the Corporate Debtor to refund the amount with interest to all the depositors on 31.03.2018. This order was challenged by the Corporate Debtor in the Appellate Tribunal. However, it could not succeed.&nbsp;From the above facts, it is clear that each of the applicant&nbsp;herein, was entitled to receive his amount deposited with the Corporate Debtor by the end of March 2018. However, none of them got their amount back and the Corporate&nbsp;Debtor committed a default in making payment of his financial debt of the total amount of Rs.90,44,010/-.
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Financial Debt
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							NCLT held that the&nbsp;applicants herein are the Financial Creditors within the&nbsp;meaning of Section 5(7) of the Code. The amount&nbsp;invested by them in the scheme floated by the Corporate&nbsp;Debtor as the financial debt within the meaning of Section&nbsp;5(8) (a) of the Code.
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Join Application
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							NCLT admitted the application and held that this application is jointly filed by the number of depositors&nbsp;and it is maintainable. The Corporate Debtor did not contested the claim of the Financial Creditors that is has committed default in paying the Financial Debt of Rs.90,44,010/-.&nbsp;The application is defect free and hence, it is directed the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor to be admitted in CIRP.
