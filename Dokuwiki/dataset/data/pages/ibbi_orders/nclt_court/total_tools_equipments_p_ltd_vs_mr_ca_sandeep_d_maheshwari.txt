	<h1>
		NCLT/Adjudicating Authority has the power to condone the delay in submitting claim to Liquidator before the distribution of Assets &ndash; Total Tools &amp; Equipments (P) Ltd. Vs. Mr. CA Sandeep D. Maheshwari &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		July 21, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		The Adjudicating Authority held that in the case of UCO Bank v. Nicco Corporation Ltd. (2018) 289 NCLT, Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT Kolkata Bench vide its order dated 14.02.2018 dealt with a similar situation whereby delay was condoned and the opportunity was granted to the creditor to re-submit the claim. Further, in M/s Globe Express Services v. M/s MM Cargo, the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT, New Delhi Bench vide order dated 21.05.2018 has observed that "it is only after distribution of Assets that no further claim can be entertained". Therefore, it is evident that this Tribunal has the power to condone the delay in submitting the claim to Liquidator before the distribution of Assets.
	</p>

							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<p>
						The present Application has been filed by M/s. Total Tools &amp; Equipments (P.) Ltd. (Applicant) under Section 42 and 60(5) of the IBC read with Rule 11 and 32 of the NCLT Rules, 2016 seeking the following reliefs:
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							a. Set aside the impugned order of rejection, by an email of Liquidator/Respondent, rejecting the claim of the Applicant dated 17.03.2022;
						</li>
						<li>
							b. Condone the delay of 3 days in filing this Application, by the Applicant, before this Hon&rsquo;ble Tribunal;
						</li>
						<li>
							c. Direct the Liquidator/Respondent to consider/admit the Applicant&rsquo;s&nbsp;claim as Operational Creditor of the Corporate Debtor in terms of Form- B.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							This Appeal is filed under Section 42 of the Code which gives power to this Adjudicating Authority to allow the submission of claim to Liquidator and condone the delay if there is a sufficient cause in filing the delayed claim before the Liquidator. Further, the Applicant placed its reliance upon Rule 177 of Companies (Court) Rules, 1959 which provides that, if any creditor fails to file proof of his debt with the liquidator within the time specified in the advertisement referred to in Rule 148, such creditor may apply to the court for relief and the court may adjudicate upon the debt and direct the liquidator to do so.
						</li>
						<li>
							In the case of
							<em>
								 UCO Bank v. Nicco Corporation Ltd.&nbsp;(2018) 289 NCLT
							</em>
							 , Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT Kolkata Bench vide its order dated 14.02.2018 dealt with a similar situation whereby delay was condoned and the opportunity was granted to the creditor to re-submit the claim. Further, in
							<em>
								 M/s Globe Express Services v. M/s MM Cargo
							</em>
							 , the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT, New Delhi Bench vide order dated 21.05.2018 has observed that &ldquo;
							<em>
								 it is only after distribution of Assets that no further claim can be entertained
							</em>
							 &ldquo;. Therefore, it is evident that this Tribunal has the power to condone the delay in submitting the claim to Liquidator before the distribution of Assets.
						</li>
						<li>
							In the light of the above discussion, we are of the considered view that the delay in filing the claim before the Liquidator on the part of the Applicant does not appear to be willful or intentional and, therefore, this Tribunal is of the considered view that delay in lodging for claim before the Liquidator deserves to be allowed. Accordingly, the appeal/IA under Section 42 is allowed with an order that the Liquidator shall verify and pass an appropriate speaking order on the claim lodged by the Applicant in terms of Section 40 of the Code. The IA is disposed of in aforesaid terms.
						</li>
						<li>
							Accordingly, the present Application is
							<strong>
								 Allowed
							</strong>
							 .
							<strong>
								 (p16-19)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
