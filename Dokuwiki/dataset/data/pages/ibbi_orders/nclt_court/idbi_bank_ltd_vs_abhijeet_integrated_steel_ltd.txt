		The latin maxim, nulluscommodumcaperepotest de injuria sua propria, will apply to the present case also. The Financial Creditor cannot be allowed to take advantage of its own wrong &ndash; IDBI Bank Ltd. Vs. Abhijeet Integrated Steel Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		July 19, 2022
	</div>
	<p>
		In the case of State Bank of India v N. S. Engineering Projects Private Limited (2022) 631 NCLT, we have taken the view that having contributed towards the default by their own conduct, the Financial Creditor cannot be allowed to maintain a p 7 application against the Corporate Debtor. The findings in that case will apply on all fours in a fact-situation such as the present case. Therefore, we have no hesitation in holding that in the present case also, the present p 7 application deserves to be dismissed on the ground of contributory role played by the Financial Creditor, forcing the default on the part of the Corporate Debtor. The latin maxim, nulluscommodumcaperepotest de injuria sua propria, will apply to the present case also. The Financial Creditor cannot be allowed to take advantage of its own wrong.
	</p>

						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						To briefly recall the facts, there has been no effective disbursements on the part of the Financial Creditor after 27 December 2012. The disbursements made thereafter are all appropriate either on the same day or the very next day towards interest. Therefore, these disbursements have remained on paper, and have contributed towards increased liability on the part of the Corporate Debtor to repay the amounts owed to the Financial Creditor.
					</p>
					<p>
						In the case of
						<em>
							 State Bank of India v N. S. Engineering Projects Private Limited&nbsp;(2022) 631 NCLT
						</em>
						 , we have taken the view that having contributed towards the default by their own conduct, the Financial Creditor cannot be allowed to maintain a p 7 application against the Corporate Debtor. The findings in that case will apply on all fours in a fact-situation such as the present case. Therefore, we have no hesitation in holding that in the present case also, the present p 7 application deserves to be dismissed on the ground of contributory role played by the Financial Creditor, forcing the default on the part of the Corporate Debtor. The latin maxim,
						<em>
							 nulluscommodumcaperepotest de injuria sua propria
						</em>
						 , will apply to the present case also. The Financial Creditor cannot be allowed to take advantage of its own wrong.
					</p>
					<p>
						There cannot be a mechanical admission of the application under p 7 of the Code just because a debt and a default is established. Primacy has to be given to the ultimate objective of the Code, which is to achieve a resolution of the Corporate Debtor.In order to be satisfied in terms of the provisions of p 7(5), of the Code, the role of the petitioning creditor in the alleged default must also be examined.&nbsp;Considering the totality of circumstances, CP (IB) No.1676/KB/2018 shall stand
						<strong>
							 rejected
						</strong>
						 . However, the Financial Creditor is at liberty to pursue its remedies under any other law as may be available to it.
					</p>
