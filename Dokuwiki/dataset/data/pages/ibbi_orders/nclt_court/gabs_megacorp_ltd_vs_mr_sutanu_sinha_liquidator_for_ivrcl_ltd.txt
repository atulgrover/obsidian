						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The instant Application is filed by M/s. Gabs Mega Corp Limited, Auction purchaser, who is the sole successful bidder in the E-Auction process conducted by the Liquidator on 27.02.2020 for sale of the Corporate Debtor as going concern for an amount of Rs. 1654.77 Crores. Although Liquidator has conducted e-auction for sale of Corporate Debtor as a going concern, the same was not and was kept on hold in view of the interim order passed by the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT vide order dated 06.09.2019 in Company Appeal Nos. 918-919 of 2019.&nbsp;The Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT vide its final order dated 29.05.2020, dismissed the&nbsp;Appeal against the order of Liquidation and vacated the interim order dated&nbsp;06.09.2019. Subsequent, to the said order, the liquidator has issued&nbsp;demand notice dated 03.06.2020 to the Applicant, requesting for payment&nbsp;of the sale consideration to be completed within three months at agreed&nbsp;upon consideration of Rs. 1654.77 Crores, with no interest for the first&nbsp;month and interest @12% p.a. for the remaining two months. The said&nbsp;period of three months completed on 03.09.2020. Now that the Applicant&nbsp;herein is seeking to grant an extension of three months to the Applicant&nbsp;with from 03.09.2020 for completing the sale of the Corporate&nbsp;Debtor as a going concern at the agreed auction price ofRs. 1654.77 Crores&nbsp;without any interest from 03.07.2020 to 03.12.2020.&nbsp;
						</p>
						<p>
							It is submitted before this Adjudicating Authority that the Applicant is&nbsp;willing to continue with the purchase of the Corporate Debtor at the agreed&nbsp;upon price, even though, the Covid-19 pandemic situation being in the&nbsp;nature of an act of God, was not foreseen by the parties concerned at the&nbsp;time of auction. It is further submitted that though the Covid-19 pandemic&nbsp;situation decreases the value of the Corporate Debtor significantly, the&nbsp;Applicant is still willing to honour the auction price, in purchasing the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor as a going concern. It is therefore, clear that the&nbsp;Applicant is not seeking for any re-evaluation of the agreed auction&nbsp;purchase price but is only seeking extension of payment schedule without&nbsp;any interest due to the unforeseen Covid-19 situation.
						</p>
						<p>
							It is a fact that the current Covid-19 pandemic and its fall out has thrown&nbsp;us unprecedented challenges and has put the entire global economy&nbsp;into an uncertain situation of total disorder. Owing to the situation caused&nbsp;by the Covid-19 pandemic, many a restructuring plans have been&nbsp;jeopardized and it is not uncommon in the present days to see corporate&nbsp;entities to come up with pleas of waivers, exclusions, concessions,&nbsp;relaxation etc.,
						</p>
						<p>
							In view of the Covid-19 pandemic and complete disruption of the&nbsp;economic activities in the country, the Government of India has announced&nbsp;a number of concessions, relaxations, waivers, extensions etc., in relation to the economic sector. The IBBI has amended the CIRP Regulations as&nbsp;well as the Liquidation Regulations by inserting Regulation 40C and 47A,&nbsp;respectively.
						</p>
						<p>
							Considering the submissions, facts and circumstances of case, as well as&nbsp;the economic scenario emerging due to Covid-19 Pandemic and it&rsquo;s fall&nbsp;out, this Adjudicating Authority observes that extension of time period&nbsp;enabling for completion of the purchase of the Corporate Debtor as a going&nbsp;concern would be in the interest of all stakeholders, rather than going for&nbsp;liquidation in a piecemeal manner, which should only be as a last&nbsp;resort. That a sale as a going concern would at any time realize a better&nbsp;value than that of a piecemeal sale, which undoubtedly would result in&nbsp;diminishing the value of the Corporate Debtor. Therefore, when the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor is sold as a going concern, it results in continuation of&nbsp;the economic activities. The object of the code is maximization of the&nbsp;value of the Corporate Debtor. The value maximization of the Corporate&nbsp;Debtor takes place when it is kept as a going concern, since, it will result&nbsp;in putting back the Corporate Debtor on track, further leading to promotion&nbsp;of entrepreneurship and balance the interest of the stakeholders.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT, in the matter of&nbsp;
							<em>
								<strong>
									S. C. Sekaran Vs. Amit Gupta &amp;&nbsp;
								</strong>
							</em>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Ors.[2019]  02 NCLAT
								</strong>
							</em>
							, inter-alia, has held as under:
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								&ldquo;Only on failure of revival, the&nbsp;Adjudicating Authority and the Liquidator will&nbsp;first proceed with the sale of company&rsquo;s assets&nbsp;wholly and thereafter, if not possible to sell the&nbsp;
							</em>
							<em>
								company in part and in accordance with law. &ldquo;
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							In view of the above discussion, this Adjudicating Authority is of the&nbsp;opinion that the extension of 90 days&rsquo; time period for completing the sale&nbsp;of Corporate Debtor as a going concern is justiciable and would be in the&nbsp;interest of the Corporate Debtor and its stakeholders. Accordingly, this&nbsp;Adjudicating Authority hereby grants an extension of a period of 90 days&nbsp;from 03.09.2020, to the Applicant for completing the purchase of the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor as going concern, which shall be completed in&nbsp;accordance with the Regulation 33 read with Schedule &mdash; I of Insolvency&nbsp;and Bankruptcy Board of India (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016.&nbsp;With regard to the prayer of the Applicant seeking for waiver of interest&nbsp;from 03.07.2020 to 03.12.2020, since, this Adjudicating Authority has&nbsp;granted extension of 90 days period for completing the sale as going&nbsp;concern from 03.09.2()20 to 03.12.2020, the obligation to pay the interest&nbsp;shall commence from 03.10.2020 in terms of Regulation 33 read with&nbsp;Schedule &mdash; I of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Liquidation&nbsp;Process) Regulations, 2016. Accordingly, the Applicant shall be liable&nbsp;to pay interest @ 12% p.a for the period commencing from 03.10.2020&nbsp;to 03.12.2020.
						</p>
						<p>
							Insofar as the waiver for payment of interest for the period from&nbsp;03.10.2020 to 03.12.2020 is concerned, in view of the aforesaid&nbsp;discussions and observations, this Adjudicating Authority is of the&nbsp;opinion that the same shall be left to the commercial wisdom and&nbsp;discretion of the stakeholders. The Applicant may place the said request&nbsp;with the stakeholders for arriving at a decision.&nbsp;With the above observations, IA No. 634/2020 stands of.
						</p>
