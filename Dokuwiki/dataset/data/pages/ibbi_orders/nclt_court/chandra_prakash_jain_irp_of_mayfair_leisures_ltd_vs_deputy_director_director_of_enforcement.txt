	<h1>
		Moratorium under Section 14 of IBC would not apply to the PMLA which is a distinct and special statute having its own objective and as such p 14 would not bar a proceeding under the PMLA Act &ndash; Chandra Prakash Jain IRP of M/s. Mayfair Leisures Ltd. Vs. Deputy Director, Director of Enforcement &ndash; NCLT Ahmedabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		March 10, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		The Adjudicating Authority held that the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court of Madras in the matter of Deputy Director, office of the Joint Directorate of Enforcement vs. Asset Reconstruction Company of India Ltd. and others (2020) 98 HC observed that NCLT has no jurisdiction to go into the matters governed under the PMLA, 2002 and, therefore, Section 14, having consequent upon an order passed by the Adjudicating Authority declaring moratorium, would not apply to the PMLA which is a distinct and special statute having its own objective and as such p 14 would not bar a proceeding under the Act.
	</p>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							M/s. Mayfair Leisure Limited is the Corporate Debtor and was admitted in CIRP vide order dated 02.06.2020 by this Adjudicating Authority filed by the Financial Creditor i.e. Bank of India under p 7 of the IBC.
						</li>
						<li>
							The property was already attached by the ED vide its provisional attachment order dated 24.04.2018. The said order was confirmed by the Hon&rsquo;ble PMLA Appellate Tribunal vide its order dated 03.12.2018. The Hon&rsquo;ble PMLA Appellate Tribunal vide order dated 12.05.2020 had directed that the status of the property of the Corporate Debtor has to be maintained as it
							<br />
							 was on 07.04.2018 during the course of investigation of the money laundering under PMLA, 2002 which was initiated vide ECIR/AMZO/03/2018 based on CBI, ACB, Gandhinagar, FIR no. 029 2018 A 0006.
						</li>
						<li>
							This application is filed by the Applicant under p 60(5) and 14 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (IBC) read with Rule 11 of NCLT Rules, 2016 (NCLT Rules) seeking release of attachment of property by the Enforcement Directorate, Ahmedabad.
						</li>
						<li>
							It is further submitted by the Applicant(IRP) that in view of order dated 12.05.2020 passed by Hon&rsquo;ble PMLA Appellate Tribunal, he is not able to take the possession of the property nor he is able dispose it off. Further, the ED has not even filed its claim with the Applicant. Further, the Applicant submitted that vide letter dated 17.06.2020 he had intimated ED about initiation of CIRP of the Corporate Debtor. In response to the letter, ED confirmed vide letter dated 26.06.2020 that the immovable assets of the Corporate Debtor are attached by their office. The Applicant in response issued another letter dated 21.07.2020 and requested the ED to release the attached property in order to take charge of the Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority held that the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court of Madras in the matter of
						<em>
							 Deputy Director, office of the Joint Directorate of Enforcement vs. Asset Reconstruction Company of India Ltd. and others&nbsp;(2020) 98 HC
						</em>
						 observed that NCLT has no jurisdiction to go into the matters governed under the PMLA, 2002 and, therefore, Section 14, having consequent upon an order passed by the Adjudicating Authority declaring moratorium, would not apply to the PMLA which is a distinct and special statute having its own objective and as such p 14 would not bar a proceeding under the Act.
					</p>
					<p>
						It is clear that the proper recourse to be resorted by the &lsquo;Corporate Debtor&rsquo; is to approach the &lsquo;Competent Forum&rsquo; under the PMLA, 2002 to its logical end or any other &lsquo;Jurisdictional Forum&rsquo; (other than the purview of IBC, 2016,) in the manner known to Law and in accordance with Law. In view thereof, this application stands rejected.
						<strong>
							 (p15-16)
						</strong>
					</p>
