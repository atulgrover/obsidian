		<h1>
			NCLT invoking Rule 15 of NCLT Rules, 2016 extends timelines for implementation of the Resolution Plan in the CIRP of R.D. Rubber Reclaim Ltd. &ndash; Glix Securities Pvt. Ltd. Vs. R.D. Rubber Reclaim Limited RP Ms. Mamta Binani &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			July 24, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			An application has filed by Glix Securities Pvt. Ltd., the Successful Resolution Applicant of R.D.Rubber Reclaim Limited (Corporate Debtor), inter alia praying for extension of timelines for implementation of the Resolution Plan. The Adjudicating Authority observed that there is no specific provision in the Code that specifies what should be done in cases where a successful resolution applicant applies to the court for extension of timelines either on account of force majeure circumstances or otherwise. Further, once a resolution plan has been approved by the adjudicating authority, the Committee of Creditors (CoC) ceases to exist. Therefore, there is no way that the Adjudicating Authority can direct the CoC to consider the request. It is upto the Adjudicating Authority to find a way out in such circumstances, by invoking rule 15 of the National Company Law Tribunal Rules, 2016.(p9)
		</p>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							An application has filed by Glix Securities Pvt. Ltd., the Successful Resolution Applicant of R.D.Rubber Reclaim&nbsp;Limited (the Corporate Debtor), inter alia praying for extension of timelines for&nbsp;implementation of the Resolution Plan.&nbsp;The Applicant is the Successful Resolution Applicant in the CIRP of the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor. The Resolution Plan submitted by the Applicant was&nbsp;approved by the CoC on 06.11.2020 and it has passed muster of this&nbsp;Adjudicating Authority on 11.05.2021.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that there is no specific provision in the Insolvency &amp; Bankruptcy Code, 2016,&nbsp;that specifies what should be done in cases where a successful resolution&nbsp;applicant applies to the court for extension of timelines either on account of&nbsp;force majeure circumstances or otherwise. Further, once a resolution plan&nbsp;has been approved by the adjudicating authority, the Committee of&nbsp;Creditors (CoC) ceases to exist. Therefore, there is no way that the&nbsp;Adjudicating Authority can direct the CoC to consider the request. It is&nbsp;upto the Adjudicating Authority to find a way out in such circumstances,&nbsp;by invoking rule 15 of the National Company Law Tribunal Rules, 2016.
							<strong>
								(p9)
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							It is held that after the Applicant submitted its Resolution Plan, the circumstances in the&nbsp;country have changed drastically. There has been a significant increase in&nbsp;Covid-19 cases in a second wave. The second surge of the pandemic has&nbsp;supervened in a major way, affecting lakhs of people. Multiple business and&nbsp;financial entities have suffered tremendously, and the resulting&nbsp;interruptions in cash flow and working capital cannot be gainsaid. Supply&nbsp;chains have been disrupted and the economy has also suffered. We are&nbsp;convinced that there is a force majeure circumstance necessitating our&nbsp;intervention in the best interests of the corporate debtor.&nbsp;We also specifically note the undertaking given by Mr Ratnanko Banerji,&nbsp;learned Senior Counsel appearing for the Applicant, upon specific&nbsp;instructions that the Applicant shall not take any advantage of the alleged&nbsp;theft that is stated to have taken place in the premises of the corporate&nbsp;debtor, and that the present application has been made only due to the&nbsp;second wave of the Covid-19 pandemic. The Applicant shall file the&nbsp;undertaking by way of an affidavit in the Registry within a period of&nbsp;fifteen days from today, and the order passed herein shall have effect only&nbsp;if this part of the order is complied with, failing which the consequences&nbsp;for violation of the approved Resolution Plan shall swiftly follow.&nbsp;IA No.496/KB/2021 is, therefore, allowed with the above observations.
							<strong>
								(p10-11)
							</strong>
