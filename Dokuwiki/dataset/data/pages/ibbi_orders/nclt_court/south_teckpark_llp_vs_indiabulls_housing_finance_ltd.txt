	<h1>
		There is no requirement for impleading application of any other party before admission of an application under p 7 of the Code, 2016 &ndash; South Teckpark LLP Vs. M/s. Indiabulls Housing Finance Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Bengaluru Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		July 1, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		In this case, M/s. South Teckpark LLP prays to implead in Section 7 application who holds 20.41% of shares in the Corporate Debtor. The Adjudicating Authority held that in an application filed under Section 7 of the Code, 2016 the Applicant Financial Creditor and the Corporate Debtor alone are the necessary parties. In the pre-admission stage is only required to satisfy the requirements under p 7 i.e., there being Financial debt and default on the part of the Corporate Debtor.
	</p>

						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						This petition is filed under Section 60(5) of the IBC, 2016 r/w Rule 11 of the NCLT Rules,2016 by M/s. South Teckpark LLP (Impleading Applicant)with a prayer to permit the applicant who holds 20.41% of shares in the Corporate Debtor to implead and come on record as a party in the main petition of Section 7 of IBC.
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority held that in an application filed under Section 7 of the Code, 2016 the Applicant Financial Creditor and the Corporate Debtor alone are the necessary parties. In the pre-admission stage is only required to satisfy the requirements under p 7 i.e., there being Financial debt and default on the part of the Corporate Debtor. Hence there is no requirement for impleading application of any other party before admission of an application under p 7 of the Code, 2016.&nbsp;In view of the aforesaid facts and circumstances, IA 353 OF 2023 stands dismissed.
