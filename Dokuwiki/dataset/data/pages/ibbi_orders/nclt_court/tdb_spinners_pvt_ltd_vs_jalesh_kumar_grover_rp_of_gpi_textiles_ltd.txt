		<h1>
			Award passed by the MSME Facilitation Council is a public document and on the face of it the rejection of the claim in CIRP is not tenable &ndash; TDB Spinners Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Jalesh Kumar Grover RP of GPI Textiles Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Chandigarh Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			October 22, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority observed that once it is shown that the claim of the applicant is backed by an award passed by the MSME Facilitation Council and that there was no stay against the same either in the appeal filed under Section 34 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 or from any other court, the action of the Resolution Professional in rejecting the claim of the applicant on the ground that there was no due shown in the books of the corporate debtor against the applicant is unsustainable. Award passed by the MSME Facilitation Council is a public document and on the face of it the rejection of the claim of the applicant is not tenable.
		</p>
	</div>
</p>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The applicant submits that since the respondent-corporate debtor&nbsp;failed to pay the dues, it has obtained an award dated 26.11.2014 from the&nbsp;Madhya Pradesh Micro and Small Enterprises Facilitation Council, Bhopal vide&nbsp;award dated 26.11.2014. The petition filed by the respondent-corporate debtor&nbsp;under Section 34 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 challenging the&nbsp;said Award is pending as on date and that no stay of the award was granted in&nbsp;the said petition filed by the respondent-corporate debtor. The applicant submits&nbsp;that inspite of the award of the MSME Facilitation Council in its favour and that&nbsp;though there was no stay on the said award, the respondent-Resolution&nbsp;Professional illegally and arbitrarily rejected its claim.&nbsp;On the other hand, the&nbsp;respondent-Resolution Professional&nbsp;submits that since as per the books of the corporate&nbsp;debtor no due was shown against the applicant, it was not entitled for admission&nbsp;of its claim.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that once it is shown that the claim of the applicant is&nbsp;backed by an award passed by the MSME Facilitation Council and that there was&nbsp;no stay against the same either in the appeal filed under Section 34 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 or from any other court, the action of the&nbsp;Resolution Professional in rejecting the claim of the applicant on the ground that&nbsp;there was no due shown in the books of the corporate debtor against the&nbsp;applicant is unsustainable. Award passed by the MSME Facilitation Council is a&nbsp;public document and on the face of it the rejection of the claim of the applicant is&nbsp;not tenable.&nbsp;In these circumstances and for the aforesaid reasons, the instant CA&nbsp;No. 259/2019 is allowed and the respondent-Resolution Professional is directed&nbsp;to consider the claim of the applicant, in accordance with the Code and&nbsp;Regulations made thereunder.&nbsp;Accordingly, CA No. 259/2019 is disposed of.&nbsp;
							<strong>
								(p5-7)
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							In another CA, M/s Tanmay Pure Spun, an operational creditor of the respondent-corporate debtor M/s GPI Textiles Limited filed the instant CA under Section 60(5)&nbsp;of IBC 2016 questioning the action of the respondent-IRP in rejecting its claim. The applicant questions the rejection of its claim on the ground that it&nbsp;has obtained an award from the MSME Facilitation Council however, no copy of&nbsp;the award is filed either with its claim or with the instant CA. However, the&nbsp;applicant has given the details such as the number and date of the award.&nbsp;Though the applicant in the claim Form at Sr. No. 11 mentioned that the Arbitral&nbsp;Award dated 26.11.2014 in MSMEFC No. 502/2013 was enclosed to the claim&nbsp;but even when the respondent in its reply to the CA specifically contended that no&nbsp;copy of any such award was enclosed to the claim, the applicant has not chosen&nbsp;to file the copy of the award by filing any rejoinder by enclosing the copy of the&nbsp;said award.
						</p>
						<p>
							The respondent while submitting that the applicant failed to furnish a&nbsp;copy of the award said to have been passed by the MSME Facilitation Council in&nbsp;its favour further submitted that the claim of the applicant was belated and hence,&nbsp;there is no illegality in its action in rejecting the claim.&nbsp;In view of the settled principle of law that the time prescribed for&nbsp;submission of the claims is not mandatory whereas it is only directory, we are&nbsp;inclined to direct the respondent-Resolution Professional to consider the claim of&nbsp;the applicant, in accordance with law. It is also to be seen that the delay in&nbsp;submission of the claim of the applicant cannot be treated as abnormal.
						</p>
						<p>
							Though in normal circumstances, it is the duty of any claimant to&nbsp;furnish all the required documents but since the applicant furnished the details of&nbsp;the award i.e. number and date and since the non-submission of the copy of the&nbsp;award is the main reason for rejection of its claim, we dispose of this CA&nbsp;permitting the applicant to furnish the copy of the award passed in its favour by&nbsp;the MSME Facilitation Council within two weeks from today, to the respondent-Resolution Professional and if the applicant submits the copy of the award within&nbsp;the said specified period, the respondent shall consider the same in accordance&nbsp;with the Code and Regulations made thereunder.
