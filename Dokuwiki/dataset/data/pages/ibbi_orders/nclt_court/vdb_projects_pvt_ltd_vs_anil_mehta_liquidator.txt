	<h1>
		NCLT cancelled sale of Pratibha Industries Limited on a going concern &amp; directed Liquidator to get assets valued again and issue fresh e-Auction sale notice &ndash; VDB Projects Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Anil Mehta, Liquidator of Pratibha Industries Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		December 29, 2022
	</div>
	<p>
		The Adjudicating Authority held that it not inclined to give benefit of provision of 90 days to pay balance sale consideration as per amended clause 12 of Schedule I of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016. Relying on Circular issued by the IBBI, we hold that benefit cannot be granted as the liquidation order in the proceeding was passed before the amendment dated 25.07.2019 benefit under amended clause 12, could not be granted. Therefore, EAuction dated 15.02.2022 is null and void as bad in law.
	</p>
</div>

						<strong>
							 I. Case Reference
						</strong>
					</p>
					Case Citation:(2022) 963 NCLTCase Name:VDB Projects Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Anil Mehta, Liquidator of Pratibha Industries Ltd.Corporate Debtor:Pratibha Industries Ltd.Application/Appeal No.:IA 1469/MB/2022 In CP (IB) 3558/MB/2018Judgment Date:23-Dec-22Court/Bench:NCLT Mumbai BenchMember (Judicial):Shri Justice Pradeep Narhari DeshmukhMember (Technical):Shri Shyam Babu Gautam
					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority held that:
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							Facts of the case in the light of NCLAT judgement in
							<em>
								 M/s. Visisth Services Limited Vs. S.V. Ramani &amp; Ors.&nbsp;(2022) 33 NCLAT
							</em>
							 , where the Bench concluded that sale of a Company as a &lsquo;Going Concern&rsquo;&nbsp;means sale of both assets and liabilities, if it is stated on &lsquo;as is where is&nbsp;basis&rsquo;. The same corum by order in Shivshakti Interglobe Exports Pvt Lid v. KTC Foods Pvt Ltd.
							<em>
								 (2022) 192 NCLAT
							</em>
							 dated 25.02.2022 held that in a Corporate Debtor sale as a going concern in Liquidation Proceedings without its dissolution in terms of Regulation 32(e) of the Liquidation Process Regulations, 2016, it is essential to see that the Corporate Debtor is not burdened by any past or remaining unpaid outstanding liabilities prior to the sale of the Company as a going concern and after payment of the sale proceeds disturbed in accordance with Section 53 of the Code. The same was held as it will saddle the new purchaser with numerous liabilities which will ultimately defy the object of IBC, 2016.
							<strong>
								 (p82)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							Further, considered the discussion paper published by IBBI on the Going Concern sale of the Corporate Debtor, where it was held discussed that the Going Concern sale means the sale of Corporate Debtor with the liabilities.
							<strong>
								 (p83)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							perused the Regulations outer limit for Going Concern sale and liquidation which prescribes a period of 90 days from the date of receipt of Letter of Intent for the payment of sale consideration, if any sale has to be consummated as a Going Concern.
							<strong>
								 (p84)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							This Adjudicating Authority not inclined to give benefit of provision of 90 days to pay balance sale consideration as per amended clause 12 of Schedule I of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016. Relying on Circular issued by the IBBI, we hold that benefit cannot be granted as the liquidation order in the proceeding was passed before the amendment dated 25.07.2019 benefit under amended clause 12, could not be granted. Therefore, EAuction dated 15.02.2022 is null and void as bad in law.
							<strong>
								 (p85)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							This Tribunal vide order dated 03.06.2022, directed the applicant to&nbsp;deposit the sum of Rs.67.86 Crores along with interest at the rate of 12% on or before 06.06.2022 which was not complied by the Applicant.&nbsp;Thus, it can be inferred that time and again opportunity was given to Applicant and the same has not been complied with. In the aforesaid backdrop the Applicant is in clear breach of the aforesaid order, hence sale stands cancelled.
							<strong>
								 (p86)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							The Liquidator is directed to get assets valued again and issue fresh EAuction sale notice in accordance with Liquidation Regulations as at 01.04.2018 (applicable) and complete Auction expeditiously.&nbsp;With the aforesaid observations Application IA No. 1469 of 2022 In CP No. 3558 of 2018, is stands disposed of.
							<strong>
								 (p87-88)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
