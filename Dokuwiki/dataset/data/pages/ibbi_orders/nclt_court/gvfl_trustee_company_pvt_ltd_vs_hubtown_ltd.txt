		<h1>
			Share purchase with exit option/Annual Put Option cannot be considered as a debt which is disbursed against consideration of time value for money &ndash; GVFL Trustee Company Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Hubtown Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			December 24, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority held that a shareholder is different from a lender. Equity is not a debt and as such any contract for acquisition of shareholding in a body corporate can never result in the formation of a debt. Share purchase in HBT Mehsana by GVFL with exit option of inter alia &ldquo;Annual Put Option&rdquo; cannot be considered as a debt which is disbursed against consideration of time value for money. The Bench also held that Internal Rate of Return (IRR) cannot be equated with interest payments.
		</p>
	</div>
</p>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Facts of the case
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							GVFL Trustee Company Pvt Ltd (GVFL) is an Applicant who&nbsp;have filed Section 7 Petition&nbsp;against Hubtown Limited for a debt by way of equity investment&nbsp;in shares of Hubtown Bus Terminal (Mehsana) Pvt Ltd for a total&nbsp;amount of Rs.4,30,54,200/- as principal and Rs.9,96,95,800/- as&nbsp;Internal Rate of Return (IRR) calculated at 26% of the principal&nbsp;up to 31.08.2018. As per the Petitioner there is a default under&nbsp;Section 7 of IBC, 2016 as its &ldquo;put option&rdquo; was not entertained&nbsp;when the said demand notice dated 02.01.2018 was sent to the&nbsp;Respondent M/s. Hubtown Limited demanding exit by way of&nbsp;&ldquo;put option&rdquo;.&nbsp;The issue before the Bench is whether GVFL is a Financial&nbsp;Creditor and whether the debt claimed in the Petition is a&nbsp;Financial Debt as per the IBC 2016.
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority held that:
						</p>
						<ul>
							<li>
								This Bench has to see whether the claim of GVFL as a&nbsp;Shareholder of HBT Mehsana in exercise of its &lsquo;put option&rsquo;&nbsp;tantamount to a financial debt. The Bench is of the view that&nbsp;
								<strong>
									a&nbsp;
								</strong>
								<strong>
									shareholder is different from a lender
								</strong>
								. The shareholder&nbsp;undertakes the risk by investing in shares and derives its return by&nbsp;way of profits in the form of dividends and appreciation in the&nbsp;value of shareholding, i.e., capital gains. In contrast, the Lender&nbsp;gives loans for which the payment is by way of Interest.
								<strong>
									(p9)
								</strong>
							</li>
							<li>
								The Bench notes that as per the Share Subscription and Shareholders Agreement(SHA), GVFL invested in HBT&nbsp;Mehsana by purchasing the shares of ILFS group. This cannot be&nbsp;termed as an investment of GVFL by way of a loan. The money&nbsp;paid by GVFL to acquire the share of HBT Mehsana cannot be&nbsp;construed as a consideration for time value of money and it was&nbsp;solely for the purchase of shares of HBT Mehsana held by ILFS&nbsp;group to become a shareholder in the Company. The Bench also&nbsp;notes that as per shareholder of HBT Mehsana, GVFL had&nbsp;acquired various rights including that of appointing a Director, exercising votes in AGM/ EGM and exercising veto votes. It is&nbsp;abundantly clear to the Bench that no voting rights ever accrues&nbsp;to a Financial Creditor in any AGM/ EGM. Therefore, the rights&nbsp;enjoyed by GVFL in HBT Mehsana like exercising votes in the&nbsp;AGM/ EGM are typically the rights of a Shareholder and not a&nbsp;Financial Creditor. This is because,&nbsp;
								<strong>
									Equity is not a debt and as&nbsp;
								</strong>
								<strong>
									such any contract for acquisition of shareholding in a body&nbsp;
								</strong>
								<strong>
									corporate can never result in the formation of a debt
								</strong>
								. The Share Purchase Agreement(SPA)&nbsp;and SHA are both contracts in relation to GVFL acquisition of&nbsp;equity shareholding in HBT Mehsana.
								<strong>
									(p10)
								</strong>
							</li>
							<li>
								The Bench is of the view that share purchase in HBT Mehsana by&nbsp;GVFL with exit option of inter alia &ldquo;Annual Put Option&rdquo; cannot&nbsp;be considered as a debt which is disbursed against consideration&nbsp;of time value for money.&nbsp;
								<strong>
									The Bench also notes that Internal Rate&nbsp;
								</strong>
								<strong>
									of Return (IRR) cannot be equated with interest payments
								</strong>
								. The&nbsp;relevance of IRR for an investor in shares is in relation to&nbsp;expected profit and dividend pay out and capital appreciation of&nbsp;the shares which is totally different to the interest which is return&nbsp;for any investment by way of loan.
								<strong>
									(p11)
								</strong>
							</li>
							<li>
								The Bench is of the view that the GVFL may be entitled to this&nbsp;claim under the SHA as a Shareholder of HBT Mehsana.&nbsp;However, the Bench has no doubt that the claim of GVFL cannot&nbsp;be termed as a Financial Debt as contemplated under IBC.&nbsp;Therefore, the MA 2412/2019 regarding maintainability of&nbsp;CP(IB)-4129/2018 is &ldquo;Allowed&rdquo; and CP No. CP(IB)-4129/2018&nbsp;is not maintainable under Section 7 of IBC and therefore is&nbsp;&ldquo;
								<strong>
									dismissed
								</strong>
								&rdquo;.
								<strong>
									(p12)
								</strong>
							</li>
						</ul>
					</div>
				</div>
