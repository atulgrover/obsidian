		<h1>
			Whether the document, claims and counter-claims are a genuine or forged ones, it cannot be determined under the summary jurisdiction of NCLT &ndash; M/s Three C Universal Developers Pvt. Ltd. Vs. M/ s. Gulshan Homz Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT New Delhi Bench Court-II
		</h1>
		<div>
			January 5, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority observed that in the absence of any Loan Agreement and relevant Board Resolution, merely on the basis of the Form 26AS, it cannot be presumed that any Loan was disbursed by the Applicant to the Corporate Debtor which could be treated as the Financial Debt. With regard to the referred MoU and resultant claims and counter claims between the parties, this Bench is of the view, that whether the document, claims and counter-claims are a genuine or forged ones, it cannot be determined under the summary jurisdiction of this Tribunal and can be decided by the forum of competent jurisdiction. The Applicant has failed to demonstrate that the debt claimed in the Part IV of its Application is arising out of any loan transactions and is a Financial Debt. The Application is accordingly Dismissed.
		</p>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that in the absence of any Loan&nbsp;Agreement and relevant Board Resolution, merely on the basis of the&nbsp;Form 26AS, it cannot be presumed that any Loan was disbursed by&nbsp;the Applicant to the Corporate Debtor which could be treated as the&nbsp;Financial Debt.&nbsp;That in its reply, the Corporate Debtor has given explanations&nbsp;towards the payment as reflected in the bank statements of the&nbsp;Applicant and stated that the same were made pursuant to the MoU&nbsp;dated 14.06.2017 executed between the parties.&nbsp;Per contra, the Applicant has denied the existence of such an&nbsp;MoU and has stated that it is a forged and fabricated document. It is&nbsp;added by the Applicant that the Ex-Director of the Applicant has also&nbsp;filed a police complaint dated 05.01.2021 with SHO, New Friends&nbsp;Colony New Delhi against the Corporate Debtor alleging forgery and&nbsp;fabrication.
						</p>
						<p>
							With regard to the above referred MoU and resultant claims&nbsp;and counter claims between the parties, this Bench is of the view, that&nbsp;whether the document, claims and counter-claims are a genuine or&nbsp;forged ones, it cannot be determined under the summary jurisdiction&nbsp;of this Tribunal and can be decided by the forum of competent&nbsp;jurisdiction.&nbsp;In sequel to the above discussion, we conclude that the&nbsp;Applicant has failed to demonstrate that the debt claimed in the Part&nbsp;IV of its Application is arising out of any loan transactions and is a&nbsp;Financial Debt. The Application is accordingly&nbsp;
							<strong>
								Dismissed.(p28-34)
							</strong>
						</p>
