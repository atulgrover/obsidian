	<h1>
		Whether Cheques dated prior to commencement of CIRP can be encashed during the Moratorium u/s 14 of IBC &ndash; Ms. Vaishali Patrikar, RP Vs. Darshan Developers and Others &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		January 8, 2024
	</div>
	<p>
		Hon'ble NCLT Mumbai Bench holds that Section 14 of the Code provides that the Adjudicating Authority shall by order declare moratorium for prohibiting, amongst others, transferring, encumbering, alienating or disposing of by the corporate debtor any of its assets or any legal right or beneficial interest therein. Upon commencement of CIRP, the Bank Accounts of the Corporate Debtor are to be managed and operated by the appointed Insolvency Professional. Further, we find that if the argument of the Ld. Counsel for the Respondent that Section 14 of the Code does not apply to cheques dated prior to commencement of CIRP but intended to be cleared after funds are available therein, we feel that this will defeat the basic object of scheme of the Code which mandates resolution of claims of creditors out of assets of the Corporate Debtor available as on Insolvency Commencement date. Hence, we are of considered view that these cheques were encashed in violation of provisions of Section 14 of the Code.
	</p>

					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							CIRP commenced vide Order dated 03.08.2020 passed by NCLT on an Application filed under p 7 of Code by Vistra (ITCL) India Ltd.
						</li>
						<li>
							An amount of Rs. 41,00,000/- was debited from the account of the CD held in Indusind Bank in favour of M/s Darshan Developers (Respondent No.1/R1) vide cheque bearing no. 791035 and an amount of Rs. 50,00,000/- was debited from the account of the CD held in RBL Bank in favour of R1 in vide cheque bearing no. 37 on 06.08.2020.
						</li>
						<li>
							Respondent No. 3 (Suspended Board of Directors of Corporate Debtor) claims that the cheques were issued on 31.07.2020.
						</li>
						<li>
							The alleged date of issuance of the cheques is 3 days prior to the admission order.&nbsp;Further, a perusal of the bank account shows that&nbsp;when the cheques were issued there was not sufficient amounts&nbsp;with the Corporate Debtor to even get these encashed.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<p>
						This Bench had distinguished the decision in
						<em>
							 Pratim Bayal v. Tata Motors Finance Solutions Limited&nbsp;(2023) 405 NCLT
						</em>
						 in its decision delivered in
						<em>
							 Associated Road Carriers v. Indusar Global Ltd IA 664 of 2020
						</em>
						 and held that the cheques encashed after commencement of CIRP and dated post CIRP commencement date could not have been encashed in view of moratorium coming into force in view of Section 14 of the Code.
						<strong>
							 (p4.2)
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						In the present case, it is undisputed position that the cheques were issued on 31.7.2020, however the Corporate Debtor&rsquo;s relevant Bank account at the relevant date had no funds available to honour the cheques on that day. It follows therefrom that these cheques, though dated 31.07.2020, were intended to be encashed as and when the funds are available in the relevant Bank Account of the Corporate. In other words, these cheques were issued in anticipation of funds being made available in the Corporate Debtor&rsquo;s account in future. This clearly demonstrates the intent of the issuer of the cheques that these cheques were to be presented for payment in future. It is undisputed fact that these cheques were presented for payment on 6.8.2020 out of funds credited in Corporate Debtor&rsquo;s account on 4.8.2020 and CIRP commenced on 3.8.2020. In other words, these cheques could not have intended to be paid prior to 4.8.2020.
						<strong>
							 (p4.3)
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						Having said so, we find that the Section 14 of the Code provides that the Adjudicating Authority shall by order declare moratorium&nbsp;for prohibiting, amongst others, transferring, encumbering,&nbsp;alienating or disposing of by the corporate debtor any of its assets&nbsp;or any legal right or beneficial interest therein. Upon&nbsp;commencement of CIRP, the Bank Accounts of the Corporate&nbsp;Debtor are to be managed and operated by the appointed&nbsp;Insolvency Professional. Further, we find that if the argument of&nbsp;the Ld. Counsel for the Respondent that Section 14 of the Code&nbsp;does not apply to cheques dated prior to commencement of CIRP&nbsp;but intended to be cleared after funds are available therein, we feel&nbsp;that this will defeat the basic object of scheme of the Code which&nbsp;mandates resolution of claims of creditors out of assets of the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor available as on Insolvency Commencement date.&nbsp;Hence, we are of considered view that these cheques were&nbsp;encashed in violation of provisions of Section 14 of the Code.
						<strong>
							 (p4.4)
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						We are also conscious of the fact that no money was due and payable to the Respondent No. 1 by the Corporate Debtor and payment was to be made as an accommodation to Respondent No. 1 on returnable basis.
						<strong>
							 (p4.5)
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						In view of the foregoing discussion, we have no hesitation to direct the Respondent No. 1 and 2 to jointly and severally refund the amount of Rs. 91,00,000/- to the Corporate Debtor within 7 days. Since, the Applicant has not made any prayer in terms of Section 66 of the Code, under which Respondent No. 3 to 7, as the case may be, could be proceeded in our considered view, we are not inclined to pass any order against Respondent No. 3 to 7. However, the Applicant shall be at liberty to file appropriate application seeking their contribution as well to the assets of the Corporate Debtor in terms of Section 66 of the Code.
						<strong>
							 (p4.6)
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						We do not find any merit in prayer for reimbursement of costs.&nbsp;In view of the foregoing, IA 3921/2022 is allowed and disposed of&nbsp;accordingly.
						<strong>
							 (p4.7)
						</strong>
					</p>
