						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							On 12.03.2020 an application has been filed under Section 9 of the Code&nbsp;against M/s. Seguro Foundations &amp; Structures Pvt Ltd. The matter was listed before&nbsp;this Tribunal on 16.09.2020. Notice was issued to the Corporate Debtor and the&nbsp;matter adjourned to 07.10.2020. Notice to the Corporate Debtor was served and AD&nbsp;card returned is on record. But the Corporate Debtor did not make representation&nbsp;before this Tribunal. Thereafter, the Operational Creditor filed this Interlocutory&nbsp;Application to implead M/s. Inkel Limited as additional Corporate Debtor in the&nbsp;application. The Corporate Debtor company have been acquired by M/s Inkel Ltd. and is having a control stake of over 65% of the shares of the Corporate Debtor with effect from 01.12.2017. The operations of Corporate Debtor were managed, controlled and operated by the proposed Corporate Debtor.
						</p>
						<p>
							The question that arises for consideration is that whether the proposed party&nbsp;can be impleaded as an additional Corporate Debtor in an application under Section&nbsp;9?
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority held that in the light of the decision in&nbsp;
							<em>
								Mrs. Mamatha Vs. AMB Infrabuild Pvt. Ltd. &amp; Ors.&nbsp;[2018]  114 NCLAT
							</em>
							&nbsp;and considering the&nbsp;facts and circumstances of the case, it is clear that the proposed additional Corporate&nbsp;Debtor is the holding company of the M/s. Seguro Foundations and Structures Pvt Ltd.&nbsp;Therefore, the Application under Section 9 would be maintainable against both of them&nbsp;jointly. Hence, this Tribunal is of the view that M/s. Inkel Limited, should be impleaded as&nbsp;additional Corporate Debtor.&nbsp;Registry is directed to carryout necessary impleadment in the IBA and issue&nbsp;notice to them through e-mail in addition to normal procedure. Applicant is also directed&nbsp;to serve notice to the newly impleaded additional Corporate Debtor along with a copy of&nbsp;IBA and produce proof of service with an affidavit before next date fixed. Additional&nbsp;Corporate debtor shall file its Counter within two weeks.
						</p>
