	<h1>
		The delay in filing Appeal u/s 42 of IBC can be condoned even in the absence of the application u/s 5 of Limitation Act, 1963 but there shall at least be an oral prayer for condonation of delay, the delay cannot be condoned Suo-Moto &ndash; Southern Power Distribution Company of Telangana Ltd. Vs. Priyadarsini Ltd. Rep by Liquidator, Mr. Krishna Mohan Gollamudi &ndash; NCLT Hyderabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		July 24, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		The NCLT Hyderabad Bench held that there is no bar to exercise by the Court/Tribunal of its discretion to condone delay, in the absence of a formal application it is not always necessary to file an application for condonation of delay before the Court or Tribunal, and delay can be condoned even in the absence of the Application under Section 5 of Limitation Act. Therefore, there shall at least be an oral prayer for condonation of delay, stating the reasons for not filing the Appeal within 14 days. Therefore, when the Application itself is being bared by limitation and for condonation of delay not even a single ground is pleaded, the delay cannot be condoned &lsquo;Suo-Moto&rsquo;.
	</p>
</div>

							 I. Case Reference
						</strong>
					</p>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							This is an Application filed by the Applicant under Section 42 of the IBC, 2016 praying the Tribunal to set aside the rejection of part claim of the Applicant Company i.e Rs.28,28,14,013/- and to direct the Respondent to verify and admit the rejected portion of the claim of the Applicant Company amounting to Rs.28,28,14,013/-.
						</li>
						<li>
							The impugned communication has been sent way of email dated 06.07.2021, to the Applicant. This Application however has been preferred on 22.02.2022. Thus there is a delay of 209 days in filing the present Appeal.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The delay in filing the Appeal can be condoned under Section 5 of limitation Act as held by Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT in the ruling of
							<em>
								 Canara Bank Vs. Commercial Tax Department Madhya Pradesh &amp; Anr.&nbsp;(2023) 342 NCLAT
							</em>
							 , wherein it was held that; the delay in filing the Appeal under Section 42 is clearly condonable while exercising the power under Section 5 of the Limitation Act.
						</li>
						<li>
							Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court of India, in
							<em>
								 Sesh Nath Singh &amp; ANR Vs Baidyabati Sheoraphuli Co-operative Bank Ltd and Anr.&nbsp;(2021) 49 SC
							</em>
							 as held that Section 5 of the Limitation Act, 1963 does not speak of any application. The Section enables the Court to admit an application or Appeal if the applicant or the appellant, as the case may be, satisfies the Court that he had sufficient cause for not making the application and/or preferring the Appeal, within the time prescribed.
						</li>
						<li>
							Although, it is the general practice to make a formal application under Section 5 of the Limitation Act, 1963, in order to enable the Court or Tribunal to weigh the sufficiency of the cause for the inability of the appellant/applicant to approach the Court/Tribunal within the time prescribed by limitation, there is no bar to exercise by the Court/Tribunal of its discretion to condone delay, in the absence of a formal application it is not always necessary to file an application for condonation of delay before the Court or Tribunal, and delay can be condoned even in the absence of the Application under Section 5 of Limitation Act.
						</li>
						<li>
							Therefore, there shall at least be an oral prayer for condonation of delay, stating the reasons for not filing the Appeal within 14 days.
						</li>
						<li>
							On a careful perusal of the petition, we are unable to find even a whisper as to why the Appeal was not preferred within 14 days from the date of receipt of the communication, leave alone the prayer for condonation of delay.
						</li>
						<li>
							<strong>
								 Therefore, when the Application itself is being bared by limitation and for condonation of delay not even a single ground is pleaded, the delay cannot be condoned &lsquo;Suo-Moto&rsquo;
							</strong>
							 . Hence the question of giving a direction to the liquidator to consider the claim of the Applicant does not arise. Therefore, the Application is dismissed as not maintainable.
							<strong>
								 (p7-11)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
