	<h1>
		Adjudicating Authority(NCLT) under IBC, 2016 is not the right fora to deal with revocation of attachment made by Enforcement Directorate under PMLA, Act, 2002 during CIRP &ndash; Mr. Palaniappan Liquidator of Nathella Sampath Jewelry Pvt. Ltd. Vs. The Joint Director Directorate of Enforcement &ndash; NCLT Chennai Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		February 20, 2024
	</div>
	<p>
		In this case, the attachment order passed by the ED is well after the date of commencement of CIRP. Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT Chennai Bench holds that: (i) The attachment made is as per the ingredients of the provisions of the PMLA Act, 2002 and that the same is to be dealt with under the relevant provisions of the said act only. The Code only pertains to the questions concerning CIRP &amp; Liquidation Proceedings of the Corporate Debtor. (ii) The concept of &lsquo;Attachment&rsquo; made as per Section 5 (1) of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 cannot be a subject matter of proceedings under Section 60(5) of the code, in a way making it clear that this Adjudicating Authority is not the right &lsquo;FORA&rsquo; to deal with revocation of attachment made under the PMLA, Act, 2002. (iii) Thereby, making it obvious that a remedy under PMLA act cannot be claimed before this Adjudicating Authority under the IBC, 2016 Code.
	</p>

						<strong>
							 Brief about the decision:
						</strong>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The Corporate Debtor was admitted into Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process(CIRP) vide order dated
							<strong>
								 23.04.2018
							</strong>
							 . The provisional attachment were made vide Order dated
							<strong>
								 31.07.2018
							</strong>
							 by the Directorate of Enforcement(ED) after commencement of CIRP of the Corporate Debtor. The period of moratorium kicks in right away once the CIRP&nbsp;is initiated.
						</li>
						<li>
							In the instant matter, the attachment order passed by the ED is well after the date of commencement of CIRP in a way making it obvious that the ED has acted upon the assets belonging to the Corporate Debtor during the moratorium period.
						</li>
						<li>
							On perusal of the attachment order dated 31.07.2018 it is understood that the order is passed under Section 5 (1) of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002, which by itself has a set of stipulated rules and regulations governing appeals against such attachment orders before the Appellate Tribunal, (PMLA).
						</li>
						<li>
							Moreover, from the submission made by the applicant, it is brought to the knowledge of this Adjudicating Authority that the Resolution Professional of the Corporate Debtor has already preferred an appeal before the Appellate Authority and the same is still pending. Considering the prevailing situation of the Corporate Debtor and the actions taken by the RP/Liquidator, it is seen that nothing herein bothers the Applicant/Liquidator to proceed in the chosen way before the Hon&rsquo;ble Appellate Tribunal (PMLA) to lift the provisional attachment.
						</li>
						<li>
							Thus, it is quite evident that
							<strong>
								 the attachment made is as per the ingredients of the provisions of the PMLA Act, 2002 and that the same is to be dealt with under the relevant provisions of the said act only.
							</strong>
							 Moreover, it is time and again retreated by the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT that the Code only pertains to the questions concerning CIRP&nbsp;&amp; Liquidation Proceedings of the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							Keeping that in mind,
							<strong>
								 the concept of
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 &lsquo;Attachment&rsquo; made as per Section 5 (1) of the Prevention of Money&nbsp;Laundering Act, 2002 cannot be a subject matter of proceedings&nbsp;under Section 60(5) of the code, in a way making it clear that this&nbsp;Adjudicating Authority is not the right &lsquo;FORA&rsquo; to deal with&nbsp;revocation of attachment made under the PMLA, Act, 2002. Thereby,&nbsp;making it obvious that a remedy under PMLA act cannot be claimed&nbsp;before this Adjudicating Authority under the IBC, 2016 Code.
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							Accordingly, in terms of the instant facts and circumstances, we deem it fit to dismiss the application as not maintainable in law and facts.
							<strong>
								 (p23-27)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
