					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority had already granted extension of time for a period 30 days which has expired. Further extension of time has been sought. Section 12(3) of the Code empowers the Adjudicating Authority to extend the duration of CIRP beyond 180 days for such further period not exceeding 90 days. The proviso to said p, however, provides that extension of the period of CIRP shall not be granted more than once. The issue, therefore, is whether extension can be granted for second time where the extension has been granted earlier for 30 days. The Adjudicating Authority held that the language of p 12(3) speaks the intention of legislature. The provision cannot take away the effect of the main provision or to remove any doubt in relation to its implementation. If another extension is not granted beyond the extension of 30 days, it will render p 12(3) of the Code otiose. Accordingly, the Adjudicating Authority granted extension for a further period of sixty days.
