		<h1>
			Whether there is exemption of compliances under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 to the corporate debtor undergoing CIRP process &ndash; Bohra Industries Ltd. through its RP Vs. National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Jaipur Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			October 22, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority observed that it is to be seen that the fine was levied for the delay in making certain compliances. In view of the fact that due to the failure of the Board of Directors of the corporate debtor in managing the company in a proper manner, the company went into the CIRP process. After the company was taken over by the applicant Resolution Professional, certain compliances have been made, may be belatedly and with regard to other compliances, the Resolution Professional explained the impediments being faced by him. In view of the moratorium declared in terms of Section 14 of the Code and also in view of the fact of making certain compliances subsequently and also in view of the fact that the Resolution Professional is facing certain reasonable impediments with regard to certain other compliances, the impugned order of National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. is unsustainable. Burdening the corporate debtor with imposition of fines further is against to the interest of the corporate debtor and also against to the object of the Code.
		</p>
	</div>

							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Facts of the case
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							This application has been filed by the Resolution Professional of&nbsp;Bohra Industries Limited i.e., the Corporate Debtor against the National&nbsp;Stock Exchange of India Ltd. under Section 14 read with Section 60(5) of&nbsp;IBC, 2016 seeking to quash and set aside letter dated&nbsp;23.06.2020 of the respondent National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. In spite of the fact that the corporate debtor is undergoing&nbsp;CIRP, the respondent vide its letter dated 12.06.2020 levied fine on the&nbsp;corporate debtor for the delay in making certain compliances in terms of&nbsp;Regulation 7(3) of SEBI (Listing Obligations &amp; Disclosure Requirements)&nbsp;Regulations, 2015.
						</p>
						<p>
							The National Stock&nbsp;Exchange of India Ltd. not agreed with the Resolution Professional with&nbsp;regard to delay in submission of shareholding pattern for quarter ending&nbsp;30.09.2019 on the ground that as per Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, every listed entity is required to submit shareholding&nbsp;pattern within 21 days from end of the quarter and there was no exemption&nbsp;to the corporate debtor even though the corporate debtor was under CIRP&nbsp;process. Same reasoning was given by the National Stock Exchange of&nbsp;India Ltd. for non-compliance with regard to the prior intimation of the Board&nbsp;meeting for considering the financial results.
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that it is to be seen that the fine was levied for the delay in making&nbsp;certain compliances. In view of the fact that due to the failure of the Board of&nbsp;Directors of the corporate debtor in managing the company in a proper&nbsp;manner, the company went into the CIRP process. After the company was&nbsp;taken over by the applicant Resolution Professional, certain compliances&nbsp;have been made, may be belatedly and with regard to other compliances,&nbsp;the Resolution Professional explained the impediments being faced by him.&nbsp;In view of the moratorium declared in terms of Section 14 of the&nbsp;Code and also in view of the fact of making certain compliances&nbsp;subsequently and also in view of the fact that the Resolution Professional is&nbsp;facing certain reasonable impediments with regard to certain other&nbsp;compliances, the impugned order of National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. is&nbsp;unsustainable. Burdening the corporate debtor with imposition of fines&nbsp;further is against to the interest of the corporate debtor and also against to&nbsp;the object of the Code.&nbsp;In the circumstances and for the above reasons, the instant I.A.&nbsp;is allowed and the imposition of fine on the corporate debtor for delay in&nbsp;making the subject compliances is condoned. However, either the R.P., if&nbsp;impediments are removed or the Successful Resolution Applicant in the event of approval of the Plan, shall act in accordance with law in complying&nbsp;with the required compliances.&nbsp;Accordingly, I.A. No.208/JPR/2020 is disposed of.&nbsp;
