	<h1>
		Whether a joint petition u/s 9 of IBC by more than one individual/employee can be maintained if individually they do not fulfill the threshold limit of Rs. 1 crore &ndash; Mr. Brajesh Mishra and Others Vs. Dolphin Offshore Shipping Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		November 17, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		In Section 9 application, NCLT Mumbai Bench held that: (i) In this context a reference can, be made to the law laid down in Sadashiv Nomaya Nayak &amp; Ors. Vs. Gammon Engineers &amp; Contractors Pvt. Ltd. (2023) 455 NCLAT, whereby it has been held that if an individual claim of each of the Operational Creditor, the amount of debt is less than rupees one lakh (as the threshold limit was at that time), it can be rejected being not maintainable. (ii) It has to be held that the present Petition is not maintainable as the individual claim of each of the joint Petitioners do not meet the threshold limit of Rs. 1 crore. (iii) Dismissed.
	</p>

							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							This Petition has been filed u/s 9 of the Code, 2016 jointly by as many as 23 Petitioners through their authorized representative Mr. Brijesh Mishra for total claimed amount in respect of all the Operational Creditors is Rs. 2,86,11,281/-.
						</li>
						<li>
							The none of the Petitioners individually has a claim of more than Rs. 1 crore.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Operational Creditors are the employees of M/s&nbsp;Dolphin Offshore Shipping Ltd. and some of them are still&nbsp;serving the company. However, some of them have already&nbsp;resigned from their jobs. The Corporate Debtors have not&nbsp;paid the legal dues of the Operational Creditor.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Issue
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<p>
						Whether a joint Petition u/s 9 by more than one individual can be maintained if individually they do not fulfill the threshold limit of Rs. 1 crore.
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							In this context a reference can, be made to the law laid&nbsp;down in
							<em>
								 Sadashiv Nomaya Nayak &amp; Ors. Vs. Gammon Engineers &amp; Contractors Pvt. Ltd.&nbsp;(2023) 455 NCLAT
							</em>
							 , whereby it has been held that if an individual claim of each of the Operational Creditor, the amount of debt is less than rupees one lakh (as the threshold limit was at that time), it can be rejected being not maintainable.
						</li>
						<li>
							In the light of the above brief discussion and the law laid down on the above cited cases, it has to be held that the present Petition is not maintainable as the individual claim of each of the joint Petitioners do not meet the threshold limit of Rs. 1 crore.
						</li>
						<li>
							Resultantly, the present Company Petition is
							<strong>
								 dismissed
							</strong>
							 with no order as to costs.
							<strong>
								 (p10-12)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
