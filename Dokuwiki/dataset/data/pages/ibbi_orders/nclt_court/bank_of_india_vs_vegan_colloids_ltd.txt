	<h1>
		Damages claim of EPFO pertaining to the period of moratorium cannot be entertained in terms of provision of IBC, 2016 &ndash; Bank of India Vs. Vegan Colloids Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Chandigarh Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		October 9, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		In this case, Liquidator has raised objections regarding the continuation of proceedings before the EPFO Authority in view of the moratorium in terms of Section 33(5) of IBC being in force by NCLT Chandigarh Bench order dated 10.10.2018. Ignoring the objections made, the EPFO Authority have ordered levy of damages for belated remittance made during the period 21.01.2016 to 31.03.2020 and Rs.11,18,184/- was directed to be deposited within 15 days under Section 14(b) and in another order under Section 17(q) an amount of Rs.5,71,768/- was demanded. NCLT Chandigarh Bench referred Section 238 of IBC and held that: (i) The EPFO Authorities have not filed their claim during the period of CIRP and also during the period of liquidation. The EPFO Authorities has failed to take steps to ensure compliance with the timeline provided under IB Code. (ii) We do not find any good ground or reason to entertain the claim at this stage of dissolution, as liquidator has already sold asset and distributed it among the stakeholders. (iii) However, the EPFO Authorities are entitled to pursue their claims, if permitted by law. (iv) Te claims of EPFO pertaining to the period of moratorium cannot be entertained in terms of provision of IB Code, 2016.
	</p>

					<p>
						Heard the submissions made by the learned counsel for the Liquidator as well as counsel for the EPFO, the order dated 30.03.2022 shows that the proceedings under Section 14(b) &amp; 7Q of Employees Provision Funds and Miscellaneous Provision Act, 1952 was initiated for levy of damages for the period 21.01.2016 to 31.03.2020. It transpires from the submissions of learned counsel for the liquidator that Liquidator has raised objections regarding the continuation of proceedings before the EPFO Authority in view of the moratorium in terms of Section 33(5) of IB Code being in force by NCLT Chandigarh Bench order dated 10.10.2018. Ignoring the objections made, the EPFO Authority have ordered levy of damages for belated remittance made during the period 21.01.2016 to 31.03.2020 and Rs.11,18,184/- was directed to be deposited within 15 days under Section 14(b) and in another order under Section 17(q) an amount of Rs.5,71,768/- was demanded. In this matter, Sh. Mohak Sharma, Advocate appeared on behalf of the Liquidator made submissions which transpires that the EPFO Authorities have not given due weightage to the provisions of IBC and only relied upon on the provisions of the Employees Provision Funds and Miscellaneous Provision Act, 1952. The counsel for the Liquidator also submitted that the Books of Accounts of the petitioner were not showing arrears of provident fund.
					</p>
					<p>
						This Adjudicating Authority is of the view that Section 238 of IB Code clearly provides that the provisions of the Code shall have affect notwithstanding anything in consistent therewith contained in any other law for the time being in force or any instrument having effect by virtue of any such law which means the provisions of IBC will have overriding effect of all other laws and also the preamble of IB Code clearly says that alteration in the order of priority of payment of Government dues is one of the changes which IBC has brought in the present matter. The EPFO Authorities have not filed their claim during the period of CIRP and also during the period of liquidation. The EPFO Authorities has failed to take steps to ensure compliance with the timeline provided under IB Code. We do not find any good ground or reason to entertain the claim at this stage of dissolution, as liquidator has already sold asset and distributed it among the stakeholders. However, the EPFO Authorities are entitled to pursue their claims, if permitted by law. The counsel for the EPFO Authorities has also referred various decisions in favour of EPFO Authorities. However keeping in view the stage of the matter, this Tribunal is of the view that the claims of EPFO pertaining to the period of moratorium cannot be entertained in terms of provision of IB Code, 2016.
					</p>
					<p>
						Thus, application bearing IA No. 622/2022 stands disposed of.
