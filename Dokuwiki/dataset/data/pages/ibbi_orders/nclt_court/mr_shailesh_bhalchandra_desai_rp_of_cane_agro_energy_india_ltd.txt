	<h1>
		There is no bar in Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (IBC) to proceed against each separate Personal Guarantor for same debt | Pendency of Resolution Plan for approval of NCLT u/s 31 of IBC even in which payment of entire debt has been considered cannot be a ground for dismissal of insolvency proceedings against Personal Guarantor u/s 95 of IBC &ndash; Mr. Shailesh Bhalchandra Desai &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		February 10, 2024
	</div>
	<p>
		In Section 95 application, Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT Mumbai Bench holds that there is no bar in Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (IBC) to proceed against each separate Personal Guarantor for same debt. Moreover, pendency of CIRP (Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process) against the Principal Borrower does not debar lender from proceeding against Personal Guarantor. Additionally, pendency of Resolution Plan approval also cannot be a ground for dismissal of proceedings against the Personal Guarantor. However, Hon&rsquo;ble Bench rejects the application on the ground of period of limitation.
	</p>

								 Facts of the case
							</strong>

					<ul>
						<li>
							The Present Application is filed under Section 95 of the IBC, 2016 by Bank of Baroda (Financial Creditors) for the purpose of initiating insolvency process against Mr. Amir Jamal Dodhia (Personal Guarantor/Respondent) for recovery of Rs.50,14,15,575 as on 31.10.2020 plus unapplied interest w.e.f. 01.11.2020 and other relevant charges till the date of full and final payment.
						</li>
						<li>
							The date of default is stated to be
							<strong>
								 10.02.2018
							</strong>
							 . The Financial Creditor issued notice on
							<strong>
								 12.12.2017
							</strong>
							 under p 13(2) of the SARFAESI Act,2002 invoking the guarantee and calling upon the Guarantor to pay the said amount within 60 days.&nbsp;The Financial Creditor issued another demand notice on
							<strong>
								 31.10.2020
							</strong>
							 addressed to&nbsp;the Principal Borrower and Personal Guarantor and is relying on this notice for&nbsp;limitation.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Corporate Debtor was brought under CIRP under Section 7 of the IBC vide order dated 25.09.2019.
						</li>
						<li>
							Contention of the Personal Guarantor that the Applicant has filed the Application and seeking relief against the&nbsp;Respondent in respect of the loan already paid off/ settled of the principal&nbsp;borrower i.e. Calchem Industries (India) Limited, under the Resolution Plan&nbsp;(which is pending approval), will be considered as payment of the entire debt&nbsp;due and payable by the borrower under the loan transaction facilities granted by&nbsp;Bank of Baroda to the Borrower i.e. Calchem Industries ( India ) Limited, and&nbsp;there is no cause of action in favour of the Applicant against this Respondent.
						</li>
					</ul>
