		<h1>
			NCLT condones the delay of 16 days in filing of claim in liquidation process &ndash; Commissioner Department of Trade and Taxes Government of NCT of Delhi Vs. Mahesh Sureka &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			December 29, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The present application filed by the Commissioner Department of Trade and Taxes Government of NCT, Delhi under Section 60 (5) of Code&nbsp;read with Rule 11 of the NCLT Rules, 2016 seeking condonation of delay in filing the claim before the Liquidator. The Liquidator present in person and submits that there is a delay of 16 days in filing the claim. He further submits that he has no objection if delay is condoned. This Bench having considered the value of the claim, deems it fit and proper to condone the delay of 16 days. The Liquidator is directed to verify the claim in terms of waterfall mechanism prescribed under Section 53 of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.&nbsp;
		</p>
