		<h1>
			Whether the WhatsApp conversation between the parties can be admitted as evidence &ndash; Satya Sadasiva Basava Prasad Maley Vs. Pattela Projects Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Amaravati Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			February 24, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			The points that arise for consideration are: 1. Whether this application is maintainable and whether it is within the pecuniary jurisdiction of this Tribunal. 2. Whether the WhatsApp conversation between the Parties can be admitted as evidence. 3. Whether the Petitioner can be termed as Financial Creditor and whether the amount i.e., admittedly received by the Corporate Debtor can be termed as financial debt within the meaning of Section 5 (8) of IBC.
