						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							This is an application filed by the Liquidator, seeking leave of this Adjudicating Authority in terms of proviso to p 33(5) of the Code, for filing an application/petition challenging the impugned arbitration award dated 13.06.2019, under p 34 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
						</p>
						<p>
							It is stated that the liquidator came to learn about the said Arbitral Award passed by the Sole Arbitrator in the arbitration proceedings between one Ascot Realty Private Limited and the Corporate Debtor herein, when a claim was lodged with the liquidator. After examining the claim in consultation with his legal advisor, the liquidator is of the opinion that there is a good basis for challenging the impugned arbitral award.
						</p>
						<p>
							The time for challenging the arbitral award has expired in the normal course. The Liquidator seeks to take recourse to the order dated 10th January, 2022 in Suo Moto Writ proceedings being SMW (C) No. 3 of 2020, excluding the period of limitation falling between 15.03.2020 and 28.02.2022, and further requesting for extending period, in respect of any legal proceeding for a further period of 90 days startingfrom01.03.2022. Thus, the extended period of limitation in the light of the Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court&rsquo;s Order, in the said Suo Moto Writ proceedings will expireon30.05.2022. Considering the reasons adduced in the application, we deem it proper to grant leave to the Liquidator to file appropriate applications/petitions under p 34 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
						</p>
