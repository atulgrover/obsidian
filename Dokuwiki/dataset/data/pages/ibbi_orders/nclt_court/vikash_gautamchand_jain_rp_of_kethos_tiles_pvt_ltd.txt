<p>
	<strong>
		Brief about the decision:
	</strong>
</p>
<p>
	<em>
		<strong>
			Facts of the case
		</strong>
	</em>
</p>
<ul>
	<li>
		The Adjudicating Authority vide its order dated&nbsp;
		<strong>
			04.01.2024
		</strong>
		&nbsp;admitted M/s. Kethos Tiles Private Limited (Corporate Debtor) under Section 54C of the IBC, 2016 r.w. Rule 4 of Insolvency and Bankruptcy (Pre-Packaged Insolvency Resolution Process) Rules, 2021.
	</li>
	<li>
		While base resolution plan submitted by the Corporate Debtor is under consideration, the CoC noted that the period of PPIRP comes to an end on expiry of 120 days from the date of admission of PPIRP on 03.05.2024 and the CoC requested the RP to file an application seeking extension of PPRIP period of 60 days.
	</li>
	<li>
		The resolution for seeking extension of PPIRP period was passed with 91.75% voting on 02.05.2024 by E-voting.&nbsp;
	</li>
	<li>
		The Applicant has filed this application seeking extension of time of PPIRP period by 60 days after the date of completion of PPIRP (120 days) i.e., on 03.05.2024.
	</li>
</ul>
<p>
	<em>
		<strong>
			Decision of Adjudicating Authority
		</strong>
	</em>
</p>
<ul>
	<li>
		A plain reading of Section 54D of Insolvency &amp; Bankruptcy Code, 2016 reveals that a time period of 120 days from the date of commencement of PPIRP is&nbsp;provided in the Act. As per Section 54D(3), if no&nbsp;Resolution Plan is approved by CoC the RP shall file&nbsp;an Application for Termination of PPIRP. Contrary to&nbsp;the Section Resolution Professional in the present&nbsp;matter has filed an Application seeking extension of&nbsp;time.
	</li>
	<li>
		It is seen that the Counsel for the Applicant relied upon Section 12 of the IBC,2016 to be applied in the PPIRP matters. The Sections which shall be applies to Chapter III-A governing PPIRP mutatis mutandis are stated through Section 54P.
	</li>
	<li>
		The Resolution Professional has not complied with the direction given under the provisions of Section 54D and 54K (11) (12).
	</li>
	<li>
		In view of the above findings and as no resolution Plan has been approved within the specific time&nbsp;period which is 120 days, we are constrained to order&nbsp;Termination of Pre-Packaged Insolvency Resolution&nbsp;Process of the Corporate Debtor initiated by the order&nbsp;dated 04.01.2024.
	</li>
	<li>
		It is also seen that the RP has misled the Tribunal and has not performed the duties as mandated under the code.&nbsp;The Registry of this Tribunal is directed to send a copy of this order to IBBI and the Indian Institute of Insolvency Professionals of ICAI, the IPA of the RP for taking necessary action in this regard.
	</li>
	<li>
		In view of the above observations and averments, the present Application is&nbsp;
		<strong>
			Rejected
		</strong>
		, PPIRP initiated vide order dated 04.01.2024 is hereby terminated and Corporate Debtor is released from rigor of law. Hence IA/769(AHM)2024 is rejected and C.P.(IB) &amp; Pre-Packaged)/1(AHM)2023 is dismissed accordingly.
	</li>
</ul>
