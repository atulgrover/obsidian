		<h1>
			The Demand Notice to the Personal Guarantor is required mandatorily under Section 95(4)(b) of IBC 2016 read with Rule 7 (1) of the AAAIRPPG&nbsp;Rules, 2019 &ndash; State Bank of India Vs. Ms. Jaya Singh (Personal Guarantor of M/s. Energo Engineering Projects Ltd.) &ndash; NCLT New Delhi Bench Court-II
		</h1>
		<div>
			September 9, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			NCLT held that since the Applicant has not served the Demand Notice to the Personal Guarantor, as required mandatorily under Section 95(4)(b) of IBC 2016 read with Rule 7 (1) of the AAAIRPPG Rules, 2019, we are not inclined to proceed further in the matter. Further, the date of NPA has also not been disclosed by the Applicant. Overall, the Application lacks material pleadings as to how the debt has become due and payable. That we are constrained to observe that the Application has been filed in a casual manner by the Applicant Bank. The Applicant should have been diligent enough in filing the Application under Section 95 of IBC 2016, since under this p the moment the Application is filed, interim moratorium under Section 96(1)(a) of IBC 2016 gets triggered, which could cause prejudice to other creditors of the Corporate Debtor. The Petition is Dismissed.
		</p>
	</div>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							Under consideration is the Application preferred by State Bank of&nbsp;India (the Applicant/Financial Creditor), under Section 95(1) read&nbsp;with Rule 7(2) of the AAAIRPPG&nbsp;Rules, 2019. The Application is filed by the SBI for initiating the&nbsp;Insolvency Resolution Process against&nbsp;Personal Guarantor of&nbsp;the Corporate&nbsp;Debtor.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that it is worthwhile to refer to the contents of the Rule 7 (1) of the AAAIRPPG Rules, 2019, which reads as&nbsp;below:
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								&ldquo;7. Application by creditor.- (1) A demand notice&nbsp;under clause (b) of sub-p (4) of p 95 shall be&nbsp;served on the guarantor demanding payment of the&nbsp;amount of default, in Form B.&rdquo;
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							Since the Applicant has not served the Demand Notice to the Personal&nbsp;Guarantor, as required mandatorily under Section 95(4)(b) of IBC 2016&nbsp;read with Rule 7 (1) of the AAAIRPPG&nbsp;Rules, 2019, we are not inclined to proceed further in the matter.&nbsp;That we also observe that the facts in the synopsis written at Page&nbsp;1-2 of the Application are at variance with the facts mentioned in the Part&nbsp;III of the Application. Further, the date of NPA has also not been disclosed&nbsp;by the Applicant. Overall, the Application lacks material pleadings as to&nbsp;how the debt has become due and payable.&nbsp;That we are constrained to observe that the Application has been&nbsp;filed in a casual manner by the Applicant Bank. The Applicant should have&nbsp;been diligent enough in filing the Application under Section 95 of IBC 2016,&nbsp;since under this p the moment the Application is filed, interim&nbsp;moratorium under Section 96(1)(a) of IBC 2016 gets triggered, which could&nbsp;cause prejudice to other creditors of the Corporate Debtor. The Petition&nbsp;is Dismissed.
							<strong>
								(p10-13)
							</strong>
						</p>
