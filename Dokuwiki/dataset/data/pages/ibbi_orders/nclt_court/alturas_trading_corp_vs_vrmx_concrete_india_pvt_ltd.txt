		<h1>
			Whether the Del Credere Agent is an Operational Creditor under the Code &ndash; M/s. Alturas Trading Corp.&nbsp;Vs. M/s VRMX Concrete India Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Chennai Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			November 17, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			In commercial parlance, a Del credere Agent is a person who is engaged by the Principal to assist in supply of goods or services by contacting potential buyers on behalf of the Principal. The difference between the other Agent and the Del credere Agent is that the Del credere Agent guarantees to make the payment to the Principal even if the buyer fails to make payment to the Principal before the due date while ordinary agent does not guarantee recovery of dues from Debtor. The Del credere Agent is incentivised in ensuring that the Credit / Sale is effected to the parties who are financially capable of borrowing the payment. Accordingly in the event of inability to recover the sale proceeds, the Del Credere Agent is liable to compensate the Principal.
		</p>
	</div>

							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that a perusal of the Agreement reveals the fact that&nbsp;the Operational Creditor herein is acting as an Agent for the&nbsp;Principal Company namely, M/s. Bharat Cement Corporation&nbsp;Private Limited and as such as per the terms of the Agreement, the&nbsp;alleged Operational Creditor herein would be entitled to certain&nbsp;commission only upon realization of the sale proceeds from the&nbsp;parties. In commercial parlance, a Del credere Agent is a person&nbsp;who is engaged by the Principal to assist in supply of goods or&nbsp;services by contacting potential buyers on behalf of the Principal.&nbsp;The difference between the other Agent and the Del credere Agent&nbsp;is that the Del credere Agent guarantees to make the payment to&nbsp;the Principal even if the buyer fails to make payment to the&nbsp;Principal before the due date while ordinary agent does not&nbsp;guarantee recovery of dues from Debtor. The Del credere Agent is&nbsp;incentivised in ensuring that the Credit / Sale is effected to the&nbsp;parties who are financially capable of borrowing the payment.&nbsp;Accordingly in the event of inability to recover the sale proceeds,&nbsp;the Del Credere Agent is liable to compensate the Principal.
						</p>
						<p>
							From the Agreement entered into between the Operational&nbsp;Creditor and M/s. Bharat Cement Corporation Private Limited,&nbsp;there is no nexus between the Operational Creditor and the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor herein. Also, as per the averments made in the&nbsp;Application, it is seen that the Invoices are raised by M/s. Bharat&nbsp;Cement Corporation Private Limited against the Corporate Debtor&nbsp;and not by the Operational Creditor and under the said&nbsp;circumstances, the Applicant herein cannot be considered as an&nbsp;Operational Creditor in relation to the Corporate Debtor. Further,&nbsp;the goods have been supplied by the M/s. Bharat Cement&nbsp;Corporation Private Limited and the Operational Creditor is acting&nbsp;only as an Agent.
