		<h1>
			Each Financial Creditors can file separate CIRP application under Section 7 of IBC. The filing of the application under p 7 of the IBC has not in any way restrained or restricted the right of a Financial Creditor in filing any similar petition under p 7 of the IBC seeking CIRP of the same Corporate Debtor &ndash; West Bengal Financial Corporation Vs. Brahm (Alloys) Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			June 16, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			Financial Creditor PNB has filed the present CP, being one of the Financial Creditors of the Corporate Debtor in its own right, available to it under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016. Similarly, the applicant West Bengal Financial Corporation, the applicant herein may also be one of the Financial Creditors independently. The applicant, West Bengal Financial Corporation has challenged right of the Financial Creditor to file the present C.P. for the reason that the applicant has the first charge over the properties and assets of the Corporate Debtor. The Adjudicating Authority held that the IBC Code provides equal rights to all the Financial Creditors to proceed against Corporate Debtor and if any of the application is admitted, the others can always submit their claims before the IRP/RP appointed in the matter to claim their debts as per the law. Once a Corporate Debtor is in default, and put under CIRP, the rights of the Financial Creditors would be ascertained on the basis of their status whether they are secured creditors or unsecured creditors. If any one of the them claims to be a secured creditor holding security in the shape of assets of the Corporate Debtor, he would have the right either to dispose of the security and claim its debts, or to relinquish its security and claim its debts along with other Financial Creditors.
		</p>
	</div>
</p>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The applicant, West Bengal Financial Corporation has challenged right of the Financial Creditor to file the present C.P.(IB) No. 118 /KB/2021 for the reason that the applicant has the first charge over the properties and assets of the Corporate Debtor. It may be mentioned that p 5(7) defines &ldquo;Financial Creditor, to mean that any person to whom a financial debt is owed and includes a person to whom such debt has been legally assigned or transferred to&rdquo;. Financial Creditor Punjab National Bank has filed the present CP, being one of the Financial Creditors of the Corporate Debtor in its own right, available to it under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016. Similarly, the applicant West Bengal Financial Corporation, the applicant herein may also be one of the Financial Creditors independently. It may also file its own proceedings before any authorities including the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court where ever it has a right to sue the Corporate Debtor but to say that the Financial Creditor does not have any right to proceed against the Corporate Debtor under the provisions of the Code, 2016, is a plea in the present application which has to be rejected in limine.&nbsp;
							<strong>
								The IBC Code provides equal rights to all the Financial Creditors to proceed against Corporate Debtor and if any of the application is admitted, the others can always submit their claims before the IRP/RP appointed in the matter to claim their debts as per the law. Once a Corporate Debtor is in default, and put under CIRP, the rights of the Financial Creditors would be ascertained on the basis of their status whether they are secured creditors or unsecured creditors. If any one of the them claims to be a secured creditor holding security in the shape of assets of the Corporate Debtor, he would have the right either to dispose of the security and claim its debts, or to relinquish its security and claim its debts along with other Financial
							</strong>
							&nbsp;
							<strong>
								Creditors.(p22)
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							In the present case only because the applicant had fought certain litigation with the Corporate Debtor, in which certain orders had been passed by the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court of Calcutta, pursuant to which, some part payment is stated to have been received by the applicant, and there is some balance amount to be received by the applicant, this Adjudicating Authority cannot entertain a plea from the applicant that the petition under p 7 of the Code filed by the Financial Creditor against the Corporate Debtor is collusive one.&nbsp;
							<strong>
								The filing of the application under p 7 of the Code has not in any way restrained or restricted the right of the applicant in filing any similar petition under p 7 of the Code before this Adjudicating Authority seeking CIRP of the Corporate Debtor on the basis of the financial debt due to the applicant from the Corporate Debtor.(p23)
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							With these observations and directions, the present IVN.P.(IBC)/6/2021 is dismissed being devoid of any substance or merits. The applicant may avail all its remedies either before this Adjudicating Authority or any other forum if and when it chooses to do so.&nbsp;
							<strong>
								(p24)
							</strong>
						</p>
