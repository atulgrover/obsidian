		<h1>
			Concept of Going Concern Sale under IBC to Sale of &ldquo;Corporate Debtor as a going concern&rdquo; under&nbsp;Regulation 32(e) and sale of &ldquo;Business of Corporate Debtor as a&nbsp;going concern&rdquo; under Regulation 32(f) &ndash; M.S. Viswanathan Vs. Pixtronic Global Technologies Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Chennai Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			March 22, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			There are two going concern sales defined under Regulation 32 of IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016. The first one pertains to Sale of "Corporate Debtor as a going concern" under Regulation 32(e) and sale of "Business of Corporate Debtor as a going concern" under Regulation 32(f).

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Liquidator has already sold the Corporate Debtor as a going&nbsp;concern to the Respondent and has filed this application seeking&nbsp;approval of the same.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority explains the Going Concern concept as:
						</p>
						<ul>
							<li>
								In so far as Sale as a going concern is concerned, the term&nbsp;&lsquo;going concern&rsquo; means all such assets and the liabilities, which&nbsp;constitute an integral business or the Corporate Debtor, that must&nbsp;be transferred together, and the consideration must be for the&nbsp;business or the Corporate Debtor. The buyer of the assets and&nbsp;liabilities should be able to run business without any disruption.
							</li>
							<li>
								There are two going concern sales defined under Regulation&nbsp;32 of IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016. The first one&nbsp;pertains to Sale of &ldquo;
								<em>
									Corporate Debtor as a going concern
								</em>
								&rdquo; under&nbsp;Regulation 32(e) and sale of &ldquo;
								<em>
									Business of Corporate Debtor as a&nbsp;
								</em>
								<em>
									going concern
								</em>
								&rdquo; under Regulation 32(f).
							</li>
							<li>
								In the sale of &ldquo;
								<em>
									Corporate Debtor as a going concern
								</em>
								&rdquo; under&nbsp;Regulation 32(e) of IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016 the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor will not be dissolved. In this part of sale, the entire&nbsp;business, assets and liabilities, including all contracts, licenses,&nbsp;concessions, agreements, benefits, privileges, rights or interests of&nbsp;the Corporate Debtor will be transferred to the acquirer. The existing the Corporate Debtor will be transferred to the acquirer. The existing&nbsp;shares of the Corporate Debtor will not be transferred and shall be&nbsp;extinguished.
							</li>
							<li>
								In the sale of &ldquo;
								<em>
									Business of Corporate Debtor as a going&nbsp;
								</em>
								<em>
									concern
								</em>
								&rdquo; under Regulation 32(f) of IBBI (Liquidation Process)&nbsp;Regulations, 2016, the entire business(s) along with assets and liabilities, including intangibles, will be transferred as a going&nbsp;concern to the acquirer,&nbsp;without transfer of the Corporate Debtor,&nbsp;and therefore, the Corporate Debtor will be dissolved. The existing&nbsp;shares will be extinguished. The remaining assets, other than those&nbsp;sold as part of business will be sold and the proceeds thereof will be&nbsp;used to meet the claims under Section 53 of IBC, 2016
							</li>
							<li>
								Sale of a Company as a &lsquo;Going Concern&rsquo; means sale of both&nbsp;assets and liabilities, if it is stated on &lsquo;as is where is basis&rsquo;. The&nbsp;Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT in the matter of&nbsp;
								<em>
									M/s. Visisth Services Ltd. Vs. Mr.&nbsp;
								</em>
								<em>
									S. V. Ramani, Liquidator of United Chloro-paraffins Pvt.&nbsp;
								</em>
								<em>
									Ltd.&nbsp;(2022)  33 NCLAT&nbsp;
								</em>
								held that as per&nbsp;Regulation 32A of the IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016,&nbsp;the Sale as a &lsquo;Going Concern&rsquo; means sale of assets as well as&nbsp;liabilities and not assets sans liabilities. We conclude that Sale of a&nbsp;Company as a &lsquo;Going Concern&rsquo; means sale of both assets and&nbsp;liabilities, if it is stated on &lsquo;as is where is basis&rsquo;.&nbsp;
								<strong>
									(p11-15)
								</strong>
							</li>
						</ul>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority holds that the present Application has been filed under Regulation 32(e)&nbsp;of IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016 seeking approval for&nbsp;sale of the &ldquo;Corporate Debtor as a going concern&rdquo;. Apart from the&nbsp;above, the Liquidator has also sought for certain relief and&nbsp;concession.&nbsp;Thus, the Application filed by the Liquidator for sale of&nbsp;&ldquo;Corporate Debtor as a going concern&rdquo; stands allowed. The Liquidator, after distributing the proceeds to the stakeholders as per&nbsp;Section 53 of IBC, 2016 may file an Application under Regulation&nbsp;45(3) of the IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016 for closure&nbsp;of Liquidation process.
							<strong>
								(p16-17)
							</strong>
						</p>
