		<h1>
			NCLT allows belated claim of GST condoning a delay of around 452 days of submitting the claim &ndash; Joint Commissioner of Commercial Taxes &amp; GST, Odisha Vs. Mr. Manish Jain RP of Krishna Ferro Products Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Cuttack Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			November 27, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			In this case, The Applicant had lodged its claim with the RP on 15.01.2021 when there was a delay of around 452 days of submitting the claim, the Respondent/RP has refused to consider the same on the grounds of delay as the same was submitted beyond the cut-off date. The Adjudicating Authority held that in view of the facts and circumstances of the case, Covid-19 pandemic and corresponding lockdowns etc. and more particularly in the interest of revenue of the Exchequer, as a special case this Adjudicating Authority is inclined to allow this appeal and condoned the delay of 452 days in lodging the claim by the Applicant, namely, Joint Commissioner of Commercial Taxes &amp; G.S.T., C.T. &amp; G.G.T. Circle, Rourkela-Il, Odisha.
		</p>
	</div>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							In this case, The Applicant had lodged its claim with the RP on&nbsp;
							<strong>
								15.01.2021
							</strong>
							&nbsp;when there was a delay of around 452 days of submitting the claim, the Respondent/RP has refused to consider the same on the grounds of delay as the same was submitted beyond the cut-off date. The RP has&nbsp;earlier issued the Public Announcement inviting claims on 10.10.2019 fixing&nbsp;the last date of submission as 18.10.2019 and 90 days therefrom expired on&nbsp;
							<strong>
								02.01.2020
							</strong>
							. Applicant states that the said Public Announcement dated&nbsp;10.10.2019 was not within the knowledge of the applicant in spite of his&nbsp;repeated pursuance with the recovery of arrear tax demand from the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor. Further, Shutdown and Lockdown was declared&nbsp;throughout India including in the State of Odisha on account of COVID-19&nbsp;pandemic since 23.03.2020. Owing to such restrictions, the normal&nbsp;functioning of the offices of the applicant has also been affected. Such factor&nbsp;has led to the present proceeding under the IBC not coming within the&nbsp;knowledge of the applicant.&nbsp;
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority held that in view of the facts and circumstances of the case, Covid-19 pandemic and&nbsp;corresponding lockdowns etc. and more particularly in the interest of revenue of the Exchequer, as a special case this Adjudicating Authority is inclined to&nbsp;allow this appeal and condoned the delay of 452 days in lodging the claim by&nbsp;the Applicant, namely, Joint Commissioner of Commercial Taxes &amp; G.S.T.,&nbsp;C.T. &amp; G.G.T. Circle, Rourkela-Il, Odisha.
						</p>
