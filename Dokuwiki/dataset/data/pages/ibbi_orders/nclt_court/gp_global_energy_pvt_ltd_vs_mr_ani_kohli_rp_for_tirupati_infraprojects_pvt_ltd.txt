		<h1>
			Adjudicating Authority cannot maintain any application for modification or implementation of any condition post approval of a Resolution Plan &ndash; G.P. Global Energy Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Mr. Ani Kohli RP for Tirupati Infraprojects Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Principal Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			November 28, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The AA held that the first issue is regarding the maintainability of this application post approval of the Resolution Plan without any conditions which is filed under Rule 11 of NCLT Rules. In this context it is reiterated that the Resolution Plan was approved on 04.01.2020 which provides that a Resolution Plan should be unconditional thus, the Applicant cannot raise an objection or try to modify at this stage post approval by filing this application. The plea taken by the Applicant cannot be accepted that during the pendency of the applications filed by the applicant in relation to the waivers and extension of time for making payment. The Applicant cannot be exempted for non-payment as per the payment schedule provided in the Resolution Plan and after undertaking to pay.
		</p>

						<p>
							<strong>
								&nbsp;II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority vide Order dated 05.07.2021 framed certain issues as under:
						</p>
						<p>
							(i) Whether this Application is maintainable or not in view of the approval of the Resolution Plan without any conditions and
						</p>
						<p>
							(ii) Whether this Bench is vested with the power wither to modify or alter preview the Order passed by this Bench approving the Resolution Plan.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority held that:
						</p>
						<ul>
							<li>
								The first issue is regarding the maintainability of this application post approval of the Resolution Plan without any conditions which is filed under Rule 11 of NCLT Rules. In this context it is reiterated that the Resolution Plan was approved on 04.01.2020 which provides that a Resolution Plan should be unconditional thus, the Applicant cannot raise an objection or try to modify at this stage post approval by filing this application. The plea taken by the Applicant cannot be accepted that during the pendency of the applications filed by the applicant in relation to the waivers and extension of time for making payment. The Applicant cannot be exempted for non-payment as per the payment schedule provided in the Resolution Plan and after undertaking to pay.
								<strong>
									(p11)
								</strong>
							</li>
							<li>
								At this stage, we can refer to the judgment of Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT in the matter of&nbsp;
								<em>
									R.G.G. Vyapaar Pvt Ltd Vs Arun Kumar Gupta &amp; Anr. Company&nbsp;(2018)  151 NCLAT&nbsp;
								</em>
								decided on 31.08.2018 held that Adjudicating Authority has no jurisdiction to reopen resolution under Section 31of IBC.&nbsp;Regarding the second issue, this Authority cannot maintain any application for modification or implementation of any condition post approval of a Resolution Plan. It would be appropriate to refer judgment passed by the Hon&rsquo;ble Apex Court in the matter of&nbsp;
								<em>
									Rahul Jain Vs Rage Scans Pvt Ltd and Ors.,&nbsp;[2019]  05 SC
								</em>
								&nbsp;decided on 08.11.2019 wherein it is held that once the resolution plan attained finality, it cannot be modified by the Order of Tribunal. Thus, both the issues are decided against the Applicant as it cannot blow hot and cold at the same time and not make payment as per the terms of the Resolution Plan.
								<strong>
									(p12-13)
								</strong>
							</li>
							<li>
								In CA-1090(PB)/2020, the submissions made by the Respondent cannot be accepted as post approval of the Resolution Plan, this Authority does not have any jurisdiction under provisions of IBC to modify or to revisit the plan even when an application is filed. In addition to this, the Resolution Plan is approved only when it is unconditional and in the present case an undertaking has been given by the Respondent which specifies its intentions. The Respondent cannot subsequently deny and raise certain conditions which cannot be part of a Resolution Plan or and may even amount to modification of the approved Resolution Plan and violate the process as stipulated in IBC.
								<strong>
									(p19)
								</strong>
							</li>
						</ul>
