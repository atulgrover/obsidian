		<h1>
			In absence of any deed or the other documents, the assets found in the temple&nbsp;which is within the premises of the corporate debtor to be regarded as the assets of the corporate debtor and therefore to form part of the liquidation estate of the corporate debtor &ndash; M/s LML Limited (In Liquidation) Through Liquidator, Arun Gupta &ndash; NCLT Allahabad Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			July 12, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			Liquidator seeking direction for donating the temple assets of the Almighty Deity("Temple Assets") situated within the premises of M/s LML Limited, which is under Liquidation. Adjudicating Authority observed that though the valuable objects found in temple are not recorded as "assets" in the balance sheet of the corporate debtor but as the temple is in the factory premises of the corporate debtor and there is nothing on record to show that the temple does not belong to corporate debtor or any interest has been created in favour of third party by the corporate debtor as there is no deed of dedication or any manager appointed to manage the assets of temples. Thus, in absence of any deed or the other documents, the assets found in the temple which is within the premises of the corporate debtor to be regarded as the assets of the corporate debtor and therefore to form part of the liquidation estate of the corporate debtor.
		</p>

							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The present application has been filed under p 60(5)(c) of the IBC, 2016 on behalf of the liquidator for seeking direction for donating the temple assets of the Almighty Deity (&ldquo;Temple Assets&rdquo;) situated within the premises of M/s LML Limited, which is under Liquidation.&nbsp;It is stated that the liquidator after taking control of the assets and&nbsp;liabilities of the corporate debtor, came to know that at in its premises a temple is situated having the collection of valuable objects including crowns (mulcut), coins, bell, Pooja Utensils, Jewellery and other items and the same has not recorded as &ldquo;Assets&rdquo; in the Balance Sheet for the period ended on 23rd March, 2018, thus it is contented that the temple assets belongs to the Almighty Deity and cannot be treated as a part of liquidation estate of the corporate debtor.
						</p>
						<p>
							Adjudicating Authority observed that though the valuable objects found in temple are not recorded as &ldquo;assets&rdquo; in the balance sheet of the corporate debtor but as the temple is in the factory premises of the corporate debtor and there is nothing on record to show that the temple does not belong to corporate debtor or any interest has been created in favour of third party by the corporate debtor as there is no deed of dedication or any manager appointed to manage the assets of temples. Thus, in absence of any deed or the other documents, the assets found in the temple&nbsp;which is within the premises of the corporate debtor to be regarded as the assets of the corporate debtor and therefore to form part of the liquidation estate of the corporate debtor.
						</p>
