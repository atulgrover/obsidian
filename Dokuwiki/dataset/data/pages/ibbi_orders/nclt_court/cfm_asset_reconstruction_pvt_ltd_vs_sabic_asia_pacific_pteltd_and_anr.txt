	<h1>
		An Operational Creditor cannot be directed to state on oath that there are no further facts or documents with respect to Insolvency Petition filed u/s 9 of IBC &ndash; CFM Asset Reconstruction Pvt. Ltd. Vs. SABIC Asia Pacific Pte.Ltd. and Anr. &ndash; NCLT Ahmedabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		February 1, 2024
	</div>
	<p>
		In this case, the applicant desires direction to be issued to Operational Creditor to file some documents and also confirms certain facts on oath in p 9 application. Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT Ahmedabad Bench holds that: (i) The Operational Creditor cannot be directed to file documents and facts under oath. According to the Operational Creditor, it has already filed these documents in the main matter. The applicant also has all the documents which it has already produced. (ii) Moreover, the Operational Creditor cannot be directed to state on oath that there are no further facts or documents with respect to this insurer. (iii) If there is anything, the Operational Creditor will face the consequences for non-disclosure.
	</p>

					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The Operational Creditor filed an application to initiate CIRP against the Corporate Debtor, viz., JBF Industries Ltd.
						</li>
						<li>
							The applicant herein i.e. CFM Asset Reconstruction Pvt. Ltd., was added to the party in main petition, i.e. CP (IB) No. 55 of 2021, as he intervened in the application.
						</li>
						<li>
							The present IA is filed by the Intervener / CFM Asset Reconstruction Pvt. Ltd. (acting in its capacity as trustee of CFMARC Trust &ndash; 88) duly registered as an Asset Reconstruction Company with the Reserve Bank of India under SARFAESI Act, seeking disclosure from the Operational Creditor i.e. SABIC Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd.
						</li>
						<li>
							The applicant / intervener submitted that it is necessary to produce the document i.e. subrogation form / agreement entered into between the Operational Creditor and the Insurer in relation to the payment made by the Insurer which forms subject matter of the captioned petition.
						</li>
						<li>
							Hence, prayed for directing the Operational Creditor to place on record under oath all the facts and documents and confirm that there are no other documents, etc. executed between the Operational Creditor and Insurer pertaining to the payment made by the Insurer.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							By filing this IA, the applicant desires direction to be issued to Operational Creditor to file some documents and also confirms certain facts on oath. The applicant has already filed relevant documents in the main matter. The applicant also discloses documents by way of filing this application and also in the main application.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Operational Creditor cannot be directed to file documents and facts under oath. According to the Operational Creditor, it has already filed these documents in the main matter. The applicant also has all the documents which it has already produced.
						</li>
						<li>
							Moreover, the Operational Creditor cannot be directed to state on oath that there are no further facts or documents with respect to this insurer. If there is anything, the Operational Creditor will face the consequences for non-disclosure. Hence, keeping in view of the facts mentioned above, this application is liable to be rejected. Hence, application is
							<strong>
								 rejected.(p7)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
