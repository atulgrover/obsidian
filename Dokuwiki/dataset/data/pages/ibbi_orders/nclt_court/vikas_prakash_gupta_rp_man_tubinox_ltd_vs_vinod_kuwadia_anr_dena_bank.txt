		<h1>
			Corporate Debtor entered into a Tripartite Agreement with a related party between CIRP admission order reserved date and pronounced date is total violation of Section 66 of the Code &ndash; Vikas Prakash Gupta, RP, Man Tubinox ltd. Vs. Vinod Kuwadia &amp; Anr. Dena Bank &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			October 22, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority observed that the Respondent No.1 to 4 who are suspended Directors of the Corporate Debtor Company were aware that the Petition was Reserved for Orders and just before the Insolvency commencement date, when they knew that there is no reasonable prospect of avoiding the CIRP in respect of the Corporate Debtor Company, carried on with the intent of defrauding the creditors of the Corporate Debtor Company by sub-leasing a large chunk of the only asset of the Corporate Debtor to a related party who happens to be the son-in-law of one of the suspended Directors. The Bench has no doubt in its mind that the suspended Directors were fully aware of the impending CIRP and also knew that restrictions under Section 14 of the Insolvency Code would kick in. Therefore, just before the commencement of the CIRP, in total violation of Section 66 of the Code, entered into a Tripartite Agreement with a related party, Maan Tubes Ltd. It is also important to note that when the sub-lease was granted on 29.06.2019, they did not inform the Madhya Pradesh Industrial Development Corporation that the Sub-lease is null and void as the Corporate Debtor which has Sub-leased the property is no longer with the original promotors but with the Interim Resolution Professional/ Resolution Professional. It is also, therefore, clear that M/s Maan Tubes Ltd has concealed information from the Madhya Pradesh Industrial Development Corporation in defrauding the creditors of the Corporate Debtor Company.
		</p>

							This Application has been filed by the Resolution Professional of&nbsp;Man Tubinox Ltd u/s 14(1)(b) r/w Section 66 of the IBC, 2016&nbsp;for reversal of transaction illegally conducted by handing over the&nbsp;property of the Corporate Debtor to the Respondents M/s Man&nbsp;Tubinox Ltd during the Moratorium period.

						<p>
							The RP mentions that the Corporate Debtor&nbsp;holds lease right over an area admeasuring 61629.44 sq. mtrs&nbsp;located Pithampur,&nbsp;Dist. Dhar, Madhya Pradesh. This lease hold right was obtained&nbsp;from Madhya Pradesh Audyogik Kendra Vikas Nigam&nbsp;(MPAKVN), Indore and the lease deed was executed on&nbsp;22.06.2015 for a period of 30 years. This lease deed was to expireon 21.06.2045.&nbsp;The Corporate&nbsp;Debtor entered into a Sub-Lease Deed dated 01.04.2019 with&nbsp;another Company viz. Maan Tubes Ltd for assigning a lease hold&nbsp;right in respect of 25,261.25 sq. mtrs out of the total area of&nbsp;61,629.44 sq. mtrs which was obtained by the Corporate Debtor from Madhya Pradesh Audyogik Kendra Vikas Nigam&nbsp;(MPAKVN), Indore.
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that the Respondent No.1 to 4 who are&nbsp;suspended Directors of the Corporate Debtor Company&nbsp;were aware&nbsp;that the Petition was Reserved for Orders and just before the&nbsp;Insolvency commencement date, when they knew that there is no&nbsp;reasonable prospect of avoiding the CIRP in respect of the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor Company, carried on with the intent of&nbsp;defrauding the creditors of the Corporate Debtor Company by&nbsp;sub-leasing a large chunk of the only asset of the Corporate&nbsp;Debtor to a related party who happens to be the son-in-law of one&nbsp;of the suspended Directors.&nbsp;The Bench has no doubt in its mind that the suspended Directors&nbsp;were fully aware of the impending CIRP and also knew that&nbsp;restrictions under Section 14 of the Insolvency Code would kick&nbsp;in. Therefore, just before the commencement of the CIRP, in total&nbsp;violation of Section 66 of the Code, entered into a Tripartite&nbsp;Agreement with a related party, Maan Tubes Ltd. It is also&nbsp;important to note that when the sub-lease was granted on&nbsp;29.06.2019, they did not inform the Madhya Pradesh Industrial&nbsp;Development Corporation that the Sub-lease is null and void as&nbsp;the Corporate Debtor which has Sub-leased the property is no&nbsp;longer with the original promotors but with the Interim Resolution&nbsp;Professional/ Resolution Professional. It is also, therefore, clear&nbsp;that M/s Maan Tubes Ltd has concealed information from the&nbsp;Madhya Pradesh Industrial Development Corporation in&nbsp;defrauding the creditors of the Corporate Debtor Company.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority directed that:
						</p>
						<p>
							(i) Cancels the Tripartite Agreement executed on 25.10.2019&nbsp;during the Moratorium period in violation of Section 14 of&nbsp;the IBC. The Bench further directs the Madhya Pradesh&nbsp;Industrial Development Corporation Limited to cancel its&nbsp;registration of the Tripartite Agreement as Mr. Vinod&nbsp;Kuwadia of Maan Tubinox Ltd has fraudulently and without&nbsp;any authority of the Resolution Professional to whom the&nbsp;responsibility of running the Corporate Debtor has passed u/s
							<br/>
							74.
						</p>
						<p>
							(ii) The General Manager, Madhya Pradesh Industrial&nbsp;Development Corporation is also directed to cancel its Order&nbsp;dated 29.06.2019 by which it allowed the Sub-lease of land&nbsp;admeasuring 24,281.2 sq. mtrs to M/s. Maan Tubinox&nbsp;Limited.
						</p>
						<p>
							(iii) This Bench finds that each of the Corporate Debtor exdirector/ promoter have violated Section 66 and also Section&nbsp;14 of the IBC and, therefore, in terms of Section 74 of the&nbsp;IBC, impose a fine of Rs.5 lakh (Rupees Five Lakh Only).&nbsp;On each of them. This fine will be credited to the bank&nbsp;account of the Corporate Debtor Company within five&nbsp;working days from the pronouncement of this Order.
						</p>
						<p>
							The RP is directed to file compliance report within 3 weeks of&nbsp;the pronouncement of this Order.
