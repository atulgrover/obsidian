	<h1>
		Whether Revenue/Profit earned from Secured Assets from the date of communication by Secured Creditor to Liquidator to realize Secured Assets asset under Section 52 of IBC till date of possession of Secured Assets by Financial/Secured Creditor will be part of Liquidation Estate? &ndash; KRS Erectors Pvt. Ltd. Vs. J.C. Flowers Asset Reconstruction Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Hyderabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		April 2, 2024
	</div>
	<p>
		In this important judgment, Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT Hyderabad Bench held that: (i) From carefully peruse to Regulation 21A(1) of the Liquidation Regulations, 2016, it is very much clear that secured assets shall be presumed to be part of the liquidation estate only when secured creditor fail to inform the liquidator of its decision to relinquish its security interest to the liquidation estate or realize its security interest, as the case may be, in Form C or Form D of the Schedule II within 30 days, which admittedly is not applicable in this case on hand. (ii) If secured creditor intimate its decision in time as prescribed in regulation, the secured assets do not form part of liquidation estate at any point of time. (iii) If assets cannot be treated as part of liquidation estate, the income derived from these assets also cannot be part of liquidation estate.
	</p>

					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							NCLT vide its order dated 27.08.2018 ordered the initiation of the liquidation process of the Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Respondent has by way of its letter/ communication dated
							<strong>
								 26.09.2018
							</strong>
							 , indicated that the Respondent proposes to realize/dispose of the Secured Asses for due repayment of the Outstanding Amounts in accordance with the provisions of Section 52 of the Code.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Respondent has taken possession of the Bhadrada Project on
							<strong>
								 09.01.2019
							</strong>
							 .
						</li>
						<li>
							Liquidator vide notice dated 18.03.2019 had called upon the Respondent to immediately transfer all the revenues in relation to Bhadrada Project pertaining to the period up to
							<strong>
								 09.01.2019
							</strong>
							 into the liquidation account.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Issue
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<p>
						Whether receivables from the charged assets from the date of communication by financial creditor to liquidator, conveying that it proposes to realize/dispose of the Secured Assets asset till date of possession of secured assets by financial creditor will be a part of liquidation Estate?
					</p>
					<p>
						<strong>
							<em>
								 Decision of Adjudicating Authority
							</em>
						</strong>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							Liquidation of Corporate debtor was ordered on 27.08.2018 and respondent/financial creditor exercised its right on 26.09.2018, to retain the exclusively charged Secured Assets including Bhadrada Project and enforce independently which is well within the time line stipulated under regulation 21A(1).
							<strong>
								 (p15)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							From carefully peruse to Regulation 21A(1) of the Liquidation Regulations, 2016, it is very much clear that secured assets shall be presumed to be part of the liquidation estate only when secured creditor fail to inform the liquidator of its decision to relinquish its security interest to the liquidation estate or realize its security interest, as the case may be, in Form C or Form D of the Schedule II within 30 days, which admittedly is not applicable in this case on hand.
							<strong>
								 (p16)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							If secured creditor intimate its decision in time as prescribed in regulation, the secured assets do not form part of liquidation estate at any point of time. Therefore, applicants contention that secured assets shall be treated as part of liquidation estate from the date of intimation till date of possession or disposal is not correct and is based on wrong presumptions.
							<strong>
								 (p16)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							Consequently,
							<strong>
								 if assets cannot be treated as part of liquidation estate, the
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 income derived from these assets also cannot be part of liquidation
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 estate.(p17)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							The Adjudicating Authority concludes that
							<strong>
								 Receivables from the charged assets from the date of communication by financial creditor to liquidator, conveying that it proposes to realize/dispose of the Secured Assets asset till date of possession of secured assets by financial creditor cannot be treated as a part of liquidation Estate. The point is accordingly decided.
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 (p18)
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							In the above background, the prayer sought in this application cannot be granted, hence the application is dismissed however without costs.
							<strong>
								 (p19)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
