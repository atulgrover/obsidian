<p><strong> Mr. Narayanam Nageswara Rao </strong> <br /> v. <br /> <strong> Mr. K. Sivalingam (RP) and Anr. </strong></p>

<p><em> For Appellant(s) </em> : Ms. Siva Praneetha, Advocate&nbsp;</p>

<hr />
<p><strong> Brief about the decision: </strong></p>
<p><em> <strong> Facts of the case </strong> </em></p>
<ul>
<li>That vide order dated 24.06.2022 the Corporate Debtor was admitted into CIRP. The Corporate Debtor is a MSM Enterprise.</li>
<li>The Applicant being a suspended director/promoter, submitted a Resolution Plan in October 2022. However, the Applicant was initially excluded from <br /> the provisional list of eligible Resolution Applicants on 30.10.2022, due to missing documents. A revised list on 03.11.2022, included the Applicant, <br /> contingent upon providing the required documentation.</li>
<li>The Resolution Professional issued a notice on 24.02.2024, inviting fresh bids. The Applicant was under the impression that previous Expressions of Interest would remain valid unless they opted to submit new one. Despite having submitted an Expression of Interest earlier, the Applicant intended to submit a fresh one.</li>
<li>However, the Respondents disqualified the Applicant from participating in the CIRP in the 18th Committee of Creditors meeting on 07.03.2024, citing Section 29A(b) &amp;(f) of the Code based on the following allegation:</li>
<li>
<ul>
<li>a. That the Applicant and a related party of the Applicant have been declared as willful defaulters.</li>
<li>b. That a connected party of the Applicant herein has been prohibited by the Securities and Exchange Board of India from trading in securities and accessing securities markets.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>It is averred that first allegation of having been declared as &lsquo;willful defaulter&rsquo;, is currently being challenged in the High Court of Telangana, and an order suspending the willful defaulter tag has been temporarily suspended since 27.04.2023.</li>
<li>The second allegation pertains to the connected party of the Applicant, which allegedly barred from the securities market, which was denied by the applicant in an exchange of emails between the applicant and 1st respondent. In the e-mail sent by Resolution Professional to the Applicant containing a link to the SEBI debarred list and an attached Excel sheet from BSE India&rsquo; s website, only Unique Client Code (UCC) of the Corporate Debtor was disabled and applicant&rsquo;s name does not find place in the said list.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong> <em> Question </em> </strong></p>
<p>Whether the decision of the Committee of Creditors dated 07.03.2024 disqualifying of the Applicant warrants the interference of Tribunal? If so, for what relief?</p>
<p><em> <strong> Decision of Adjudicating Authority </strong> </em></p>
<ul>
<li><strong> From the Explanation-I read with Section 29A(j) of IBC, the same speaks about connected person to the Resolution Applicant and not to the Corporate Debtor. </strong></li>
<li>The Resolution Applicant herein, being an ex-director of the Corporate Debtor, contention of the respondent that the applicant is disqualified to submit resolution plan as CD is debarred by SEBI and that the applicant is a connected party to the CD is not maintainable.</li>
<li>Further, legislature while passing the Bill, has given some special relaxation under Section 29A to MSME promoters so that they are allowed to participate in the resolution process of corporate debtor.</li>
<li>Therefore, in the above backdrop, the impugned decision made by the Committee of Creditors in the 18th Meeting held on 07.03.2024 disqualifying the Applicant, needs our interference. The point is accordingly decided.</li>
<li>In view of the above, IA 897/ 2024 is allowed and the decision taken by the 2nd Respondent in the 18th Committee of Creditors meeting dated 07.03.2024 is set aside to the extent of Agenda No. 8 and declare that the Applicant is eligible to submit a Resolution Plan and consequently, permit the Applicant to participate as a Resolution Applicant.</li>
<li>The prayers as sought in IA no 898/2024 are consequential to the prayers sought in IA no 897/2024, hence this application is also allowed.</li>
<li>In terms of our above findings , IA 897/2024 and IA 898/2024 are allowed and disposed of however without costs. <strong> (p8-12) </strong></li>
</ul>
