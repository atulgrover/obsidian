	<h1>
		A Liquidator is not on the panel of the Bank is not a valid and reasonable reason to remove/replace | Wisdom/Right for change of Liquidator is not available to SCC | In Liquidation Process Regulation 31A(11), SCC has to record specific reason for replacement of the Liquidator &ndash; State Bank of India Vs. Mr. Ramakant Gupta Liquidator of Sujyot Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Ahmedabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		January 20, 2024
	</div>
	<p>
		In this case, SBI has preferred an IA on behalf of SCC for replacement of the present liquidator only with the reason that the present Liquidator is not on the panel of the State Bank of India. In this important decision on replacement of Liquidator, Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT Ahmedabad Bench holds that: (i) The IBBI (Liquidation) Regulation 31A(11) specifically states that the Liquidator can be replaced by the SCC after recording the reasons in the minutes of the SCC. (ii) It is noticed that there exist a marked difference for change of Resolution Professional and change of Liquidator. During CIRP the COC has the wisdom to replace the RP if there is majority of more than 66% without assigning any specific reason for such replacement as such option is available as a matter of right. In the Liquidation process, the SCC has to record specific reason for replacement of the Liquidator. (iii) Wisdom or right for change of liquidator is not available to SCC in the Liquidation process. (iv) The applicant bank had stated only single reason to replace the present Liquidator that the Liquidator is not on the panel of the State Bank of India. In our view; it cannot be a valid and reasonable reason to change or to replace the Liquidator.
	</p>

					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The present application is filed under Section 60(5) of the Insolvency Bankruptcy Code, 2016 and Bankruptcy Board of India (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016, read with Rule 11 and 32 of National Company Law Tribunal Rules, 2016 seeking to replace the Liquidator of the Corporate Debtor, Sujyot Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.
						</li>
						<li>
							The SBI requested that the present Liquidator is not empanelled with the SBI as a Liquidator and hence requested to replace the liquidator- Mr. Ramakant Gupta with Mr. Sachin Bhattbhatt who is empanelled with the SBI.
						</li>
						<li>
							It is stated that such agenda for replacement of liquidator was discussed in the Second Meeting of Stakeholder consultation Committee dated 20.12.2023 whereby it was resolved to replace Mr. Ramakant Gupta the present respondent as liquidator and to appoint Mr. Sachin Bhattbhatt as a Liquidator in place of Mr. Ramakant Gupta hence this application.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The State Bank of India has preferred the present application on behalf of SCC for replacement of the present liquidator who is Respondent herein only with the reason that the present Liquidator is not on the panel of the State Bank of India.
						</li>
						<li>
							It is essential to emphasize that in accordance&nbsp;with the statutory mandate outlined in&nbsp;Regulation 31A of the IBBI (Liquidation Process)&nbsp;Regulations, 2016, a mandatory Written Consent&nbsp;Form, as stipulated in Form AA of Schedule II, is&nbsp;a prerequisite for the proposed liquidator&rsquo;s&nbsp;appointment. In addition to the requisite 66%&nbsp;voting percentage to pass the resolution, this&nbsp;form must be submitted before the Adjudicating&nbsp;Authority for the purpose of replacing the&nbsp;liquidator. It is noteworthy that such written&nbsp;consent has been attached in the present&nbsp;application.
						</li>
						<li>
							Moreover, during its 2nd Meeting, the SCC had&nbsp;approved the agenda of replacement of the&nbsp;Liquidator with 100% voting
							<strong>
								 without giving any
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 reasons for the replacement of the Liquidator.
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							<strong>
								 The IBBI (Liquidation) Regulation 31A(11)&nbsp;specifically states that the Liquidator can be&nbsp;replaced by the SCC after recording the reasons&nbsp;in the minutes of the SCC.
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							<strong>
								 It is noticed that there exist a marked difference&nbsp;for change of Resolution Professional and change
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 of Liquidator. During CIRP the COC has the
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 wisdom to replace the RP if there is majority of&nbsp;more than 66% without assigning any specific&nbsp;reason for such replacement as such option is&nbsp;available as a matter of right.
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 In the Liquidation
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 process, the SCC has to record specific reason
							</strong>
							<strong>
								 for replacement of the Liquidator
							</strong>
							 .
						</li>
						<li>
							Nowhere in the instant application, the Applicant&nbsp;had recorded any reason, neither the applicant is leveling any allegations nor incompetency on&nbsp;part of the liquidator who is performing his duties in the manner it should be performed.
						</li>
						<li>
							In our view
							<strong>
								 wisdom or right for change of liquidator is not available to SCC in the Liquidation process,
							</strong>
							 recording the above we
							<strong>
								 reject
							</strong>
							 the present application as we hold
							<strong>
								 no cognizant reason for replacement of the Liquidator are provided in the present application.
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							The applicant bank had stated only single reason to replace the present Liquidator that
							<strong>
								 the Liquidator is not on the panel of the State Bank of India. In our view; it cannot be a valid and reasonable reason to change or to replace the Liquidator.
							</strong>
						</li>
						<li>
							The Liquidator is directed to complete the liquidation process expeditiously as per law. Accordingly, with these observations the present application is dismissed and is disposed off. No order to cost.
						</li>
					</ul>
