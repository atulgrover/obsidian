						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Corporate Debtor had executed the projects. An amount of&nbsp;US$133,735.08/- remained outstanding towards the projects in Iran and Iraq&nbsp;(a &amp; b) and an amount of Rs. 8,75,248/- (Eight Lakh Seventy-Five&nbsp;Thousand Two Hundred Forty-Eight) remained outstanding against the&nbsp;project at sea (Indian Project).
						</p>
						<p>
							Meanwhile the Applicant came to know about the initiation of the CIRP of&nbsp;the Corporate Debtor. On 3rd June, 2020 the Applicant by an email&nbsp;submitted its claim before the Liquidator for payment of its dues. The&nbsp;Liquidator by an email of the even date declined to entertain the claim, the&nbsp;same having been filed beyond the time limit prescribed for it.&nbsp;Thereafter, the Applicant filed the present Application. It is submitted in the&nbsp;Application that the Applicant was not aware of the public advertisement its
							<br/>
							failure to submit its claim before the Liquidator was thus neither deliberate&nbsp;nor intentional. It accordingly made the following prayers:
						</p>
						<p>
							In view of the facts mentioned above, the petitioner prays&nbsp;Hon&rsquo;ble President &amp; Members of NCLT to condone the delay in&nbsp;filing the claim before IRP and request you to kindly direct the&nbsp;Resolution Professional to admit and consider the claim.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Liquidator in her reply submitted that since the claim by the Applicant&nbsp;was made after the stipulated date, she could not accept the same. She&nbsp;however, would consider the same if the Tribunal so directs.&nbsp;The Corporate Debtor has been put under liquidation. The liquidation&nbsp;process has not been completed. Considering the subsequential claim of the&nbsp;Applicant it would not be inappropriate to direct the Liquidator to consider&nbsp;the claim of the Applicant as per law. Besides no prejudice thereby would&nbsp;be caused to any side. Hence ordered.
						</p>
						<p>
							<br/>
							NCLT held that the Application be and the same is allowed on contest. The delay in&nbsp;submitting the claim by the Applicant with the Liquidator is condoned. The&nbsp;Applicant is directed to submit its claim in proper form within a period of&nbsp;two weeks hence. On receipt of the claim the Liquidator shall verify and&nbsp;consider the same in terms of the provisions of the Code and Regulations&nbsp;made there under. The Liquidator shall deduct Rs. 1,00,000/- (Rupees One&nbsp;Lakh only) from the admitted claim, if any, towards the cost of this&nbsp;Application and credit it to the account of the Corporate Debtor.
						</p>
