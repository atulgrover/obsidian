	<h1>
		There cannot be two simultaneous CIRP proceedings against a Corporate Debtor &ndash; Vrundavan Residency Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Mars Remedies Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Ahmedabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		January 24, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		The present Applicant claiming to be a financial creditor had filed the main Application CP(IB) 300/2020 for initiation of CIRP of the Corporate Debtor, during the pendency of CP(IB) 804/2019. In view of initiation of CIRP of the Corporate Debtor in CP(IB)804/2019, the present Applicant financial creditor was given liberty to file its claim before IRP appointed in CP(IB) 804/2019. The Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court has merely granted stay in CIRP proceeding in order to decide the appeal filed against the order of Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT directing NCLT to admit the IB application. The final outcome of the appeal before the Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court would be either setting aside of order of initiation of CIRP of the Corporate Debtor or continuance of CIRP proceedings of the Corporate Debtor in CP(IB) 804/2019. Since there cannot be two simultaneous CIRP proceedings against the same Corporate Debtor, hence the outcome of the proceedings before Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court shall decide the fate of Corporate Debtor.
	</p>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The applicant had filed an insolvency petition under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 being CP (IB) No. 300 of 2020 for initiating CIRP against the Respondent M/s. Mars Remedies Pvt Ltd. The said petition was rejected vide order dated 22.3.2021, reported at&nbsp;(2021) 507 NCLT.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Applicant being aggrieved by order dated 22.03.2021, filed Company Appeal before Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT and Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT vide order dated 04.03.2022, reported at&nbsp;(2022) 203 NCLAT, allowed the Appeal and set aside the order of NCLT.
						</li>
						<li>
							Thereafter, the Respondent being aggrieved preferred&nbsp;Civil Appeal No. 4823/2022&nbsp;before Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court and vide order dated 01.08.2022 said civil appeal was dismissed as withdrawn.
						</li>
						<li>
							Thereafter when the main petition was taken up afresh, in view of initiation of CIRP of the Corporate Debtor in CP(IB) 804/2019, the main petition was disposed of vide order dated 07.09.2022.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court&nbsp;vide Order dated 26.09.2022&nbsp;was pleased to stay further proceedings as regards resolution process in CP(IB) 804/2019.
						</li>
						<li>
							Thus, the Applicant filed this instant application seeking restoration&nbsp;of main petition and passing of consequential order of admission of Respondent into CIRP.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<strong>
							<em>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</em>
						</strong>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The Corporate Debtor was admitted into CIRP on 07.09.2022 in CP (IB) 804/2019 on an application filed by financial creditor BDH Industries Ltd. The said CIRP order was passed in view of order dated 07.02.2022 passed by Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT. In the meantime, order dated 07.02.2022 of Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT was challenged before Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court, where an order for stay of all further proceedings as regards the resolution process in CP(IB) 804/2019 is passed on 26.09.2022.
						</li>
						<li>
							The present Applicant claiming to be a financial creditor had filed the main Application CP(IB) 300/2020 for initiation of CIRP of the Corporate Debtor, during the pendency of CP(IB) 804/2019. In view of initiation of CIRP of the Corporate Debtor in CP(IB)804/2019, the present Applicant financial creditor was given liberty to file its claim before IRP appointed in CP(IB) 804/2019.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court has merely granted stay in CIRP proceeding in order to decide the appeal filed against the order of Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT directing NCLT to admit the IB application. The final outcome of the appeal before the Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court would be either setting aside of order of initiation of CIRP of the Corporate Debtor or continuance of CIRP proceedings of the Corporate Debtor in CP(IB) 804/2019. Since there cannot be two simultaneous CIRP proceedings against the same Corporate Debtor, hence the outcome of the proceedings before Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court shall decide the fate of Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							The prerequisites to revive/restore the main IB Application of present Applicant is clearly mentioned as &lsquo;to restore the application in case CP(IB) 804 of 2019 get settled or the order is set aside&rdquo;. Neither of the conditions are existing at present. Admittedly the matter is not settled and setting aside is yet premature if it has to happen in view of the pendency of the said issue before Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court.
						</li>
						<li>
							Hence, we are of the considered view that the present application cannot be considered at this stage. However, the present Applicant can avail the remedy of restoring the main application subject to the outcome of the appeal before Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court in CP(IB) 804/2019.&nbsp;In view of the foregoing, instant application is rejected and disposed of.
							<strong>
								 (p5-10)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
