	<h1>
		Act of mismanagement can be duly taken care under the avoidance provisions contained in IBC, NCLT cannot proceed in Company Petition filed u/s 241 of the Companies Act, 2013 &ndash; Mrs. Urmila Bairagra Vs. Bairagra Builders Pvt. Ltd. &amp; Ors. &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		August 17, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		NCLT Mumbai Bench, in Company Petition filed u/s 241 of the Companies Act, 2013 held that: (i) Petition has been filed with the sole ulterior motive of thwarting the imminent action from the Respondent no. 6 or its successor to claim the mortgage over the property of Respondent no. 1. This Tribunal considers it fit and appropriate to impose a costs of Rs. 1,00,000/- on the Petitioner for filing this Petition for the purpose other than relief provided u/s 242 under the garb of act of Oppression or Mismanagement. (ii) Act of the mismanagement, if there is any, can be duly taken care under the avoidance provisions contained in IBC, and this Bench cannot proceed further against the Corporate Debtor, if the petitioner seeks any relief from it in the petition on account of commencement of moratorium u/s 14 of IBC.
	</p>
</div>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 I. Case Reference
						</strong>
					</p>
					Case Citation:(2023) 486 NCLTCase Name:Mrs. Urmila Bairagra Vs. Bairagra Builders Pvt. Ltd. &amp; Ors.Application/Appeal No.:CP No.415/MB-IV/2021Judgment Date:10-Aug-23Court/Bench:NCLT Mumbai BenchMember (Judicial):Shri Kishore VemulapalliMember (Technical):Mr. Prabhat Kumar
					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						This Company Petition is filed by Smt. Urmila Bairagra u/s 241 of the Companies Act, 2013 in the matter of M/s Bairagra Builders Pvt Ltd. (Respondent no. 1), a company incorporated on 09.11.1995, with the Petitioner being one of original Subscribers.
					</p>
					<p>
						During the hearing on 27.07.2023, Ld. Counsel for the Petitioner informed this Bench that the Respondent No. 1 Company has already been admitted into CIRP by this Bench vide order dated 16.05.2023 passed in C.P. (IB)/1225(MB)2021.
					</p>
					<p>
						The NCLT held that:
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							This bench is of the view that this Petition has been filed with the sole ulterior motive of thwarting the imminent action from the Respondent no. 6 or its successor to claim the mortgage over the property of Respondent no. 1. This Tribunal considers it fit and appropriate to impose a costs of Rs. 1,00,000/- on the Petitioner for filing this Petition for the purpose other than relief provided u/s 242 under the garb of act of Oppression or Mismanagement.
						</li>
						<li>
							Nonetheless, this Bench also feels that act of the mismanagement, if&nbsp;there is any, can be duly taken care under the avoidance provisions contained in IBC, and this Bench cannot proceed further against the Corporate Debtor, if the petitioner seeks any relief from it in the petition on account of commencement of moratorium u/s 14 of IBC.
						</li>
						<li>
							In view of the aforesaid discussion, this Petition is dismissed as infructuous.
						</li>
					</ul>
