	</p>
	<p>
		On 25.08.2023, the following order was passed:
	</p>
	<p>
		<em>
			<strong>
				 &ldquo;This application under Section 9 of IBC, 2016 has been filed by M/s. Fedex Express Transportation and Supply Chain Services (India) Private Limited. The Applicant herein is seeking initiation of CIRP against M/s. Zipker Online Services Private Limited, the Corporate Debtor herein.
			</strong>
		</em>
	</p>
	<p>
		<em>
			<strong>
				 The matter was heard and order was reserved on 17.04.2023. Subsequently, it came to light that the Corporate Debtor-Company has been struck of by the RoC. The Corporate Debtor filed a short reply affidavit enclosing therein a copy of the Company Master Data which shows that the Corporate Debtor-Company has been struck of by the RoC but the date is not mentioned in the said MCA Data. This matter was listed for seeking clarification on 10.08.2023 and again on 25.08.2023 to enable the Applicant to place on record the relevant documents showing the date of the striking off of the company by the RoC.
			</strong>
		</em>
	</p>
	<p>
		<em>
			<strong>
				 However, no one appeared on behalf of the Applicant on both the dates. We, therefore, direct the Court Officer to send e-notice along with the copy of this order to the Learned Counsel for the Applicant as well as Applicant in person.
			</strong>
		</em>
	</p>
	<p>
		<em>
			<strong>
				 List the matter on 05.09.2023.&rdquo;
			</strong>
		</em>
	</p>
	<p>
		Today, the matter has been again listed for clarification. Despite e-notice being sent by the Court Officer and several opportunities being granted to the Applicant to appear before this Tribunal, no one has appeared on behalf of the
		<br />
		 Applicant. The Ld. Counsel appears on behalf of the Corporate Debtor.
	</p>
	<p>
		As per the records the name of the Corporate Debtor has been struck off by the RoC. Further, no one has appeared on behalf of the Operational Creditor to clarify the same or to controvert the said fact. We are of the considered view that CIRP cannot be initiated against a Company which has already been struck off by the ROC. Therefore, Section 9 application is
		<strong>
			 dismissed.
		</strong>
	</p>
	<p>
		IA
		<strong>
			 dismissed.
		</strong>
