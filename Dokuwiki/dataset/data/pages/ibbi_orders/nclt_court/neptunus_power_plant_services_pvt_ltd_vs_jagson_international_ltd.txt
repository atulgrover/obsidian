	<h1>
		In the absence of any clause in Invoice regarding interest on delayed payment, Operational Creditor, even being a MSME, cannot club interest with principal to meet threshold limit under Sec. 4 of IBC &ndash; Neptunus Power Plant Services Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Jagson International Ltd. &ndash; NCLT New Delhi Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		January 12, 2024
	</div>
	<p>
		In this case, the Operational Creditor has claimed an amount of Rs 87,67,953/- towards principal and amount of Rs 33,00,000.54/- towards interest. Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT New Delhi Bench observes that the entire claim is based on invoices issued by the Operational Creditor to the Corporate Debtor. The Operational Creditor has claimed to be an MSME and has charged interest being an MSME. The Operational Creditor has not shown any clause in the invoice which shows that interest is leviable in case of default or late payment. In the absence of any such clause Operational Creditor cannot claim interest.
	</p>

						<strong>
							 Brief about the decision:
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						This Application has been filed by M/s. Neptunus Power Plant Services Pvt. Ltd., the Applicant/Operational Creditor herein, before this Adjudicating Authority, under Section 9 of the IBC&nbsp;for initiating the CIRP, declaring moratorium and for appointment of IRP, against M/s. Jagson International Ltd., the Respondent/Corporate Debtor, on the ground that the Corporate Debtor defaulted/failed to clear the outstanding amount of Rs. 1,32,99,727.58/- along with interest @ 18% p.a. The Date of Default is 18.03.2020.
					</p>
					<p>
						The Operational Creditor has claimed an amount of Rs. 87,67,953/- towards principal and amount of Rs 33,00,000.54/- towards interest. The entire claim is based on invoices issued by the Operational Creditor to the Corporate Debtor. The Operational Creditor has claimed to be an MSME and has charged interest being an MSME. The Operational Creditor has not shown any clause in the invoice which shows that interest is leviable in case of default or late payment. In the absence of any such clause Operational Creditor cannot claim interest.
					</p>
					<p>
						The Operational Creditor has failed to satisfy the minimum pecuniary threshold for default being Rs 1 Crore as mandated in the amendment to Section 4 of IBC, 2016.
					</p>
					<p>
						It is well settled that if the Corporate Debtor raises a plausible contention about a pre-existing dispute, which is not just a moonshine or feeble legal argument, it would suffice for the Adjudicating Authority to reject the application filed under Section-9 of the Code.
					</p>
					<p>
						We are of the view that there is existence of the &lsquo;Dispute&rsquo;. It is clear from the emails and is evident from the fact that on several occasions the issue for defective goods was discussed between the parties both orally and in written correspondence.
					</p>
					<p>
						The Application bearing IB-827/ND/2020 filed by the Applicant under Section 9 of the Code r/w Rule 6 of the Adjudicating Authority Rules for initiating CIRP against the Respondent is hereby dismissed.
					</p>
