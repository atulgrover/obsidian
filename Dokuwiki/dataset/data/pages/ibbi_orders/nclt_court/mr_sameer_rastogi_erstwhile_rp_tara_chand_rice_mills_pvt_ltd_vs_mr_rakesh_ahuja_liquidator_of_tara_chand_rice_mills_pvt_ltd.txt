					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						The issues for adjudication in this case are:
					</p>
					<p>
						(1) Whether the present application for the payment to the Resolution Professional for the period beyond what was expressly approved by the COC is maintainable; and
					</p>
					<p>
						(2) Who will decide the fees, if any, due to applicant- erstwhile Resolution Professional as the COC has not been made a party to the application.
					</p>
					<p>
						To recapitulate in the present case the original CIRP concluded on 11.09.2018, was extended upto 10.12.2018. The CoC had approved the professional fees during the CIRP or any extension. The period from 11.12.2018 till the RP had managed the affairs of corporate debtor upto 12.02.2019, has been without any approval of the CoC, as raised by the respondent in its reply, has been deliberated upon. Taking up the issue of the claim of fees of the RP, a reference is made to the provisions of Section 23(1) of the Code.
					</p>
					<p>
						In the present case, in the absence of any order from this Adjudicating Authority appointing a liquidator, the applicant&rsquo;s continuation as a RP is not in contravention of any provisions of IBC. We draw support from the order of Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT on almost similar facts in the case of
						<em>
							 CoC M/s. Smartec Build Systems Pvt. Ltd. Vs. B. Santosh Babu &amp; Ors.&nbsp;[2020] 10 NCLAT.
						</em>
						 Respondent&rsquo;s contention that no fees is payable for the extended period as the same was not ratified by the CoC is untenable.
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority held that we are aware of the decision by Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT in
						<em>
							 CA Rita Gupta (Erstwhile RP in the matter of Shilpi Cable Technologies Ltd.) Vs. M/s. Shilpi Cable Technologies Ltd. Through its Liquidator Mr. Huzefa Fakhri Sitabkhan.&nbsp;(2022) 549 NCLAT
						</em>
						 , where the Hon&rsquo;ble Tribunal considered the case of the RP who could not submit its claim before the CoC as the same was defunct after the initiation of liquidation Keeping in view the above observation of the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT and the fact that in the present case, company is already under liquidation, we direct the liquidator to disburse the professional fee of RP at the rate approved by CoC for the period from 21.12.2018 to 12.02.2019 i.e, upto the period ending with order of liquidation. The said amount be disbursed to erstwhile Resolution Professional before any other amount to be distributed as per Section 53 of the Code, 2016 as the said fees comes under the heading under p 5(13) i.e., insolvency resolution process cost. In view of above, the present application is allowed and disposed of accordingly.
