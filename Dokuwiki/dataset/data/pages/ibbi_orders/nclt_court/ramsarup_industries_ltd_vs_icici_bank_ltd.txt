	<h1>
		Sale of certain assets imported from abroad were lying at Durgapur as customs duty was not paid, is in violation of p 14 of the Code and the Resolution Professional is entitled to take possession of the assets of the Corporate Debtor &ndash; Ramsarup Industries Ltd. Vs. ICICI Bank Limited &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		July 3, 2018
	</div>
	<p>
		Certain assets imported from abroad were lying at Durgapur as customs duty was not paid. The respondents issued e-auction notice for sale of those assets. The Resolution Professional as well as the Corporate Debtor requested the respondents not to go ahead with the e-auction. However, the respondents proceeded as per e-auction notice. The Resolution Professional filed an application under p 14 of the Code praying for directions to respondents not to proceed with the e-auction of the machineries. NCLT held that the sale is in violation of p 14 of the Code and the Resolution Professional is entitled to take possession of the assets of the Corporate Debtor. The remedy available to respondents is to submit claim for the amount due with the Resolution Professional. It also observed that the sale proceeding being void ab initio as per p 14 of the Code, the sale becomes null and void and the buyer is entitled to get back the money.
	</p>
