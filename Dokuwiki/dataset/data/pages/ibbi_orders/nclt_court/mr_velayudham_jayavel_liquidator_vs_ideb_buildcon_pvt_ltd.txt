<section data-post-type="post" data-banner-layout="layout-2">
	<div>
		<h4>
			Liquidator has power to call for Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM) of a Subsidiary Company of the Corporate Debtor in terms of the proviso to Section 98(1) and 100 of Companies Act, 2013 read with Section 36(3)(d) of IBC &ndash; Mr. Velayudham Jayavel, Liquidator of IDEB Projects Pvt. Ltd. Vs. IDEB Buildcon Pvt. Ltd. and Ors. &ndash; NCLT Bengaluru Bench
		</h4>
		<div>
			May 4, 2024
		</div>
		<p>
			Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT Bengaluru Bench held that the shares of the Corporate Debtor in the Subsidiary Company is a part of the Liquidation estate under Section 36(3)(d) of the IBC and hence power is vested to the Liquidator to take such measures to protect and carry on the business for the benefit of the Corporate Debtor. As such since the Corporate Debtor is a shareholder of the Subsidiary Company, and under Section 100 of the Companies Act, 2013 entitled to call for EGM, the same can be called by the Liquidator who is only acting on behalf of the Corporate Debtor. Under Section 98 of the Companies Act, NCLT has limited power to adjudicate the calling of the EGM and cannot decide on the agenda of calling such EGM.
		</p>
	</div>
</section>
<p>
	<em>
		<strong>
			 Facts of the case
		</strong>
	</em>
</p>
<ul>
	<li>
		Respondent No. 1 is a private company and is a wholly owned subsidiary of IDEB Projects Pvt Ltd (Corporate Debtor).
	</li>
	<li>
		CIRP was initiated against the IDEB Projects Pvt. Ltd. vide order dated
		<strong>
			 29/03/2019
		</strong>
		 and thereafter, Liquidation proceedings were initiated vide order dated 08/11/2019.
	</li>
	<li>
		The Petitioner (Liquidator of IDEB Projects Pvt. Ltd.) issued a requisition notice on 08/06/2022 to the board of directors of the Subsidiary Company (R1) and requested them to call for an EGM on 16/06/2022 for the purpose of appointing new directors for the Subsidiary Company.
	</li>
	<li>
		Thereafter, the Subsidiary Company issued letter dated 29/06/2022 to the Liquidator and refused the convene EGM without prior approval and authorisation from NCLT.
	</li>
	<li>
		Owing to the above, the Liquidator issued another notice on 19/07/2022 to the Respondent No. 2 &amp; 3 (Erstwhile directors of the Corporate Debtor) and Respondent No. 2, being the only other shareholder failed to attend the said EGM.
	</li>
	<li>
		The EGM failed for want of quorum 18/08/2022 because since there are only two Shareholders in the Subsidiary Company, the other shareholder is not attending the meetings and there is no quorum to hold meeting.
	</li>
	<li>
		Hence, this Application has been filed on 29/08/2022 by Liquidator of IDEB Projects Pvt. Ltd. u/s 98 of the Companies Act, 2013 r/w Rule 11 of the NCLT Rules, 2016 against IDEB BUILDCON Pvt. Ltd. seeking to direct holding of Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM) of the Subsidiary Company in terms of the proviso to Section 98(1) of the Companies Act, 2013.
	</li>
</ul>
<p>
	<em>
		<strong>
			 Decision of Adjudicating Authority
		</strong>
	</em>
</p>
<ul>
	<li>
		The shares of the Corporate Debtor in the Subsidiary Company is a part of the Liquidation estate under Section 36(3)(d) of the IBC and hence power is vested to the Liquidator to take such measures to protect and carry on the business for the benefit of the Corporate Debtor.
		<strong>
			 (p7)
		</strong>
	</li>
	<li>
		As such since the Corporate Debtor is a shareholder of the Subsidiary Company, and under Section 100 of the Companies Act, 2013 entitled to call for EGM, the same can be called by the Liquidator who is only acting on behalf of the Corporate Debtor.
		<strong>
			 (p7)
		</strong>
	</li>
	<li>
		Under Section 98 of the Companies Act, NCLT has limited power to adjudicate the calling of the EGM and cannot decide on the agenda of calling such EGM, it is observed in the order of Hon&rsquo;ble High Court of Bombay in
		<em>
			 Invesco Developing Markets Fund v. Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd.&nbsp;(2022) ibclaw.in 287 HC
		</em>
		 .
		<strong>
			 (p8)
		</strong>
	</li>
	<li>
		In the present case, since there are only two Shareholders in the Subsidiary Company, the other shareholder is not attending the meetings and there is no quorum to hold meeting, therefore, the Liquidator has filed the present Application u/s 98 of the Companies Act, 2013 for convening the meeting of the EGM of the Subsidiary Company. It is observed that unless the Tribunal grants the relief sought by the Applicant u/s 98 of the Companies Act, 2013, it would not possible to the Liquidator to hold or to conduct the meeting.
		<strong>
			 (p10)
		</strong>
	</li>
	<li>
		Upon considering the facts and circumstances and the pleadings of the present Application, the instant Petition is allowed and the Liquidator is directed to hold an EGM of the Subsidiary Company as specified u/s 98 of the Companies Act, 2013 to make necessary statutory compliance of the Company. For the purpose of quorum, the Applicant who is holding 99.9% shares of the Subsidiary Company shall constitute the quorum of the said meeting either in-person or through proxy.
	</li>
	<li>
		Accordingly, C.P.No.50 of 2023 is disposed of.
	</li>
</ul>
