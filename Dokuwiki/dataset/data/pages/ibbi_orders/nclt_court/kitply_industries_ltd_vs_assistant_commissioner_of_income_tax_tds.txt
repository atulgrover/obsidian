					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						A perusal of Section 14 (1) (a) amongst other things, reveals that on and from the insolvency commencement date, the institution of suits, continuation of pending suits or
						<em>
							 proceedings
						</em>
						 before any court, tribunal or
						<em>
							 any other authority
						</em>
						 is not permissible in view of the prohibition given in Section 14 (1) (a) of the CD, 2016. The proceeding(s) before the ITD which had resulted in freezing of the bank accounts in the name of the CD is a proceeding of quasi-judicial nature and being so, such a proceeding is a &ldquo;
						<em>
							 proceeding before any other authority&rsquo;
						</em>
						 as contemplated in the aforesaid provision of law and as such, continuation of the same during the period when the moratorium is in operation is illegal in view of the prohibitions, rendered in Section 14(1)(a) of the Code and therefore, same becomes untenable in law.
					</p>
					<p>
						It may be stated here that CIRP, so initiated under the order dated 01.11.2018 is about to be completed very soon, which also shows that moratorium promulgated under Section 14 of the Code of 2016 on 01.11.2018 is still in progress and, therefore, the freezing of the bank accounts of the CD, being the continuation of the proceeding before the ITD, is not permissible under the law. Consequently, the order (s) under which all those bank accounts in the name of the CD have been freezed is/are hereby declared illegal. The fact that at no point of time, the concerned ITD had made its stand, on the controversy under consideration, known to either RP or this authority makes such a conclusion inevitable. Resultantly, the prayer No.1
						<em>
							 and
						</em>
						 2 in the application under consideration are allowed.
					</p>
