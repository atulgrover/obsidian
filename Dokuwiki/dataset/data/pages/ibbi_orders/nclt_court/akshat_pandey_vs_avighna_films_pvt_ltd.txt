	<h1>
		Whether an investment made by the Director of the Company falls under the definition of Operational Debt? &ndash; Akshat Pandey Vs. Avighna Films Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		August 23, 2022
	</div>
	<p>
		The Adjudicating Authority held in this instant matter the Petitioner, who is also one of the director of the Corporate Debtor, invested money in the Corporate Debtor for production of a movie. In light of the above facts and circumstances we are of the view that an Investment made by the director of the Company does not fall under the purview of an Operational Debt under the Code.
	</p>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						This is a Company Petition filed under p 9 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 by Akshat Pandey (Operational Creditor) seeking to initiate CIRP against Avighna Films Private Limited (Corporate Debtor).&nbsp;The issue that arises in this instant matter is that whether an investment&nbsp;made by the Director of the Company falls under the definition of Operational&nbsp;Debt?.
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority held that as envisaged under p 5(21) of the Code, an Operational Debt means a claim in respect of the provision of goods or services including employment or a debt in respect of the re-payment of dues arising under any law for the time being in force and payable to the Central Government, any State Government or any local authority.&nbsp;Further, p 5(20) of the Code an &lsquo;Operational Creditor&rsquo; meaning a person to whom an Operational debt is owed and includes any person to whom such debt has been legally assigned or transferred.
					</p>
					<p>
						As per the definition of the term Operational Debt in the Code, it is clear that a debt is considered to be an operational debt, when it is confined under three categories:
					</p>
					<p>
						(i) Goods, or;
						<br />
						 (ii) Services (including employment), or;
						<br />
						 (iii) a debt in respect of the re-payment of dues arising under any law for the time being in force and payable to the Central Government, any State Government or any local authority
					</p>
					<p>
						However in this instant matter the Petitioner, who is also one of the director of the Corporate Debtor, invested money in the Corporate Debtor for production of a movie. In light of the above facts and circumstances we are of the view that an Investment made by the director of the Company does not fall under the purview of an Operational Debt under the Code. Hence, C.P. (IB) No. 178/KB/2021 the stands dismissed. However, the Petitioner is at liberty to pursue its remedy under law, if any.
						<strong>
							 (p16-19)
						</strong>
					</p>
