						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the Case
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Question of law
								</strong>
							</em>
							<br/>
							1. Whether the adjudicating authority has the jurisdiction of judicial&nbsp;review and appoint an independent valuer to conduct fresh&nbsp;valuation of Intangible assets of CD?
						</p>
						<p>
							2. Whether there is any error apparent on the face of valuation&nbsp;reports submitted by the two registered valuers contrary to the&nbsp;accounted balance sheets of the CD for the year 2018?
						</p>
						<p>
							<strong>
								Scope of Judicial Review
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The scope of judicial review is drawn from Sec.30, Sec.31 read with Sec.60(5)(c) and the ratio laid down by Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court in its recent judgement in Committee of Creditors of Essar Steel India Limited v. Satish Kumar Gupta &amp; Ors. [2019]  07 SC
						</p>
						<p>
							The Counsel for the Applicant conceded that there is no element of fraud but only compares the value assigned to the intangible assets in the financial statement for the year 2017 &amp; 2018 with the Information Memoranda and claims that this is an error apparent on the face of it. I conclude that the Warning letters issued by the USFDA is curable and entails the steps for compliance therein. Be that as it may, the reasoning of the Valuers for ascribing the nil value is absolutely untenable in the interest of maximizing the assets of the CD. The value of Intangible assets of a going concern making profits even during CIRP cannot be stripped of under the premise of warning&nbsp;letters. No legal rights of any of the parties is affected if the exercise of fresh valuation is carried out, at best would assist the better valuation of CD as a going concern, though there were only two Resolution applicants willing infuse funds and revive the CD. Though the valuers whose report is being questioned are not made parties to this application, I am entitled to look at the report and try to ascertain just what they are saying, in order to determine the extent to which they assist in the relevant debate.
						</p>
						<p>
							In view of the judgement of Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court in the case of&nbsp;
							<em>
								<strong>
									COC&nbsp;
								</strong>
							</em>
							<em>
								<strong>
									of Essar Steel India Ltd v. Satish kumar Gupta &amp; Ors [2019]  07 SC
								</strong>
							</em>
							, wherein it was held that discretion can be exercised to extend time to complete the CIRP process in exceptional cases and empowered to grant a relief, a fresh valuation exercise is ordered.
						</p>
						<p>
							Therefore, it is ordered that the Resolution Professional take steps to appoint a fresh Valuer with a limited scope of valuing the Intangible asset considering the International standard of Valuation of a Pharmaceutical Company and submit his report within a period of two weeks of receipt of the order copy and the COC is directed to reconsider the valuation submitted by the third Valuer. The Misc. Application is disposed off with the above direction.
						</p>
