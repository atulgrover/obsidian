		<h1>
			AA directs to Secured Creditor to handover the possession of the assets forming part of the security interest that have not been sold within 180 days as per Regulation 21A Liquidation Process Regulations, 2016 &ndash; Mr. Subodh Kumar Agarwal, the liquidator of Nippon Alloy Ltd. Vs. South India Bank Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			May 17, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			In the instant application, there are two Respondents having charge over two different sets of assets of the Corporate Debtor. The respondent No. 1 failed to realise the security interest within 180 days. However, due to an order of attachment having been issued by the Government of Andhra Pradesh on the said assets, a writ petition being W.P. has been instituted by the Respondent No. 1, on 17.11.2021, before the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court of Andhra Pradesh. In light of the said writ petition being sub judice, no action can be taken by the Adjudicating Authority in this regard till the disposal of the said writ petition. On the other hand, Respondent No. 2, in spite of having been given ample opportunity to file a reply- affidavit, has failed to do so. However, the communication that took place between the Liquidator and Respondent No. 2 on 28.06.2021 indicates that Respondent No. 2 sold one of the four properties of the Corporate Debtor but failed to sell the rest even after six months from commencement of liquidation. As such, the rest of the assets under the charge of the Respondent No. 2 should become a part of the liquidation estate.
		</p>

							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Facts of the case
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Liquidator/Applicant filed a liquidation application under p 33 of the Code, vide order dated 08.12.2022, the Adjudicating Authority ordered for liquidation of the Corporate debtor. Two Secured Financial Creditors of the Corporate debtor, named South Indian Bank (being Respondent No. 1) and Union Bank of India (being respondent No. 2) filed the Claim in Form D before the Liquidator, as they did not relinquish their security interest.
						</p>
						<p>
							The E-Auction was held on 24.03.2021 and the eligible bidders participated in the bidding Process and accordingly, the Liquidator issued Letter of Intent to the Successful Applicant and executed the Sale Notice and Assignment Agreement in favour of Successful Bidders. On the basis of the proceeds received from the said Successful Bidders, the Liquidator distributed the proceeds to the Stakeholders as per the p 53 of the Code and applicable regulations.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Liquidator communicated to Respondents No. 1 &amp; 2 via several mails to handover the assets as the 180 days had elapsed on 06.06.2021 and as per regulation 21A of the IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations 2016, the Respondents were required to pay the liquidation process cost and also handover the possession of the property given to them as security to the Liquidator. The Liquidator communicated to the respondents that more than 9 months had elapsed and the respondents had not handed over the possession of the assets of the Corporate Debtor, thereby totally ignoring the express provisions and the spirit of the Code.
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							Decision of the Adjudicating Authority is summarised in the following points:
						</p>
						<p>
							Regulation 21A of the IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations 2016 provides that a secured creditor is required to intimate its decision of not relinquishing the security interest within 30 days from the liquidation commencement date, failing which, the assets covered under the security interest shall be presumed to be part of the liquidation estate. In the instant application, Form D was submitted by Respondents No. 1 &amp; 2 on 29.12.2020 and 06.01.2021 respectively. In both cases, the Form D was submitted within 30 days from the liquidation commencement date.
						</p>
						<p>
							Further, Regulation 21A of the IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations 2016 provides that where a secured creditor does not relinquish security interest and proceeds to realise its security interest, it shall, within 90 days from the liquidation commencement date, pay to the liquidator a) Insolvency resolution process costs and liquidation costs in full; b) Workmen&rsquo;s dues for the period of twenty-four months preceding the liquidation commencement date, as it would have shared in case it had relinquished the security interest.&nbsp;As such, both Respondents No. 1 &amp; 2 are bound to pay to the liquidator the&nbsp;abovementioned sums.
						</p>
						<p>
							Further, Regulation 21A also provides that where a secured creditor proceeds to realise its security interest, it shall pay the excess of the realized value of the asset, which is subject to security interest, over the amount of his claims admitted, to the liquidator within 180 days from the liquidation commencement date. This indicates that the realization of assets needs to be completed within 180 days. In the instant application, wherein the liquidation commencement date was 08.12.2020, the realization of assets was to be completed within 06.06.2021.
						</p>
						<p>
							In the instant application, there are two Respondents having charge over two different sets of assets of the Corporate Debtor. Respondent No. 1, after submitting Form D on 29.12.2020, took symbolic possession of the assets under its charge on 14.06.2021 and sold the said assets by public auction. Respondent No. 1 invited bids by issuing an auction sale notice dated 28.09.2021 and declared the successful bidder on 06.11.2021. Meanwhile on 30.09.2021, the Liquidator filed the instant application. In this case, the respondent No. 1 failed to realise the security interest within 180 days. However, due to an order of attachment dated 20.02.2015 having been issued by the Government of Andhra Pradesh on the said assets, a writ petition being W.P. No. 27270 of 2021 has been instituted by the Respondent No. 1, on 17.11.2021, before the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court of Andhra Pradesh. In light of the said writ petition being&nbsp;
							<em>
								sub judice
							</em>
							, no action can be taken by the Adjudicating Authority in this regard till the disposal of the said writ petition.
						</p>
						<p>
							On the other hand, Respondent No. 2, in spite of having been given ample opportunity to file a reply- affidavit, has failed to do so. However, the communication that took place between the Liquidator and Respondent No. 2 on 28.06.2021 indicates that Respondent No. 2 sold one of the four properties of the Corporate Debtor but failed to sell the rest even after six months from commencement of liquidation. As such, the rest of the assets under the charge of the Respondent No. 2 should become a part of the liquidation estate.
						</p>
						<p>
							In view of the above facts and circumstances, this Bench, hereby orders as follows: &ndash;
						</p>
						<p>
							a) Respondents No. 1 and 2 are directed to pay to the liquidator the liquidation costs and Workmen&rsquo;s dues for the period of twenty-four months preceding the liquidation commencement date, if not already paid.
						</p>
						<p>
							b) The application, to the extent of the Liquidator taking possession of the assets forming part of the security interest for Respondent No. 1, is hereby dismissed. The dismissal of the present application shall not stand in the way of any other remedy pursued by the liquidator in a manner known to law.
						</p>
						<p>
							c) Respondent No. 2 is directed to handover the possession of the assets forming part of the security interest that have not been sold yet, to the liquidator immediately.
						</p>
						<p>
							d) Further, regarding the property that has been sold by Respondent No. 2 in realisation of their security interest, Respondent No. 2 is directed to provide to the Liquidator, an account of the sale details and to pay to him the excess of the realized value of the said asset.
						</p>
						<p>
							With these directions, I.A. No. 904/KB/2021 shall stand disposed of.&nbsp;List main CP. No. 1889/KB/2019 for reporting progress.
