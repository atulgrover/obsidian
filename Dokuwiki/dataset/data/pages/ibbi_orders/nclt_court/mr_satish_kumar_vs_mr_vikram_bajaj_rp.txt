	<h1>
		Right to visit Project Site to Homebuyers/Allottees during Corporate Insolvency of a Real Estate Project &ndash; Mr. Satish Kumar and 47 Ors. Vs. Mr. Vikram Bajaj RP &ndash; NCLT New Delhi Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		February 9, 2024
	</div>
	<p>
		In this case, IAs filed by Homebuyers to allow access to units purchased, to commence commercial activity from their units, to obtain electrical connections and carry out necessary alterations in the units etc. Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT New Delhi Bench holds that: (i) After initiation of the CIRP, it is the duty of the Resolution Professional to protect, preserve and maintain the status quo of the value of the assets of the Corporate Debtor in toto. (ii) The Conduct of Resolution Professional to not allow free Ingress and Egress to the Applicants/Homebuyers is not good in law. (iii) Directs the Resolution Professional to restore the right to visit the Project Site to the Applicants/Homebuyers. (iv) Once the Resolution Plan has been approved by the CoC, the Adjudicating Authority can&rsquo;t go back to look into the nittygritty&rsquo;s involved in the CIRP of the Corporate Debtor.
	</p>
</div>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 Brief about the decision:
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<strong>
							<em>
								 Grounds of the IA
							</em>
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						The present Application has been filed by Mr. Satish Kumar and 47 Ors., the Applicant (purchased commercial units/residential units in the said project launched by the Corporate Debtor) under Rule 11 of the NCLT Rules, 2016 for seeking the following reliefs:
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							i. Allow the applicants access to the units purchased by them through duly registered lease deeds.
						</li>
						<li>
							ii. Allow the applicants to commence commercial activity from their units.
						</li>
						<li>
							iii. Allow the applicants to obtain electrical connections and carry&nbsp;out necessary alterations in the units which may be permitted to be&nbsp;demarcated in the presence of Resolution Professional or his&nbsp;authorised representative.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The Resolution Plan was approved by the CoC in its 7th meeting dated 20.05.2019 by 74.44% voting share in respect of the CIRP of the Corporate Debtor after considering its feasibility and viability. Thereafter, the Resolution Professional filed IA to seek approval of the Resolution Plan under Section 30(6) read with Section 31 of IBC, 2016 on 27.05.2019, which is pending adjudication before this Adjudicating Authority.
						</li>
						<li>
							The structure of the project is not complete and no construction activity has taken place in the last 7-8 years and lacks basic amenities such as water, electricity, sewage, sanitation, lifts, ventilation, fire safety, escalators etc., is thus unfit and unsafe and does not comply all structural fire safety laws.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							After initiation of the CIRP, it is the duty of the Resolution Professional to protect, preserve and maintain the status quo of the value of the assets of the Corporate Debtor in toto.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Conduct of Resolution Professional to not allow&nbsp;free Ingress and Egress to the Applicants/Homebuyers is not good&nbsp;in law.
						</li>
						<li>
							Therefore, we direct the Resolution Professional to restore&nbsp;the right to visit the Project Site to the Applicants/Homebuyers.&nbsp;The Applicants/Homebuyers are directed to maintain the status&nbsp;quo while visiting the Project Site and take appropriate prior&nbsp;permissions from the Resolution Professional for visiting the&nbsp;Project Site in advance to avoid security inconveniences.
						</li>
						<li>
							Coming to the factual matrix of the present Application, it is a settled law that once the Resolution Plan has been approved by the CoC, the Adjudicating Authority can&rsquo;t go back to look into the nittygritty&rsquo;s involved in the CIRP of the Corporate Debtor. Therefore, this Adjudicating Authority cannot entertain the present Application which is not sustainable.
						</li>
						<li>
							We advice the Resolution Professional as well as the Applicants/Homebuyers to make their best endeavors to get the ultimate essence in the implementation of the Code and in the interest of justice to expedite the process of Resolution of the Corporate Debtor under the Code.
						</li>
					</ul>
