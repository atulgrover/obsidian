						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							In present appeal, the amount claimed by the applicant is based on the delayed&nbsp;payment of the invoice and interest thereon at the rate of 24% per&nbsp;annum.&nbsp;Whether the Applicant is entitled to claim&nbsp;interest at the rate of 24% per annum based on the invoice during&nbsp;business transaction between the parties is the subject matter of the&nbsp;trial and cannot be decided in summary suit. This Adjudicating&nbsp;Authority cannot isolate one invoice and conclude that amount is due and payable. Furthermore, this application has been filed to&nbsp;claim the interest at the rate of 24% per annum for the delayed&nbsp;payment to the said invoice. Hence, whether the applicant is&nbsp;entitled to the interest at the rate of 24% per annum cannot be&nbsp;decided, without trial based on the invoice which is not signed by&nbsp;the Corporate Debtor in token of acceptance. There are several&nbsp;other transactions and business dealings but the applicant has filed&nbsp;only one invoice. Hence, there is clearly suppression of material&nbsp;facts.
						</p>
						<p>
							It is admitted by the counsel for applicant that, amount due&nbsp;under invoice has been paid by Corporate Debtor. The question&nbsp;before us is whether Operational Creditor is entitled to claim&nbsp;&ldquo;interest on the delayed payment&rdquo;. If so, what is the rate of interest&nbsp;the applicant is entitled to and from which period is the amount, if&nbsp;any, is due and payable? Furthermore, whether the rate of interest&nbsp;was mutually agreed between parties and both are bound by it?&nbsp;We cannot conclude that based on the copy of invoice that applicant is entitled for the interest at the rate of 24% per annum&nbsp;since it is the subject matter of the City Civil Court to adduce&nbsp;evidence and detailed trial is required to conclude whether any&nbsp;interest is due and payable by the Corporate Debtor to the&nbsp;Creditor, under invoice?&nbsp;In terms of the above circumstances, this application&nbsp;IBA/294/2020 is hereby dismissed. No costs.
