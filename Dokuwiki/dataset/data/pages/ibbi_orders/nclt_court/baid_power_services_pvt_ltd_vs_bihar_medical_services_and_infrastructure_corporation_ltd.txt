	<h1>
		Whether an award under MSMED Act, 2006 could be treated as an award enforceable and executable before NCLT when the award itself is under challenge before High Court &ndash; Baid Power Services Pvt. Ltd. Vs. M/s. Bihar Medical Services and Infrastructure Corporation Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		May 19, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		The issue that crops up for determination is whether the Award under MSMED Act, 2006 could be treated as an award enforceable and executable before this Adjudicating Authority when the Award itself is under challenge before the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court. But before that the decisions cited by Ld. Counsels would be necessary to be gone into. The Adjudicating Authority held that in present case it is noticed that the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court is seized of the issues on legality and propriety of the MSMED Award, which forms basis of the claim here. Therefore, judicial propriety demands that this Adjudicating Authority should await the outcome of said proceedings pending in a higher forum before further delving into the maintainability or merits of the matter. The instant matter is therefore adjourned sine die with liberty to mention as and when the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court decides the Writ Petition.
	</p>

					</p>

						The issue that crops up for determination is whether the Award under MSMED Act, 2006 could be treated as an award enforceable and executable before this Adjudicating Authority when the Award itself is under challenge before the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court. But before that the decisions cited by Ld. Counsels would be necessary to be gone into.
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority held that in present case it is noticed that the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court is seized of the issues on legality and propriety of the MSMED Award, which forms basis of the claim here. Therefore, judicial propriety demands that this Adjudicating Authority should await the outcome of said proceedings pending in a higher forum before further delving into the maintainability or merits of the matter. The instant matter is therefore adjourned sine die with liberty to mention as and when the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court decides the Writ Petition.
