		<h1>
			The mandate with requirement of obtaining of 90% of approval votes of CoC under Section 12A of IBC is not required if an application for withdrawal have already being filed before the constitution of CoC &ndash; Punjab National Bank Vs. Mr. Harshad S Deshpande, RP &ndash; NCLT Mumbai Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			December 29, 2021
		</div>
		<p>
			The Corporate Debtor settled the dues of the original Operational Creditor pursuant to the settlement agreement between the parties on 04.09.2021 soon after initiation of admission of CIRP on 03.08.2021. The IRP upon receipt of Form FA immediately has filed an application to withdraw the CIRP against the Corporate Debtor. The CoC was constituted post filing of the Petition seeking withdrawal of the CIRP against the Corporate Debtor. The first meeting of COC was held on 29.09.2021. The mandate with requirement of obtaining of 90% of approval votes of CoC was not required as the Application for withdrawal had already being filed before the constitution of CoC. The question now remains to be answered is whether the Adjudicating Authority can allow a withdrawal of CIRP against Corporate Debtor on an application filed under Section 12A before constitution of COC and in the present factual matrix that the CoC happened to be constituted at a latter date. AA referred judgment of the Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court in Swiss Ribbons Pvt. Ltd. [2019]  03 SC and held that no prejudice would be caused to the Financial Creditors whose Petition is pending adjudication before NCLT, if the withdrawal of CIRP against the Corporate Debtor is allowed in view of Form FA being filed and that the settlement agreement has been entered between the parties.
		</p>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Corporate Debtor settled the dues of&nbsp;the original Operational Creditor pursuant to the settlement&nbsp;agreement between the parties on 04.09.2021 soon after initiation of&nbsp;admission of CIRP on 03.08.2021. The IRP upon receipt of Form FA&nbsp;immediately has filed an application to withdraw the CIRP against the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor. The CoC was constituted post filing of the Petition&nbsp;seeking withdrawal of the CIRP against the Corporate Debtor. The&nbsp;first meeting of COC was held on 29.09.2021. The mandate with&nbsp;requirement of obtaining of 90% of approval votes of CoC was not&nbsp;required as the Application for withdrawal had already being filed&nbsp;before the constitution of CoC.
						</p>
						<p>
							It is clear that once a code gets triggered by admission of CIRP&nbsp;against the Corporate Debtor, it is necessary that the body which is&nbsp;to oversee the resolution process must be consulted before any&nbsp;individual Corporate Debtor is allowed to settle his claims. This being&nbsp;a collective action is a proceeding in rem. The moot question now&nbsp;remains to be answered is whether the Adjudicating Authority can&nbsp;allow a withdrawal of CIRP against Corporate Debtor on an application&nbsp;filed under Section 12A before constitution of COC and in the present&nbsp;factual matrix that the CoC happened to be constituted at a latter&nbsp;date. The Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court in&nbsp;
							<em>
								Swiss Ribbons Pvt. Ltd. vs. Union&nbsp;
							</em>
							<em>
								of India&nbsp;[2019]  03 SC&nbsp;
							</em>
							has categorically held that at any stage where the CoC is not&nbsp;yet constituted, a party can approach NCLT directly, the Tribunal may&nbsp;in exercise of the inherent powers under Rule 11 of NCLT Rules may&nbsp;allow or disallow an application for withdrawal of CIRP. The claim and&nbsp;rights of other creditors as it stands is not prejudiced/altered by the&nbsp;withdrawal of CIRP of Corporate Debtor.
						</p>
						<p>
							This Bench is of the considered opinion that no prejudice would be&nbsp;caused to the Financial Creditors namely the to the Financial Creditors&nbsp;whose Petition vide C.P. No. 248 of 2018 and C.P. 3572 of 2019 is&nbsp;pending adjudication before NCLT, if the withdrawal of CIRP against&nbsp;the Corporate Debtor is allowed in view of Form FA being filed and&nbsp;that the settlement agreement has been entered between the parties.&nbsp;Therefore, the objection of the Financial Creditors in I.A. 2440 of 2021&nbsp;in C.P. 2390 of 2019 is untenable as he has already enforced his&nbsp;Financial debt vide C.P. No. 3572 of 2019 and the same is pending&nbsp;for adjudication before Bench 2 of NCLT, Mumbai Bench. Further, his&nbsp;rights as a creditor are no way altered/ affected by withdrawal of CIRP&nbsp;against the Corporate Debtor. It is also brought to our notice that one&nbsp;of the member of CoC has consented to the said withdrawal.&nbsp;This Bench allows withdrawal of CIRP against the Corporate Debtor,&nbsp;the Corporate Debtor is relieved from the rigour of the CIRP and&nbsp;erstwhile management is reinstated to the board of Corporate Debtor.&nbsp;I.A. 1990 of 2021 is allowed and disposed off.

								(p13-17)
