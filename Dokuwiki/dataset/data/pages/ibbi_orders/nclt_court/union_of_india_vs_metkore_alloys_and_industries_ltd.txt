	<h1>
		If Liquidation Process under IBC is completed and the Company is taken over by the Successful Bidder, application u/s 272(1)(e)/271(c) of Companies Act, 2013 cannot be maintained &ndash; Union of India Vs. Metkore Alloys and Industries Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Hyderabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		September 1, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		In this case, an application is filed u/s 272(1)(e) read with p 271(c) of Companies Act, 2013, read with p 243 of the Companies Act, 1956 read with rule 11 of the NCLT Rules, 2016 seeking winding up of the Metkore Alloys and Industries Ltd. NCLT Hyderabad Bench held that the company already underwent the process of liquidation and Liquidator was appointed, who declared the successful bidder and had sought for closure of the liquidation process. In similar application, Hyderabad Bench-1 passed an order returning the application therein by observing that the continuation of proceedings against the respondent is impermissible under Law. The application was returned, giving liberty to the applicant after a decision is taken on approval/rejection of the resolution plan in the ongoing CIRP process. The said Bench relied on orders passed by the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court of Calcutta in Avani Projects &amp; Infrastructures Ltd Vs Ornate Tradcom Pvt.Ltd CA 92 of 2019. Since in this case, the liquidation process is completed and the company is taken over by the successful bidder, this application cannot be maintained against the CD and is hence dismissed.
	</p>
</div>

		</strong>

	<p>
		<em>
			<strong>
				 Facts of the case
			</strong>
		</em>
	</p>
	<p>
		This application is filed u/s 272(1)(e) read with p 271(c) of Companies Act, 2013, read with p 243 of the Companies Act, 1956 read with rule 11 of the NCLT Rules, 2016 seeking winding up of the Respondent Company, namely, Metkore Alloys and Industries Limited and for the following reliefs:
	</p>
	<ul>
		<li>
			a) An order winding-up of the Respondent Company in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013;
		</li>
		<li>
			b) Appoint an Official Liquidator to take charge of the affairs of the Company and wind up the Company in accordance with law;
		</li>
		<li>
			c) An order awarding costs of this Petition in favour of the Petitioner and against the Respondent Company; and
		</li>
		<li>
			d) Such other or further order/s as this Tribunal may deem it fit and proper in facts and circumstances of the case.
		</li>
	</ul>
	<p>
		Counsel for the Petitioner&nbsp;submits that the provisions of Section 271(c) r/w 272(1) (e) 1/w 273 (1) (b) to (e) of the Companies Act, 2013 r/w 243 of the Companies Act, 1956 read with Rule 11 of the National Company Law Tribunal Rules, 2016, provide for an order of winding of a company or such other orders as the Tribunal may deem fit on the ground that affairs of a company are being conducted in a manner prejudicial to the public interest.
	</p>
	<p>
		<em>
			<strong>
				 Decision of NCLT
			</strong>
		</em>
	</p>
	<p>
		An Order was passed in IA No.1196/2023, which is an application moved for closure of Liquidation Process. Since the successful bidder came forth for purchase of the company, we allowed the said application. From the above, it can be noted that the company already underwent the process of liquidation and Liquidator was appointed, who declared the successful bidder and had sought for closure of the liquidation process. In similar application, Hyderabad Bench-1 passed an order returning the application therein by observing that the continuation of proceedings against the respondent is impermissible under Law. The application was returned, giving liberty to the applicant after a decision is taken on approval/rejection of the resolution plan in the ongoing CIRP process. The said Bench relied on orders passed by the Hon&rsquo;ble High Court of Calcutta in
		<em>
			 Avani Projects &amp; Infrastructures Ltd Vs Ornate Tradcom Pvt.Ltd CA 92 of 2019
		</em>
		 .
	</p>
	<p>
		Since in this case, the liquidation process is completed and the company is taken over by the successful bidder, this application cannot be maintained against the CD and is hence dismissed.
	</p>
