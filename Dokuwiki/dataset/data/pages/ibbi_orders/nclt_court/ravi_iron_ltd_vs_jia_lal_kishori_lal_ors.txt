		<h1>
			Since there is a specific provision under the IBC (Section 238A of IBC) about the limitation, Section 25(3) of Indian contract act is not applicable &ndash; Ravi Iron Ltd. Vs. Jia Lal Kishori Lal Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT New Delhi Bench Court-V
		</h1>
		<div>
			January 5, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			The Applicant placed reliance on Section 25(3) of the Indian Contract Act and submits that since, there is a promise to pay the amount, therefore, the case of the Applicant comes under the purview of Section 25(3) of the Indian Contract Act and the provision of the Limitation Act is not applicable here. The Adjudicating Authority held that it is admitted position of law that the IBC is a complete code in itself and has overriding effect on provisions with IBC. Section 238A IBC, 2016, deals about the limitation and it says that the limitation Act is applicable. Since there is a specific provision under the IBC, which says that the limitation Act is applicable, we are unable to accept the submissions of the Applicant that the Section 238 of IBC is not applicable and Section 25(3) of Indian contract act is applicable.
		</p>
	</div>
</p>

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							Ld. Counsel for the Applicant submits that though the invoices&nbsp;were raised on 10.12.2007 &amp; 09.01.2008, but the matter was referred to the&nbsp;mediation and vide order dated 16.11.2015, the matter was settled and&nbsp;some cheques were issued, which amounts to the&nbsp;acknowledgement of debt. He also placed reliance on Section 25(3) of the&nbsp;Indian Contract Act and submits that since, there is a promise to pay the&nbsp;amount, therefore, the case of the Applicant comes under the purview of&nbsp;Section 25 (3) of the Indian Contract Act and the provision of the Limitation&nbsp;Act is not applicable here.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority held that it is admitted position of law that the IBC is a complete code in itself&nbsp;and has overriding effect on provisions with IBC.&nbsp;Section 238A IBC, 2016, deals about the&nbsp;limitation and it says that the limitation Act is applicable. Since there is a&nbsp;specific provision under the IBC, which says that the limitation Act is&nbsp;applicable, therefore, at this juncture, we would also like to refer to the&nbsp;judgment of the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT in&nbsp;
							<em>
								Prayag Polytech Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Gem Batteries&nbsp;
							</em>
							<em>
								Pvt. Ltd.&nbsp;(2019)  362 NCLAT
							</em>
							, the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT held that &ldquo;
							<em>
								As regard relying on Section 10&nbsp;
							</em>
							<em>
								of the Contract Act, 1872, in our view IBC is a complete code in itself.&nbsp;Section 238 of IBC has overriding effect on provisions inconsistent&nbsp;
							</em>
							<em>
								with IBC
							</em>
							&ldquo;.&nbsp;In sequel to above, Hence, we are unable to accept the submissions of the&nbsp;Applicant that the Section 238 of IBC is not applicable and Section 25(3) of&nbsp;Indian contract act is applicable. Since the date of default as per the&nbsp;averments made in Part IV of the application is on 10.01.2008, so, the&nbsp;application is hopelessly barred by limitation.&nbsp;Accordingly, the application is dismissed being barred by limitation.
							<strong>
								(p8-12)
							</strong>
						</p>
