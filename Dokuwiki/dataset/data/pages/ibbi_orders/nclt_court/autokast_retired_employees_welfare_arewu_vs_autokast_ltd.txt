	<h1>
		The claims of workmen are classified as &ldquo;service claim&rdquo; and &ldquo;welfare claims&rdquo;. The service is considered as operational debt but the welfare claim does not consider as operational debt &ndash; Autokast Retired Employees welfare (AREWU) Vs. M/s. Autokast Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Kochi Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		November 23, 2022
	</div>
	<p>
		This petition u/s 9 of IBC is filed in respect of retirement benefits due of the petitioners payable by the respondent. In the petition it is captioned as retirement benefit/outstanding arrears of pay etc. The claims of workmen are classified as &ldquo;service claim&rdquo; and &ldquo;welfare claims&rdquo;. The service is considered as operational debt but the welfare claim does not consider as operational debt. This position is explained by Hon&lsquo;ble NCLAT in Kishore K Lonkar Hindustan Antibiotics Ltd. In this petition it is not separately mentioned what is the quantum of &ldquo;service claim&rdquo; and &ldquo;welfare claim&rdquo; in the absence of any material in this regard the due amount of petitioner cannot be considered as an operational debt. Thus, this point is answered.
	</p>
