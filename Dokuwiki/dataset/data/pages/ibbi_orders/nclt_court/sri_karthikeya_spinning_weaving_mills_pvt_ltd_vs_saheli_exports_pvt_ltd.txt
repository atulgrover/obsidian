						<p>
							The question before us is regarding the maintainability of the above application i.e. &ldquo;whether this Tribunal can entertain the present application u/s 9 of the IBC pending disposal of Appeal u/s 37 of Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996?&rdquo;
						</p>
						<p>
							<em>
								<strong>
									Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
								</strong>
							</em>
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority holds that that the object of the IBC Code is not to replace debt adjudication and enforcement under other Acts including Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996. Appeal u/s 37 of Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 is presently pending before Hon&rsquo;ble Madras High Court which clearly indicates that there is a Pre-Existing dispute between both the parties. The present matter is squarely covered under the Judgments of Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court in the matters of&nbsp;
							<em>
								Mobilox Innovations Private Limited vs. Kirusa Software Private Limited&nbsp;[2017]  01 SC
							</em>
							&nbsp;and&nbsp;
							<em>
								K. Kishan V/s Vijay Nirman Company Pvt. Ltd.&nbsp;[2018]  01 SC
							</em>
							. Since a pre-existing dispute exists between the parties, this Application is dismissed. No order as to costs.
						</p>
