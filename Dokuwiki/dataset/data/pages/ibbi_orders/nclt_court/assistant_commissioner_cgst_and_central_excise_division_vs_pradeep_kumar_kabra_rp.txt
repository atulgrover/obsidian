	<h1>
		When Resolution Plan is approved by CoC and the same is pending for approval before Adjudicating Authority, no intervention is allowed against the decision taken by the RP in admission of claim amount &ndash; Assistant Commissioner CGST and Central Excise Division Vs. Pradeep Kumar Kabra RP of Cengres Tiles Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Ahmedabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		September 20, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		NCLT Ahmedabad Bench held that even if the claim amount is admitted in full then also the operational creditor will not be paid more than what is proposed to be paid and therefore, at this stage when the resolution plan is approved by the CoC and the same is pending for approval before this Adjudicating Authority, we are not inclined to intervene in the decision taken by the RP.
	</p>

					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							This application is filed under Section 60(5) of IBC by Assisstant Commissioner, CGST and Central Excise Division (Applicant) against Pradeep Kumar Kabra Resolution Professional (RP) (Respondent) of the corporate debtor-M/s Cengres Tiles Limited seeking direction to the RP to accept and admit the claim of the applicant amounting to Rs.39,86,04,041/- (Form-C) instead of admitted claim amount of Rs.11,76,90,942/-.
						</li>
						<li>
							The CoC approved the resolution plan of Thirdeye Tradelink Private Limited (in consortium with Turba International LLP, Baluram Lahoti, Radheshyam Nuwal) with 99.86% votes in its 11th CoC meeting which was held on 24.01.2023 and the application under p 30(6) of the Code for approval of the said resolution plan was filed on 20.03.2023 before this Adjudicating Authority.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							It is also noted that the liquidation value is Rs.49,88,25,122/- as such if the said liquidation value was to be considered in the event of liquidation&nbsp;as a total amount to be distributed as per p 53 then the operational&nbsp;creditors would have got nothing. Similarly, we find that the proposed plan value is Rs.45,20,00,000/- and if that was to be distributed in accordance with p 53 then also nothing would have remained for the operational creditors.
						</li>
						<li>
							Considering the facts of the case we are of the view that even if the claim amount is admitted in full then also the operational creditor will not be paid more than what is proposed to be paid and therefore, at this stage when the resolution plan is approved by the CoC and the same is pending for approval before this Adjudicating Authority, we are not inclined to intervene in the decision taken by the RP.
						</li>
						<li>
							With this IA 439 of 2023 is disposed of.
							<strong>
								 (p6-8)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
