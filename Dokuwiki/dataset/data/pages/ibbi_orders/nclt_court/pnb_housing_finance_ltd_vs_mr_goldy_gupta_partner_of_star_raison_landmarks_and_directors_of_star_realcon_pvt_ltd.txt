	<h1>
		Commencement of CIRP against the Corporate Debtor is not a condition precedent for maintaining an application under Section 95 of the IBC, 2016 filed for initiating IR Process against the Personal Guarantor of the Corporate Debtor before the NCLT &ndash; PNB Housing Finance Ltd. Vs. Mr. Goldy Gupta Partner of M/s. Star Raison Landmarks and Directors of Star Realcon Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT New Delhi Bench Court-II
	</h1>
	<div>
		November 11, 2021
	</div>
	<p>
		In the present case, though the CIRP has not been initiated against the Corporate Debtor, as revealed by the parties, the Applicant/Creditor has filed Applications No. CP. (IB)-408/2021 titled as M/s. PNB Housing Finance Ltd. Vs. M/s. Star Realcon Pvt. Ltd. and CP. No. (IB)-407/2021 titled as M/s. PNB Housing Finance Ltd. Vs. M/s. Pinnacle Housing Pvt. Ltd against the Borrowers/Corporate Debtors before this Adjudicating Authority to initiate CIRP against them. Hence, we find that the facts of the present case and issues raised herein are akin to the facts of the Company Petition No. (IB)-395(ND)/2021- PNB Housing Finance Ltd. Vs. Mr. Mohit Arora, decided by this Bench recently vide Order dated 29.09.2021 and the issues raised therein. Therefore, in the present case having the similar facts and issues, we would like to reiterate that in the event of the Application(s) for Insolvency and Bankruptcy (IB) being pending for initiation of CIR Process before this Adjudicating Authority, commencement of CIRP against the Corporate Debtor is not a condition precedent for maintaining an application under Section 95 of the IBC, 2016 filed for initiating IR Process against the Personal Guarantor of the Corporate Debtor before the NCLT.
	</p>

						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						The Adjudicating Authority observed that:
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The present Application has been preferred by M/s PNB Housing&nbsp;Finance Ltd. (the Applicant/Financial Creditor), under Section 95(1)&nbsp;read with Rule 7(2) of the IBC(AAA&nbsp;for IRP for Personal Guarantors to Corporate&nbsp;Debtor) Rules, 2019, for initiating the Insolvency Resolution Process (IR Process) against Mr. Goldy Gupta (Personal Guarantor&nbsp;/Debtor), who is the Partner of M/s Star Raison Landmarks and&nbsp;Directors of Star Realcon Pvt. Ltd. (Corporate Debtor).
						</li>
						<li>
							In the present case, though the CIRP has not been initiated&nbsp;against the Corporate Debtor, as revealed by the parties, the&nbsp;Applicant/Creditor has filed Applications No. CP. (IB)-408/2021 titled as&nbsp;M/s. PNB Housing Finance Ltd. Vs. M/s. Star Realcon Pvt. Ltd. and CP.&nbsp;No. (IB)-407/2021 titled as M/s. PNB Housing Finance Ltd. Vs. M/s.&nbsp;Pinnacle Housing Pvt. Ltd against the Borrowers/Corporate Debtors&nbsp;before this Adjudicating Authority to initiate CIRP against them.&nbsp;Hence, we find that the facts of the present case and issues raised herein&nbsp;are akin to the facts of the&nbsp;Company Petition No. (IB)-395(ND)/2021&ndash;&nbsp;PNB Housing Finance Ltd. Vs. Mr. Mohit Arora, decided by this Bench&nbsp;recently vide Order dated 29.09.2021 and the issues raised therein.
						</li>
						<li>
							Therefore, in the present case having the similar facts and issues,&nbsp;we would like to reiterate that in the event of the Application(s) for&nbsp;Insolvency and Bankruptcy (IB) being pending for initiation of CIR Process&nbsp;before this Adjudicating Authority, commencement of CIRP against the&nbsp;Corporate Debtor is not a condition precedent for maintaining an&nbsp;application under Section 95 of the IBC, 2016 filed for initiating IR Process&nbsp;against the Personal Guarantor of the Corporate Debtor before the NCLT.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Applicant has averred in its application that the Personal&nbsp;Guarantor has committed, prima facie, default in making repayment of the&nbsp;loan, for which he has given the personal guarantee to the Applicant/&nbsp;Creditor on behalf of the Corporate Debtors/Borrowers. Hence, we are&nbsp;inclined to proceed in the matter in accordance with the law. As&nbsp;regards, the issue of limited notice to the personal guarantor as held by&nbsp;the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT in the matter of Ravi Ajit Kulkarni V. State Bank of&nbsp;India in
							<em>
								 (2021) 396 NCLAT
							</em>
							 vide its&nbsp;judgement dated 12.08.2021, since the Personal Guarantor has already&nbsp;caused appearance on the advance notice, we do not see any need for&nbsp;issuing further notice.
							<strong>
								 (p19-21)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
