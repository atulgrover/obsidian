		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority observed that it appears that it is undisputed fact that the copy of resolution plan has not been provided to the suspended management. Further the law has been well settled by the Hon'ble Supreme Court in the case of Vijay Kumar Jain Vs. Standard Chartered Bank &amp; Ors. reported in [2019]  24 SC. In view of the above, the suspended management must be provided with the copy of the resolution plan. However, the resolution professional can take an undertaking from members of the erstwhile Board of Directors to maintain confidentiality. In the present case, the reply of resolution professional to the objection of the suspended management, states about the 4th CoC meeting, wherein the procedure to obtain the data was informed. Hence, the 4th CoC meeting talks about the access to be provided to RA &amp; CoC members and nowhere it talks about the access to be provided to the suspended management.

							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							This application under Section 30(6) read with Section 31 of IBC is filed by the applicant-Resolution Professional of the corporate debtor- Rajpal Abhikaran Pvt. Ltd. for approval of the Resolution Plan submitted by Agarwal Real City Pvt. Ltd. The suspended management filed their objections to the present application. One of the objection placed by the suspended management is that the suspended management being the participant of the CoC meetings was never provided with the copies of the resolution plan placed before the CoC which is against the provisions of the Code.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority observed that it appears that it is undisputed fact that the copy of resolution plan has not been provided to the suspended management. Further the law has been well settled by the Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court in the case of&nbsp;
							<em>
								Vijay Kumar Jain Vs. Standard Chartered Bank &amp; Ors.
							</em>
							&nbsp;reported in&nbsp;
							<em>
								[2019]  24 SC
							</em>
							. In view of the above, the suspended management must be provided with the copy of the resolution plan. However, the resolution professional can take an undertaking from members of the erstwhile Board of Directors to maintain confidentiality. In the present case, the reply of resolution professional to the objection of the suspended management, states about the 4th CoC meeting, wherein the procedure to obtain the data was informed. Hence, the 4
							<sup>
								th
							</sup>
							&nbsp;CoC meeting talks about the access to be provided to RA &amp; CoC members and nowhere it talks about the access to be provided to the suspended management.
						</p>
						<p>
							Further, the Adjudicating Authority held direct the resolution professional to provide the resolution plans to the suspended management and then convene a meeting of the CoC and the CoC will deliberate on the resolution plans afresh and either reject them or approve them with the requisite majority, after which, the further procedure detailed in the Code and the Regulations will be followed. Further, the resolution professional and CoC may consider the other objections of the suspended management, if relevant. It is to be done within two weeks. We may indicate that the time has been utilized in these proceedings must be excluded from the period of the resolution process of the corporate debtor as has been held in&nbsp;
							<em>
								Vijay Kumar Jain Vs. Standard Chartered Bank &amp; Ors.
							</em>
							&nbsp;
							<em>
								[2019]  24 SC.
							</em>
							&nbsp;IA/12(MP)2022 to come up in cause list after the resolution&nbsp;professional files minutes of the meeting of the CoC as above.
