	<h1>
		Can a Secured Creditor withhold the amount received under an Insurance Policy after the date of approval of the Resolution plan? &ndash; SMC Infrastructures Pvt. Ltd. Vs. State Bank of India &ndash; NCLT Hyderabad Bench
	</h1>
	<div>
		September 8, 2023
	</div>
	<p>
		The Bank submitted that the policy amount was payable prior to initiation of the CIRP process but was only remitted post approval of Rplan. Resolution Professional failed to collect and monitor the Policy, as a result, it did not reflect in the assets of the Corporate Debtor and the SRA bid for the company without taking the amount into consideration. The CoC also approved the plan without having any knowledge about this amount. Surrender of policy and payments due occurred prior to approval of the Resolution Plan, therefore, the Bank being a secured creditor is entitled to the amount. NCLT Hyderabad Bench held that any money with the third parties like creditors in form of security deposits, margin moneys, term deposit receipts or retention money will be released to corporate debtor. Further this amount can be considered as retention money also as this was retained by insurance company on account of lock in period clause in the Insurance Policy. From the above it is very clear that the amount payable by the insurance company belongs to the corporate debtor and the corporate debtor which is under the new management is entitled for the same. Further in terms of the approved resolution plan there is no such clause which provides for payments of receivables to the creditors.
	</p>
</div>

							 I. Case Reference
						</strong>
					</p>

State Bank of IndiaCorporate Debtor:GKC Projects Ltd.Application/Appeal No.:IA.No.354 of 2023 in CP (IB) NO. 461/7/HDB/2019Judgment Date:25-Aug-23Court/Bench:NCLT Hyderabad BenchMember (Judicial):Shri Venkata Ramakrishna Badarinath NandulaMember (Technical):Shri Charan Singh
					<p>
						<strong>
							 II. Brief about the decision
						</strong>
					</p>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Facts of the case
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							The Corporate Debtor had procured an insurance policy, viz., SBI LifeFlexi Smart Plus dated 14.10.2015 for its employee, namely, Mr. Raja Sekhar under Employer and Employee Scheme from SBI Life Insurance Company Limited with a term of 5 years with effective from 08.10.2015 and Mr. K V Raja Sekhar, Managing Director of the Corporate Debtor was nominated as the life assured under the said policy.
						</li>
						<li>
							NCLT approved the Resolution plan filed by the Successful Resolution Applicant vide its order dated 30.03.2021. In accordance with the terms of the Approved Resolution Plan, the management of Corporate Debtor has changed, and Successful Resolution Applicant is currently in control of the affairs of the Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							The Applicants have requested the State Bank of India/Respondent(Financial Creditor) for release of the amounts received in lieu of the Policy commencing from 05.01.2022 vide various Emails, but the Respondent neither transferred the same nor responded to the emails sent by Corporate Debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							Respondent is one of the Financial Creditor of Corporate Debtor whose claims are being dealt with Applicant No.1.
						</li>
						<li>
							Thus, the present Application is filed stating that the SBI is not&nbsp;replying to the Applicants regarding the amounts received from SBI Life Insurance Company Limited, which is illegal and further stated that there are no such restrictions placed/imposed under the Resolution Plan approved for the Corporate Debtor giving right to the SBI to withhold the said amounts.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Submission of the SBI/Financial Creditor/Secured Creditor
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							With regard to the Scheme of the Insurance Policy, premium is paid by the Corporate Debtor in the name of its key managerial personnel in order to protect the Company against the risk of certain contingencies.
						</li>
						<li>
							The details regarding Insurance amount have not been mentioned in the information memorandum and the amount due from the Company towards the surrender value of the policy was not included in the assets of the Company and the amount was not taken into consideration by the Resolution Professional (RP) while valuing the Corporate Debtor in the CIRP.
						</li>
						<li>
							The CIRP process was concluded without any reference to or consideration of this amount. The RP should collect the information regarding the list of assets and financial and operational payments for the past two years and also should monitor the assets of the corporate debtor.
						</li>
						<li>
							the policy amount was payable in 2020 prior to initiation of the CIRP process but was only remitted on 20.04.2021.
						</li>
						<li>
							Resolution Professional failed to collect and monitor the Policy, as a result, it did not reflect in the assets of the Corporate Debtor and the SRA bid for the company without taking the amount into consideration.
						</li>
						<li>
							The CoC also approved the plan without having any knowledge about this amount. Surrender of policy and payments due occurred prior to approval of the Resolution Plan, therefore, the Respondent being a secured creditor is entitled to the amount.
						</li>
					</ul>
					<p>
						<em>
							<strong>
								 Decision of the Adjudicating Authority
							</strong>
						</em>
					</p>
					<ul>
						<li>
							In accordance with the approval of the Resolution plan, the corporate debtor is now under the control of the SRA/Applicant no.1 and its bank account maintained with the respondent bank is also operated upon by the new management of corporate debtor/SRA. The petitioner&nbsp;argued that if this bank account would not have been maintained in the&nbsp;SBIs bank, the money even then would have come into their account with some other banks and Respondent bank would not have any say or control on the funds. Petitioner further submitted that Respondent/SBI is mis-utilizing their position of being a banker to them.
						</li>
						<li>
							According to the Resolution plan there cannot be any exclusions in relation to any assets of the Corporate Debtor. With regard to the Insurance Policy namely SBI Life-Flexi Smart Plus bearing policy number 1M007936701 dated 14.10.2015 for the Managing Director of the Applicant No.2 under Employer and Employee Scheme, the benefits of the policy will come back to the Applicant No.2 only.
						</li>
						<li>
							Though this insurance policy per se is not covered under this clause as neither financial creditors nor resolution professional were having knowledge of it, but the intent of this clause is very clear that any money&nbsp;with the third parties like creditors in form of security deposits, margin&nbsp;moneys, term deposit receipts or retention money will be released to corporate debtor. Further this amount can be considered as retention money also as this was retained by insurance company on account of lock in period clause in the Insurance Policy.
						</li>
						<li>
							From the above it is very clear that the amount payable by the insurance company belongs to the corporate debtor and the corporate debtor which is under the new management is entitled for the same. Further in terms of the approved resolution plan there is no such clause which provides for payments of receivables to the creditors.
						</li>
						<li>
							On the basis of the facts and legal position as stated above, the respondent/SBI is not entitled to the said amount. Therefore, we direct respondent/SBI to allow the claim amount under the said insurance policy credited in the Applicant no.1 account to be used by Applicant No.1.&nbsp;The Application is allowed and disposed of accordingly.
							<strong>
								 (p12-13)
							</strong>
						</li>
					</ul>
