		<h1>
			When Liquidator did not receive even a single bid initially, it was imperative on the part of the liquidator to have extended the auction by reasonable time to enable the intending bidders to participate in the process &ndash; Chinar Steel Segment Centre Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Raj Singhania, Liquidator of Gontermann-Peipers (India) Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Kolkata Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			April 21, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			The Adjudicating Authority set aside the entire auction process observing that the successful bidder is an LLP having with Rupees One Lakh as the Capital. The AA held that the liquidator could not have ignored this facet of auction, which apparently can have far reaching consequences for CD to continue as a going concern along with its 500 employees and incurring other costs. There is a complete silence on this by liquidator or Stakeholder&rsquo;s Committee except for taking technical plea/s to oppose it. That the liquidator did not enter upon due-diligence to verify as to how an LLP with Rupees One Lakh as the Capital will be able to continue the Corporate Debtor as a going concern and pay to its 500 employees. Having authorization to conduct multiple auctions and while having time in his hand and with a view to get best possible offer, when he did not receive even a single bid initially, it was imperative on the part of the liquidator to have extended the auction by reasonable time to enable the intending bidders to participate in the process and place their bids in accordance with terms of original notice inviting bid.
		</p>
