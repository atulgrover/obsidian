		<h1>
			Doctrine of Merger in case of subsequent appellate court order &ndash; Arrow Engineering Ltd. Vs. Golden Tobacco Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Ahmedabad Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			June 15, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			In this case, Hon'ble NCLAT in its order dated 02.12.2021 in the last para specifically directed the Adjudicating Authority to pass consequential orders within one month from the date on which copy of this order is produced before the Adjudicating Authority. Learned Counsel appearing for the Corporate Debtor stated that as per the Doctrine of Merger, order of Hon'ble NCLAT merged into the order of Hon'ble Supreme Court on 05.05.2022 and one month period mentioned in the order of Hon'ble NCLAT giving liberty to the parties to enter into settlement be considered from 06.05.2022. The Adjudicating Authority relied on Doctrine of Merger with observations of Hon'ble Supreme Court and Hon'ble High Court in various matters and held that in compliance of the order dated 02.12.2021 passed by the Hon'ble NCLAT which is merged in the order passed by Hon'ble Supreme Court dated 05.05.2022, this application is admitted and moratorium as described under Section 14 of IBC, 2016 is triggered from the date of this order.

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							The IB Application was filed by Arrow Engineering Limited, a Financial Creditor against the Golden Tobacco Limited (Corporate Debtor) for initiation of CIRP. This Bench vide&nbsp;order dated 25.01.2021&nbsp;dismissed the application on the ground of not being maintainable. The Financial Creditor impugned the said order before Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT and the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT Principal Bench vide order reported at&nbsp;(2021)  558 NCLAT&nbsp;allowed the appeal by setting aside the order dated 25.01.2021 with further directions to the Adjudicating Authority to admit the application filed u/s 7 of the IBC, 2016 and pass consequential orders. The said order of Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT was impugned by the Corporate Debtor by way of Civil Appeal No. 7715 of 2021 and Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court vide order&nbsp;dated 05.05.2022&nbsp;dismissed the appeal.
						</p>
						<p>
							Learned Counsel appearing for the Corporate Debtor stated that Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT in its order dated 02.12.2021 in the last para specifically directed the Adjudicating Authority to pass consequential orders within one month from the date on which copy of this order is produced before the Adjudicating Authority, and further mentioned that during this period, it is open to the parties to endeavour to enter into settlement. He further submitted that as per the Doctrine of Merger, order of Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT merged into the order of Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court on 05.05.2022 and one month period mentioned in the order of Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT giving liberty to the parties to enter into settlement be considered from 06.05.2022.
						</p>
						<p>
							The Adjudicating Authority relied on Doctrine of Merger with observations of Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court and Hon&rsquo;ble High Court in various matters which are mentioned below:
						</p>
						<ul>
							<li>
								In Gojer Bros (P) Limited. Vs. Ratan Lal Singh and further in A.V. Papayya Sastry Vs. Government of Andhra Pradesh reported in (2007) 4 SCC 221, 236.
							</li>
							<li>
								In CIT Vs. Amritlal Bhogilal &amp; Company reported in (1959) SCR 713
							</li>
							<li>
								The Hon&rsquo;ble Bombay High Court in CIT Vs. Tejaji Farasram Kharawala reported in (1953) SCC Online Bom 28
							</li>
						</ul>
						<p>
							As a sequeal to above discussion and in compliance of the order&nbsp;dated 02.12.2021&nbsp;passed by the Hon&rsquo;ble NCLAT which is merged in the order passed by Hon&rsquo;ble Supreme Court&nbsp;dated 05.05.2022, this application is admitted and moratorium as described under Section 14 of IBC, 2016 is triggered from the date of this order.
