<h1>Whether there is any locus standi of a Promotor/Suspended Director to intervene in an application filed by the Resolution Professional for seeking possession of the property from the respondent who has entered into a lease agreement with the Corporate Debtor? &ndash; G. Ramakrishna Reddy Vs. Dantu Indu Sekhar RP for Nexus Feeds Ltd. and Anr. &ndash; NCLT Hyderabad Bench</h1>
<div>March 20, 2024</div>
<p>Hon&rsquo;ble NCLT Hyderabad Bench held that applicant who is suspended director of the corporate debtor has no locus standi to intervene in an application filed by the resolution professional for seeking possession of the property from the respondent who is a third party and has no connection with the applicant.</p>

<p><strong> IN THE NATIONAL COMPANY LAW TRIBUNAL </strong> <br /> Hyderabad Bench</p>
<p><strong> G. Ramakrishna Reddy </strong> <br /> v. <br /> <strong> Dantu Indu Sekhar (RP) and Anr. </strong></p>
<p>Intervention Application (IBC) No. 42 of 2023 in IA. No. 1217/2023 in CP (IB) No. 389/9/HDB/2020 <br /> Decided on 11-Mar-24</p>

<p><strong> Add. Info: </strong></p>
<p><em> Corporate Debtor </em> :&nbsp;Nexus Feeds Ltd.</p>
<p><em> For Appellant(s) </em> :&nbsp;Bommineni Vivekananda, Advocate&nbsp;</p>
<hr />
<p><strong> Brief about the decision: </strong></p>
<p><em> <strong> Facts of the case </strong> </em></p>
<ul>
<li>The present application is filed by the Ex-promoter and Suspended Director of Board with a prayer to implead himself as a party Respondent in I.A. No. 1217 of 2023 in CP (IB) No. 389/9/HDB/2020.</li>
<li>the Corporate Debtor received a security deposit of Rs. 1 Cr in the year 2019 towards lease security from M/s. Nakshatra Feeds Limited in the form of stocks and the same was reflected in the audited financials. As the operations were affected due to Covid 19, M/s. Nakshatra Feeds Limited requested for a reduction of lease rent from Rs.15 lakhs to Rs.7.50 lakhs and the same was agreed and duly paid. The said fact was confirmed by the Applicant in every meeting of the CoC and M/s. Nakshatra Feeds Limited has been paying the rent accordingly till December, 2022 and stopped from January, 2023 onwards. It is further submitted that the receivables on account of lease rent arrived by the IRP/RP are not correct as all the rent details were remitted through Bank accounts, copies of which were already submitted to Resolution Professional.</li>
</ul>
<p><em> <strong> Decision of Adjudicating Authority </strong> </em></p>
<ul>
<li>The Corporate Debtor entered into a Registered lease agreement dated 21.08.2019 with M/s. Nakshatra Feeds Limited wherein the company has given on lease the factory premises and machinery from 01.04.2019 till 31.03.2024. Accordingly, the lease period is still&nbsp;subsisting. We find that the contentions put forth by the Applicant are essentially&nbsp;identical to those articulated by M/s. Nakshatra Feeds Limited in their reply in&nbsp;IA. 1217/2023. The applicant has failed to submit any proof evidencing that it is&nbsp;a stakeholder in any way in M/s Nakshatra Feeds Limited, respondent in IA no&nbsp;1217/2023 and therefore any order in 1217/2023 will have no bearing on the&nbsp;applicant in the present application. The Applicant also failed to present any fresh&nbsp;grounds to substantiate their petition for intervention in the Interlocutory&nbsp;application under consideration.</li>
<li>Keeping in view the above facts , we decide that applicant who is suspended director of the corporate debtor has no locus standi to intervene in an application filed by the resolution professional for seeking possession of the property from the respondent who is a third party and has no connection with the applicant. The point is accordingly answered.</li>
<li>In view of the answer to the point for our consideration, we decide that this application is liable to be dismissed. Hence dismissed and disposed without costs. <strong> (p5-7) </strong></li>
</ul>
