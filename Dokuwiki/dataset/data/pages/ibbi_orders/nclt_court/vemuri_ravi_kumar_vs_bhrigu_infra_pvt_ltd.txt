		<h1>
			NCLT directs class of Financial Creditors/Homebuyers to present on next hearing since they are not co-operating &ndash; Vemuri Ravi Kumar Vs. Bhrigu Infra Pvt. Ltd. &ndash; NCLT Hyderabad Bench
		</h1>
		<div>
			July 8, 2022
		</div>
		<p>
			CoC comprises of 7 real estate allotees, and so far there are no operational creditors. The Adjudicating Authority held that appointing an authorised representative on behalf of majority of the Class of financial creditor would be an additional burden and results in increasing the CIRP costs. At the same time the interest of justice would be served by allowing all the 7 class of financial creditors to form part of the CoC. Therefore, in view of the peculiar facts and circumstances we direct the IRP to proceed with the representation of all the 7 financial creditors instead of they being represented by authorized representative. Further, it held that since it is represented by Learned Counsel for IRP that class of financial creditors are not co-operating, let the Class of Financial Creditors be present on the next hearing.

						<p>
							<strong>
								II. Brief about the decision
							</strong>
						</p>
						<p>
							Learned Counsel for RP submitted that pursuant to Regulation 4(A) Publication regarding the choice of authorised representatives out of 7 class of financial creditors 4 have chosen one IRP as their authorized representative and the therefore, the IRP in terms of regulation 16(A) had decided to include said authorized representative. Therefore leave of this Tribunal is sought under this Petition. During the course of hearing, it is ascertained that COC comprises of 7 real estate allotees, and so far there are no operational creditors. It is stated that even the fee of IRP is so far not paid by the financial creditors. Considering the fact that there are only 7 class of financial creditors we feel that appointing an authorised representative on behalf of majority of the Class of financial creditor would be an additional burden and results in increasing the CIRP costs. At the same time the interest of justice would be served by allowing all the 7 class of financial creditors to form part of the COC.
						</p>
						<p>
							Therefore, in view of the peculiar facts and circumstances we direct the IRP to proceed with the representation of all the 7 financial creditors instead of they being represented by authorized representative.
						</p>
						<p>
							Issue notice to all the financial creditor. In the meanwhile Applicant/Financial Creditor is directed to pay the amount as per the agreement between the IRP and Financial Creditor dated 17.03.2022. Since it is represented by Learned Counsel for IRP that class of financial creditors are not co-operating, let the Class of Financial Creditors be present on the next hearing. The IRP to take notice to all of them to appear
