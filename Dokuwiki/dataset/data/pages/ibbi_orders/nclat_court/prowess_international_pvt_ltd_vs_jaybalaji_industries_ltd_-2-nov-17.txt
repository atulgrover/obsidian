	<h2>
		Prowess International Pvt. Ltd vs Jaybalaji Industries Ltd on 2 November, 2017
	</h2>
	<p>
		NATIONAL COMPANY LAW APPELLATE TRIBUNAL NEW DELHI Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 250 of 2017IN THE MATTER OF :Prowess International Pvt. Ltd. ... Appellant VersusJaybalaji Industries Ltd. ... RespondentPresent: For Appellant : Shri Akhilesh Shrivastava, Advocate with Ms. Suhita Mukhopadhyay, PCS ORDER
	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		02.11.2017 This appeal has been filed by the appellant under Section 61 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (hereinafter referred to as 'I&amp;B Code') against order dated 31st August, 2017 passed by the National Company Law Tribunal, Kolkata Bench, Kolkata (hereinafter referred to as 'Tribunal') in C.P. No. 29/KB/2017. By the impugned order, the Tribunal rejected the petition pferred by the appellant for winding- up under Sections 433, 434 and 439 of the Companies Act, 1956.
	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		A petition for condonation of 13 days' delay has also been pferred along with this appeal.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		At this stage, before condonation of delay, it is desirable to decide whether the appeal under Section 61 of the I&amp;B Code is maintainable or not.
	</p>
	<p title="Fact" data-structure="Facts">
		Admittedly, the appellant initially filed one winding-up petition against the respondent-Jaybalaji Industries Ltd., under Sections 433, 434 -2- and 439 of the Companies Act, 1956 before the Hon'ble Calcutta High Court, which was dismissed on 21st September, 2016. Thereafter, the appellant filed another Company Petition under Sections 433, 434 and 439 of the Companies Act, 1956 before the Tribunal on 5th January, 2017. The Tribunal, having noticed different facts including the provisions of Section 9 of the I&amp;B Code, held that the petition under Sections 433, 434 and 439 of the Companies Act, 1956 pferred on 5th January, 2017 was not maintainable.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		In view of the fact that the petition was filed under Sections 433, 434 and 439 of the Companies Act, 1956, which is held to be not maintainable by the Tribunal, we hold that the appeal under Section 61 of the I&amp;B Code is not maintainable.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		The appeal is accordingly dismissed. No costs.
	</p>
