	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		15.05.2017- These appeals under section 61 of Insolvency &amp; Bankruptcy Code, 2016 ("I &amp; B Code" for short) have been pferred by Appellant/ Operational Creditors M/s. J.R Agro Industries Pvt. Ltd., 3rd against order(s) dated March, 2017 etc, whereby the application pferred by Appellants were adjourned for another date.
	</p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		2. The grievance of the appellants was that the Adjudicating Authority has not passed the order within 14 days as pscribed under section 9 of the I &amp; B Code, which may render their applications infructuous.
	</p>
	<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">
		3. On 10th April, 2014, when the matter was taken up, this Appellate Tribunal issued notice on the Respondents.
	</p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		4. Today when the matter was taken up, Ld. Counsel for the 9th Appellant(s) brought to our notice three order(s) all dated May, 2017, passed by Adjudicating Authority in corresponding applications under section 9, whereby the Adjudicating Authority has pointed out the defects to be removed by each of the Appellants within 7 days. Ld. Counsel for the Appellant submits that the defects will be removed in course of day.
	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		5. The question as to whether 14 days' period pscribed under sub- section (5) of section 9 of the 'I &amp; B Code' is mandatory or directory fell for consideration before the Appellate Tribunal in "Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 9 of 2017, M/s. J.K. Jute Mills Company Limited Vs. M/s. Surendra Trading Company". In the said appeal, the Appellate -3- Tribunal held that the 14 days period pscribed under sub-section (5) of Section 9 being a procedural law is directory.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		6. In view of decision in "M/s J.K.Jute Mills Company Limited" now no further finding is required to be given in regard to same issue in these appeals. The appellants are allowed to complete the records within time granted by the Adjudicating Authority, if not completed. All the appeals stand disposed of with the aforesaid observations. No cost.
	</p>
