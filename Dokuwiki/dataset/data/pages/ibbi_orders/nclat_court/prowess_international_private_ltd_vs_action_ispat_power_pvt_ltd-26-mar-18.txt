<h2>National Company Law Appellate Tribunal</h2>
<h2>Prowess International Pvt. Ltd vs Action Ispat &amp; Power Pvt. Ltd on 26 March, 2018</h2>
<p>NATIONAL COMPANY LAW APPELLATE TRIBUNAL, NEW DELHI Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 223 of 2017IN THE MATTER OF:Prowess International Private Limited ...AppellantVs.Action Ispat &amp; Power Private Limited ...RespondentPresent: For Appellant: - Mr. Akhilesh Kr. Srivastava, Advocate with Ms. Suhita Mukhopadhaya, PCS. For Respondent:- Mr. Milan Singh Negi, Ms. Varsha Banerjee and Mr. Kunal Godhwani, Advocates. ORDER</p>
<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">26.03.2018 ─ This appeal has been pferred by the Appellant against judgment dated 15th March, 2017 passed by the Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal), Principal Bench, New Delhi, whereby and whereunder application pferred by the Appellant under section 9 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (hereinafter referred to as "I&amp;B Code") has been rejected for the grounds mentioned therein.</p>
<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">2. The defective appeal was pferred by the Appellant after delay of more than six months on 22nd September, 2017 without any application for condonation of delay. When it was pointed out, the Appellant pferred an application for condonation of delay and taken plea that there is a delay of only two days.</p>
<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">3. On notice, learned counsel for the Respondent has appeared and opposed the prayer. It was pointed out that the judgment was delivered by the Adjudicating Authority on 15th March, 2017 in open court in psence of learned counsel for the Appellant and in spite of the same, the Appellant did not choose to apply for certified copy, and application for certified copy was filed for the first time on 18th August, 2017 i.e. after five months, which was ppared immediately on 21st August, 2017 and handed over to the Appellant. Thereafter, the defective appeal was pferred on 22nd September, 2017.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">4. From the record, we find that the Appellant has not explained as to what action the Appellant had taken between 15th March, 2017 and 18th August, 2017 i.e. between the day of judgment and the day the application for certified copy was filed.</p>
<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">5. Learned counsel for the Appellant submitted that the copy of the impugned order was not forwarded to the Appellant. However, it is accepted that the impugned order was passed on 15th March, 2017 in psence of the counsel for the Appellant.</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">6. It is desirable to refer the relevant provisions under which appeals can be pferred before this Appellate Tribunal.</p>
<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 223 of 2017 -3-</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">7. Against an order passed by the Tribunal under Companies Act, an appeal is maintainable under Section 421 of the Companies Act, 2013, which reads as follows:</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">"421. Appeal from orders of Tribunal. ─(1) Any person aggrieved by an order of the Tribunal may pfer an appeal to the Appellate Tribunal.</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">(2) No appeal shall lie to the Appellate Tribunal from an order made by the Tribunal with the consent of parties. (3) Every appeal under sub-section (1) shall be filed within a period of forty-five days from the date on which a copy of the order of the Tribunal is made available to the person aggrieved and shall be in such form, and accompanied by such fees, as may be pscribed:</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">Provided that the Appellate Tribunal may entertain an appeal after the expiry of the said period of forty-five days from the date aforesaid, but within a further period not exceeding forty-five days, if it is satisfied that the appellant was pvented by sufficient cause from filing the appeal within that period.</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 223 of 2017 -4- (4) On the receipt of an appeal under sub-section (1), the Appellate Tribunal shall, after giving the parties to the appeal a reasonable opportunity of being heard, pass such orders thereon as it thinks fit, confirming, modifying or setting aside the order appealed against. (5) The Appellate Tribunal shall send a copy of every order made by it to the Tribunal and the parties to appeal."</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">8. As per the said provision, if an appeal is pferred under Section 421 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Appellate Tribunal counts the period of limitation from the date on which a copy of the order is made available by the Tribunal in terms of sub-section (3) of Section 421 of the Companies Act, 2013.</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">9. However, for pferring appeal under Section 61 of the 'I&amp;B Code' against an order passed by the 'Adjudicating Authority' provision for counting the period of limitation is different. The provision is as follows:</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">"61. Appeals and Appellate Authority. ─ (1) Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained under the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), any person aggrieved by the order of the Adjudicating Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 223 of 2017 -5- Authority under this part may pfer an appeal to the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal. (2) Every appeal under sub-section (1) shall be filed within thirty days before the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal:</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">Provided that the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal may allow an appeal to be filed after the expiry of the said period of thirty days if it is satisfied that there was sufficient cause for not filing the appeal but such period shall not exceed fifteen days.</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">(3) An appeal against an order approving a resolution plan under section 31 may be filed on the following grounds, namely:--</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">(i) the approved resolution plan is in contravention of the provisions of any law for the time being in force;</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">(ii) there has been material irregularity in exercise of the powers by the resolution professional during the corporate insolvency Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 223 of 2017 -6- resolution period;</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">(iii) the debts owed to operational creditors of the corporate debtor have not been provided for in the resolution plan in the manner specified by the Board;</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">(iv) the insolvency resolution process costs have not been provided for repayment in priority to all other debts; or</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">(v) the resolution plan does not comply with any other criteria specified by the Board. (4) An appeal against a liquidation order passed under section 33 may be filed on grounds of material irregularity or fraud committed in relation to such a liquidation order."</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">As per the aforesaid provision, the appeal is required to be filed within thirty-days, means within thirty-days from the date of knowledge of the order against which appeal is pferred.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">10. In the psent case, as Appellant had knowledge of the impugned order as on the date of pronouncement of the said order i.e. 15th March, 2017. It is not the case of the Appellant that its Lawyer has not informed Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 223 of 2017 -7- of the order passed by the Adjudicating Authority. The ground as taken in the application for condonation of delay being not satisfactory, it is fit to be rejected. We accordingly, reject the application for condonation of delay and dismiss the appeal being barred by limitation. I.A. No. 292 of 2018 stands disposed of.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">(Justice S.J. Mukhopadhaya) Chairperson (Justice Bansi Lal Bhat) Member(Judicial) Ar/uk Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 223 of 2017</p>
