<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">2. Jaishankar V. Nair takes notice for respondents No. 1 and 2. Since the 2nd petitioner is only a Resolution Professional appointed by the National Company Law Tribunal, Kochi, for the 1 st petitioner, prima facie I am satisfied that 2nd petitioner cannot be hauled up in a criminal case, that too in a repsentative capacity as repsenting the 1st petitioner.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">3. Therefore, it is directed that the 2nd petitioner shall not be required to appear in person before the Special Court for PMLA cases in S.C.No. 4/ 2023 before the SPE/CBI-III, Ernakulam until further orders. It is clarified that this order shall not stand in the way of the trial court proceeding against the 1st petitioner and other accused.</p>
