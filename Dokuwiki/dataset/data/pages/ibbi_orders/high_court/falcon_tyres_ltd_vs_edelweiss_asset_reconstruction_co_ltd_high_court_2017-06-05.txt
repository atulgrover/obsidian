Ltd.,A Company registered underThe Companies Act, 1956 andRegistered as a SecuritizationAnd Asset ReconstructionCompany pursuant to Section 3Of the Securitization andReconstruction of FinancialAssets and Enforcement ofSecurity Interest Act, 2002 -2-As a Trustee of EARC Trust andHaving its registered office atEdelweiss House, Off CST Road,Kalina, Santacruz (East),Mumbai - 400 098. ...RESPONDENT (By Sri.Vikram Trivedi, Adv. for Ms.Anuparna Bordoloi &amp; Sri.Rahul Jain, Advs. For C/respondents) . . . . This writ petition is filed under Articles 226 and227 of the Constitution of India praying to quash theorder dated 12.04.2017 passed by the learned NationalCompany Law Tribunal at Bengaluru at Annexure `A'. This writ petition coming on for orders, this day,the Court made the following: ORDER
	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		Though the issue of maintainability is raised in the instant petition, considering that a detailed consideration on merits is not required herein, the said question is left open to be considered in an appropriate petition if the question of law in that regard arises for consideration.
	</p>
	-3-
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		2. The petitioner is before this Court assailing the order at Annexure `A' to the petition. The grievance of the petitioner is that the order admitting the petition is passed without providing opportunity to the petitioner and without hearing the petitioner herein in that regard.
	</p>
	<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">
		3. The respondents have filed their additional counter/objections. Though the contentions as put forth is referred to therein the respondents in the light of having a consideration of the matter including the question relating to admission of the petition before the National Company Law Tribunal (for short 'NCLT') have conceded that the order impugned at Annexure `A' be set-aside and the NCLT be directed to reconsider the matter afresh by hearing the learned counsel for both the parties before considering the admission of the petition before it.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		3. Apart from the fact that the respondents have conceded to it, since it would also resolve the issue that -4- is raised in the instant petition, without affecting the right of the parties, and would also avoid protraction of the litigation at this stage itself, the order at Annexure `A' is set-aside.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		The NCLT is directed to provide opportunity of hearing to the learned counsel for all the parties before it and thereafter consider the petition in accordance with law including on the issue relating to the admission of the petition before it.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		All contentions are left open to be considered therein which shall be considered in an expeditious manner.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		Petition is accordingly disposed of.
	</p>
