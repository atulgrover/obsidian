RESPONDENTS (By Sri.K.N.Phanindra, Sr.Adv. for Smt.Vaishali Hegde, Adv. for R1 and R2) This Writ Petition is filed under <a href="/doc/1712542/">Articles 226</a> and <a href="/doc/1331149/">227</a> of the Constitution of India praying to issue a writ in the nature of certiorari or any other appropriate writ order dated 11.12.2018 passed by the National Company Law Tribunal, Chennai in MA 632/2018 filed in CP/39/IB/2018 as arbitrary, illegal and without authority of law produced at Annexure-A to the W.P. This Writ Petition coming up for Preliminary Hearing, this day, P.S. Dinesh Kumar J., made the following: 3 ORDER</p>
<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">State have filed this writ petition challenging order dated 11.12.2018 passed by the National Company Law Tribunal, Chennai in MA No.632/2018 in company petition No.CP/39/IB/2018, setting aside the order passed by the Under Secretary to Government, Commerce &amp; Industries Department (Mines), State of Karnataka, rejecting the deemed extension of mining lease bearing No.ML 2293 and directing respondent Nos.1 to 3 therein to execute the Supplemental Deeds extending the period of Mining Lease up to 31.03.2020 in accordance with<a href="/doc/151443940/"> S.8A(6)</a> of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957, within a period of two weeks from the date of receipt of a copy of the impugned order.</p>
<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">2. Though several contentions were urged by Sri A.S.Ponnanna, learned Addl. Advocate General on behalf of the State, during the course of hearing, Sri K.N. Phaneendra, learned Senior Advocate appearing for the 4 contesting respondent No.1 submits that without pjudice to all contentions raised by the State as also the contesting respondent, this Court may set aside the impugned order and relegate the parties to the Tribunal enabling the State to respond to the notice issued by the Tribunal and contest the matter in MA No. 632/2018 arising out of company petition No.CP/39/IB/2018.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">3. In view of the submissions made by Sri Phaneendra, learned Senior Advocate, we have not gone into the merits of the case.</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">Hence the following:</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis">ORDER The order dated 11.12.2018 passed by the National Company Law Tribunal, Chennai in MA No.632/2018 in company petition No.CP/39/IB/2018 is set aside. The application filed by the first respondent herein in MA No.632/2018 is restored on the file of the NCLT, Chennai. 5</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis">Both the parties shall appear before the NCLT, Chennai on 05.04.2019 without awaiting any notice from the Tribunal. Liberty is reserved to the State Government to answer the notice dated 29.11.2018 issued by NCLT.</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis">The writ petition is disposed of with the aforesaid observations. All contentions of both parties are kept open.</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis">No costs.</p>
