<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">(PER : HONOURABLE THE ACTING CHIEF JUSTICE ANANT S. DAVE) Learned advocate for the petitioner seeks permission to withdraw this petition filed under <a href="/doc/1712542/">Articles 226</a> and <a href="/doc/1331149/">227</a> of the Constitution of India with a view to approach the appellate authority - National Company Law Appellate Tribunal and the remedy so provided under Section 61 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, against the order impugned passed by the National Company Law Tribunal.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Page 1 of 2 C/SCA/1554/2018 ORDER</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Permission as prayed for is granted. The petition stands disposed of as withdrawn.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">It is made clear that in view of the request as above and availability of alternative efficacious inbuilt remedy under Section 61 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, we have not entered into merits of the contentions raised in the petition. Notice is discharged.</p>
