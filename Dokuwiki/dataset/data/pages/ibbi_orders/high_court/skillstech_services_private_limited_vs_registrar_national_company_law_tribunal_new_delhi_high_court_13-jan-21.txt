<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">1. This hearing has been done by video conferencing.</p>
<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">2. The psent petition has been filed by the Petitioner seeking listing of its petition, under Section 9 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, before the appropriate bench of the National Company Law Tribunal (hereinafter, "NCLT").</p>
<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">3. The case of the Petitioner is that the Registrar of the NCLT has failed to even list the Petitioner's matter before the appropriate bench of NCLT, on the ground that the threshold of the pecuniary jurisdiction of the NCLT has now been amended by a notification dated 24th November, 2020, from Rs.1 lakh, to Rs.1 crore.</p>
<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">4. Mr. George, ld. counsel for the Petitioner, submits that the question as to whether the NCLT has the pecuniary jurisdiction or not, cannot be decided by the Registrar of the NCLT, but in fact the same ought to be looked into and Signature Not Verified Digitally Signed By:PRATHIBA M SINGH W.P.(C) 474/2021 Page 1 of 3 Signing Date:16.01.2021 00:42 Signature Not Verified Digitally Signed By:DINESH SINGH NAYAL Signing Date:16.01.2021 11:32:43 determined by an appropriate bench of the NCLT, after appciating the fact situation involved. Reliance is placed upon the view of the NCLT, Kochi in IA No. 175/KOB/2020 in IBA/34/KOB/2020 titled M/s Tharakan Web Innovations Pvt. Ltd. v. Cyriac Njavally, wherein the Tribunal has held that if disputes had arisen prior to the outbreak of the pandemic, the said notification may not apply, as the notification cannot be made applicable retrospectively.</p>
<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">5. Mr. Harish Vaidyanathan, ld. Counsel appearing for the Respondent submits that the said judgment of the NCLT, Kochi Bench has been stayed by the Kerala High Court.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">6. This court is of the opinion that the question as to whether the NCLT has jurisdiction to entertain a particular case or not cannot be determined by the Registrar in the administrative capacity. The Registrar would have to place the matter before the appropriate bench of the NCLT, for the said question to be judicially determined. The appropriate bench of the NCLT would have to then, take a considered view as to whether notice is liable to be issued in the matter or not.</p>
<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">7. The question as to whether the notification dated 24 th March, 2020 applies to a particular petition that has been filed prior to the said notification or not is also a question to be determined by the Bench of the NCLT and not by the Registrar of the Tribunal.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">8. Accordingly, it is directed that the petition under section 9 of the IBC, moved by the Petitioner before the NCLT, shall be placed by the Registrar, NCLT before an appropriate bench for proceeding further in accordance with law. The listing of the petition is directed to be done within a period of ten days from today.</p>
