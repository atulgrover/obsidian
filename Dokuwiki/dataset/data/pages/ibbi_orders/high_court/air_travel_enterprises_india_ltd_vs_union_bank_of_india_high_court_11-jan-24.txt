	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		The petitioner only seeks that until such time as the National Company Law Tribunal, Kochi Bench, issues orders on Ext.P5 Stay Petition in Ext.P4 Appeal, all action pursuant to Ext.P1 be deferred.
	</p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		2. Sri.Sudheer R. - learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that Ext.P4 Appeal, along with Ext.P5 Stay Petition, are being considered by the Appellate Tribunal today; and that his client is confident of obtaining orders.
	</p>
	<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">
		3. Sri.Sadchith P.Kurup - learned Standing Counsel for the respondent - Bank affirmed the afore and submitted that, for the afore reason itself, this writ petition is not maintainable.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		4. I notice from the pleadings that Ext.P1 is the order of the National Company Law Tribunal, Kochi Bench, against which, certainly, the petitioner obtains a statutory appellate remedy. Since they have admittedly invoked the same through Ext.P4 Appeal, I am of the view that it would not be necessary for this Court to consider the merits of the matter in any manner whatsoever; and also of the view that this writ petition is not hence maintainable.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		5. Suffice to say, all which the petitioner seems to require is a breathing time to move the Appellate Tribunal and obtain orders, if they are entitled.
	</p>
	WP(C) NO. 1202 OF 2024 3
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		In the afore circumstances, I close this writ petition; however, directing the respondent not to pursue further action consequent to Ext.P1 until 31.01.2024.
	</p>
