According to the Petitioners, this is an indication as to the true and proper interptation of <a href="/doc/161264586/">Section 435</a> of the Companies Act, 2013 and <a href="/doc/745933/">Section 436</a> of I.B. Code. Thus for all the above reasons, the impugned proceedings have been instituted by the Respondents (Complainant) in the Court of Addtional Sessions Judge, were not sustainable for want of jurisdiction. As a consequence order, 'issue process' passed by the learned Additional Sessions Judge against the Petitioners, in a complaint by the Respondents/Board was without jurisdiction and therefore not sustainable equally. It is therefore to be held that Special Court "Issue Process", under Sectionwhich is to try offences under the I.B. Code is the Special Court established under <a href="/doc/117888039/">Section 435 (2) (b)</a> of the Companies Act, 2013 which consists of Metropolitan Magistrate or Judicial Magistrate First Class. The Petition is therefore allowed in terms of prayer clause (a).</p>
