Not only that, on 01.12.2020 the disciplinary committee of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) restrained respondent No.4 from accepting any process or assignment under the Code, till he was exonerated of all charges. Respondent No.4, allegedly in a spiteful manner, arising from the aforesaid facts and circumstances filed a complaint Case under Section 156(3) Cr.P.C. against the petitioners under Sections 420,467,468,471,120-B, IPC and Sections 55,56 of the IT Act. An FIR was registered on 14.03.2021. Learned counsel submits that the petitioner apphends arrest arising from the FIR No.287/2021 dated 14.03.2021 and on the alternative seeks transfer of the investigation from Police Station &ndash;Sihani Gate, Ghaziabad, UP where the FIR is registered to the Central Bureau Investigation, ACB, New Delhi along with the investigation pending against respondent No.4. We did put to learned counsel for the petitioners as to why recourse in case like this has been had to under Article 32 of the Constitution of India. Learned counsel only submits that this is an exceptional case.

	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		On hearing learned counsel for the parties, we are only inclined to consider the issue of transfer of investigation to the CBI for investigation which is the alternate prayer (b). Let notice issue confined to the aforesaid aspect.  3 In the meantime, the further proceedings in pursuance to FIR No.287/2021 dated 14.03.2021 registered with Police Station Sihani Gate, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh are stayed.
