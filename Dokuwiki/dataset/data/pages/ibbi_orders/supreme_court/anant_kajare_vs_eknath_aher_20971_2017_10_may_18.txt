<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">We have since been informed that such a joint meeting has, in fact, taken place on 07.05.2018 with the following Members psent:</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning">1. Devendra Jain, Resolution Professional (RP) of Citrus Check Inn Ltd. (CCIL) and Royal Twinkle Star Club Limited (RTSCL).</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning">2. Mr. Amit Karkhanis, Advocate for Petitioner Society &ndash; Citrus Welfare Society (CWS)</p>

<p title="Court's Reasoning">8. Ms. Maninder Cheema, SEBI Repsentative.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning">9. Mr. Ankit Bhansali, SEBI Repsentative.&rdquo; Ultimately, everybody jointly consented to the Supme Court giving certain directions with regard to the formation of a Sale-cum-</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Monitoring Committee, and the steps to be taken by such Committee.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">After hearing all learned Counsel appearing on behalf of the various parties before us, we direct that a Sale-cum-Monitoring Committee be set up comprising of the Resolution Professional, one SEBI Repsentative, one Investor Repsentative and one Repsentative of CCIL and RTSCL and their associates/sister concerns.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">This four-Member Committee will then appoint Registered Valuers to value the properties that have been unearthed during the insolvency process of CCIL, RTSCL, which will include assets of their associates/sister concerns.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">We also appoint M/s Deloitte as the special auditor to carry out the Forensic Audit not only of CCIL and RTSCL, but of their associates/sister concerns, which at psent number 88. If anymore sister or associate concerns are unearthed after the date of this order, they shall conduct a Forensic Audit for the same.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">We have been informed that the statement made in our order dated 26.04.2018 that attachment orders have already been obtained is not correct: apparently, only injunction orders have been obtained qua the assets in question.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">We, therefore, hereby attach all the properties of CCIL, RTSCL as well as assets and other properties of their associates/sister concerns. Pursuant to this attachment, we direct that after valuation has been done a sale of each of these properties will take place under the aegis of the NCLT.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Mr. Divan has moved I.A. No. 57067/2018, which is allowed to the aforesaid extent.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Insofar as IA No. 57071/2018 is concerned, we permit the petitioner to add the promoters and Directors of other persons mentioned in prayer (a) thereof, and further direct that all these persons disclose on affidavit their personal assets world-wide in affidavits filed before this Court within a period of eight weeks from today.</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">We request the Committee to do their job in right earnest, and submit a Report to this Court within a period of four months from today.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">The bank accounts of the below mentioned Companies shall stand defrozen so that the aforesaid Committee can defray expenses of carrying out this order from the said accounts.</p>
