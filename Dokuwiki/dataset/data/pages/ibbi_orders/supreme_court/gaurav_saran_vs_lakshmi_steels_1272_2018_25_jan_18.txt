<h2>Gaurav Saran vs M/S Lakshmi Steels Through Its ... on 25 January, 2018</h2>
<h3>Bench: Rohinton Fali Nariman, Navin Sinha</h3>
<p>IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION CIVIL APPEAL NO. 1272 OF 2018 (Arising out of S.L.P. (C) No. 3172 OF 2018) (D. NO. 415 of 2018) GAURAV SARAN &amp; ANR. Appellant(s) VERSUS M/S LAKSHMI STEELS &amp; ANR. Respondent(s) O R D E R</p>
<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">Permission to file Special Leave Petition is granted. Heard learned Counsel appearing for the appellants. Leave granted.</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">A Petition under Section 9 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 has been admitted by the National Company Law Tribunal, New Delhi on 27.09.2017. Since that date, by way of a Memorandum of Understanding dated 16.12.2017, the matter has been settled between the parties.</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">A copy of the Memorandum of Understanding dated 16.12.2017 executed between the parties is made part of the record of this Court, and by using our powers under Article 142 of the Constitution of India, we set aside the impugned order dated 27.09.2017 passed by the National Company Law Tribunal.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Accordingly, the appeal is disposed of. Pending applications filed in the matter also stand disposed of.</p>
