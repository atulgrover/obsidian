<h2>
	Gammon India Ltd. vs Neelkanth Mansions And Infrastructure ... on 20 November, 2020
</h2>
<h3>
	Bench: D.Y. Chandrachud, Indu Malhotra
</h3>
<pre>
	 IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION Civil Appeal No of 2020 (D No 13202 of 2020) Gammon India Ltd .... Appellant(s) Versus Neelkanth Mansions and Infrastructure Pvt Ltd ....Respondent(s) ORDER
</pre>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
	1 Section 62 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016 provides a period of forty five days from the date of the receipt of an order of the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal for filing an appeal before this Court. Under sub-section (2), this Court is empowered to condone a delay of a further period not exceeding fifteen days for sufficient cause.
</p>
<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
	2 The appeal is barred by limitation since the delay of 51 days is beyond the period of delay which can be condoned under sub-section (2) of Section 62.
</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
	3 Hence, the appeal is dismissed on the ground that it is barred by limitation.
</p>
