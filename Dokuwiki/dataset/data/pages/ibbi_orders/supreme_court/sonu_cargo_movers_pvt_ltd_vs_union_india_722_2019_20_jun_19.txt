		Signature Not Verified The grievance of the petitioners was that their matter was Digitally signed by MANISH SETHI Date: 2019.06.20 being heard by a Bench in which there was no technical member. 16:41:34 IST Reason:
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning">
		Today it is pointed out that an order has been issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs appointing the technical members in  2 the Bench of the National Company Law Tribunal (&ldquo;NCLT&rdquo;) at Ahmedabad.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning">
		We therefore, dispose of these petitions with a direction that the case of the petitioners be heard by a Bench comprising of a judicial member and a technical member.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning">
		Ms. Meenakshi Arora, learned senior counsel has raised the issue that despite judgment passed in Swiss Ribbons Private Ltd. &amp; Anr. vs. Union of India &amp; Ors. (2019) 4 SCC 17, it is not the Law Ministry but the Ministry of Corporate Affairs which is ordering the appointment of the members of the NCLT.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning">
		We are not going into that question in this case and leave it open for decision in an appropriate case.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning">
		Pending application(s), if any, shall stand disposed of.
