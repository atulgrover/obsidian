		Anubhav Anilkumar Agarwal vs Bank Of India on 15 October, 2020
	</h2>
</p>
<p>
	<h3>
		Bench: L. Nageswara Rao, Hemant Gupta, Ajay Rastogi
	</h3>

<p>
	<p>
		IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION CIVIL APPEAL NO. 3412 OF 2020 ANUBHAV ANILKUMAR AGARWAL Appellant(s) VERSUS BANK OF INDIA &amp; ANR. Respondent(s) O R D E R
	</p>
</p>
<p>
	<p title="Fact" data-structure="Facts">
		Respondent No. 1, who is the financial creditor, filed a petition under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, in February, 2019. The appeal filed by the appellant against the said order was dismissed. The appellant has filed review petition. By order dated 29.07.2020, the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (hereinafter referred to as &lsquo;NCLAT&rsquo; for brevity) issued notice in the review petition but did not pass any interim order.
	</p>
</p>
<p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		Mr. Mukul Rohatgi, learned senior counsel appearing for the appellant, submits that the review petition has been adjourned thrice by the NCLAT and in Signature Not Verified the meanwhile, NCLT is proceeding further against the Digitally signed by Nidhi Ahuja Date: 2020.10.16 17:41:34 IST Reason: properties of the appellant.  Taking note of the above submissions, we direct the NCLAT to decide the review at the earliest not later than two weeks from today.
	</p>
</p>
<p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		The civil appeal stands disposed of.
	</p>
