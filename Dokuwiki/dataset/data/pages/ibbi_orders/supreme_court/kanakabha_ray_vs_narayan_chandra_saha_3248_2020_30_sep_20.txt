<p>
	1 IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION CIVIL APPEAL NO(S). 3248/2020 KANAKABHA RAY APPELLANT(S) VERSUS NARAYAN CHANDRA SAHA &amp; ORS. RESPONDENT(S) O R D E R
</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
	1. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
</p>
<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
	2. We are not inclined to interfere with the impugned decision dated 18.08.2020 passed by the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal, New Delhi in Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No.687 of 2020.
</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
	3 However, it is made clear that none of the observations made by the National Company Law Tribunal or National Company Law Appellate Tribunal, as the case may be, which unwittingly reflects on the conduct or character of the appellant, shall be taken as final observation. In other words, dismissal of this appeal will be Signature Not Verified no reflection on the character and conduct of the Digitally signed by MEENAKSHI KOHLI appellant as such.
</p>
<p>
	Date: 2020.10.06
</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
	17:01:20 IST Reason:
</p>
<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
	4. We were informed by the learned senior  2 counsel appearing for respondent No.1/caveator that the Committee of Creditors of the Company has already acted upon the impugned decision by appointing a new Resolution Professional. That is an additional reason for disposing of this appeal whilst leaving all contentions raised by the appellant open.
</p>
<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
	5. If the appellant has any claim against the company in respect of his professional fees or expenses, he is free to make a formal application in that behalf to the Tribunal, which shall be considered on its own merits in accordance with law.
</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
	6. Besides this, nothing more is required to be stated in this order.
</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
	7. The appeal and pending application(s), if any, are disposed of accordingly.
