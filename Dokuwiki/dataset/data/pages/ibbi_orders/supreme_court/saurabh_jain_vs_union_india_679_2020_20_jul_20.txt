	<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
		Having heard Mr. Manan Kumar Mishra, learned Senior Counsel for the petitioners for sometime, we are of the view that at page 115 of the Writ Petition it has been made clear that the Ministry of Finance itself has, by a Circular, directed personal guarantees issued by promoters/managerial personnel to be invoked. According to the petitioners, despite this Circular, Public Sector Undertakings continue not to invoke such guarantees resulting in huge loss not only to the public exchequer but also to the common man.
	</p>

With these observations, the petition is allowed to be withdrawn to do the needful.
