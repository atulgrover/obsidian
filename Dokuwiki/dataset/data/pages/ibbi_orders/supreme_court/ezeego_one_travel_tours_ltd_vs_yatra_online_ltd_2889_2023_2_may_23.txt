	<p>
		 1 IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION CIVIL APPEAL NO.2889 OF 2023 EZEEGO ONE TRAVEL AND TOURS LIMITED APPELLANT(S) VERSUS YATRA ONLINE LIMITED RESPONDENT(S) O R D E R
	</p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		Heard Mr. Ritin Rai, learned senior counsel for the appellant and Dr. Abhishek Manu Singhvi, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the respondent.
	</p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		Mr. Ritin Rai, learned counsel for the appellant submits that the matter has been settled. Dr. Abhishek Manu Singhvi, learned senior counsel would submit that, as per the agreement an amount of Rs.1.60 Crores will be paid in full and final settlement by the respondent to the appellant today by RTGS.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		Learned senior counsel for the appellant then prays for permission to withdraw the appeal. The civil appeal is dismissed as withdrawn.
	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		However, we make it clear that we have not gone into the question of law and hence we leave it open.
