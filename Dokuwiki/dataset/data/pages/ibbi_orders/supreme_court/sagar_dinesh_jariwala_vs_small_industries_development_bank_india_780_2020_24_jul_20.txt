	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		This petition has been filed seeking transfer of the CP (IB) No. 1880/NCLT/MB/2018 titled as &ldquo;Small Industries Development Bank of India v. Swiss Ribbons Private Limited&rdquo; pending before the NCLT, Mumbai Bench to the NCLT, Ahmedabad Bench.
	</p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		Heard Ms. Aastha Mehta, learned counsel for the petitioner and Mr. Madhusudan Sharma, learned counsel appearing for the Dispute Resolution Professional concerned with M/s Swiss Ribbons Pvt. Ltd. (respondent No.2).
	</p>
	<p title="Fact" data-structure="Facts">
		The record indicates that the proceedings under Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016 were initiated against respondent No.2 before the NCLT, Ahmedabad Bench. However, since technical member was not available to conduct the proceedings in the Ahmedabad Bench, the proceedings were transferred from Ahmedabad to Mumbai. Signature Not Verified
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
		Subsequently, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs have Digitally signed by MEENAKSHI KOHLI Date: 2020.07.29 appointed additional technical members for the Ahmedabad Bench 13:45:12 IST Reason:
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
		 1
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		and accordingly since 25.07.2019, the pision bench of the NCLT at Ahmedabad is effectively functioning. Therefore, there is no reason as to why the competent jurisdictional bench should not conduct the proceedings.
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		Having noted the above and upon due consideration of submissions made by the rival counsel, this transfer petition stands allowed. Consequently, the CP (IB) No. 1880/NCLT/MB/2018 titled as &ldquo;Small Industries Development Bank of India v. Swiss Ribbons Private Limited&rdquo; pending before the NCLT, Mumbai Bench is transferred to the NCLT, Ahmedabad Bench.
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		Let the records of the case be transferred to the concerned court without delay.
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		The transfer petition is disposed of with the above order.
