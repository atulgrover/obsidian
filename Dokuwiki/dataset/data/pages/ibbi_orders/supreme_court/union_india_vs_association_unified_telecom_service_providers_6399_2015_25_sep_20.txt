<h2>
	Union Of India Through Secretary vs Association Of Unified Telecom Service ... on 25 September, 2020
</h2>
<h3>
	Bench: S. Abdul Nazeer, B.R. Gavai
</h3>
<p>
	1 IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION MISCELLANEOUS APPLICATION NO(S) 1667-1738 OF 2020 IN MISCELLANEOUS APPLICATION NO(S) 725-796 OF 2020 IN CIVIL APPEAL NOS. 6328-6399 OF 2015 UNION OF INDIA THROUGH SECRETARY &hellip;APPELLANT (S) VERSUS ASSOCIATION OF UNIFIED TELECOM SERVICE PROVIDERS OF INDIA AND ORS. &hellip;.RESPONDENT(S)/ APPLICANT(S) O R D E R
</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
	We have heard learned counsel for the parties. In paragraph &lsquo;23&rsquo; of the Judgment in Union of India v. Association of Unified Telecom Service Providers of India Etc.Etc. &ndash; M.A.(D)No.9887 of 2020 in Civil Appeal No(s).6328-6399 of 2015, this Court has held as under :
</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis">
	&ldquo;23. We consider it appropriate that the aforesaid various questions should first be considered by the NCLT. Let the NCLT Signature Not Verified consider the aforesaid aspect and pass a Digitally signed by GEETA AHUJA Date: 2020.10.01 14:36:10 IST Reason: reasoned order after hearing all the parties. We make it clear that it being a jurisdictional question, it requires to be  2 gone into at this stage itself. Let the question be decided within the outer limits of two months. We also make it clear that we have observed on the merit of the case, and we have kept all the questions open to be examined by the NCLT.&rdquo; The various questions which are to be decided by the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) are enumerated in paragraphs &lsquo;18&rsquo; to &lsquo;22&rsquo; of the said judgment.
</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis">
	Learned senior counsel, M/s. Ranjit Kumar along with Ravi Kadam, appearing for the applicants &ndash; Monitoring Committees of Aircel Ltd., Aircel Cellular Ltd. and Dishnet Wireless Ltd. (Respondent NO.9-Aircel Entities) submit that the Resolution Proceedings of the applicants has been approved by the NCLT under Section 31 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 and that an appeal has already been filed against the Approval Order of the NCLT by the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) before the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT). Therefore, the proceedings in the case of the applicants pertaining to the Approval Order are at large and pending consideration before the NCLAT and not the NCLT. It is further submitted by learned senior counsel  3 that the NCLAT may be directed to decide the questions framed by this Court in the case of the applicants.
</p>
<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">
	Mr. Tushar Mehta, learned Solicitor General appearing for the Union of India, has no objection for the same.
</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
	In view of above, we direct the NCLAT to first consider the various questions framed in paragraphs &lsquo;18&rsquo; to &lsquo;22&rsquo; of the Judgment, mentioned above, and pass a reasoned order in accordance with paragraph &lsquo;23&rsquo; thereof.
</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
	With the above clarification, the Misc. applications are disposed of.
</p>
