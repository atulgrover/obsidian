		Heard learned counsel for the parties. It is agreed between the parties that it would be desirable that the appeals pending before the National  3 Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) bearing Nos. Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) Nos. 486, 488, 475, 478, 480, 489, 506, 547, 544 and 630 of 2020 are withdrawn to this Court and heard alongwith these appeals to avoid further delay in the execution of the scheme.
