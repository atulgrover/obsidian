	<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">
		1. Learned counsel for the respondent &ndash; Bank has submitted that the matter may not be kept pending and, instead, may be sent back to the learned Tribunal for consideration of the admission of the insolvency application after issuing limited notice to the Corporate Debtor. In this regard, a decision of this Court in Innoventive Industries Limited vs. ICICI Bank and another1 has also been referred by the learned counsels for the parties. Signature Not Verified Digitally signed by VINOD LAKHINA Date: 2018.03.28 17:38:37 IST Reason: 1 (2018) 1 SCC 407 [para 28 and 30]  2
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		2. In view of the above stand taken on behalf of the respondent - Bank, we allow this appeal and set aside the order of the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal and the order of the Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal) and remand the matter to the Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal), Chennai for a de novo consideration after issuing limited notice to the corporate debtor.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		3. The appeal is disposed of in the above terms.
