	<p>
		In view of the subsequent development and as National Asset Reconstruction Company Limited is now accepting the proposal, we set aside the impugned order as well as the judgment/order dated 27.06.2023 passed by the National Company Law Tribunal1, Special Bench-I, Chennai, and remit the matter to the NCLT, to examine and follow the procedure established by law in terms of Section 12A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.
	</p>

	<p>
		We request the NCLT to decide the matter expeditiously. Pending application(s), if any, shall stand disposed of.
