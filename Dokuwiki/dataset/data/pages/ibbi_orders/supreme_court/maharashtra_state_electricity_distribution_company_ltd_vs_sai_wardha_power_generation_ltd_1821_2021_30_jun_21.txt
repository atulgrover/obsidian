	<p>
		IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION CIVIL APPEAL NO.1821 OF 2021 MAHARASHTRA STATE ELECTRICITY DISTRIBUTION COMPANY LIMITED ...Appellant(s) Vs. SAI WARDHA POWER GENERATION LIMITED &amp; ANR. ...Respondent(s) O R D E R
	</p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		In view of the expss provisions of Section 61(2) of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal was right in rejecting the appeal on the ground that it was filed beyond the condonable period of 15 days after the expiry of the period of limitation of 30 days pscribed therein. However, Mr. Vikas Singh, learned senior counsel for the appellant seeks liberty to move the National Company Law Tribunal by invoking Rule 11 of the National Company Law Tribunal Rules, 2016. The appellant shall have such liberty, if permissible in law.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		With the above observation, the appeal is dismissed.
	</p>
