		Punjab National Bank vs Ajmer Singh Bhullar on 9 August, 2019
	</h2>
	<h3>
		Bench: Rohinton Fali Nariman, Surya Kant
	</h3>
	<p>
		 1 IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION M.A. NO. 873 OF 2019 IN CIVIL APPEAL NO. 9973 OF 2018 PUNJAB NATIONAL BANK &amp; ANR. Appellant(s) VERSUS AJMER SINGH BHULLAR &amp; ORS. Respondent(s) O R D E R
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		This Court, by its order dated 25.09.2018, recorded that a logical culmination must take place in a winding up of the corporate debtor, and also recorded that the promoter directors may not be evicted till such time that it is necessary to evict them once the winding up process actually commences. We had also recorded that such directors will cooperate with the Resolution Professional, and will not instigate any workers so far as the rest of the property is concerned.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		The liquidation process has since begun, but we are informed, and particularly on a reading of the Press Release dated 27th and 29th October, 2018 it is clear that contrary to this order, the said promoter directors are abusing the privilege granted by this Court. Signature Not Verified Though they have been served with the psent M.A. they have chosen Digitally signed by R NATARAJAN Date: 2019.08.14 16:48:38 IST not to appear.
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
		Reason:
	</p>
	 2
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		We may also in passing mention that the NCLT, by its order dated 31.01.2019, had permitted the Punjab National Bank to file a fresh application for eviction of these persons when the publication for sale of the assets begins, which has already taken place.
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		We, therefore, direct that the respondents to this application be evicted, with police help, if necessary, within a period of four weeks from today.
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		Miscellaneous Application is allowed in the aforesaid terms.
