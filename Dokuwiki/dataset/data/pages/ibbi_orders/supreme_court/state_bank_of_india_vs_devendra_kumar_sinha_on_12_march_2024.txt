		We have considered the impugned judgment, which arises out of Writ Petition (Civil) no. 11907/2016 pferred by respondent no. 1&ndash; Devendra Kumar Sinha, challenging the proceedings initiated by the appellant &ndash; State Bank of India under the Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 20021, by issue of notice under Section 13(4) of the SARFAESI Act.

	<p>
		In view of the said position, the impugned judgment is set aside. Consequently, the appellant&ndash; State Bank of India is entitled to proceed under the SARFAESI Act, including the notice under Section 13(4), which was impugned and quashed vide the impugned judgment.
	</p>
	<p>
		However, we clarify that it will be open to respondent no. 1 and other respondents, if aggrieved, to initiate and take steps in accordance with the provisions of the SARFAESI Act.
	</p>
	<p>
		It is pointed out that respondent no. 1&ndash; Devendra Kumar Sinha had made an application for a one-time settlement which was approved but some payments were not made. We make no comments in this regard.
	</p>
	<p>
		The appeal is allowed and disposed of in the above terms. Pending application(s), if any, shall stand disposed of.
