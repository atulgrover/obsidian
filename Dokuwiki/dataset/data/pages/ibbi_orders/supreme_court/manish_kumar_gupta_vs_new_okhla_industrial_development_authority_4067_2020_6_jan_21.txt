The limitation to file an appeal to this Court under Section 62 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016 1 expired on 27 October 2020. The appeal has been filed on 28 October 2020. Hence, having regard to the provisions of sub-section (2) of Section 62 of the IBC, the appeal is dismissed.
