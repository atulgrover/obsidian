	<h2>
		B Prashanth Hegde vs State Bank Of India on 21 October, 2019
	</h2>
	<h3>
		Bench: Rohinton Fali Nariman, V. Ramasubramanian
	</h3>
	<p>
		 IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION CIVIL APPEAL NO. 8010 OF 2019 B PRASHANTH HEGDE Appellant(s) VERSUS STATE BANK OF INDIA &amp; ANR. Respondent(s) O R D E R
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
		Heard the learned Senior Counsel appearing for the parties. Following our judgment in Sagar Sharma and Another vs. Phoenix ARC Pvt. Ltd. And Another, 2019 SCC OnLine SC 1332, we set aside the impugned judgment of the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal, New Delhi dated 26.09.2019.
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
		The NCLAT will now re-examine the question of limitation having regard to the judgments in B.K. Educational Services Private Limited vs. Parag Gupta and Associates, 2018 SCC OnLine SC 1921 as well as Sagar Sharma and Another (supra) applying Article 137 of the Limitation Act, as expeditiously as possible.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		The appeal is allowed in the aforesaid terms. Further pending proceedings will be held in abeyance. Pending applications stand disposed of.
	</p>
