	<h2>
		Gopal Krishan Bathla vs Crown Realtech Pvt. Ltd. on 11 January, 2021
	</h2>
	<h3>
		Bench: A.M. Khanwilkar, B.R. Gavai, Krishna Murari
	</h3>
	<pre>
		 1 IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION CIVIL APPEAL NO. 2726 OF 2020 GOPAL KRISHAN BATHLA Appellant(s) VERSUS CROWN REALTECH PVT. LTD. &amp; ANR. Respondent(s) O R D E R
	</pre>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		Heard learned counsel for the parties. Considering the stand taken by respondent No.2, through counsel that the settlement adverted to by the opposite party cannot be given effect to and has not been taken forward, the larger issue raised by the appellant about the jurisdiction of the National Company Law Tribunal under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 would not survive for our consideration.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		Hence, while leaving that question open, we dispose of this appeal in view of the stand taken by respondent No.2 and also because it has been brought to our notice that subsequent to the impugned order, the Committee of Signature Not Verified Creditors have already approved the revival plan. Digitally signed by DEEPAK SINGH Date: 2021.01.12 18:03:43 IST Reason:
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
		We have not expressed any view either way about the correctness of the said plan. That issue will have to be  2 considered in appropriate proceedings, if and when occasion arises.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		The Civil Appeal is disposed of accordingly. Pending applications, if any, stand disposed of.
	</p>
