	<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
		In an earlier order dated 24.07.2017, this Bench had observed that in view of Rule 8 of the I &amp; B (Application to Adjudicating Authority) Rules, 2016, the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal prima facie could not avail of the inherent powers recognised by Rule 11 of the National Law Appellate Tribunal Rules, Signature Not Verified2016 to allow a compromise to take effect after Digitally signed by SHASHI SAREEN Date: 2017.11.14 17:13:28 IST Reason: admission of the insolvency petition. We are of the view that instead of all such orders coming to the Supme Court as only the Supme Court may utilise its  2 powers under Article 142 of the Constitution of India, the relevant Rules be amended by the competent authority so as to include such inherent powers. This will obviate unnecessary appeals being filed before this Court in matters where such agreement has been reached. On the facts of the psent case, we take on record the settlement between the parties and set aside the NCLAT order.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		As a result, the appeal is allowed in the aforesaid terms.
	</p>
