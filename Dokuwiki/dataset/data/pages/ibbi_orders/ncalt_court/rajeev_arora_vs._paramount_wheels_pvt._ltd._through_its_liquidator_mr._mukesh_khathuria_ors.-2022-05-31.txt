		(Virtual Mode) 31.05.2022: Heard Learned Counsel for the Appellant. Learned Counsel for Respondent No. 2 is psent.

	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		2. This Appeal has been filed against the Order dated 25th January, 2022 passed by the Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench, Court-III) in I.A. No. 220/2021. By which Order, the Adjudicating Authority has directed for liquidation. The Appellant who was the ex-Director of the Corporate Debtor being MSME filed a Resolution Plan in the year 2020 which plan was approved by the Adjudicating Authority on 09th September, 2020. 2 Subsequent to the Approval of the Resolution Plan by the Adjudicating Authority, the Plan could not be implemented nor Performance Guarantee was submitted by the Appellant. State Bank of India filed an Application for liquidation on which Application, an Order has been passed on 25.01.2022 for Liquidation.
	</p>
	<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">
		3. The Learned Counsel for the Appellant submits that audited accounts were not maintained by the Resolution Professional due to which the Appellant could not comply with the submissions of Performance Guarantee and other conditions. He submits that there has been correspondence with the Resolution Professional and it was on account of Resolution Professional's inaction that Appellant could not comply. He further submits that he is only aggrieved by the Observations made in the Order against the Appellant in paragraph 4 of the Order impugned and he has no grievance with regard to the Order of Liquidation.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		4. We have considered the submissions of Learned Counsel for the Appellant and perused the record.
	</p>
	<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">
		5. The Appellant who was the Successful Resolution Applicant whose plan was approved as early as September, 2020, could not implement the plan for more than a period of one year. The Performance Guarantee which was required to be submitted, has not been submitted even in the period of one year. Learned Counsel for the Appellant submits that requirement of submissions Performance Guarantee within seven days was done away by the Monitoring Committee. Be that as it may, the net result is that neither Performance Guarantee was Company Appeal (AT) Ins. No. 604 of 2022 3 submitted nor plan was implemented hence the submissions of the Appellant that entire blame is on the RP does not commend us. The Appellant was Successful Resolution Applicant and he cannot escape from his responsibility to implement the plan. We thus do not find any error in the order impugned. The Appeal is dismissed.
	</p>
