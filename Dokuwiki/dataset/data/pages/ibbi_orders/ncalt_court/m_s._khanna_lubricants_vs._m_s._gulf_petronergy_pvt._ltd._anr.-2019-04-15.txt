		3. A creditor is entitled to assign its debt to another person but a debtor cannot assign a debt payable by it to a third party in absence of any provision to assign debt of a debtor. This apart, as we find that there is p-existence of dispute due to which the application under Section 9 is not maintainable, we are not inclined to interfere with the impugned order dated 17th April, 2018. The appeal is dismissed. No costs.
