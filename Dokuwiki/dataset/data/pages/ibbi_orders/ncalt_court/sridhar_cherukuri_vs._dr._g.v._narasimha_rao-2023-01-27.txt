Union of India &amp; Ors. (2012) 11 SCC 1, Page 90.

Viewed in that perspective, the IA No. 34 of 2023, fails.
	</p>

		Result:
	</p>

		In fine, IA No. 34 of 2023 in Comp. App (AT) (CH) (INS.) No. 13 of 2023 is dismissed. No costs.
