<h2>Mr. Nitin Chandrakant Desai vs Edelweiss Asset Reconstruction ... on 1 August, 2023</h2>
<h3>Author: Ashok Bhushan</h3>
<h3>Bench: Ashok Bhushan</h3>
<p>NATIONAL COMPANY LAW APPELLATE TRIBUNAL PRINCIPAL BENCH, NEW DELHI Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 1022 of 2023IN THE MATTER OF:Nitin Chandrakant Desai ...AppellantVersusEdelweiss Asset Reconstruction Ltd. &amp; Anr. ...RespondentsPresent: For Appellant: Advocate Anju Jain, Advocate Hitesh Sachar For Respondent: Advocate R.P. Agrawal, Advocate Vidhisha Haritwal, For R-1 ORDER</p>
<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">01.08.2023: Heard Learned Counsel for the Appellant. Learned Counsel for the Respondent is also psent.</p>
<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">2. This Appeal has been filed against the Order dated 25th July, 2023 by which order the Adjudicating Authority has admitted Section 7 Application filed by the Respondent.</p>
<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">3. Learned Counsel for the Appellant submits the Application was barred by Section 10A which submission has wrongly been rejected.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">4. We have considered the submissions and perused the record.</p>
<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">5. The Adjudicating Authority has considered the submission of Appellant and dealt with it in paragraph 8:</p>
<p style="padding-left: 30px;" title="Analysis of the law">"The Financial Creditor has successfully proved the existence of "debt" and "default" through the record of default issued by the NESL apart from other security -2- documents and loan documents. The date of default is mentioned as 31.01.2020 in the NESL Certificate in the record of default and the above Company Petition being filed on 26.07.2022 is well within limitation. Certainly the above date of default does not fall within 10A period and the argument of Corporate Debtor with regard to the default during 10A period needs to be rejected on merits. The Financial Creditor has also suggested the name of Mr. Jitender Kothari as proposed IRP to be appointed in this matter and thus the above Company Petition satisfies all the legal requirements for admission and this Bench did not find any valid reason to dismiss the same."</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">6. The Adjudicating Authority has noticed and returned a finding that the default recorded in the NESL is 31.01.2020. The default on 31.01.2020 is obviously prior to the Section 10 A period. When default has been committed by the Corporate Debtor prior to Section 10A period, any default committed during the Section 10A period can not be held to bar the application which is filed on the basis of default prior to Section 10A and subsequent to Section 10A period.</p>
<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">7. Learned Counsel for the Appellant submits that restructuring was also permitted on 17.08.2020. The restructuring itself indicated that there has been default subsequent to Section 10A period i.e. 31st March, 2021 which is mentioned in paragraph 7.8. The Application which has been filed under Section 7 gives the detail for Part-IV of the Application which part of the appeal itself indicate the date of default as 31.01.2020. Learned Counsel for the Appellant has referred to Page 83 and 84 of the Appeal Paper Book. At page Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 1022/2023 -3- 84, date of default due date has been mentioned as 31st March, 2019 for the financial year 2018-19. Reading of the Application indicates that default was committed by the Corporate Debtor prior to Section 10A period.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">8. We are satisfied that no error has been committed by the Adjudicating Authority in admitting Section 7 Application. There is no merit in the Appeal, the Appeal is dismissed.</p>
