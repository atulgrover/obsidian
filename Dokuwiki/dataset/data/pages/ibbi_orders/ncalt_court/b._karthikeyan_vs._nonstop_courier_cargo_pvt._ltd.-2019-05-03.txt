	<h2>
		National Company Law Appellate Tribunal
	</h2>
	<h2>
		B.Karthikeyan vs Nonstop Courier And Cargo Pvt Ltd on 3 May, 2019
	</h2>
	<p>
		 NATIONAL COMPANY LAW APPELLATE TRIBUNAL NEW DELHI Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 476 of 2019IN THE MATTER OF:B. Karthikeyan ...AppellantVersusNonstop Courier &amp; Cargo Pvt. Ltd. ...RespondentPresent:For Appellant : Mr. S. Ramajayam, Advocate ORDER
	</p>
	<p title="Fact" data-structure="Facts">
		03.05.2019 The Appellant filed an application u/s 9 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (for short, the 'I&amp;B Code') against 'M/s. Nonstop Courier and Cargo Private Limited' which has been rejected by order dated 14th March, 2019 by the Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal), Division Bench, Chennai. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the Appellant submits that the respondent failed to refund the deposit amount of Rs. 2.50 Lakhs deposited by the Appellant with the 'Corporate Debtor'. It is submitted that the aforesaid amount of Rs. 2.50 Lakhs deposited to get franchisee rights over Alwarpet and T. Nagar branches of 'M/s. Nonstop Courier and Cargo Private Limited'.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		2. From the aforesaid submission, it is clear that the Appellant has neither supplied any goods nor provided any services and it has only deposited money for Franchisee rights and as such it cannot be termed as 'Operational Creditor' in terms of Section 9 of the I&amp;B Code. In view of the matter the application under Section 9 being not maintainable, we are not inclined to interfere with the 2 impugned order dated 14th March, 2019. However, we are not expssing any opinion as to whether in the facts and circumstances of the case the Appellant can claim to be a 'Financial Creditor' for initiation of proceeding u/s 7 of the I&amp;B Code.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		The appeal is dismissed with aforesaid observations.
	</p>
