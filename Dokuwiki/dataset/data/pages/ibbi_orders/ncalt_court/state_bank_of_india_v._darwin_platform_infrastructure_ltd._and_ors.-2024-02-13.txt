	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		The above section enables a party to institute a suit, pfer an appeal or make an application on the day the court reopens where the pscribed period for any suit, appeal or application expires on the day when the court is closed.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		13. The crucial words in Section 4 of the 1963 Act are "pscribed period". What is the meaning of these words?
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		14. Section 2(j) of the 1963 Act defines:
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		"2.(j) 'period of limitation' [which] means the period of limitation pscribed for any suit, appeal or application by the Schedule, and 'pscribed period' means the period of limitation computed in accordance with the provisions of this Act;"
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		Section 2(j) of the 1963 Act when read in the context of Section 34(3) of the 1996 Act, it becomes amply clear that the pscribed period for making an application for setting aside an arbitral award is three months. The period of 30 days mentioned in the proviso that follows sub-section (3) of Section 34 of I.A. No. 5067 of 2023 In Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 1354 of 2023 -22- the 1996 Act is not the "period of limitation" and, therefore, not the "pscribed period" for the purposes of making the application for setting aside the arbitral award. The period of 30 days beyond three months which the court may extend on sufficient cause being shown under the proviso appended to sub-section (3) of Section 34 of the 1996 Act being not the "period of limitation" or, in other words, the "pscribed period", in our opinion, Section 4 of the 1963 Act is not, at all, attracted to the facts of the psent case."
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		32. The ratio of the above judgment is categorical that the period of 30 days which the Court may extend on sufficient cause is not the period of limitation hence the benefit of Section 4 is applicable only with regard to period of limitation pscribed for filing an application.
