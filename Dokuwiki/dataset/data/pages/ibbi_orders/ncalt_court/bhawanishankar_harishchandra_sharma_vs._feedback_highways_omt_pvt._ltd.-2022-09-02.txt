	<h2>
		Bhawanishankar Harishchandra Sharma vs Feedback Highways Omt Private Limited ... on 2 September, 2022
	</h2>
	<p>
		NATIONAL COMPANY LAW APPELLATE TRIBUNAL PRINCIPAL BENCH, NEW DELHI Company Appeal (AT) (Ins.) No. 217 &amp; 218 of 2022IN THE MATTER OF:Bhawanishankar Harishchandra Sharma ...AppellantVersusFeedback Highways OMT Pvt. Ltd. &amp; Anr. ...RespondentsPresent:For Appellant: None for AppellantFor Respondents: Mr. Ravi Raghunath, Adv. For IRP. ORDER
	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		15.09.2022: This matter has been put up before us by the Registry in pursuance of the request made by the Registrar vide its letter dated 12.09.2022.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		Vide our order dated 02.09.2022, the Registrar was directed to return the amount of Rs. 1,37,63,889/- deposited in fixed deposit in his name with the interest accrued upon it to the Appellant within a period of 15 days from the date of passing of the said order.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		It is averred by the Registrar that in compliance of the order dated 16.03.2022 passed by this Tribunal, the Appellant had deposited Rs. 1,37,63,889/- in the form of demand draft and not through fixed deposit and the said amount was deposited in the Government Head, which is a non- interest-bearing account, therefore, the deposits made by the Appellant did not bear any interest. As such the Registry is unable to return the amount of Rs. 1,37,63,889/- with interest as directed.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		We have perused the letter dated 12.09.2022 filed by the Registrar, seeking appropriate orders, in context of the aforesaid factual matrix and are of the considered opinion that it is an error of omission which can be corrected by invoking Rule 11 of NCLAT Rules, 2016. Hence, it is directed that the observations made in Para 26 of our order dated 02.09.2022 shall be read as under:-
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning">
		"As per the aforesaid order, the Appellant had deposited of Rs. 1,37,63,889/- in the name of the Registrar of this Tribunal and since, the appeal has been allowed, therefore, the Registrar is directed to refund the aforesaid amount within a period of 15 days from the date of passing of this order"
	</p>
