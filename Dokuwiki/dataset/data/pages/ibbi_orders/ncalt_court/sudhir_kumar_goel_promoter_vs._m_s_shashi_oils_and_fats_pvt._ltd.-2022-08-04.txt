<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">41. Even Section 54 of the Code is clear to that extent that a Liquidator can make an Application once the assets of the Corporate Debtor are completely liquidated, and the Appellant herein does not form a proper and a necessary party to the said application. It is further pertinent to mention herein that the proceedings under IBC are proceedings in rem and for the purpose of a Resolution.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Furthermore, if no resolution is achieved, then the assets of the Company are sold and the creditors of the Corporate Debtor are sold and the amount realised is distributed amongst the Creditors in terms of Section 53 of the Code. Once the distribution is complete, the Company is legally dissolved. In view thereof, the Ex- directors/promoters i.e. the Appellants herein do not have any locus".</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Company Appeal (AT) (Ins) No. 676 of 2021&amp; Company Appeal (AT) (Ins) No. 99 of 2022 -17-</p>
<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">12. The Ld. Counsel for the Respondents have stressed that once a dissolution order has been passed by the Adjudicating Authority there is no merit in the case and the case deserves to be dismissed.</p>
<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">13. It was also submitted by the Counsels for the Respondents that there is no equity in IBC &amp; hence requested the Tribunal to dismiss their applications.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">14. We have gone through the impugned order and examined submissions made by the Ld. Counsels for the parties, particularly facts disclosed in the Memo of Appeal filed by the Appellants. Accordingly, we observe as follows:</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning">(i) It is not in dispute &amp; rather accepted by the Appellants in their own Memo of Appeal at pg-9 that on 23.06.2011 on account of adverse financial situation the company/corporate debtor defaulted in the payment of installments to Allahabad Bank due to which the account of the Company was declared NPA.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning">(ii) It is also not in dispute that Allahabad Bank assigned all its title interest &amp; benefits in respect of the debts of the Company to Respondent No.2 through a deed of assignment at a price of Rs.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">5.75 Crore.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">(iii) CIRP proceedings against the Company was initiated on 20.03.2018. This is also not in dispute that on 17.12.2018, CoC rejected the Resolution Plan submitted by the Appellants. The Appellant submitted the revised proposal on 03.01.2020. Revised Proposal was approved &amp; accepted by Financial Creditor but since Company Appeal (AT) (Ins) No. 676 of 2021&amp; Company Appeal (AT) (Ins) No. 99 of 2022 -18- March/April 2020 Country was put under lockdown by the Government and as also from 22.03.2020 due to global pandemic no application could be filed before NCLT for approval of the revised proposal duly accepted by the Financial Creditor.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Liquidator was however informed. Thereafter the, liquidator proceeded with auction notice from 12.08.2020 &amp; on 29.08.2020 and finally the property was sold to the successful bidder for Rs.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">3.78 Crore.</p>
<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">(iv) Considering all these aspects CA No. 143 of 2021 seeking dissolution of the Company was filed by the liquidator of the Company/Respondent No.1 which was listed on 07.07.2021 which was not served on the Appellants and the Adjudicating Authority allowed the same IA and the Company was purportedly dissolved on 15.07.2021.</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">(v) Once the Company is dissolved under Section 54 of the Code, nothing remains. Section 54 of the IBC is reproduced below:</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">"54. Dissolution of corporate debtor - (1) where the assets of the corporate debtor have been completely liquidated, the liquidator shall make an application to the Adjudicating Authority for the dissolution of such corporate debtor.</p>
<p title="Analysis of the law">(2) The Adjudicating Authority shall on application filed by the liquidator under sub-section (1) order that the corporate debtor shall be dissolved from the date of that order and the corporate debtor shall be dissolved accordingly.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Company Appeal (AT) (Ins) No. 676 of 2021&amp; Company Appeal (AT) (Ins) No. 99 of 2022 -19- (3) A copy of an order under sub-section (2) shall within seven days from the date of such order, be forwarded to the authority with which the corporate debtor is registered".</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">(vi) In view of provisions contained in Section 54 of the IBC, once after the completion of liquidation an application is filed by the liquidator of a Corporate Debtor for its dissolution to the Adjudicating Authority, who has no option but to pass an order of dissolution. In the psent case the Adjudicating Authority has simply complied with the provisions under Section 54(2) of the Code.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">(vii) It is very much clear that the Company is fully dissolved. This dissolution happens when the company is liquidated.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">15. Before parting with order it is apt to clarify that there is no need to go into the matter further as to when the Appellants submitted the OTS/ other proposals &amp; the Financial Creditor/CoC have not accepted their proposals. The equity though not applicable under IBC, even remotely same cannot come into play at this juncture. The role of this Appellate Tribunal is also restricted within the four walls of the 'Code' &amp; passing of order under Section 54 of the Code brings the Corporate Debtor to a closed chapter.</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">Company Appeal (AT) (Ins) No. 676 of 2021&amp; Company Appeal (AT) (Ins) No. 99 of 2022 -20-</p>
<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">16. Accordingly, we do not find any merit in the appeals and it deserve to be dismissed. Both appeals are dismissed. No order as to costs.</p>
