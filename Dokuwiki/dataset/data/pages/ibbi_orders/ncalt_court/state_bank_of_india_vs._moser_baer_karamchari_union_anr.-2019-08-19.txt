		State Bank Of India vs Moser Baer Karamchari Union &amp; Anr on 19 August, 2019
	</h2>
	<p>
		 NATIONAL COMPANY LAW APPELLATE TRIBUNAL, NEW DELHI Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019(Arising out of Order dated 19th March, 2019 passed by the AdjudicatingAuthority (National Company Law Tribunal), Principal Bench in C.A. No.19 (PB) of 2019 in C.P. No. (IB) 378(PB)/ 2017))IN THE MATTER OF:State Bank of India ...AppellantVs.Moser Baer Karamchari Union &amp; Anr. ...RespondentsPresent: For Appellant: - Mr. Ramji Srinivasan, Senior Advocate with Mr. Ankur Mittal, Mr. Abhay Gupta, Ms. Sylona Mohapatra, Mr. Nikhil Ramdev, Advocates. For Respondents:- Mr. Swarnendu Chatterjee and Ms. Shriya Maini, Advocates for R-1. Mr. Abhishek Anand and Mr. Tushar Tyagi, Advocates for R-2. J U D G M E N TSUDHANSU JYOTI MUKHOPADHAYA, J.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		On 14th November, 2017, pursuant to an application under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 ("I&amp;B Code" for short), the 'Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process' was initiated against the 'Corporate Debtor', wherein finally on 20th September, 2018, the order of liquidation was passed by the Adjudicating Authority 2 (National Company Law Tribunal), Principal Bench, New Delhi, and the workmen stood discharged under Section 33(7) of the 'I&amp;B Code'.
	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		2. According to the Liquidator, by e-mail dated 5th December, 2018, he categorically denied the payment of the gratuity fund, the provident fund and the pension fund pferentially and included the same for the payments under the waterfall mechanism under Section 53 of the 'I&amp;B Code'.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		3. In January 2019, the 'Moser Baer Karamchari Union' filed CA No. 19(PB)/ 2019 with prayer that the directions be issued to the Liquidator to exclude the amount due to them towards 'Provident Fund', 'Pension Fund' and Gratuity Trust Fund' from the waterfall mechanism envisaged under Section 53 of the 'I&amp;B Code' and pay them the 'Provident Fund Dues', 'Pension Fund Dues' and 'Gratuity Fund Dues' as these will not constitute part of the liquidation estate.
	</p>
	<p title="Fact" data-structure="Facts">
		4. The Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal), Principal Bench, New Delhi, by impugned order dated 19th March, 2019, allowed the CA No. 19(PB)/ 2019 and held that the 'Provident Fund Dues', 'Pension Fund Dues' and 'Gratuity Fund Dues' cannot be part of Section 53 of the 'I&amp;B Code'. The 'State Bank of India', a 'Secured Creditor', has challenged the order in this appeal. Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 3
	</p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		5. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the Appellant- 'State Bank of India' submitted that for the purpose of distribution of assets of the 'Corporate Debtor' under Section 53 of the 'I&amp;B Code', dues of employees as mentioned in sub-clause (c) of sub-section (1) therein includes the contribution of 'Provident Fund'.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		6. It placed reliance on Explanation below Section 53 to suggest that the 'workmen's dues' shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in Section 326 of the Companies Act, 2013.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		7. Reliance has been placed on Explanation (iv) below Section 326 of the Companies Act, 2013, which relates to 'Overriding Preferential Payments' and Explanation (iv) below Section 326, it is mentioned that all sums due to any workman from the provident fund, the pension fund, the gratuity fund or any other fund for the welfare of the workmen, maintained by the Company is covered by term "workmen's dues".
	</p>
	<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">
		8. Learned counsel for the Appellant- 'State Bank of India' also relied on Section 327 of the Companies Act, 2013 which related to 'Preferential Payments' and submitted that the sums due to the workman from the provident fund or any other fund for the welfare of the workmen, maintained by the Company, be treated as 'workmen dues'.
	</p>
	<p title="Petitioner's Argument" data-structure="PetArg">
		9. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the 'Resolution Professional' submitted that Section 36 (3) of the 'I&amp;B Code' defines the Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 4 components of the liquidation estate and also lays down what forms the liquidation estate. Sub-section (3) therein is subject to sub-section (4). Sub-section (4) (a) (iii) specifically excludes from the liquidation estate or all sums due to any workman or employee from the provident fund, the pension fund and the gratuity fund.
	</p>
	<p title="Respondent's Argument" data-structure="RespArg">
		10. Therefore, it is submitted that the workmen have the first charge on the aforesaid funds.
	</p>
	<p title="Issue" data-structure="Issue">
		11. The question arises for consideration in this appeal is whether the provident fund, pension fund and gratuity fund come within the meaning of assets of the 'Corporate Debtor' for distribution under Section 53 of the 'I&amp;B Code'.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		12. Section 36 of the 'I&amp;B Code' deals with 'Liquidation Estate' for the purpose of liquidation. As per sub-section (1) of Section 36, for the purpose of liquidation, the liquidator shall form an estate of the assets mentioned in sub-section (3), which will be called the liquidation estate in relation to the 'Corporate Debtor' and reads as follows:
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		"36. Liquidation estate.─ (1) For the purposes of liquidation, the liquidator shall form an estate of the assets mentioned in sub-section (3), which will be called the liquidation estate in relation to the corporate debtor.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 5 (2) The liquidator shall hold the liquidation estate as a fiduciary for the benefit of all the creditors. (3) Subject to sub-section (4), the liquidation estate shall comprise all liquidation estate assets which shall include the following: -
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(a) any assets over which the corporate debtor has ownership rights, including all rights and interests therein as evidenced in the balance sheet of the corporate debtor or an information utility or records in the registry or any depository recording securities of the corporate debtor or by any other means as may be specified by the Board, including shares held in any subsidiary of the corporate debtor;
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(c) tangible assets, whether movable or immovable;
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(d) intangible assets including but not limited to intellectual property, securities Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 6 (including shares held in a subsidiary of the corporate debtor) and financial instruments, insurance policies, contractual rights;
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(f) any assets or their value recovered through proceedings for avoidance of transactions in accordance with this Chapter;
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(i) all proceeds of liquidation as and when they are realised.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(4) The following shall not be included in the liquidation estate assets and shall not be used for recovery in the liquidation: -
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		13. From sub-section (4) (a) (iii) of Section 36, it is clear that all sums due to any workman or employee from the provident fund, the pension fund and the gratuity fund, shall not be included in the liquidation estate assets and cannot be used for recovery in the liquidation.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		14. Section 53 relates to 'Distribution of assets', as mentioned hereunder:
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		"53. Distribution of assets.─(1) Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in any law enacted by the Parliament or any State Legislature for the time being in force, the proceeds from the sale of the liquidation assets shall be distributed in Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 9 the following order of priority and within such period as may be specified, namely: -
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(a) the insolvency resolution process costs and the liquidation costs paid in full;
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(b) the following debts which shall rank equally between and among the following:
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(i) workmen's dues for the period of twenty-four months pceding the liquidation commencement date; and
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(ii) debts owed to a secured creditor in the event such secured creditor has relinquished security in the manner set out in section 52;

	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	<p title="Analysis of the law">

	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		(i) it is hereby clarified that at each stage of the distribution of proceeds in respect of a class of recipients that rank equally, each of the debts will either be paid in full, or will be paid in equal proportion within the same class of recipients, if the proceeds are insufficient to meet the debts in full;

	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		(ii) the term "workmen's dues" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in section 326 of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013)."

	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		15. From sub-section (1) of Section 53, it is clear that the proceeds from the sale of the liquidation assets of the 'Corporate Debtor', the distribution is to be made in order of priority and within such period and in such manner as provided thereunder.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		16. In terms of sub-section (4) (a) (iii) of Section 36, as all sums due to any workman or employees from the provident fund, the pension fund Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 12 and the gratuity fund, do not form part of the liquidation estate/ liquidation assets of the 'Corporate Debtor', the question of distribution of the provident fund or the pension fund or the gratuity fund in order of priority and within such period as pscribed under Section 53(1), does not arise.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		17. The 'workmen's dues' is mentioned in clause (b) (i) of Section 53(1), which are the dues for the period of twenty-four months pceding the liquidation commencement date.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		18. In view of the aforesaid specific provisions, the Explanation (iii) below Section 53, for the purpose of meaning of 'workmen's dues', the Appellant cannot derive the meaning as assigned to it in Section 326 of the Companies Act, 2013, including the Explanation below it.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		19. Section 326 of the Companies Act, 2013, since its amendment w.e.f. 1st December, 2016 provides 'overriding pferential payments', relevant of which reads as follows:
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		"326. Overriding Preferential Payments. (1) In the winding up of a company under this Act, the following debts shall be paid in priority to all other debts:--
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		(a) workmen's dues; and Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 13
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		(b) where a secured creditor has realised a secured asset, so much of the debts due to such secured creditor as could not be realised by him or the amount of the workmen's portion in his security (if payable under the law), whichever is less, pari passu with the workmen's dues:
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		Provided that in case of the winding up of a company, the sums referred to in sub-clauses (i) and (ii) of clause (b) of the Explanation, which are payable for a period of two years pceding the winding up order or such other period as may be pscribed, shall be paid in priority to all other debts (including debts due to secured creditors), within a period of thirty days of sale of assets and shall be subject to such charge over the security of secured creditors as may be pscribed.
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis">
		(2) The debts payable under the proviso to sub-
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		section (1) shall be paid in full before any payment is made to secured creditors and thereafter debts payable under that subsection shall be paid in full, unless the assets Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 14 are insufficient to meet them, in which case they shall abate in equal proportions.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		Explanation.--For the purposes of this section, and section 327--
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		(a) "workmen'', in relation to a company, means the employees of the company, being workmen within the meaning of clause (s) of section 2 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (14 of 1947);
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		(b) "workmen's dues'', in relation to a company, means the aggregate of the following sums due from the company to its workmen, namely:--
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(i) all wages or salary including wages payable for time or piece work and salary earned wholly or in part by way of commission of any workman in respect of services rendered to the company and any compensation payable to any workman under any of the Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 15 provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (14 of 1947);
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(ii) all accrued holiday remuneration becoming payable to any workman or, in the case of his death, to any other person in his right on the termination of his employment before or by the effect of the winding up order or resolution;
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(iii) unless the company is being wound up voluntarily merely for the purposes of reconstruction or amalgamation with another company or unless the company has, at the commencement of the winding up, under such a contract with insurers as is mentioned in section 14 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 (19 of 1923), rights capable of being transferred to and vested in the workmen, all amount due in respect of any compensation or Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 16 liability for compensation under the said Act in respect of the death or disablement of any workman of the company;
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(iv) all sums due to any workman from the provident fund, the pension fund, the gratuity fund or any other fund for the welfare of the workmen, maintained by the company;
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law">
		(c) "workmen's portion'', in relation to the security of any secured creditor of a company, means the amount which bears to the value of the security the same proportion as the amount of the workmen's dues bears to the aggregate of the amount of workmen's dues and the amount of the debts due to the secured creditors."
	</p>
	<p title="Precedent Analysis" data-structure="Precedent">
		20. There is a difference between the distribution of assets and pference/ priority of workmen's dues as mentioned under Section 53(1)
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		(b) of the 'I&amp;B Code' and Section 326(1) (a) of the Companies Act, 2013. It has also been noticed that Section 53(1) (b) (i) which relates to Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 17 distribution of assets, workmen's dues is confined to a period of twenty- four months pceding the liquidation commencement date.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		21. While applying Section 53 of the 'I&amp;B Code', Section 326 of the Companies Act, 2013 is relevant for the limited purpose of understanding 'workmen's dues" which can be more than provident fund, pension fund and the gratuity fund kept aside and protected under Section 36(4) (iii).
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		22. On the other hand, the workmen's dues as mentioned in Section 326(1) (a) is not confined to a period like twenty-four months pceding the liquidation commencement date and, therefore, the Appellant for the purpose of determining the workmen's dues as mentioned in Section 53(1) (b), cannot derive any advantage of Explanation (iv) of Section 326 of the Companies Act, 2013.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		23. This apart, as the provisions of the 'I&amp;B Code' have overriding effect in case of consistency in any other law for the time being enforced, we hold that Section 53(1) (b) read with Section 36(4) will have overriding effect on Section 326(1) (a), including the Explanation (iv) mentioned below Section 326 of the Companies Act, 2013.
	</p>
	<p title="Analysis of the law" data-structure="Section">
		24. Once the liquidation estate/ assets of the 'Corporate Debtor' under Section 36(1) read with Section 36 (3), do not include all sum due to any workman and employees from the provident fund, the pension fund and the gratuity fund, for the purpose of distribution of assets under Section Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 396 of 2019 18 53, the provident fund, the pension fund and the gratuity fund cannot be included.
	</p>
	<p title="Court's Reasoning" data-structure="CDiscource">
		25. The Adjudicating Authority having come to such finding that the aforesaid funds i.e., the provident fund, the pension fund and the gratuity fund do not come within the meaning of 'liquidation estate' for the purpose of distribution of assets under Section 53, we find no ground to interfere with the impugned order dated 19th March, 2019.
	</p>
	<p title="Conclusion" data-structure="Conclusion">
		The appeal is accordingly, dismissed. No costs.
