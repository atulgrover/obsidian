# Case Reference - Power Finance Corporation Limited Vs. Mr. Mahender Kumar Khandelwal

# Case Reference

- Case Citation: (2020) ibclaw.in 162 NCLT
- Case Name: Power Finance Corporation Limited Vs. Mr. Mahender Kumar Khandelwal
- Corporate Debtor: KSK Mahanadi Power Company Ltd
- Appeal No.: IA 234/2020 in CP(IB)492/07/HDB/2019
- Judgment Date: 9-May-20
- Court/Bench: NCLT Hyderabad Bench
- Act: Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code 2016
- Member (Judicial): Shri K. Anantha Padmanabha Swamy

# Brief about the decision

Application was filed by the CoC seeking replacement of the IRP in accordance with section 22 of the Code. The IRP filed an application seeking the CoC to reconsider its decision and to continue him as RP. He claimed that the decision of the CoC has been based on a disciplinary order passed by the IBBI and it has not recorded any specific reason for the same. The Adjudicating Authority observed that pursuant to stay on the IBBI order by the HC, the IRP cannot be excluded from the consideration of the CoC, yet it is only the prerogative of the CoC to decide whether to continue with the same IRP or replace him with another RP. While allowing the application to replace the RP, it held that in accordance with section 22 of the Code, no reasons are to be recorded for effecting such replacement by the CoC.
