====== Our Pricing ======

===== Pay-It-Forward =====
We believe in **sharing knowledge and tools freely**. All our free offerings are designed to help you grow while giving back to the IBC professional community. These services include
  - **Chatbot Access** ODRchat, IBCchat, VALchat for instant support and dispute resolution guidance
  - **Directory Listing** Get listed as a mediator, professional, or valuer for better visibility
  - **Academic Resources** Access mock tests, case laws, case studies, and study materials
  - **Internship Placements** Connect with opportunities to grow your expertise
</WRAP>

<WRAP half column>
===== Pay-Per-Use =====
For professionals seeking **custom AI solutions and full software support**, we offer paid plans with complete setup and integration:
  - **Digital AI Agents** – Tailored AI assistants for case analysis, decision support, and workflow automation
  - **Frontend & Backend Setup** – Full deployment of your web and internal systems
  - **Premium Support** – Technical assistance, onboarding, and ongoing maintenance
