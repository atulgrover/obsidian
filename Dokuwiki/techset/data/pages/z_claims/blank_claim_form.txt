\\

Process Details - Provide basic information about the insolvency process.
  * process-type: Select the type of process (e.g., CIRP, Liquidation, Personal Guarantor process).
  * process-commencement-date: Enter the official start date in dd-mm-yyyy format.

Professional's Details - This section contains the Insolvency Professional’s (IP) details. Usually pre-filled. Confirm accuracy.
  * professional-name
  * professional-address
  * professional-email
  * professional-phone

Claimant Details - Provide your personal or entity information.
  * claimant-name: Your full legal name / company name.
  * claimant-address: Complete communication address.
  * claimant-type: Choose: Financial Creditor / Operational Creditor / Employee / Other.
  * claimant-email: Valid email for communication.
  * claimant-phone: Mobile number.
  * claimant-pan-no: PAN number (mandatory for entities/individuals).
  * claimant-aadhar-no: Aadhaar number (if applicable).
  * claimant-entity: Type of entity (Individual, Proprietorship, Partnership, LLP, Company).

Claim Details - Explain the financial claim.
  * claim-principal-amount: Principal outstanding in INR.
  * claim-interest-amount: Interest claimed till commencement date.
  * claim-interest-calculation: Basis of interest: rate, period, and calculation method.
  * claim-nature: Nature of claim (e.g., loan, supply of goods/services, dues, damages).

Claim Questions - Describe the background and supporting details.
  * details-document-list: List all documents attached (invoices, agreements, ledgers, bank statements).
  * details-debt-incurred: How and why the debt arose.
  * details-mutual-dealing: Any mutual transactions or adjustments.
  * details-security-held: Details of security, collateral, guarantees (if any).
  * details-assignment-transfer: If the claim was assigned or transferred, provide details.
  * details-dispute-story: Any disputes related to the debt.
  * details-litigation-story: Ongoing or past litigation, arbitration, notices.

Bank Details -Details for receiving payments (if distributions happen).
  * bank-name
  * bank-account-no
  * bank-ifsc-code
  * bank-branch-address

Document Attachments - Upload all supporting documents. Ensure files are clear and legible.
  * attachments: Agreements, invoices, loan documents, security documents, bank statements, correspondence, court orders, etc.

Click Submit Claim after verifying all information. Keep a copy of the acknowledgment for your records.
