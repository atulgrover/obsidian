===== Case Study 4 =====

**CGS Pvt. Ltd., founded in 2012, is a garment manufacturer specialising in mid-fashion garments and producing for some European brands. The company is covered under the MSME limit. It has a working capital loan and a term loan of ₹10 crores. The corporate debtor was doing well, but due to the coronavirus pandemic, it went into a liquidity crunch on account of small revenue and high fixed costs and was therefore unable to repay the interest and instalments of the loan to the bank. The current default is in excess of ₹25 lakhs. The strength of the company is its designing team, workforce, and production of garments for the European market. The management decided to go for a pre-pack insolvency process to revive the company under Section 54A of the IBC and searched for companies with a market in Europe that could provide maximum value to its stakeholders. The management negotiated and finalised the best offer and presented it to the promoters. The majority of promoters approved the decision in their meeting. The management then approached the financial creditors with the base plan. The majority of financial creditors have given their in-principle consent for the pre-pack insolvency process subject to compliance with the IBC. The Board of Directors of the Corporate Debtor nominated Resolution Professional, Mr. P. Kranti, and intimated the financial creditors. The directors have filed a pre-pack application.**
