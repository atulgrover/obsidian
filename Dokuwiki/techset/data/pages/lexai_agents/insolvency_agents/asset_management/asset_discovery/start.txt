**IV. Asset Management Division**
Responsible for tracing, valuing, securing, and disposing of assets.

**1. Asset_Discovery_Mapping_Agent**

This agent identifies, catalogs, and geolocates assets linked to the corporate debtor using structured (ROC filings, bank records) and unstructured data (emails, invoices, public databases). It creates an asset inventory map — physical, digital, and financial — tagging ownership, encumbrances, and current status to assist insolvency professionals in early-stage resolution and valuation.

**2. Asset_Realization_Agent**

Responsible for transforming identified assets into recoverable value through sale, lease, securitization, or settlement. It evaluates disposal routes (auction, direct sale, strategic investor, etc.), simulates expected recovery under each option, and coordinates workflows between valuers, auctioneers, and creditors to maximize realization proceeds.

**3. Hidden_Asset_Detector**

This agent leverages forensic accounting, transaction pattern analysis, and entity-link graphs to uncover concealed or diverted assets — such as related-party transactions, offshore holdings, or benami properties. It cross-references corporate disclosures with financial trails, blockchain records, and director relationships to trace recoverable assets otherwise hidden from books.

**4. Intangible_Asset_Identifier_Agent**

Specialized in identifying and valuing non-physical assets — trademarks, copyrights, patents, goodwill, software licenses, and customer data. It scans IP registries, digital platforms, and contract repositories to capture and assess the economic potential of intangible resources, ensuring full inclusion in resolution and liquidation value assessments.

* **Asset_Linkage_Tracing_Agent** – builds relationship graphs between debtor entities, subsidiaries, and asset-holding companies.
* **Encumbrance_Verification_Agent** – validates existing charges, liens, and collateral structures from CERSAI, MCA, and bank filings.
* **Asset_Status_Monitoring_Agent** – provides real-time updates on asset possession, deterioration, or legal disputes.
* **Asset_Recovery_Simulation_Agent** – models recovery outcomes under different liquidation or restructuring scenarios.
