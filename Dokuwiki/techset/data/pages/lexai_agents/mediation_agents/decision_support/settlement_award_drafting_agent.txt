**Settlement / Award Drafting Agent**

The Settlement / Award Drafting Agent consolidates validated clauses, factual summaries, and compliance confirmations into a final structured document. It formats the settlement or award according to prescribed layouts used by NCLT or mediation platforms, ensuring readability and legal precision.

It ensures that the final draft clearly defines obligations, timelines, rights, and enforcement mechanisms for all parties. By integrating insights from negotiation and decision-support agents, it produces a balanced, enforceable outcome that reflects mutual consent and statutory compliance.

Finally, the agent can automatically generate digital filing formats, annexures, and sign-off pages for submission or e-filing. This end-to-end automation accelerates closure of cases and enhances consistency in settlement documentation across multiple insolvency proceedings.
