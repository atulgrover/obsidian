The Dispute Categorization Agent automatically classifies disputes based on their nature—contractual, financial, operational, employment-related, or regulatory. It applies NLP and rule-based models to identify the type and sub-type of conflict using submitted documents or summaries.

This classification is critical in determining the appropriate resolution mechanism under the IBC or mediation frameworks, such as whether the dispute falls under Section 7 (financial), Section 9 (operational), or pre-pack mediation under Section 54A. Proper categorization ensures procedural alignment and correct forum selection.

The agent produces a concise dispute taxonomy report, helping Insolvency Professionals and Mediators allocate resources, assign experts, and determine complexity levels early. It forms the foundation for subsequent eligibility, jurisdiction, and readiness assessments.
