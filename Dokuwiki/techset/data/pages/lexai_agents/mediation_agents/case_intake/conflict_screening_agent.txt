The Conflict Screening Agent ensures that the appointed Insolvency Professional, mediator, or supporting professional has no conflict of interest with any party involved in the matter. It automatically cross-checks names and relationships against professional databases, prior assignments, and related-party disclosures.

By analyzing shareholding patterns, director interlocks, and previous engagements, it flags potential ethical or professional conflicts that may compromise neutrality. The agent adheres to IBBI’s Code of Conduct and mediation ethics standards.

This proactive screening protects both the professional’s credibility and the process’s fairness. It generates a conflict clearance report, which can be maintained as part of the case record for audit or regulatory review.
