**Bias Detection & Ethics Agent**

The Bias Detection & Ethics Agent ensures neutrality and fairness throughout the resolution or mediation process. It uses natural language processing and data analysis to detect potential bias in communication, decision-making, or outcome patterns. This maintains the ethical integrity of proceedings handled by insolvency professionals or mediators.

The agent monitors for preferential treatment, conflict of interest indicators, or sentiment imbalances across stakeholder communications. When detected, it provides anonymized reports or alerts to oversight bodies for corrective action.

By embedding ethical compliance into every stage of the process, it strengthens institutional trust, aligns with professional conduct standards, and promotes equitable outcomes under IBC frameworks.
