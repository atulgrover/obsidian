4. Dialogue Flow Agent

The Dialogue Flow Agent manages structured conversational flow in mediation or hearing settings, ensuring that discussions stay goal-oriented. It organizes dialogue into stages—opening statements, issue framing, bargaining, and closure—while prompting adherence to agreed agendas.

It assists mediators by generating dynamic prompts or rephrased questions to keep discussions productive and balanced. The agent can also identify when a conversation is looping or deviating from core issues, offering subtle nudges to redirect focus.

This structured conversational control enhances procedural discipline and clarity in negotiations. The Dialogue Flow Agent thereby ensures efficient use of session time while maintaining neutrality and coherence in dialogue management.
