6. BATNA/WATNA Agent

The BATNA/WATNA Agent (Best/Worst Alternative to Negotiated Agreement) helps parties and mediators assess their strategic position by simulating possible outcomes if no settlement is reached. It quantifies potential legal costs, timelines, and recovery values under litigation or liquidation scenarios.

By presenting data-driven comparisons between negotiated outcomes and alternatives, the agent enhances rational decision-making. It helps participants recognize the practical value of compromise versus the risk of prolonged dispute.

This analytical clarity empowers mediators to guide parties toward mutually beneficial settlements. The BATNA/WATNA Agent thus transforms negotiation from a positional battle into a reasoned, outcome-focused discussion.
