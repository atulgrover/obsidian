**Enforcement Tracker Agent**

The Enforcement Tracker Agent manages the post-award enforcement process when parties fail to comply with settlement or adjudicated obligations. It identifies non-compliance events, initiates reminders or demand notices, and supports the preparation of enforcement petitions or applications under the IBC or Arbitration and Conciliation Act.

It maintains a repository of all enforcement actions—such as execution proceedings, asset attachments, or recovery follow-ups—ensuring a structured view of compliance enforcement history. The agent also assists professionals by recommending the next procedural steps based on the default type and jurisdictional rules.

By automating this workflow, the agent minimizes delays in enforcing awards and strengthens the deterrence effect of non-compliance. It ensures consistent, lawful, and timely enforcement of obligations, safeguarding creditor interests and maintaining procedural rigor.
