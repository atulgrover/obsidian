====== Agentic Services for Personal Guarantors ======
**PERSONAL GUARANTORS** - Personal guarantors are individuals who have provided guarantees for debts owed by corporate debtors. Under IBC, they can be held liable for repayment in case of default, making them integral to recovering dues during insolvency processes. Personal Guarantors play a significant role in the insolvency process, and AI-driven agents can assist in tracing these individuals, guiding them on their liabilities, and facilitating their representation. These agents enable secure communication channels for guarantors to receive updates on their obligations and engage with relevant stakeholders. They also facilitate disclosure filing and settlement proposals, ensuring that guarantors are aware of their options and can make informed decisions about their involvement in the insolvency process.

  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:personal_guarantors:Personal Liability Assessment Agent (PLAA)]]** - This agent evaluates the personal financial situation of guarantors, assessing potential risks and liabilities arising from corporate debt defaults. It provides strategies to mitigate these risks through asset management and financial planning.
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:personal_guarantors:Insolvency Proceedings Navigator Agent (IPNA)]]** - Assists personal guarantors in understanding and navigating the insolvency process under the IBC, including filing procedures and legal requirements. It helps in identifying the appropriate adjudicating authority for personal guarantor insolvency applications.
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:personal_guarantors:Contract Review and Compliance Agent (CRCA)]]** - Analyzes guarantee contracts to ensure compliance with legal standards and identifies potential loopholes or clauses that could impact liability. It also monitors changes in relevant laws and regulations affecting personal guarantors.
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:personal_guarantors:Risk Management and Mitigation Agent (RMMA)]]** - Develops strategies to manage and mitigate risks associated with personal guarantees, including diversification of assets and negotiation with creditors. It provides real-time updates on market conditions that could affect liability.
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:personal_guarantors:Creditors' Negotiation Support Agent (CNSA)]]** - Assists in negotiations with creditors by analyzing debt restructuring options and providing data-driven arguments to support favorable settlement terms. It helps maintain communication channels with creditors to prevent misunderstandings.
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:personal_guarantors:Asset Protection and Optimization Agent (APOA)]]** - Identifies and optimizes assets that could be at risk due to personal guarantees, suggesting strategies to protect them from creditors. It advises on asset diversification and management to minimize exposure.
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:personal_guarantors:Legal Precedent and Case Law Analyzer Agent (LPCLA)]]** - Tracks and analyzes recent legal precedents and case laws related to personal guarantors under the IBC, providing insights into potential legal challenges and opportunities. It helps in understanding the implications of recent judgments on personal guarantor liability.
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:personal_guarantors:Financial Planning and Restructuring Agent (FPRA)]]** - Offers personalized financial planning advice to manage personal finances effectively during insolvency proceedings. It suggests restructuring strategies to ensure financial stability and minimize the impact of corporate debt defaults.
