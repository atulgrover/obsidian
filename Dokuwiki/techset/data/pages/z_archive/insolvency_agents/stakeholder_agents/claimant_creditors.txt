====== Agentic Services for Claimants ======

**CLAIMANTS** - Creditor claimants are individuals or entities with financial claims against the corporate debtor. They include financial creditors, operational creditors, and secured creditors, each with distinct rights under the IBC framework. Their claims are verified and prioritized during CIRP to ensure equitable distribution of recovered assets. Claimants play a pivotal role in guiding creditors through the claim submission, verification, and status tracking processes within the insolvency framework. These agents utilize advanced technologies such as Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to extract and validate claim details from uploaded documents. This automated process ensures accuracy and efficiency in handling claims, reducing manual errors and the time required for verification.

  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:claimant_creditors:Claim Verification and Validation Agent (CVVA)]]** - This agent verifies the legitimacy and accuracy of claims submitted by financial and operational creditors, ensuring compliance with legal requirements. It automates the process of checking claim documentation and supporting evidence. \\
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:claimant_creditors:Priority and Ranking Analysis Agent (PRAA)]]** - Analyzes the priority and ranking of claims under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), providing insights into the likely distribution of assets. It helps claimants understand their position in the creditor hierarchy. \\
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:claimant_creditors:Insolvency Process Navigator Agent (IPNA)]]** - Assists claimants in navigating the complexities of insolvency proceedings, including timelines, legal requirements, and stakeholder communication. It provides real-time updates on case progress and milestones. \\
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:claimant_creditors:Settlement Negotiation Support Agent (SNSA)]]** - Supports claimants in negotiations with the resolution professional or other creditors by analyzing potential settlement options and providing data-driven arguments. It helps in identifying mutually beneficial agreements. \\
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:claimant_creditors:Asset Recovery and Distribution Agent (ARDA)]]** - Monitors the recovery and distribution of assets during the insolvency process, ensuring that claimants receive fair compensation based on their priority and claim amount. It tracks asset sales and distribution plans. \\
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:claimant_creditors:Regulatory Compliance and Reporting Agent (RCRA)]]** - Ensures that all claims and communications comply with regulatory requirements, automating the preparation of reports and submissions to relevant authorities. It keeps claimants informed about legal obligations and deadlines. \\
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:claimant_creditors:Financial Data Analysis Agent (FDAA)]]** - Analyzes financial data related to the corporate debtor to assess potential recovery prospects and provide insights into the financial health of the company. It helps claimants make informed decisions about their claims. \\
  * **[[rbz_agenbots:insolvency_agents:stakeholder_agents:claimant_creditors:Stakeholder Communication and Management Agent (SCMA)]]** - Facilitates effective communication between claimants and other stakeholders, including the resolution professional, CoC, and NCLT. It automates routine updates and notifications, ensuring that all parties are aligned throughout the process.
