The sources strongly support the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in the **Outcome Implementation** phase, which occurs immediately after a resolution plan has been approved. This phase, often called **Post Approval Monitoring**, is crucial for ensuring the success of the restructuring and adherence to the plan's terms. AI is positioned to manage the detailed tracking, monitoring, and compliance verification necessary to prevent failures during implementation.

## AI Agents in Outcome Implementation (Post Approval Monitoring)

### 1. Plan Approval Tracking Agent / Compliance Verification Agent (Ensure Adherence to Plan Terms)

These agents focus on systematically extracting and monitoring the legal and procedural requirements embedded within the approved resolution plan or bankruptcy order.

*   **Extracting Covenants and Milestones:** AI is capable of handling the initial, critical task of extracting all **covenants and milestones** contained within an approved resolution plan.
*   **Compliance Detail:** The agent can process this information to detail exactly **what the requirement is** (e.g., filing a specific form), **which act does it fall under** (e.g., Income Tax Act, Revenue Act, or a different jurisdiction), and what the **timelines** for it are.
*   **Automated Filing Requirements:** The agent can track the specific forms that need to be filed and when, addressing the ongoing regulatory burden on the Insolvency Professional (IP) or the monitoring committee.

### 2. Performance Monitoring Agent (Track Implementation Post-Approval)

This agent leverages continuous AI analysis to monitor the actual operational and financial performance of the reorganized corporate debtor against the benchmarks set in the plan.

*   **Early Warning Dashboards:** The core function of this agent is to provide **early warning dashboards** in case the plan is **not going as per the agreed terms and conditions**. This capability enables proactive management rather than reactive troubleshooting.
*   **Real-time Updates:** AI systems can update this dashboard on an **almost real-time basis**. This sort of information is powerful because if performance starts to drift, the Committee of Creditors (CoC) or monitoring authority can quickly address the issue.
*   **Proactive Monitoring:** This shift allows monitoring to be **more proactive than reactive**. Instead of the CoC conducting multiple meetings to take stock of every minute detail, the AI updates allow for focused intervention.
*   **Benchmarking:** Globally, AI tools have been used in restructuring cases to **benchmark assumptions in turnaround plans against industry data**. This capability ensures that the ongoing performance is measured against realistic projections, highlighting potential divergence from the plan's underlying assumptions.

### Strategic Context and Benefits

The integration of AI into the implementation phase directly addresses the goals of strengthening the IBC process by improving efficiency and reducing risk:

*   **Risk Reduction:** By proactively monitoring performance and compliance, AI significantly reduces the risk of the reorganized company failing post-approval due to non-adherence, thereby protecting the investment made by the creditors.
*   **Trust and Transparency:** The ability to provide continuous, transparent, and auditable monitoring enhances trust in the overall insolvency ecosystem.
*   **Roadmap Priority:** Implementing AI-enabled monitoring of resolution plans so creditors can track compliance in real time is identified as a key goal in the **medium-term roadmap** (3 to 5 years) for AI adoption in India.
