The **Quotation Request Agent** is designed to streamline the professional engagement process for Insolvency Professionals (IPs) during CIRP and liquidation assignments. Under the IBC framework, IPs must appoint valuers, auditors, legal advisors, and other experts through a transparent and competitive quotation process. This agent automates the drafting and dispatch of standardized quotation request emails, ensuring that each communication is consistent, compliant, and aligned with IBBI’s ethical and procedural requirements.

Once the requests are sent, the agent actively tracks incoming quotations, acknowledgments, and pending responses. It can issue automated reminders to professionals who haven’t responded within stipulated timelines and maintain a centralized log of all correspondence. By integrating with email or workflow systems, it helps IPs maintain an auditable trail of communications, a key element for compliance verification and regulatory inspections under IBBI supervision.

After responses are received, the agent compiles quotations into a comparative evaluation sheet, highlighting cost, scope, and qualification parameters. This assists IPs and stakeholders in making objective, well-documented selection decisions. The **Quotation Request Agent** not only saves time and reduces administrative burden but also enhances transparency, ensuring that the selection of professionals remains fair, efficient, and fully defensible before the Committee of Creditors (CoC) and regulatory authorities.
