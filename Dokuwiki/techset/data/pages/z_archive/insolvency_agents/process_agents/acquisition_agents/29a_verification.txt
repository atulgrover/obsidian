The **Section 29A Eligibility Verification Agent** assists Insolvency Professionals (IPs) in ensuring that every resolution applicant complies with the disqualification criteria outlined in Section 29A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Before a resolution plan can be considered by the Committee of Creditors (CoC), the IP must confirm that the applicant is not ineligible due to factors like prior loan defaults, NPA status, or connections with disqualified entities. This agent automates the verification process, conducting a structured due diligence check across multiple regulatory and financial data sources.

Using integrations with public databases such as the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), CIBIL, SEBI, and court records, the agent cross-verifies directorships, shareholdings, related-party linkages, and litigation history. It employs name-matching algorithms and relationship mapping to identify indirect or hidden connections that may trigger disqualification under clauses (a) to (j) of Section 29A. This data-driven approach minimizes human error, saves time, and ensures that IPs adhere to regulatory expectations of thorough verification and objectivity.

Once the verification is complete, the agent compiles its findings into an automated **eligibility assessment report**. The report includes supporting evidence, database references, and a clear compliance or red-flag status for each applicant. This standardized documentation can be submitted to the CoC or the Adjudicating Authority (NCLT) as part of the resolution plan evaluation process. In doing so, the 29A Eligibility Verification Agent enhances procedural transparency, strengthens regulatory compliance, and protects the integrity of the resolution process.
