The **Asset Marketing Agent** is a specialized AI-driven assistant designed to support Insolvency Professionals (IPs) in promoting distressed assets or companies under the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) and liquidation. It automates the preparation of professional-grade investment teasers, pitch decks, and sale notices, ensuring that all marketing materials comply with IBBI regulations and confidentiality standards. By presenting accurate, investor-friendly information, the agent helps position each asset attractively to potential buyers while maintaining transparency and fairness in the process.

Leveraging market intelligence and analytics, the agent identifies the most relevant buyer segments — including strategic investors, private equity firms, and asset reconstruction companies. It uses data-driven targeting to match assets with buyers who have shown prior interest in similar sectors or distressed opportunities. By integrating with digital marketing channels, industry platforms, and investor networks, the agent ensures wide visibility for each opportunity within the limited timelines defined under the IBC.

In addition to outreach, the Asset Marketing Agent continuously monitors engagement data — such as teaser downloads, inquiry responses, and follow-up rates — to refine marketing efforts in real time. It can generate dashboards and reports summarizing buyer interest and campaign effectiveness, assisting IPs in demonstrating due diligence and transparency to the Committee of Creditors (CoC) and regulators. Ultimately, this agent transforms asset sale efforts into a structured, data-backed marketing process that enhances value realization and stakeholder confidence.
