The **Investor Search Agent** plays a critical role in the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework by assisting Insolvency Professionals (IPs) in identifying potential investors or resolution applicants interested in acquiring distressed companies or assets. During the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) or liquidation, time-bound discovery of suitable investors is essential to maximize value. This agent automates the scanning of open databases, financial platforms, and industry networks to shortlist strategic and financial investors aligned with the debtor’s sector and scale.

Beyond simple identification, the agent evaluates investor suitability based on parameters such as investment appetite, sector expertise, and historical turnaround experience. By applying data filters and AI-driven profiling, it helps IPs prioritize investors most likely to submit feasible and compliant resolution plans. The agent can also segment investors by domestic and international interest, enabling a broader, yet targeted, investor outreach strategy in compliance with IBC timelines.

Once potential investors are identified, the agent supports **structured communication and engagement**. It drafts personalized outreach emails, attaches investment teasers, and tracks response metrics. This systematic approach ensures transparency, reduces manual workload, and enhances the likelihood of competitive bidding. In effect, the Investor Search Agent becomes a digital business development arm for insolvency professionals—accelerating investor discovery, promoting fair participation, and ultimately contributing to improved recovery outcomes under the IBC.
