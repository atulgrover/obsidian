## AI’s Role in Financial Assessment

AI is positioned as an "assistant" or "digital superhero" that can analyze financial statements, pointing out inconsistencies that a human eye might miss. This capability is crucial for the Resolution Professional (RP) whose core objective can be lost under the **heap of responsibilities** involved in sifting through information.

### 1. Asset-Liability Analysis Agent

This agent would specifically handle the compilation and balancing of asset and liability data, which is necessary for establishing the corporate debtor’s true financial position.

*   **Financial Statement Analysis:** AI can **analyze financial statements** and **claim documents**. This analysis forms the basis of understanding the company's assets and liabilities.
*   **Data Aggregation and Matching:** AI uses **entity matching algorithms** to pull data from multiple public sources to create a comprehensive asset register. Sources for this data include **MCA filings**, **Land registries**, **GST records**, and **Balance sheets**.
*   **Discrepancy Flagging:** The agent can **flag discrepancies** if an asset appears in the balance sheet but is not in the registry data, or vice versa.
*   **Extracting Key Financial Information:** Specifically for liabilities, intelligent software is used to **extract key information from banks’ records**, reading thousands of loan documents in minutes and identifying crucial credit information, including **guarantors** and **collateral specifics**. This process is faster, **highly accurate**, and cost-effective.

### 2. Income Verification Agent

The assessment of a company’s financial health requires accurate verification of its income streams and performance drivers.

*   **Real-time Accounting Tools:** Emerging technologies used in insolvency include **real-time accounting tools**, which would provide the necessary dynamic data for income verification.
*   **Performance Analysis:** Technology helps restructuring professionals **analyse financial information**, assisting in the identification of **performance drivers**.
*   **Benchmarking Plan Assumptions:** While related to plan evaluation, the same analytical capability can be used early on. AI can benchmark income and performance expectations against **industry data** and highlight risks. This capability ensures that the RP receives a realistic financial picture, for example, noting if a plan assumes unrealistically high market growth compared to the sector’s Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR).

### 3. Net Worth Calculator (Financial Modeling)

The ultimate goal of assessing assets and liabilities is determining the corporate debtor's net worth or required liquidation value, a quantitative task perfectly suited for AI.

*   **Automating Financial Models:** AI can **run the financial models** to assess the company’s ability to meet its obligations.
*   **Quantitative Analysis:** In the subsequent stage of resolution plan evaluation, the **quantitative part is pretty straightforward**—how much is upfront, how much is deferred, etc.—and this type of complex math can be performed by an AI financial analyst. The calculation of net worth based on verified assets and liabilities falls directly within these capabilities.
*   **Decision Support:** The resulting analysis provides **data-driven insights and recommendations** based on the analysis of complex financial information, aiding the RP in making informed decisions. The aim is to create an **expert system output** that adapts with market conditions.

By deploying these agents, the RP effectively **offloads this heavy lifting** to AI, enabling them to focus their energy on **strategic aspects of the case**, such as interpreting the compiled statements and formulating recovery strategies.
