The sources extensively support the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in **Monitoring and Audit** functions within insolvency proceedings, confirming the need to detect irregularities, ensure transparency, and provide the necessary analytical tools. This function is vital for maintaining trust and accountability in the IBC ecosystem.

## AI Agents in Monitoring and Audit

### 1. Fraud & Anomaly Detection Agent

This agent addresses the critical need to identify misconduct, fraud, and dissipation of assets, which is a core investigative responsibility of the Insolvency Professional (IP) or liquidator.

*   **Financial Irregularity Detection:** AI can enhance **fraud detection capabilities**, identifying irregularities or suspicious activities in financial transactions that may contribute to insolvency.
*   **Basic Audit Functionality:** AI provides **basic audit functionality** and can be integrated to detect anomalies during the **look-back period** for avoidance transactions (such as under Section 66 filings). AI is excellent at **spotting patterns** in large sweeps of data within fractions of seconds, which is far more efficient than human scraping.
*   **Investigative Support (TAR):** **Technology Assisted Review (TAR)**, often powered by AI, can be applied to investigations where the IP needs to establish whether there has been **dissipation of assets**. TAR automates document review by using predictive coding to classify electronic documents, ranking them by relevance to help humans find necessary information quickly.
*   **Detecting "Phoenixing":** Internationally, AI is being piloted by regulators (e.g., in Australia) to detect **"phoenixing"**—where directors shut down a company and restart under a new name to avoid debts—by scanning corporate registries for suspicious patterns of behavior.
*   **Litigation Support:** During liquidation, AI can continue to pursue avoidance claims, building **robust evidence bundles** for unresolved avoidance claims and creating very robust case books.

### 2. Performance Analytics Agent (Post-Approval Monitoring)

This agent leverages AI to track the operational and financial health of the corporate debtor, particularly after a resolution plan has been approved.

*   **Milestone and Covenant Extraction:** AI can **extract all covenants and milestones** in an approved resolution plan.
*   **Early Warning Dashboards:** The agent can provide **early warning dashboards** if the plan is not proceeding according to the agreed terms and conditions. This allows the Committee of Creditors (CoC) to conduct **proactive monitoring** rather than reactive oversight.
*   **Benchmarking:** In restructuring cases globally, AI tools have been used to **benchmark assumptions in turnaround plans against industry data**. Similarly, AI can be used in the CIRP stage to run sensitivity analysis and benchmark resolution plan assumptions (e.g., projected market growth or Plant Load Factor) against industry averages to ensure realism.
*   **Liquidation Milestone Tracking:** The agent can track liquidation milestones, which is critical given that **79% of liquidations exceed the stipulated timeline**.

### 3. Audit Trail Agent (Transparency and Accountability)

The sources deem the generation of audit trails and logs as non-negotiable for AI adoption in insolvency, linking directly to the guardrails of accountability, explainability, and regulatory acceptance.

*   **Explainability Requirement:** One of the biggest criticisms of AI models is that they are "black boxes". In insolvency, this is **unacceptable**. Stakeholders need to see the **chain of reasoning**. The Audit Trail Agent's core function is to ensure that AI tools offer **audit trails**—not just answers, but why that answer was reached.
*   **Judicial Acceptance:** Without explainability and auditable logs, **AI won't gain judicial acceptance**. Judges must be comfortable that what they are seeing is a **reliable, verifiable record** and not "just a machine's guesswork".
*   **Evidentiary Standards:** AI-assisted filings are classified as electronic records and must satisfy evidentiary standards, including authenticity, certification, non-tempering, and reliable source. A human oversight or an **audit trail** helps satisfy these standards.
*   **Propriety and Trust:** Generating audit trails electronically, such as during automated VDR management or query handling, ensures the **propriety of how things were handled** and creates trails, meaning the IP is less likely to be questioned.
*   **Accountability:** Accountability for AI-generated decisions **rests with the professional who used it**. Therefore, the Audit Trail Agent is essential for the IP to demonstrate the logic applied, reinforcing the formula that **AI plus human is the only viable formula**.

In essence, these monitoring and audit agents are crucial for realizing the goal of transforming insolvency from a process associated with distrust to one characterized by speed, clarity, and **transparency**.
