## Asset Discovery Agents and Functionality

### 1. Asset Identification Agent

This agent's goal is to accurately identify and register all corporate assets, addressing the problem that assets may be scattered, titles disputed, and records outdated.

*   **Data Aggregation and Matching:** The sources detail that AI can help create a **comprehensive asset register** by using **entity matching algorithms** to pull data from multiple public sources.
*   **Source Integration:** Relevant data sources mentioned include:
    *   **MCA filings** (Ministry of Corporate Affairs)
    *   **Land registries**
    *   **GST records**
    *   **Balance sheets**
    *   **Other Public Sources**
*   **Discrepancy Flagging:** The Asset Identification Agent can immediately **flag discrepancies**. For instance, it can alert the Insolvency Professional (IP) if an asset appears in the company’s balance sheet records but is not present in the registry data, or vice versa.
*   **Visualization and Documentation:** Other technologies associated with asset identification include the **use of drones to profile large assets** such as property and land. The ability of AI to organize and process information means that even if it's not plugged into every registry, it can make the process of creating the asset register significantly easier.

### 2. Encumbrance Detection Agent (Verification and Valuation)

While the sources do not use the term "Encumbrance Detection Agent," they detail AI's role in detecting financial indicators, supporting valuation, and extracting collateral information, all of which are essential for determining encumbrances.

#### a. Encumbrance/Collateral Detection
*   **Document Analysis:** AI is highly effective at document management and processing large volumes of financial and legal records. Specialized intelligent software is used to **extract key information from banks' records**, specifically reading thousands of loan documents in minutes and identifying **collateral specifics**. This is faster, highly accurate, and more cost-effective than manual methods.
*   **Forensic and Investigative Support:** AI is utilized in investigations to establish if there has been **dissipation of assets**. Technology Assisted Review (TAR) can search the company's books and records for leads, ranking documents by relevance to help humans find necessary information quickly. Furthermore, AI provides **basic audit functionality** and can be used to identify anomalies during the "look back period" for avoidance transactions.

#### b. Valuation and Pricing
The Encumbrance Detection Agent’s output feeds directly into asset valuation, which is fraught with difficulty during liquidation because the **value that the asset is available at and the value that the market expects never quite align**.

*   **Bridging the Disconnect:** AI can help **bridge this inefficiency** by aiding lenders and liquidators in discovering the actual value.
*   **Valuation Data Points:** AI can look at various data points to inform valuation decisions:
    *   **Comparables:** It can look at **recent sales of similar assets**.
    *   **Market Data:** It can look at **sector information, demand estimations, and adjust for market conditions**.
    *   **Reserve Pricing:** AI can look at reserve price bands and give **decision guidance** to liquidators, suggesting whether to reduce the reserve price by 5% versus 10%.
*   **Highlighting Risks:** AI can highlight if a valuation looks **unusually low**. Crucially, AI does not replace human valuers but provides creditors with an **additional data point** and a reason to question or confirm a valuation.

By implementing these agents, the IP or liquidator, who is currently constrained by capacity limitations and procedural delays, gains powerful, automated tools to move quickly beyond the procedural elements and focus more time on the strategic aspects of the case.
