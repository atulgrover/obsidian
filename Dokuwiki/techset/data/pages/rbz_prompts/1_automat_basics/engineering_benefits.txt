====== Benefits of Prompt Engineering ======

Effective prompt engineering offers several important advantages, making AI systems easier to use, more reliable, and more aligned with user needs.

===== Improved model performance =====
Well-designed prompts provide clear instructions and context, helping the model produce more accurate, relevant, and useful output.

===== Reduced bias and harmful responses =====
By guiding the model’s focus and framing, prompt engineering helps minimize biased, inappropriate, or unsafe responses.

===== Increased control and predictability =====
Structured prompts allow you to influence how the AI behaves, resulting in more consistent and predictable answers that align with your goals.

===== Enhanced user experience =====
Clear, concise prompts make interactions smoother and more intuitive, improving the overall user experience and reducing frustration.
