====== Prompt engineering? ======

Effective prompt engineering relies on a few core elements. Learning these will help you communicate clearly with AI models and unlock their full capabilities.

===== Prompt format =====
The way you structure your prompt has a major effect on how the AI understands your request. Some models respond better to natural language questions, others to direct commands, and some to structured inputs with specific fields. Understanding the model preferences helps you craft better prompts.

===== Context and examples =====
Providing background information and relevant examples gives the AI a clear sense of what you want. If you want a creative story, for example, a few lines describing its tone or theme can significantly improve the output.

===== Fine tuning and adapting =====
Fine tuning the model for a specific domain can improve accuracy, but even without fine tuning you can improve results by adjusting and refining your prompts over time based on the responses you receive.

===== Multi turn conversations =====
Well designed prompts can support multi turn conversations, where the model keeps track of context across messages. This allows more natural, continuous, and helpful interactions.
