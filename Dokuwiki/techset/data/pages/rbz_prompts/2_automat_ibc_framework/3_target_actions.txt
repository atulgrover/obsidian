## 1. Claim-Related Actions

  *  Summarize admitted and rejected claims.
  *  Extract claim amounts, categories, and supporting documents.
  *  Classify claims into financial, operational, employee, statutory.
  *  Validate claims against ledger entries or bank statements.
  *  Format claim registers in IBBI-compliant templates.
  *  Reconcile creditor claims with company books.

---

## 2. CoC & Resolution Plan Actions

  *  Draft CoC meeting minutes or agendas.
  *  Compare multiple resolution plans on feasibility, viability, compliance.
  *  Score resolution plans using defined evaluation matrices.
  *  Translate CoC decisions into legally compliant summaries.
  *  Highlight deviations from Section 30(2) and Regulations.
  *  Simulate recovery distribution under different scenarios.

---

## 3. Compliance & Regulatory Actions

  *  Generate IBC forms (A–H) with structured data.
  *  Review documents for compliance with IBBI/NCLT requirements.
  *  Detect timeline delays against 180/330-day CIRP limits.
  *  Compile regulatory filings for NCLT or IBBI.
  *  Interpret new circulars and summarize implications.

---

## 4. Valuation & Financial Analysis Actions

  *  Analyze valuation reports (fair, liquidation value).
  *  Break down financial ratios relevant to insolvency.
  *  Project recovery outcomes for different classes of creditors.
  *  Identify inconsistencies in the IM and financials.
  *  Normalize financial data for IM preparation.
  *  Benchmark debtor performance against industry standards.

---

## 5. Forensic & Avoidance Transaction Actions

  *  Investigate related-party transactions.
  *  Flag preferential, undervalued, or fraudulent transactions.
  *  Trace fund flow patterns for anomalies.
  *  Compile evidence for Section 43, 45, 50, 66 applications.
  *  Summarize forensic findings for CoC.

---

## 6. Liquidation Process Actions

  *  Prepare e-auction notices and asset sale summaries.
  *  Map assets to valuation and reserve prices.
  *  Order stakeholders in waterfall sequence under Section 53.
  *  Compute distribution amounts per stakeholder group.
  *  Document liquidation process steps for filings.
  *  Track realization vs. expected recovery.

---

## 7. Outreach & Communication Actions

  *  Draft public announcements under Regulation 6/12.
  *  Compose letters/notices to creditors.
  *  Explain process steps in simple or expert language.
  *  Clarify rejection reasons for claims.
  *  Prepare bidder outreach messages or EOI materials.

---

## 8. Data & Information Memorandum Actions

  *  Assemble IM sections (assets, liabilities, litigation, operations).
  *  Structure IM data for VDR distribution.
  *  Summarize key operational or financial risks.
  *  Extract litigation details from MCA/ROC filings.
  *  Organize documents for rapid access.

---

## 9. Decision Support Actions

  *  Rank potential bidders based on eligibility and financial strength.
  *  Optimize distribution outcomes for value maximization.
  *  Recommend procedural steps for compliance issues.
  *  Predict success probability of resolution vs. liquidation.
  *  Aggregate IRP/RP progress metrics into dashboards.

---

## 10. Knowledge Transformation Actions

  *  Explain IBC sections in plain, expert, or judge-level language.
  *  Simplify complex legal or financial concepts.
  *  Rewrite documents in formal legal tone.
  *  Translate legal jargon into layperson summaries.
  *  Contrast differences between CIRP and liquidation obligations.

---
