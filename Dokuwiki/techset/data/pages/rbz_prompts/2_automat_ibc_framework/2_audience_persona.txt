# High-Utility Audience Styles for Prompting
These provide tone and clarity levels:

  *  Explain like I'm a first-time creditor filing Form B
  *  Explain like I'm a stressed promoter unfamiliar with law
  *  Explain like I'm an NCLT judge needing crisp technical points
  *  Explain like I'm a CoC member with only banking experience
  *  Explain like I'm a bidder scanning the IM for red flags
