**Code of Conduct – Agentic Professionals Network (APNet)**

As members of the **Agentic Professionals Network (APNet)**, all participants—Insolvency Professionals, financial specialists, AI practitioners, and allied professionals—are expected to uphold a **unified Code of Conduct** that reflects integrity, accountability, and ethical excellence. This Code establishes the guiding principles that ensure all professional and collaborative activities within APNet uphold the highest standards of the insolvency, finance, and agentic technology ecosystem.

**Integrity and Fair Judgment**

* **Act with Honesty and Integrity:** Members must conduct all professional and network-related activities with transparency, impartiality, and adherence to legal and professional standards.
* **Exercise Sound Judgment:** Every action and decision must be based on verified facts and merit, free from bias, self-interest, or external influence.
* **Honor Professional Commitments:** Uphold promises, deadlines, and responsibilities entrusted by clients, peers, and the APNet community.

**Compliance and Accountability**

* **Adhere to Laws and Standards:** Members must comply with all relevant insolvency, financial, technological, and professional regulations, including those prescribed by IBBI, SEBI, RBI, and other authorities.
* **Stay Informed:** Members are responsible for staying abreast of legal, technological, and ethical developments affecting their professional practice.
* **Be Accountable:** Every member is answerable for their actions and must demonstrate transparency and responsibility in their conduct.

**Confidentiality, Data Protection, and AI Ethics**

* **Protect Sensitive Information:** All client, case, and member data must remain confidential, shared only when legally required or expressly permitted.
* **Ensure Data Security:** Adopt best practices in cybersecurity, data storage, and AI governance to safeguard personal and organizational information.
* **Ethical AI Use:** Members leveraging AI tools or automation must ensure fairness, explainability, and transparency in all outputs and decision-making processes.

**Professional Competence and Growth**

* **Commit to Lifelong Learning:** Engage in continuous education on insolvency law, finance, and emerging agentic technologies to remain at the forefront of professional excellence.
* **Recognize Expertise Boundaries:** Acknowledge personal limitations and seek collaboration or expert input where necessary.
* **Encourage Knowledge Sharing:** Promote learning through mentorship, peer discussions, and collaborative projects within APNet.

**Respectful Conduct and Inclusion**

* **Foster Respect:** Maintain professionalism, courtesy, and empathy in all communications, both online and offline.
* **Champion Diversity:** Create an inclusive environment that respects different perspectives, backgrounds, and disciplines.
* **Zero Tolerance for Misconduct:** Any form of harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated under any circumstance.

**Fairness, Transparency, and Good Governance**

* **Treat All Parties Fairly:** Engage with stakeholders—including creditors, debtors, and peers—with fairness, dignity, and respect.
* **Operate Transparently:** Maintain clear, verifiable records of actions and decisions, providing rationale to relevant stakeholders when appropriate.
* **Promote Good Governance:** Support practices that enhance ethical leadership and accountability in both organizational and professional settings.

**Independence and Conflict of Interest**

* **Preserve Independence:** Avoid professional or personal situations that compromise impartiality or create conflicting interests.
* **Disclose Conflicts Promptly:** Identify and disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest before undertaking engagements.
* **Proper Brand Usage:** APNet’s name, logo, and identity may only be used with authorization and in compliance with established brand guidelines.

**Collaboration and Community Building**

* **Engage Actively:** Participate in APNet initiatives, forums, and committees that advance the profession’s collective mission.
* **Support Fellow Members:** Foster collaboration through mentorship, shared learning, and professional solidarity.
* **Encourage Innovation:** Contribute to the development of new frameworks, digital tools, and methodologies that improve professional outcomes.

**Ethics, Reporting, and Whistleblowing**

* **Report Violations:** Members must promptly report unethical behavior or Code breaches to the APNet Ethics Committee. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and impartially.
* **Protect Whistleblowers:** APNet ensures that members who report misconduct in good faith are safeguarded from retaliation.
* **Uphold Transparency:** Members should cooperate with any inquiries or reviews initiated by APNet’s governance team.

**Commitment to Excellence and Continuous Improvement**

* **Adopt Best Practices:** Strive to integrate modern, ethical, and innovative methods in professional activities.
* **Reflect and Evolve:** Engage in self-evaluation, seek feedback, and adapt to the changing landscape of insolvency and agentic technologies.
* **Contribute to the Profession:** Work collectively to elevate standards of professionalism, integrity, and innovation within the APNet community.
