Developed with transparency and accuracy in mind, Catala is part of a movement to apply formal methods to the legal domain, reducing the risk of errors in automated legal systems. It’s particularly valuable for public institutions and organizations that need to automate compliance with laws while maintaining a high level of transparency for audits and public oversight. This language is seen as a step forward in developing "law-as-code" solutions that empower legal experts to directly contribute to the design and verification of legal software.
