It allows questions to be posed in a natural language format and is tailored for answering specific legal queries in real-time, often as a single decision-making process. It integrates directly with legal databases, making it suitable for interactive legal assessments and document generation. https://www.datalex.org/dev/tools |
|{{:agent_icon.png?300}}|**[[lexai_languages:catala|Catala]]** - Catala is a specialized programming language designed to accurately encode legal logic in a way that’s readable both by legal professionals and developers. Its goal is to bridge the gap between legal text and executable code, especially for systems that rely on complex rules and regulations. Catala is distinctive in its approach to handling ambiguity and interpretation, which are common in legal language; it allows users to explicitly define contexts and exceptions, making it well-suited for encoding tax codes, regulations, and other rule-heavy domains. By providing a clear and structured way to transform legal documents into code, Catala enables the creation of reliable, compliant applications that minimize interpretation errors in legal implementations. https://catala-lang.org/|
|{{:agent_icon.png?300}}|**[[lexai_languages:lexon|Lexon]]** - Lexon is based on SYMBOLIC AI – not on generative AI. It does not suffer from hallucinations and is 100% deterministic. It is based on fully transparent algorithms rather than machine learning. Lexon is an AI tool in the original sense and helps to solve a problem that decades of AI research found to be an insurmountable challenge: the reliable interpretation of natural language. It does so in a surprising way. https://lexon.org/compiler.html|
|{{:agent_icon.png?300}}|**[[lexai_languages:open_fisca|Open Fisca]]** - OpenFrisca is an open-source engine to write rules as code. Describe your tax and benefit system, provide a situation as input (i.e income), ask for a calculation as output (i.e. income tax), and get your results! https://openfisca.org/en/|
