====== Agentic Services for Valuers ======
**REGISTERED VALUERS** - Registered valuers assess the fair market value of a corporate debtor’s assets during CIRP. Their valuations guide resolution plans and liquidation strategies to ensure transparency and accountability in asset disposition. Registered Valuers Registered Valuers play a critical role in the insolvency process by providing accurate valuations of distressed assets, ensuring transparency and informed decision-making. By leveraging AI-driven solutions, Registered Valuers can enhance efficiency, accuracy, and compliance in their operations, contributing significantly to the success of insolvency proceedings. Their role is pivotal in ensuring that stakeholders have reliable information to make informed decisions about resolution plans or liquidation strategies.
</WRAP>

  * **Asset Valuation Intelligence Agent (AVIA)** - This agent uses machine learning models to analyze market trends, historical data, and comparable assets to provide accurate and real-time valuations of tangible and intangible assets. \\
  * **Market Data Aggregator Agent (MDAA)** - Aggregates and analyzes market data from multiple sources, including stock exchanges, real estate listings, and commodity markets, to provide insights into current market conditions affecting asset values. \\
  * **Financial Statement Analyzer Agent (FSAA)** - Automates the review of financial statements, identifying discrepancies, trends, and key metrics that impact the valuation of businesses or assets. \\
  * **Risk Assessment Agent (RAA)** - Evaluates potential risks associated with an asset or business by analyzing factors like market volatility, legal disputes, and operational challenges. \\
  * **Comparable Analysis Agent (CAA)** - Conducts automated comparable analysis by identifying similar assets or businesses in the market and benchmarking their value against the subject property or company. \\
  * **Scenario Simulation Agent (SSA)** - Simulates various economic or operational scenarios to predict how changes in external factors (e.g., interest rates, inflation) might impact the value of an asset. \\
  * **Legal Compliance and Documentation Agent (LCDA)** - Ensures all valuation reports meet regulatory compliance standards by cross-referencing legal requirements and automating documentation processes. \\
  * **Intangible Asset Valuation Agent (IAVA)** - Specializes in valuing intellectual property, goodwill, patents, trademarks, and other intangible assets using advanced algorithms and industry-specific benchmarks. \\
  * **Data Cleansing and Integration Agent (DCIA)** - Prepares datasets by cleaning, integrating, and organizing financial and operational data to ensure accuracy in valuation models. \\
  * **Valuation Report Generator Agent (VRGA)** - Automates the creation of comprehensive valuation reports with detailed analysis, charts, and justifications based on inputs from other AI agents.

</WRAP>
