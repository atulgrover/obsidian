====== Agentic Services for Investors ======
**RESOLUTION APPLICANTS** - Resolution applicants propose plans for reviving or restructuring the corporate debtor’s operations. Their plans are evaluated by the CoC for feasibility, compliance with legal norms, and alignment with creditor interests. Insolvency Applicants can benefit from specialized AI agents designed to assist them in navigating the complexities of submitting resolution plans. These agents help applicants understand eligibility criteria and guide them through the process of preparing and submitting compliant plans. By auto-checking compliance with Section 29A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and other evaluation criteria, these agents ensure that applicants meet all necessary legal and procedural requirements.
</WRAP>

<WRAP column 75%>
  * **Predictive Insolvency Analytics (PIA) Agent** - This AI agent uses historical financial data and industry-specific trends to predict potential insolvency risks early. It helps investors assess the financial health of companies before they enter insolvency proceedings.\\
  * **Document Discovery and Compliance Agent (DDC)** - Automates the scanning and analysis of legal documents, contracts, and compliance records, identifying anomalies or non-compliance issues quickly. It ensures that all regulatory filings are accurate and up-to-date.\\
  * **Real-Time Financial Monitoring Agent (RTFM)** - Provides real-time updates on key performance indicators (KPIs) of a distressed business, enabling creditors and resolution applicants to make informed decisions during the resolution process.\\
  * **Fraud Detection and Forensic Analysis Agent (FDFA)** - Leverages big data and AI to identify fraudulent activities or mismanagement by analyzing patterns in financial transactions, director actions, and corporate filings.\\
  * **Valuation and Asset Optimization Agent (VAO)** - Uses machine learning models to provide accurate valuations of assets under insolvency, helping investors optimize recovery strategies or make competitive bids for acquisition.\\
  * **Integrated Resolution Workflow Agent (IRW)** - Streamlines the entire insolvency resolution process by integrating communication between stakeholders, tracking deadlines, and automating workflows to reduce delays.\\
  * **Global Insolvency Benchmarking Agent (GIB)** - Compares insolvency cases across jurisdictions using global best practices, offering insights into successful restructuring strategies from similar cases worldwide.\\
  * **Negotiation Support Agent (NSA)** - Assists in negotiations by simulating various scenarios for debt restructuring or asset sales, providing data-driven recommendations for optimal outcomes.
</WRAP>
