====== Agents for Applicants ======
**IBC APPLICANTS** - Insolvency applicants initiate CIRP by filing an application with the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT). They can be financial creditors, operational creditors, or even corporate debtors themselves seeking resolution of their financial distress.
</WRAP>

<WRAP column 75%>
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:applicant_agents:ddva|Default Detection and Verification Agent (DDVA)]]** - This agent uses machine learning to analyze financial data and detect defaults by debtors, ensuring that insolvency applications are filed with accurate and verified information. It automates the process of gathering evidence to support default claims. \\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:applicant_agents:icfaa|Insolvency Case Filing Automation Agent (ICFAA)]]** - Automates the preparation and submission of insolvency petitions by generating necessary documents and ensuring compliance with legal requirements. It streamlines communication with courts and stakeholders. \\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:applicant_agents:cima|Creditor Information Management Agent (CIMA)]]** - Manages and analyzes creditor data, ensuring that all relevant creditors are notified and involved in the insolvency process. It helps in tracking creditor claims and communications. \\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:applicant_agents:fara|Financial Analysis and Risk Assessment Agent (FARA)]]** - Conducts detailed financial analysis of the debtor to assess the likelihood of recovery and potential risks associated with the insolvency process. It provides insights into the debtor's financial health and potential restructuring options. \\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:applicant_agents:lpclra|Legal Precedent and Case Law Research Agent (LPCLRA)]]** - Assists in researching relevant legal precedents and case laws related to insolvency, helping applicants understand potential legal challenges and opportunities. It provides data-driven insights to support legal arguments. \\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:applicant_agents:scma|Stakeholder Communication and Management Agent (SCMA)]]** - Facilitates effective communication with all stakeholders, including creditors, courts, and the debtor, ensuring that all parties are informed and aligned throughout the process. It automates routine communications and updates. \\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:applicant_agents:rpea|Resolution Plan Evaluation Agent (RPEA)]]** - Analyzes and evaluates proposed resolution plans submitted by resolution applicants, providing insights into their feasibility and potential outcomes. It helps in identifying the most viable resolution options. \\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:applicant_agents:cdpa|Court Decision Prediction Agent (CDPA)]]** - Uses predictive analytics to forecast potential court decisions based on historical data and legal precedents, helping insolvency applicants prepare for different outcomes. It provides strategic advice on case preparation and argumentation.

</WRAP>
