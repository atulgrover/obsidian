====== Investors/Resolution Applicants - Agents ======
**RESOLUTION APPLICANTS** - Resolution applicants propose plans for reviving or restructuring the corporate debtor’s operations. Their plans are evaluated by the CoC for feasibility, compliance with legal norms, and alignment with creditor interests. 
</WRAP>

<WRAP column 75%>
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:investor_agents:pia|Predictive Insolvency Analytics (PIA) Agent]]** - This AI agent uses historical financial data and industry-specific trends to predict potential insolvency risks early. It helps investors assess the financial health of companies before they enter insolvency proceedings.\\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:investor_agents:ddc|Document Discovery and Compliance Agent (DDC)]]** - Automates the scanning and analysis of legal documents, contracts, and compliance records, identifying anomalies or non-compliance issues quickly. It ensures that all regulatory filings are accurate and up-to-date.\\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:investor_agents:rtfm|Real-Time Financial Monitoring Agent (RTFM)]]** - Provides real-time updates on key performance indicators (KPIs) of a distressed business, enabling creditors and resolution applicants to make informed decisions during the resolution process.\\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:investor_agents:fdfa|Fraud Detection and Forensic Analysis Agent (FDFA)]]** - Leverages big data and AI to identify fraudulent activities or mismanagement by analyzing patterns in financial transactions, director actions, and corporate filings.\\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:investor_agents:vao|Valuation and Asset Optimization Agent (VAO)]]** - Uses machine learning models to provide accurate valuations of assets under insolvency, helping investors optimize recovery strategies or make competitive bids for acquisition.\\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:investor_agents:irw|Integrated Resolution Workflow Agent (IRW)]]** - Streamlines the entire insolvency resolution process by integrating communication between stakeholders, tracking deadlines, and automating workflows to reduce delays.\\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:investor_agents:gib|Global Insolvency Benchmarking Agent (GIB)]]** - Compares insolvency cases across jurisdictions using global best practices, offering insights into successful restructuring strategies from similar cases worldwide.\\ \\
  * **[[insolvency_agents:agents:investor_agents:nsa|Negotiation Support Agent (NSA)]]** - Assists in negotiations by simulating various scenarios for debt restructuring or asset sales, providing data-driven recommendations for optimal outcomes.
</WRAP>
